Talia Small Chandelier

The most petite chandelier in the popular Talia series, the Talia Small Chandelier by Julie Neill is a masterful fusion of shimmering, mouth-blown glass and premium grade metal. Its 27 swirled glass orbs are artfully suspended from a metal basket, creating the illusion of an illuminated cloud suspended from your ceiling. The glass globes, in three sizes, are meant to be attached by hand, allowing you to personalize your chandelier to your needs. Perfectly sized for smaller entryways, dining rooms, or other intimate spaces, the Talia Small Chandelier adds a final touch of glamour that enhances both modern and traditional decor.

Berkeley strikes the perfect balance between plush seating and a clean, modern aesthetic. Hand-assembled by skilled artisans in North Carolina, the perfectly proportioned sofa is flanked by wide arms that leave extra room for resting. Crafted of hardwood certified sustainable by the Forest Stewardship Council® (FSC), the durable frame is covered in soft jade green performance velvet with neat stitching to keep the look clean yet casual. CB2 exclusive.

  • Frame is hand-assembled in North Carolina of FSC®-certified solid hardwood
  • Frame is kiln-dried for support and to prevent warping
  • Performance velvet
  • Flexolator suspension system made from recycled metal ensures a comfortable and supportive sit
  • High-density foam cushions wrapped in feathers and down
  • Removable recessed legs
  • Performance fabric features a protective finish that resists stains and fading while maintaining a soft hand
  • Tear sheet
  • Made in USA of Domestic and Imported Materials

    Fabric Care Instructions

    Blot spills immediately with a clean, absorbent cloth. Spot clean with a water-free stain remover. For stubborn or set stains, work with a professional upholstery cleaning service. (Cleaning Code Reference: S)

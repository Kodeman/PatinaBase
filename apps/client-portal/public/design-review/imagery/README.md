## Exact new prompts

### Dining

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: conceptual imagery for Patina interior-design client portal prototype, not an actual project. Create a photorealistic editorial interior photograph of a serene Midwestern dining room, solid walnut oval table, sculptural oak chairs with oatmeal linen upholstered seats, warm ivory limewash walls, a simple dark bronze pendant, large daylight window with views of green trees. Material character and sunlight are the point, inviting and lived-in but uncluttered. Natural tones walnut, ivory, moss olive, aged bronze. Wide landscape composition 3:2, eye-level, believable architecture, magazine-quality natural light, visible wood grain and fabric texture. No people, no text, no logos, no watermarks. This will pair with a warm daylight living-room concept already made for Patina. Return local image file for website use.

### Materials

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original conceptual material-board image for Patina interior-design portal UI prototype. An overhead editorial photograph of a small interior designer's material composition on a warm ivory plaster tabletop: a substantial walnut wood sample showing rich grain, a folded oatmeal linen upholstery swatch, a smaller moss-olive woven fabric sample, a pale honed limestone piece and a small aged bronze finish sample. Arrange naturally with slight overlaps and honest soft shadows, refined but tactile, no ornate styling. Soft raking daylight, natural restrained ivory/walnut/olive palette, macro textile and wood detail. Wide 3:2 landscape frame. No text, labels, people, logos, watermark or UI. Not a real client sample record, only design concept imagery.

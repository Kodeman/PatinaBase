Designing for Two People

Leah and her associate.

Leah · Solo Practitioner

Middlewest Studio. Solo for now. 4–6 active projects at any time. She wins the work, designs the work, manages procurement, coordinates installation. The portal must wear every hat with her without forcing her into multiple tools.

"I need one place where the Chen project lives. Their proposal, their invoices, the Sawkille PO, the rug decision waiting on Lin, the dining table delivery on Friday. All of it. Same place."

Andrea · Senior Associate

Joins Leah in 8 months. Will manage 3 projects independently while supporting Leah on 2 larger builds. Needs scoped access — her projects fully, Leah's projects in read-only when relevant. Expects mobile parity.

"I want my own pipeline visible at a glance. But I also want to learn from how Leah handles hers — without interrupting her workflow to ask."

The MVP supports both single-designer and multi-designer studios from day one. Studio = the organizational unit. Each designer has scoped access. Project-level permissions control who can edit financials, sign change orders, access vendor terms.

Screen 02 · Project Activation

From signed proposal to live project.

When a client signs a proposal, the system already knows everything: rooms in scope, FF&E items, phases, fees, payment milestones. Activation isn't data entry — it's three lightweight confirmations. Most designers complete it in under 15 minutes.

Proposal Signed
Step 1 · Confirm Scope
Step 2 · Set Schedule
Step 3 · Add Vendors
Project Live
Step 1 · Confirm Scope
Verify imported data

Rooms, FF&E items, fees, milestones — all pre-filled from signed proposal. Designer reviews and confirms or edits. Auto-creates room cards, FF&E line items at "Specified" stage.

Step 2 · Set Schedule
Phase dates & milestones

Set phase start dates from kickoff. System calculates phase end dates from proposal duration. Designer can adjust per phase. Approval gates auto-attach.

Step 3 · Add Vendors
Assign known sources

Optional. Pre-assign vendors to FF&E items where the designer has standing relationships. Other items can be assigned during procurement. Issues PO numbers automatically.

Click "Activate Project" → kickoff invoice generates → client gets welcome email → project appears in Active list.

Screen 08 · Team Collaboration

Studios, not just designers.

From day one, the MVP supports multi-designer studios. Studio is the org unit. Each project has a lead designer, optional support designers, and assigned vendors. Permissions cascade — studio owners see everything, support designers see what they're assigned to.

★
Studio Owner
Full access to all projects. Manages team roster, vendor relationships, billing, studio settings. Can reassign projects between designers.
◈
Lead Designer
Full access to projects they own. Can edit financials, sign change orders, manage FF&E procurement. Read-only on other team members' projects when shared.
⟡
Support Designer
Scoped access to assigned projects only. Can edit tasks, log hours, capture products. Cannot modify financials or sign change orders.
⌂
Vendor / Supplier
External user with access only to their assigned POs. Can update order status, ETA, ship dates. Cannot see other vendors' pricing or project totals.
◇
Client (Read-Only)
Sees their own project at the visibility tier the designer set. Submits decisions, signs change orders, views progress. Cannot see costs or vendor details unless permitted.
◯
Bookkeeper / Accountant
Read-only access to financials across all projects. Cannot modify project data. Exports for tax filing and reconciliation.

Roles can be assigned per project, not just at the studio level. Andrea might lead the Olsen project but support Leah on the Whitfield. Permissions follow the assignment, not the person.

Build Plan

Twelve weeks to ship.

The MVP additions ship across three sprints. Existing portal stays untouched and operational throughout — these screens add to the system, they don't replace anything. Pilot with Leah's solo workflow first, then onboard Andrea once she joins the studio.

Sprint 1 · Weeks 1–4
Foundation

Project List · Project Activation Wizard · Project Detail (read-only). Database schema for projects, rooms, tasks, FF&E items. Designer Portal API integration for proposal-to-project conversion.

Sprint 2 · Weeks 5–8
Operations

FF&E Procurement with 8-stage pipeline · Project Financials with 4-column tracker · Decisions Workflow with reminder system. Edit Mode unlock on Project Detail. PO generation and vendor notification.

Sprint 3 · Weeks 9–12
Collaboration

Change Orders with approval chain · Team Collaboration with multi-designer permissions · Vendor portal access · Client-view filtering · Audit logs · Mobile parity for site updates.

Pilot scope: Middlewest Studio + 2 additional design studios. 8–12 active projects. 3-month pilot before broader rollout.

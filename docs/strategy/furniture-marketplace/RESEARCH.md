## The business decision

A maker can buy online presence, a better trade operation, or profitable new demand. Those are different purchases with different alternatives, budget owners and economics. Patina should initially test a connected trade desk for repeatable-collection makers already serving designers, with optional discovery after actual demand is demonstrated.

The recommendation is conditional. Current company canon is studio-first; maker subscriptions would expand it. Current V14 margin comes from the maker trade discount while preserving studio markup. New software and effective commission models must be reconciled with that policy before an external offer. This research changes no adopted strategy or production behavior.

## What research changed

- Shopify added foundational B2B to inexpensive plans in April 2026. A generic trade portal needs a stronger case.
- Seven real furniture channels show widely different practices: Eastvold's custom variation; Loll's COM and freight rules; Blu Dot's omnichannel trade service; Stickley's emailed corporate-trade POs; Bernhardt's existing inventory and ordering; Room & Board's bundled coordination; OFS's material- and capacity-dependent timing.
- Roomle already connects furniture configuration to pricing and order data. Patina must demonstrate outcome, project reuse or implementation advantage rather than claim empty competitive space.
- Faire, JOOR and Material Bank provide different ways to combine software, acquisition and existing relationships. Attribution and representative ownership belong in the contract and product.
- Manufacturer gross margins are neither a seller's incremental order contribution nor a commission budget. The deck uses an explicit contribution hypothesis and tests it.
- The average seller illustration falls short of the $299 offer. More workflow value, a busier segment or a different price needs to be demonstrated.
- Base company economics can produce revenue growth while consuming roughly $732k of modeled cash deficit over three years. Support, activation, retention, designer demand and fees matter more than a large directory.

## Source and assumption discipline

Published vendor price or capability is evidence about the alternative. It is not evidence that Patina can charge a similar price, convert a buyer, retain an account, save a measured hour, or generate an incremental order. Real companies in the research are examples, not confirmed prospects.

Every Patina price, funnel, workload, conversion, retention, order value, margin, budget and pilot threshold is explicitly hypothetical. Historical financial reports are tied to their reporting scope and period. Market reports preserve their claim-level qualifications and links. The primary-source set was checked October 13, 2026; live pages can change.

No interviews, sales pipeline, verified maker commitments, measured catalog labor, launch budget or active designer-project count were supplied. The model starts with zero accounts on both sides, includes its own proposed designer acquisition, and excludes other Patina revenue. A named target list and customer records are needed before interpreting the scenarios as a forecast.

## The next evidence to buy

Recruit economic buyers around live work. Ask for recent quote/order documents, actual paid software/services, an order-level contribution calculation, the latest correction bill, and who owns the channel relationship. Sell a bounded written-price pilot; time maker, designer and Patina work separately; log refused and failed accounts; require repeated unaided use and full-price renewal. Review fulfillment and retention beyond the initial eight-week work period.

The deck recommends a staged $25k initial commitment and a named stop owner. Seven-of-ten renewal and other small-sample gates are decision rules, not validated retention rates or statistical proof. Failure should change the offer, segment or business direction before a broad build.

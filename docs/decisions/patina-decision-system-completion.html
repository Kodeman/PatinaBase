01 — Why this matters

A decision is the unit that moves a project forward.

A designer proposes options — a sofa, a stone, a layout, a budget change, a phase sign-off. The client weighs them and chooses. That choice is recorded — and it releases procurement and unlocks the next phase.

Every dollar ordered and every deadline kept passes through this loop. If decisions stall, the whole project stalls. So the decision system isn't a feature — it's the backbone.

02 — The intended lifecycle

Six steps, one loop.

This is the journey a decision is meant to take. Five of the six steps are built and working. One — the middle — was never built.

1

Create

Designer composes options, prices, a recommendation.

2

Send

Decision is delivered to the client.

3

Review

Client compares, asks, weighs the trade-offs.

4

Modify

Refine the options as the conversation evolves.

5

Decide

Client selects & e-signs; the choice is recorded.

6

Unblock

Procurement & the next phase are released.

Step 4 is dashed for a reason — we'll come back to it.

03 — Where we are today

The foundation is genuinely strong.

This is not a greenfield build. A real, working spine already exists across the database, both web portals, and a daily automation. Credit where due:

  • ✓Designer compose — multi-option form, 7 decision types, pricing, cost & lead-time deltas, recommendations, draft vs. send.
  • ✓Resolution record — a polished detail view: chosen option, value, response time, override audit, discussion.
  • ✓Client review & e-sign — select, note, and electronic consent for budget / approval / substitution choices.
  • ✓Atomic feed-through — selecting an option auto-unblocks the linked FF&E items in one transaction.
  • ✓Override-with-consent, comments, reminders, analytics — all present and wired.

A
The missing middle

A decision can be created. It can be answered. It cannot be changed.

There is no edit screen and no update path. Once a decision exists, its title, context, options, deadline, and blocking status are frozen. A draft can't be revised and then sent — it has to be deleted and rebuilt from scratch.

CostStep 4 of the loop simply doesn't exist. Designers delete & recreate to fix a typo or add an option — losing the thread, comments, and history.
In the code
routes  new · [id] · analytics · [id]/edit ✕ missing
mutations  useCreateDecision
  useUpdateDecisionStatus (status only)
  useUpdateDecision ✕ · useDeleteDecision ✕
What it should be

Reuse the existing DecisionOptionBuilder as an edit form; add useUpdateDecision / useDeleteDecision; surface Edit and Publish draft on the detail page, and re-notify the client on a material change.

B
The silent loop

Nobody gets told anything.

The notification types decision_required and decision_overdue are defined — and referenced nowhere. No alert when a decision is sent, goes overdue, or is answered. And no realtime: a designer must refresh to discover the client responded.

CostClients don't know a choice is waiting on them; designers don't know it landed. The loop runs on luck and manual checking.
Defined, never fired
notifications.ts  | 'decision_required'
              | 'decision_overdue'
grep → 0 references that emit them
realtime subscriptions → none
What it should be

Emit decision_required on send, decision_overdue on lapse, and a new decision_resolved back to the designer — through the notification center (in-app + email + preferences). Subscribe to live updates so responses appear instantly.

C
Unenforced integrity

The rules exist on paper, not in the system.

  • !Status is a free-for-all. Nothing stops a decision jumping responded → pending → expired in any order. No transition guard.
  • !No history. Edits and status changes overwrite silently — there's no audit of what changed, when, or by whom.
  • !"Blocking" is decorative. A decision flagged blocks_procurement doesn't actually block procurement. The label is informational only.
CostA designer marks a decision as blocking the purchase order — and the order can still be placed anyway. The system promises a guardrail it doesn't enforce.
What it should be

A DB-level transition guard, a decision_events history table, and real enforcement: a pending blocks_* decision halts procurement and phase advancement, surfaced in the procurement UI.

D
Incomplete surfaces

The "ecosystem" stops at the browser.

iOS

API without a face

A DecisionsAPIClient ships in the app — but there's no screen to list, view, or answer a decision on mobile. The data path exists; the experience doesn't.

Extension

Capture that dead-ends

The extension captures products from any vendor site — but offers no way to send one into a decision as an option. Capture and decide are disconnected.

Rooms

Decisions float free

Decisions link to a project and phase, but not to a room. The room scans from iOS and the choices made for that room never meet.

CostThe pitch is a connected ecosystem — capture on the web, scan on the phone, decide together. Today those threads never tie together through a decision.
E
Polish & resilience

The seams show.

  • ›The primary CTA misroutes. "+ New Decision" drops you on the clients list, not a composer.
  • ›Auth-expiry shows a raw error. A stale session yields "Decision not found" + a generic error toast instead of a clean re-login — while the dashboard reads a misleading 0 open / 100% resolved.
  • ›Help content is unauthored. Every decisions tooltip & empty-state logs No content found.
  • ›Placeholder copy ships. The clients header still reads "PLACEHOLDER pending Leah review."

05 — The critical work

Four phases. Web loop first.

Sequenced so the highest-leverage, lowest-risk work — closing the web loop — ships first, then radiates out to notifications, mobile, and the wider ecosystem.

Phase 1

Close the web loop

A complete create → modify → record loop
  • Edit & delete decisions (the missing middle)
  • Fix the "+ New Decision" entry point
  • Auth-expiry resilience & honest states
  • Reopen / extend expired decisions
Effort · S–M  |  Risk · Low

Notify & enforce

Decisions that chase themselves
  • Fire required / overdue / resolved alerts
  • Realtime live updates
  • Transition guard + history audit
  • Make "blocking" actually block
Effort · M  |  Risk · Med

iOS decision surface

  • Push notifications
  • Link room scans → decisions
Effort · M–L  |  Risk · Med
Phase 4

Capture → decide

The ecosystem, joined up
  • Extension "send as decision option"
  • Product FK + room linkage
  • Templates & bulk ops
  • Signed approval record (compliance)
Effort · L  |  Risk · Med
06 — The ask
Build step four, then make the loop speak.

Phase 1 is small, low-risk, and finishes the lifecycle the product already promises — it's the obvious first commitment. Phase 2 turns a working loop into a self-driving one. Phases 3–4 extend the same proven model to mobile and capture, turning "ecosystem" from a pitch into a fact.

Recommended: greenlight Phase 1 now; scope Phase 2 in parallel. Everything is additive — no rebuild of the strong foundation that already exists.

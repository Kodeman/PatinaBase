AR placement happens inside a room context. The designer lead IS the room project. This document maps every intersection — showing how the Room System threads through the entire application.

Integration 1

Daily Room ↔ Room System

The Daily Room home screen IS the room system's primary interface. Room chips drive the feed. Products carry spatial context from the selected room. "Add" goes directly to that room. The two systems are inseparable.

Room → Feed

Room drives the content

Tapping a room chip triggers GET /api/feed/{room_id}. The entire product list, match scores, spatial context text, and filter categories are specific to that room. Different room = completely different feed.

Feed → Room

Feed builds the room

Every "+ Add" button opens the Add-to-Room sheet with the current channel's room pre-selected. One tap confirms. The room's item count, budget, and avg match update in real-time. The room chip badge decrements as new picks are consumed.

No Rooms Yet

First-time empty state

When user has zero rooms, Daily Room shows the story zone (content hook) above a warm empty state: "Your rooms are waiting — scan a room and we'll fill this feed." The whole value proposition requires a room.

New Room Created

Instant feed channel

After scan + name completes, new room appears as a chip in the Daily Room with a "Just scanned" badge. Tapping it shows an empty feed with "Generating your picks..." loading state. Nightly pipeline fills it, or fast-path generates top 10 immediately.

Three Entry Points

Scan starts from anywhere

The Companion's "Scan a room" nudge, the "+" button in Your Spaces gallery, and the Daily Room empty state all open the same Create Room sheet. Consistent entry, consistent experience.

Companion: Journey Mode

Scan transforms The Companion

During LiDAR scanning, The Companion morphs from Resting to Journey Mode — showing completion %, current step, and coaching text. This is the only time The Companion changes shape, making scanning feel like a guided journey, not a technical process.

Post-Create Routing

Where you go after naming

After saving the room: navigate to Daily Room with the new room chip selected. If this is the user's first room, this is their first real feed experience. For additional rooms, the chip appears with a "Just scanned" badge.

Cross-Feature Map

Every Room Touchpoint

Seventeen points where rooms connect to other features. Each one is a moment where room context enriches the experience or where user action enriches the room.

1
Daily Room → Room Chips
Room drives the feed content

Room chips at top of home screen. Each scanned room = a content channel. Switching reloads the entire product feed scored for that room's dimensions, orientation, and existing items.

2
Daily Room → Spatial Context
Room data creates "why it fits"

Every product card carries room-specific text: "South-facing light will warm this walnut grain." Generated from rooms.dimensions + rooms.features.orientation + products.material_tags.

3
Daily Room → Add-to-Room
Feed card → Room item

"+ Add" on any feed card opens the Add-to-Room sheet. The room the card was scored for is pre-selected. One tap adds. Toast confirms. Feed continues — zero interruption.

4
Daily Room → Pairing
Items already in room inform pairings

"Pairs with your saved Cherry Coffee Table — matched wood tones." Cross-references room_items for the active room against designer_feedback.pairing_product_id.

5
Product Detail → Room Context
Room context travels with the tap

Tapping a product card passes room_id to the detail screen. Spatial context text and the Companion nudge ("Place in your room") both reference the source room.

6
AR → Room Scan Mesh
Room provides the spatial stage

AR placement loads the room's USD/USDZ scan data as the spatial mesh. Floor planes, walls, and detected objects from the scan enable furniture snapping and collision detection.

7
AR → Room Item Save
"Save View" adds to room

"Save View" in AR writes the product to room_items with source: "ar_placement". The room detail updates. The multi-item tray shows all items already in this room.

8
Room Detail → Budget Tracking
Room economics drive designer nudge

Room budget computed from SUM(products.price_cents) in room_items. Bar shows progress vs. quiz budget range. At 150%, Companion nudges designer.

9
Room Detail → Suggested Next
Category gaps drive next recommendation

"Your seating and surfaces are set. A rug would ground the arrangement." Generated from category coverage analysis of existing room items vs. complete room archetype.

10
Cross-Room → Move/Copy
Items flow between rooms

Long-press any item to move or copy between rooms. Move deletes from source. Copy creates new room_items entry with source: "cross_room". Spatial context regenerates for destination.

11
Cross-Room → Style Coherence
Aesthete Engine scores harmony

"Your rooms share natural materials and warm tones." Score computed from tag overlap across room compositions. Visible in Cross-Room View and included in designer lead package.

12
Profile → Room Gallery
Rooms shown as profile section

Profile's "Your Rooms" section shows horizontal scroll of room cards with scan thumbnails. Tap navigates to Room Detail. Room count shown in stats row.

13
Designer Consult → Room Attach
Rooms auto-attached to lead

Designer consultation form shows room chips for selection. Rooms with items are pre-selected. Lead package includes scan data, items, budget per room, and engagement data.

14
Notifications → Room Context
Pushes reference specific rooms

"3 new pieces for your Living Room" — push notifications reference the room with highest recent engagement. Tapping opens Daily Room with that room chip selected.

15
Dwell Time → Room Channel
Per-room behavioral intelligence

Dwell events tagged with room_context — the active room channel. Time-per-channel tells the Aesthete Engine which room is the active project. Drives push priority and notification targeting.

16
Re-Scan → Item Preservation
Non-destructive spatial update

Re-scanning a room updates dimensions and features but preserves all saved items. Spatial context text regenerates. Items flagged if they no longer fit updated dimensions.

17
QR Auth → Room Data Flows
Rooms visible in Designer Portal

QR-authenticated Designer Portal shows client's rooms as 3D previews with items, dimensions, and budget. The phone's room data is the same data Leah sees on studio.patina.cloud.

The room is the thread.

Seventeen integration points. Every major feature in the app either reads from room data, writes to room data, or both. The Daily Room feeds products from rooms. Recommendations are scored per room. AR loads room meshes. Budgets aggregate per room. Leads package room data. Notifications reference rooms. Dwell time is tracked per room channel.

The room isn't a feature you navigate to. It's the context that follows you everywhere. When you're browsing the Daily Room, you're inside a room. When you're evaluating a product, the room context traveled with you. When you're in AR, the room's walls are the stage. When you talk to a designer, the room IS the conversation starter.

Remove the room from Patina and nothing works. That's not a dependency — that's a design principle. Rooms are the connective tissue of the entire application.

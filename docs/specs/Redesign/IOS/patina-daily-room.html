Home Screen · Final Concept Design

The Daily Room

The A+C hybrid brought to life. A daily editorial story creates the morning ritual — the reason to open the app. Below it, a room-contextual product feed where every item carries spatial intelligence explaining why it fits this room specifically. One tap adds any product to the room it was made for. Every second of browsing trains the Aesthete Engine.

Screen Anatomy

Four Zones, One Scroll

The Daily Room is a single continuous scroll with four distinct zones. Each zone serves a different purpose, but together they create a feed that's worth returning to every day.

Zone 1 · Top

The Greeting

Date in DM Mono. "Your Daily Room" in Playfair. User avatar top-right. Time-aware — "Good morning" before noon, nothing after. Minimal. The date creates the daily freshness signal.

Zone 2 · Story

The Daily Story

Full-width card, 180px. Changes daily. Maker spotlight, seasonal collection, material deep-dive, or room transformation. New-dot indicator (Clay). Read-time badge. Tapping opens full-screen narrative with embedded shoppable products.

Zone 3 · Room Selector

Room Channels

Horizontal chips — each scanned room with its thumbnail, name, and "new picks" count. Switching rooms reloads the entire feed below with products scored and explained for that specific room. Context bar shows room dimensions, orientation, and window count.

Zone 4 · Feed

Room-Contextual Products

Vertical product cards with large hero images. Each card carries: match percentage, tier badge (Designer Selection vs. Style Match), maker provenance, spatial "why it fits" overlay, "Add" button targeting the selected room, and a contextual insight explaining fit. Dwell time tracked per card via viewport intersection.

Primary State

The Daily Room in Action

A returning user opens the app. The story is fresh. The room chips show new pick counts. The feed is alive with products that know about their south-facing windows and their saved coffee table.

Living Room Feed

Morning open. Daily story at top, Living Room selected. Feed shows products with spatial intelligence — "South-facing light warms this walnut" — and one-tap add.

Intelligence Layer

Dwell Time & Content Strategy

Dwell Capture

How It's Measured

Each product card fires a dwell_start event when >50% of the card enters the viewport and a dwell_end event when it exits or the user navigates away. Metadata includes: duration_ms, room_context (which room channel was active), feed_position, scroll_velocity at entry (fast scroll past vs. slow browse), expanded_insight (did they read the pairing suggestion?), and session_products_seen. Batched to API every 30 seconds.

Room Dwell

Channel Intelligence

Beyond per-product dwell, we track time per room channel. If a user spends 80% of their session in the Bedroom channel, the Aesthete Engine knows that room is the active project. This informs notification priority ("3 new picks for your Bedroom"), story selection, and push notification timing.

Daily Stories

Content Calendar

Monday: Maker Spotlight — artisan narrative with 2-3 embedded products
Tuesday: Material Deep Dive — "The Story of Reclaimed Oak"
Wednesday: Room Transformation — before/after with product list
Thursday: Design Principle — "Why Floating Furniture Changes Everything"
Friday: New Arrivals — weekly product drops
Weekend: Community — featured user rooms, seasonal styling

Re-engagement

Pull Mechanics

Badge count: "14 new picks" on app icon and top-right badge. Resets on scroll-through.
Room chip badges: Per-room new-pick counts create micro-goals ("I still have 5 new bedroom picks to see").
Story freshness: New-dot indicator (Clay) on unread stories. Read-time badge sets expectations.
Push: "3 new pieces for your Living Room — including one from a maker you saved last week."

Spatial Context

Why It Fits

Every product card carries a "why it fits" overlay on the image and an insight card below with spatial reasoning. These are generated from room scan data: dimensions, orientation, window placement, detected objects, and lighting conditions. Example: "108" width fits your long wall with 18" clearance" uses actual wall measurements from the RoomPlan scan.

Pairing Intelligence

Cross-Product Signals

The pairing suggestion below product cards shows items already saved to this room that complement the current product. "Pairs with your saved Cherry Coffee Table — matched wood tones." This drives add-to-room conversion by showing the composition taking shape. Powered by designer-taught pairing data from the designer_feedback.pairing_product_id field.

Interaction Specification

Gestures & Micro-interactions

Scroll

Vertical Feed

Natural iOS momentum scroll. Story card has parallax at 0.8x. Room chips stay pinned at top when scrolled past (sticky behavior). Feed cards have no snapping — pure fluid scroll.

Room Switch

Channel Animation

Tapping a new room chip: feed cards cross-fade (200ms), context bar text updates, filter chips reset to "All". Room chip fills Charcoal with spring animation. Subtle haptic (light impact) on switch.

Add Button

Add to Room

Tap "+ Add" → half-sheet rises with spring physics. Pre-selected room highlighted in Charcoal with ✓. Tap another room to switch. Sheet dismisses on tap-outside or swipe-down. Medium haptic on successful add.

Toast

Confirmation

After add: toast slides up from bottom (300ms spring), persists 3 seconds, includes "View →" link to room project. Feed doesn't scroll or change — zero interruption to browsing flow.

Story Card

Tap to Expand

Story card expands to full-screen with shared element transition — image scales up, gradient extends, title repositions. Back via swipe-right or ← button. Products in story have same "Add to Room" action.

Long Press

Quick Look

Long-press any product card image for a material close-up — zoomed texture detail with material name overlay. Haptic on entry (medium impact). Release to dismiss. Counts as a strong positive dwell signal.

Open it like a magazine.
Use it like a tool.

The Daily Room gives people a reason to open Patina every morning — the same way they open a favorite magazine. The daily story creates the ritual. The room channels create the relevance. The spatial intelligence makes every recommendation feel personally observed — not algorithmically guessed. And the one-tap "Add" turns passive browsing into active room building.

Behind it all, every second of browsing teaches the Aesthete Engine something new. Dwell time per product. Time per room channel. Scroll velocity past rejected items. Long-press on textures. Pairing suggestions accepted or ignored. The feed isn't just the product — it's the intelligence pipeline.

This is a home screen worth coming home to.

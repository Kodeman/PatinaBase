v0.4 — in hand, the paper is the screen.

Close · Esc

Everything from v0.1–v0.3 still works. New in v0.4 (ruling D12):

Full-bleed document
  • A document in hand fills the entire viewport. No charcoal surround, no page border or radius, no stacked edge — those are Desk-state devices, for objects ON the desk. In hand, you're IN the paper. Strict focus (D1), expressed spatially.
  • The drawer is the only persistent layer above the paper — the desk edge still showing under your work. Sheets, the log strip, popovers, and ⌘K are the only other overlays.
  • The spine and margin are sticky full-height rails. Put down, the timer, presence, and the margin items stay in reach no matter how long the FF&E table scrolls. Main content scrolls between them.
  • Pick up = raise to fill (scale-grow, ~270ms; reduced-motion: crossfade). Put down recedes to the Desk.
Also aligned with the Slice 1 build
  • The Desk headline is the date in Playfair italic, as shipped. Section labels carry the mini Strata Mark.
  • Stamps use R2 vocabulary (PRODUCTION / SHIPPED / RECEIVED…), STAGE_CONFIG canonical.
The rule
  • On the Desk: objects on a surface. Contrast, stacked edges, tabs.
  • In hand: the paper is your whole field of vision. Drawer below, overlays above, nothing else.

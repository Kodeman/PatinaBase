13of 16Procurement

The second signature does one precise thing

Signed lines become orderable.

Executing a furnishings authorization applies its immutable item snapshot to the existing project. It never creates a second project or reopens the Design Agreement.

Before signature

Specified · not authorized

Working selections can be budgeted, discussed, and revised. Procurement acts remain unavailable.

At signature

One transaction

Record consent, bind exact item snapshots, apply signed quantities and client prices, open the deposit payable.

After signature

Authorized · orderable

Only covered lines may enter quotes, purchase orders, and receiving. A changed quantity requires new authority.

The surprise: the build has overtaken the map

The portal-vs-desk gap matrix (dated June 14) calls the People book "a literal placeholder" and the whole authoring/CRM half "absent." Four days later, on the live app, that is no longer true. Walking it, I found the People Room built (a walk-in Room: a directory of everyone — clients, leads, makers, GCs, team — plus Threads, Nurture, Reviews, Portfolio, Outreach, and an Engine reconnection nudge), the decision composer built (the R55 sheet — kind picker Selection/RFI/Submittal/Sign-off/Punch, the subject taxonomy, whose-court, materialize-from-Library — ruled in this very session, already live), coordination / ball-in-court live in the document, and the full Document grammar (spine, Account band, The Work, FF&E by room, folio, letterhead instruments, colophon, margin, the timer). The gap matrix is stale; planning from it would mislead.

So this analysis is not the matrix's 103 "absent" rows. It's what actually breaks — or is missing — when you try to carry a real lead through to a client today.

The gaps, and how we close them

G1

Lead intake has no Desk / ⌘K front door

P0 · funnel front edge
What we sawFrom the Desk, ⌘K on new lead, new proposal, even new client returns only "Ask the Engine · ask & place" — there is no create/intake action. The funnel's first step (capture a lead, triage accept/decline/pass) has no obvious in-Document home; it lives in the old /portal/leads or behind People's "+ Add."
Why it mattersLead→client starts with a lead. If a designer can't intake or triage one without leaving the Document, the very first step breaks the model.
Close it: a ⌘K + Desk "new lead / capture" that births a Brief document on the Desk (R1 — a lead is the lead/Brief shape), and lead triage (Accept → Brief begins · Nurture · Pass) on the Desk and in the People Pipeline. The People Room already has the "Leads" filter and "+ Add" — promote intake to the Desk and ⌘K.
Maps to: R1 (engagement identity), the People Pipeline (just designed), gap-matrix "Lead Intake & Matching" (P1, portal-only actions).
G2

The proposal stage is read-only — no revise / follow-up / send

P0 · funnel mid
What we sawThe Desk need-line promises "Proposal expired — revise or follow up." Opening it, the Proposal · V1 section renders read-only (investment lines + "payment schedule to be confirmed") with no revise, resend, or follow-up affordance anywhere in the document — and none of the letterhead instruments (View as / Send a note) that the Project-stage document carries. The action the Desk advertises has no home.
Why it mattersThis is the conversion hinge of lead→client. A stalled/expired proposal is exactly where a designer must act — and the document offers no way to.
Close it: wire proposal authoring (the Drafting Room, Track 4) and a "revise · resend · follow up" instrument onto the Proposal section, and make the letterhead instruments (View as the client · Send a note) stage-consistent across Brief→Care, not Project-only. The Desk need-line's verb and the document's available act must match.
Maps to: Track 4 (R42–R45 proposal authoring), R27 (letterhead instruments), gap-matrix "Send Proposal / Revise Proposal" (P0).
G3

People aren't reachable from ⌘K

P1 · navigation
What we saw⌘K finds documents + ledgers + the Engine, but typing a person's name (Priya) returns only "Ask the Engine." You can reach a relationship only by walking into the People Room — there's no jump-to-person.
Close it: add people to ⌘K results (a "jump to [person] →" line), reusing the People directory query. ⌘K already pre-addresses ledgers; people are the missing noun.
Maps to: R38 (the Engine / ⌘K), the People Room directory.
G4

The timer offered a 36-hour, ~2,187 "quiet-minute" close-out

P1 · data integrity
What we sawOn opening a document, the log strip surfaced a close-out for a different doc — "Lee Townhouse – Kitchen R2 was in hand for 36h 09m … includes ~2187 quiet minutes" — i.e. an abandoned timer that ran ~36 hours, almost entirely idle, offered as a loggable entry.
Why it mattersWrite-first close-out (R20) is only honest if the proposed number is sane. A 36-hour idle log is a trust and billing hazard — exactly the kind of thing a designer would have to catch and discard manually.
Close it: guard abandoned timers — auto-pause on long idle / on session end, cap the chained-out duration, and have the close-out propose the active time (idle annotated, not summed). The 1-minute idle threshold (R10/I16) annotates; it should also bound a runaway timer.
Maps to: R20 (write-first close-out), I16 (the time system), D10 (idle annotates, never auto-trims — needs a runaway bound).
G5

Cold load flashes the old portal chrome

P2 · polish
What we sawNavigating to app.patina.cloud briefly paints the old zone nav (Today / Pipeline / Procurement / …) before resolving to the Desk.
Close it: resolve the /portal→/desk flip server-side (or render the Desk shell first) so the flipped studio never sees the retired chrome, even for a frame.
Maps to: R21 (the flip protocol), the dissolve staging.
G6

The decision composer has no obvious dismiss

P2 · UX
What we sawThe "New decision" composer didn't close on Esc or on backdrop-click; the only exit (Cancel) sits below the fold, past the option builder.
Close it: Esc-to-close + backdrop-dismiss + a quiet top close affordance, each with an unsaved-draft guard. (The composer itself is excellent — this is purely the exit.)
Maps to: R55 (the composer), D2 (nothing breaks through — applies to dismissal too).
G7

Seed / data hygiene muddies the surfaces

P2 · data
What we sawJunk proposal content (sdf, qw), a duplicated engagement (Smith Residence — Living Room (seed) and its (Copy)), and "Full Room Design" appearing three times in the In-Motion drafting strip.
Close it: a seed cleanup / de-dupe pass before the next pilot or demo, so the Desk and People reflect real shapes (and so duplicates aren't masking a real copy/activation bug).
Maps to: pilot data hygiene; verify the proposal-copy / activation path doesn't duplicate engagements.

The closure roadmap

Two P0s to make the loop clean; the rest in priority order.

First — P0
The funnel's two open ends. G1 lead intake/triage (a Desk + ⌘K "new lead" that births a Brief; triage on the Desk & People Pipeline) and G2 the proposal action (wire the Drafting Room + revise/resend/follow-up onto the Proposal section; stage-consistent letterhead instruments). These two make lead→client completable end-to-end inside the Document.
Next — P1
Reach & trust. G3 people in ⌘K (jump-to-person), G4 the runaway-timer guard (sane close-out, bounded idle). Small, high-value.
Then — P2
Polish & hygiene. G5 kill the old-chrome flash, G6 composer dismiss, G7 seed cleanup / de-dupe.
Also
Re-run the gap matrix. The June-14 sweep is stale (People, the composer, coordination are now built). A fresh pass keeps the roadmap honest — and would let us retire the matrix's "People is a placeholder" headline.

# Leah Session 05 — the glance sheet

> Status: prep · 2026-08-18 · pairs with `leah-session-05-founders-sitdown.html` (full script + capture)

**What today is.** Not status, not demo. Borrowing her expertise for the four biggest open calls, then asking her to put her face on the front of the company.

**The arc (~110 min).** Workflow 25 → MVP wedge 15 → Money 15 → Partners 10 → Client experience 15 → Founding story 15 → Close 5.

**How to run it.** Blocks 1–5 she talks, you listen. "Walk me through the last time," never "would you ever." Chase the flinch. Block 6 flips — that's your pitch, then co-write.

## The one question per block

1. **Workflow** — one real project, start to install: where did the hours go, and which phase makes her sigh?
2. **MVP** — one thing perfect at launch: capture/memory · taste recs · client-facing wow · procurement autopilot. Rank all four.
3. **Money** — react to v4: 4% client-paid project fee · 25% teaching pledge · 18% blended take. Does each survive her real clients and vendors?
4. **Partners** — what makes a maker Patina-grade? One she'd stake her name on, one she'd never allow. What do makers want that reps/1stDibs don't give?
5. **Client** — the moments (reveal, approval, the long wait, install): what should each feel like, and which belong on a phone? Would her clients scan a room?
6. **Story** — read her back her own blog draft ("That's why Kody and I started Patina…"). Still true? Then: comfort line on faces — bio · photo · video · recurring voice — and a cadence she can sustain.

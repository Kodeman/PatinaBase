## Proposed extensions and exceptions

- **FD-002 and FD-003:** Keep **Today** for a person's daily field work; clarify the project browse subtitle rather than calling Today a studio-wide overview. **FD-004 and FD-017:** Keep **Messages** for conversations. The existing copy gate in `FieldCopyAudit` remains untouched and must remain strict. Capture queues get their own state words.
- **FD-005 — exception:** The Work tile title `Hours` is also lowercased into an analytics property. Recommendation: keep the existing event value stable with a presentation-only separation before changing the tile to `Log time`; otherwise leave the rendered title alone. Do not change event taxonomy by accident.
- **FD-043 — exception:** `S4SavedTerminalScreen.transferLabel` falls through to `Queued to sync` for a local state as well as the queued state. Recommendation: distinguish `Saved on this device` from `Queued for upload` using the existing transfer phase while preserving raw enum values. `Uploading`, `Awaiting confirmation`, `Retry needed`, `Destination unconfirmed`, and server-confirmed destination must remain separate. **FD-048** replaces the tray's `placed · syncing` umbrella with `Placed · not yet confirmed`, because placement alone does not prove upload is active.
- **FD-038, FD-042 and FD-044:** Use **Resource library** only for the rendered library destination and confirmed title. A locally saved item is not server-confirmed. **FD-024:** The roster heading can use **Project directory**, without changing the underlying screen or contact rights. **FD-028:** Prefer `create a link` over internal `mint` wording. The lead list/status and new-project sheet remain unchanged: the designer portal's *creation-action* mapping does not license renaming every Field noun.
- **FD-094–FD-106:** **Photos & notes** are contextual evidence, not a site scan; keep the non-LiDAR explanation and **UNVERIFIED** warnings. Site scan review distinguishes retained local original, queued/uploading transfer, server receipt, retryable failure, and rejected transfer. Neither coverage nor an anchor estimate claims verified dimensions. General site-request proof and its consent/queue/return states are F1 (`FD-031–FD-034`); no approval or SMS consent may be inferred from a local draft.
- **FD-107–FD-114:** Permission strings in `scripts/generate_project.rb` remain truthful. Speech recognition may happen on the iPhone where supported, otherwise Apple transcribes the recording. Photos read/add permissions, microphone, camera, location, motion and Face ID retain distinct purposes. Do not edit generated Xcode settings directly.
- The shared `packages/types/src/field-config.ts` display labels belong to **L**, not Field ownership. If an F1/F2 row needs a change there, route it to L. No shared rendered PatinaDesignKit label was found that needs a change; it is deliberately unclaimed and the consumer Patina app is outside this inventory.

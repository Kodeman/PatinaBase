# Residential Project Workflow

The project operating context that tells interior-design teams what must happen next, who owns it, what evidence is required, and what downstream work an approval authorizes. Patina coordinates the record; the studio and contracted professionals retain responsibility.

## Journey

**Workflow Stage**:
One of the eleven canonical professional stages from Inquiry & Qualification through Closeout & Post-Occupancy. A studio may rename a project phase, but its canonical stage meaning remains stable.
_Avoid_: Document section, dashboard step

**Document Section**:
One of the seven narrative places in The Document: Brief, Discovery, Direction, Proposal, Project, Install, or Care. Sections present workflow state but do not own a parallel lifecycle.
_Avoid_: Workflow stage, phase status

**Workflow Track**:
The core, FF&E, or construction path through a stage. FF&E and construction may run in parallel and rejoin at site readiness.
_Avoid_: Department, team

**Gate**:
A server-evaluated entry or exit condition supported by authoritative evidence. A checked presentation item alone cannot make a gate true.
_Avoid_: Checkbox, reminder

**Evidence**:
A durable, attributable record that satisfies or explains a workflow requirement, such as an executed agreement, verified measurement, issued artifact, approval receipt, PO acknowledgement, or inspection report.
_Avoid_: Attachment

## People and Authority

**Project Party**:
A person or organization participating in the project with an explicit role, visibility, and contracted responsibility.
_Avoid_: User, contact

**Decision Lead**:
The one household representative authorized to submit the consolidated client response for a project.
_Avoid_: Primary commenter, project owner

**Co-Approver**:
A second household representative whose approval is required only when the executed agreement or a specific request says so.
_Avoid_: Commenter, follower

**Comment**:
Individual feedback attached to a specific artifact, decision, issue, or message. A comment never changes approval or commercial authority.
_Avoid_: Approval, sign-off

**Handoff**:
A sender, recipient, stage, expected response, due date, escalation path, and optional artifact reference that transfers responsibility for a next act.
_Avoid_: Notification

## Documents and Decisions

**Artifact Edition**:
The immutable version of a client-, vendor-, or project-facing record that a recipient actually reviewed or received.
_Avoid_: Live document, latest copy

**Approval Request**:
A consequential question tied to an artifact edition, authorized approver, due date, dependency, and disclosed cost, schedule, or lead-time impact.
_Avoid_: Poll, comment thread

**Changes Requested**:
An actionable client outcome that returns work for a new edition. It does not rewrite or erase the reviewed edition.
_Avoid_: Rejected

**Needs Discussion**:
A holding outcome that pauses the gate while the authorized participants clarify the decision. It is not approval or disapproval.
_Avoid_: Pending approval, rejected

**Overdue**:
A derived condition on a still-pending request whose due date has passed. It never grants authority or advances work automatically.
_Avoid_: Expired approval, auto-approved

## Commercial Work

**Procurement Rail**:
The single commercial path selected for a project: Patina merchant of record or studio-managed procurement. It determines contracting parties, funds flow, tax, returns, warranty, and risk responsibilities.
_Avoid_: Order type, payment method

**Project Selection**:
The project-owned identity of a proposed FF&E item, including room, quantity, configuration, price, readiness, and decision history. Board placements and authorization lines reference it without replacing it.
_Avoid_: Product, board item

**Review Edition**:
An immutable, nonbinding presentation of project selections that records exactly what the client saw and how authorized participants responded.
_Avoid_: Live board, authorization

**Authorization Line**:
The immutable quantity, configuration, price, terms, and commercial-rail snapshot that grants the stated authority after execution.
_Avoid_: Selection, client preference

**Production Release**:
The act that begins fabrication after every required acknowledgement, payment, drawing, sample, material, and dimension prerequisite is satisfied. It is distinct from client authorization.
_Avoid_: Ordered, approved

## Completion

**Site Readiness**:
The attributed determination that access, safety, prerequisite work, protection, utilities, inventory, and crews are ready for the planned installation scope.
_Avoid_: Substantial completion

**Closeout Book**:
The client-facing collection of final records, reconciliation, care information, warranties, manuals, service contacts, acceptance, and consent.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, project archive

**Service Case**:
A separately owned punch, claim, warranty, repair, or maintenance obligation that remains traceable after installation or operational closeout.
_Avoid_: New scope, generic task

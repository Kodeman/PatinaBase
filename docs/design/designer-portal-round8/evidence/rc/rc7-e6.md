## Product defects

- **RC7-E6-1 (P1, high confidence):** A transient owned-stack PostgREST 503/PGRST002 during the server-side role lookup routed a signed-in F01 designer to `/unauthorized`. `src/middleware.ts:25–31` consumes `data` from the role query but ignores `error`; failed read and no permitted role become indistinguishable. Preserve read failure separately from a verified forbidden identity; do not report a role denial when the DB read failed. The orchestrator reported a separate program-side fix at `28e9e624d`, not incorporated or tested in this E6 tree. The fixme is a deterministic proposed regression test, not a pass.
- **RC7-E6-2 (P2, high confidence):** Orders draft row opens the exact job, but return lands on the studio without reopening the Orders ledger or its focused row. `src/components/document/orders-ledger.tsx:316–320` closes and pushes the route without recording the opener in `rememberPaperOrigin`, unlike Post at `post-sheet.tsx:208–216`. Record the Orders origin (filter/row/scroll/focus) before navigating and restore it on job return. The `test.fixme` preserves the failing journey; no product source was changed.

- **RC7-E6-3 (P2, high confidence; static root cause, browser fixme not executed):** The stale Finish write reports “Refresh before saving” (`packages/supabase/src/hooks/use-spec-books.ts:373`), while `src/components/document/pieces/spec-fields-pane.tsx:695` only surfaces the error and does not refetch the peer row or show its value. The refused draft remains local to `SpecText` (`spec-fields-pane.tsx:279`); a manual page refresh discards it. The fixme asserts automatic row refetch, an inline alert naming the peer value, retained typed draft and focus, and retry against the new row version without a reload. The fix lane owns this product repair.

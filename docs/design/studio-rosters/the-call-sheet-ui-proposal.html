Act I · Where we are02

What was approved, and what this adds.

Nothing below reopens a settled question. The direction stands; this deck draws it.

Approved · the concept deck
  • Titles are orthogonal to tiers. Nine titles plus free text; owner / admin / member / guest untouched.
  • The rolodex is a studio asset. Studio-scoped people and companies, built by working rather than by typing.
  • The roster is the call sheet. One surface per project: studio side, client side, build & supply.
  • Three reach states. Account, field link, contact only — most of a crew never logs in.
  • Four phases, lowest blast radius first.
Added here · the drawing
  • Names on screens. Every surface sits on a real route, in a real frame.
  • Exact placements. Which sheet, which letterhead instrument, which room, which rail.
  • Every workflow drawn. Invite, title, promote, add, pick, kick off — start to finish.
  • The vocabulary fix. One name is already taken; slide 3 takes it back.
  • Seven new rulings (U1–U7) about the drawing, not the direction.
The six still open

R1 rolodex visibility · R2 member project visibility · R3 GC reach ceiling · R4 client-visible roster · R5 migration consent · R6 seats and billing. Nothing here presumes an answer. Where a screen depends on one, the screen says so on the glass — slide 17 carries a PENDING RULING R4 watermark for exactly that reason.

Close · The ask18

Seven rulings about the drawing.

The concept deck's six are still open and still yours. These seven come from putting it on paper — none of them changes the direction, all of them change a screen.

U1 · the rename

Call sheet ↔ call plan. The discovery-call checklist gives up the name and becomes the Call plan. Renames touch copy, routes, and one table. Yes or no.

U2 · per-row visibility

SHOW TO CLIENT, per person, or all-or-nothing? Only lives if R4 says the client sees a roster at all.

U3 · registry citizenship

The call sheet is the first document-scoped sheet. Does it earn a place in ⌘K and the drawer, or stay reachable only from the letterhead?

U4 · self-titling

May a member set their own title, or is titling an owner-and-admin act like tiering? Cheapest either way; pick one before the picker ships.

U5 · circles and squares

Global rule or roster-only? If global, every avatar in the portal that represents a company changes shape in one pass.

U6 · the default lens

Does the People Room open on MINE or STUDIO? STUDIO is the better product and the louder change for a solo designer.

U7 · the setup whisper

The day-one checklist on the Studio page. Is a derived to-do list allowed under "notes recede", or does it read as onboarding chrome?

And then

Rule on U1–U7 (and the concept's R1–R6), and Phase 1 starts with the Studio page on slide 5. Two additive columns, one title picker, one line under a name.

The concept deck asked whether a studio should know its crew. This one asks only where to put it — and the answer, every time, was somewhere the Document already had a drawer.

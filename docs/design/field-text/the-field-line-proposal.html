Act I · The thesis02

A tool the trades have to sign up for is a tool the trades ignore.

The Call Sheet ruled that a sub is reached by a field link, never by an account. This deck takes the next step: the text message is the whole interface for most of the people on a job, and it has to be dependable enough to finish work with.

The text is the reminder

One request, one answer, one receipt

A recognizable prompt, an obvious reply, and a receipt that tells the truth. Numbers over words. Never two clarifying questions in a row.

The link is the form

Anything longer becomes a page

Past two text segments, the message carries one patina.cloud link that is complete without a login. The link is the form; the text says why to open it.

The record is elsewhere

Text is a rail, not a ledger

Every effect lands through the same chokepoints the portal uses. A reply by text and a tap on the field page write the same row.

Make texting a dependable way to finish work, not a funnel into software.

Act II · What breaks, in the person's words05

"I replied YES. What did I just agree to?"

GPT-6 Astra read the shipped pipeline from the phone's side. Eight defects, each verified against source. The first four are about trust: the system says things that are not true.

Consent

YES grants every pending job on my phone

Consent is meant to be per engagement. The inbound handler updates every pending party row for the number, and START grants rows that were never asked. "Stop texting me" in plain words has no deterministic revocation path, despite the runbook.

sms-inbound/pipeline.ts:339–354 · grantConsent(onlyPending) keyed by phone
Receipts

"Got it, logged." But it was not.

When the database call fails, the person still receives a success receipt. The designer's composer clears on a send the endpoint reported as not sent, because the HTTP status is 200 either way.

sms-inbound/pipeline.ts:666–668 · sms-dispatch returns 200 with success:false
Links

"The link worked yesterday."

Every message with a link mints a fresh token and revokes the old one, before the quiet-hours check runs. A deferred send kills yesterday's link before the new one arrives in the morning. Rendered bodies with bearer links are stored in full.

_shared/sms.ts:275 create_field_link before :494 isQuietHours
References

"Which job is DONE 1 for?"

Digest menus are stored per phone conversation, so two projects texting the same contractor overwrite each other's numbered menu. A reply that comes a day late can close the wrong item on the wrong job.

Act II · Six principles07

Make texting a dependable way to finish work, not a funnel into software.

1

Consent in one word

The designer records the agreement in twenty seconds. The person confirms one named job with one reply. Nothing else sends first.

2

Stable references

Every prompt carries a short code that stays valid for the life of that decision. A late answer still lands on the right thing.

3

A person, always

Every menu reserves a way out to a named human. One clarifying question at most. No dead ends, no loops.

4

Truthful receipts

Sent, queued, deferred, saved, and failed are different words. Say the one that happened. Keep the draft when it did not.

5

Links that survive

One link per message, on our domain, no login. A link that expired is replaced by replying, not by calling the studio.

6

Operational only

Approvals, windows, deposits, day-of logistics. Never marketing. Client texting gets its own plainly worded consent at kickoff.

Act IV · Robustness and the pilot's evidence18

The pilot passes when these eleven things are seen to happen, not assumed.

Minimum pilot evidence
  • Two projects and two studios sharing one contractor's phone
  • STOP, then a new engagement is added
  • START from a number with no prior consent
  • A reply with an old ref code, days late
  • A database failure mid-effect
  • A duplicate inbound message id from Twilio
  • A stale or forwarded link
  • A provider failure with an error code
  • A daylight-saving boundary against quiet hours
  • An unknown sender and a wrong number
  • A photo upload that drops mid-way, on a flip phone and on mobile Safari
Already enforced in code

Idempotency on the Twilio message id. Quiet hours with deferral. Phone-global opt-out. Media size and type limits. Parse confidence gates at 0.8 and 0.5.

To add

Idempotency per trigger and template. A per-party send budget of one recurring and three event texts a day; excess folds into the next digest. A dead-end detector: two unanswered prompts escalate to a person.

Events to watch

sms_outbound_sent exists. Add prompt answered, escalated, link renewed, consent recorded by source, delivery failed by error code.

Act IV · Boundaries21

What we are not building.

A vendor portal or trade accounts

Field links and texts already reach the people who need reaching. Asking a sub to adopt our software is the failure we are designing around.

A marketing channel

The campaign is operational. Referrals, promotions, and re-engagement would need separate consent and put the registration at risk.

A second inbox for the designer

Outcomes land in the party sheet, the Desk, and the project record that already exist. No new place to check.

A conversationalist

The thread never argues, negotiates, or offers taste. Structured first, interpreted second, a person always.

The ask: approve Phase 0 as the next ticket set, use synthetic evidence with no live pilot, and limit RCS to sizing only.

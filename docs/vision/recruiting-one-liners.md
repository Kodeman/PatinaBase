# Patina — what it is to you

## To a designer

**The line.** Patina is the working document for your studio. One living page per client, from brief to install. It prompts and collects when you need it and gets out of the way when you don't.

**Why now.** It's built for the year you bring on your first designer or assistant and your workload doubles. Your clients see the same page you do — engaged every day, one agreed direction, fewer surprises.

**What it isn't.** Not a dashboard. Not a task manager. Not "AI." It's Designer-Taught Intelligence — a real designer's eye, starting with Leah's, that learns yours as you work.

**What we refuse.** We won't lock you into an expensive platform with hidden fees and unclear pricing. Pricing is one page and public. Your data exports. *(Price: pending V2.)*

**The ask.** Bring one live project. We set it up with you. If you find yourself noticing Patina after that, tell us — that's the bug.

## To a maker

**The line.** Patina puts your work in front of designers who are specifying for real clients, with your story on every piece.

**Why you.** We qualify makers by a designer's standard — heritage, material, the way a piece ages. "Patina-grade" means a designer would stake her name on it. Ten Midwest makers are already in the pipeline.

**How the money works.** Orders come through designer-led projects. *(Margin structure: pending V1 — do not quote a percentage until ruled.)*

**What it isn't.** Not a catalog site. Not a rep. Not a place where your piece sits next to a thousand imports.

**The ask.** Start with a handful of pieces, each with its provenance. We do the rest.

# Patina — the vision

> **v0.1 · 2026-09-01.** Settled in a five-question workshop (see `workshops/2026-09-01-vision-workshop.md`). Six rulings are still open (§7). Re-read this page whenever a feature is proposed, and re-cut it when a ruling lands.

## 1. What Patina is

**Designer-Taught Intelligence.** Patina works alongside real designers to surface the right items and the right work, at the right place and time.

One engine, three surfaces — **in strict order:**

1. **The Document** — the studio's working surface. The designer's tool. This is Patina for the next twelve months.
2. **The iOS app** — the studio's front door. A marketing and qualification instrument the studio owns; not a consumer product in its own right.
3. **The marketplace** — the till. Patina-grade Midwest makers, sold through designer-led projects.

Patina is a venture of **Middle West Studio** (Madison, WI). Two-person core: Kody (build / infra / ops) and Leah Kochaver (design / voice / teaching). *(Carried from `my-company.md`.)*

## 2. Who we serve

**A growing design studio at the moment it adds its first hands while its workload doubles.** Leah's studio first; then a pool of studios like hers, Ring 01 (Madison / Milwaukee) outward.

That is a trigger, not a demographic. The studio owner is delegating for the first time, her own hours are already spoken for twice, and the thing she cannot afford is a new system to learn. Acquisition is 1:1 through Leah's network — never SEO strangers.

- **Homeowners** are the *studio's* clients, arriving by word of mouth. The first homeowner beta cohort is Middle West's active design clients (Kody's words: "active Patina customers" — read as the studio's current clients; confirm). Patina does not sell to homeowners.
- **Makers** are the *studio's* vendors. Patina qualifies them ("Patina-grade") and carries the transaction.

Neither is Patina's customer in its own right — yet. The studio is.

## 3. How we make money

Two streams.

- **Floor — the studio subscription.** Studios pay for The Document. Leah's would; others will. (Price and tiers: open, §7.)
- **Upside — margin on furniture.** A piece sold through a designer-led project carries Patina margin. **This is where the first real dollar comes from.**
- **The Pledge** returns a share of *marketplace* margin to the designers who teach the engine. Working read: it rides on the upside stream and sits alongside the subscription — whether "you pay us and we pay you" survives Leah's ear is **open (V6)**. No Pledge language becomes public or contractual before counsel reviews it.

Superseded by this section: affiliate revenue (Jan spine); "designers never pay for their own client relationships" (v4 one-pager, Aug). The v4 numbers themselves (18% blended take · 4% client project fee · 25% Pledge) are still the working figures pending §7.

## 4. How we please our clients

**To the studio:** *you won't notice Patina.* It is not a place you go. It prompts and collects information when and where you need it, then gets out of the way. Success is that she doesn't notice — so we will never optimize the studio surface for engagement.

**To the homeowner:** *you're engaged every day, and you and your designer are looking at the same agreed direction.* Fewer surprises. The decision record is the relationship.

**To both:** pricing is one page, public, and stable. No lock-in, no hidden fees. Your data exports.

## 5. What sets us apart — next twelve months, ranked

1. **The Document.** One living document per engagement. No dashboards, no task manager, no tab bars. Truth-framing over taste-framing. Her own voice never qualifies. *(Description carried from `docs/design/the-document/`.)*
2. **The Pledge.** Teaching royalties back to designers — a covenant Houzz, Wayfair, and 1stDibs can't copy without rebuilding their business. *(Legal-gated until counsel signs off.)*
3. **Patina-grade makers.** Midwest heritage makers, qualified by a designer's standard, provenance carried on every piece.
4. **Capture.** Any product into the library in under ten seconds; a room scanned from a phone.
5. **The Engine.** Designer-Taught Intelligence — the long-term thesis, and the reason the ranking above exists. Not the wedge.

**What we refuse, and the customer will notice:** we will not lock studios into an expensive platform with hidden fees and unclear pricing.

## 6. Saying no to

Carried from `my-company.md` and reaffirmed:

- **The "AI" label.** Non-negotiable. It's Designer-Taught Intelligence.
- **Consumer-first thinking.** The homeowner is the studio's client. Any consumer push that outruns the studio count is drift.
- **Engagement metrics** as a success measure for the studio surface.
- **Tab / zone / dashboard UI, shadows, red/green status, badges.** One living Document, typography-first.
- **Funnel-spam growth.** 1:1 and relationship-driven.
- **Scope creep and side journeys.** A feature that doesn't serve §2's studio at §2's moment waits.
- **Launching to an empty room.** Slip rather than demo something broken.

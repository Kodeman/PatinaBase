v0.1 · cut 2026-09-01
Kody · Leah

01

What Patina is

Designer-Taught Intelligence. Patina works alongside real designers to surface the right items and the right work, at the right place and time.

One engine, three surfaces — in strict order

  1. 1

    The Document

    The studio's working surface. The designer's tool. This is Patina for the next twelve months.

  2. 2

    The iOS app

    The studio's front door — a marketing and qualification instrument the studio owns. Not a consumer product in its own right.

  3. 3

    The marketplace

    The till. Patina-grade Midwest makers, sold through designer-led projects.

Supersedes: "three equal surfaces" (my-company.md, June) · "AI-powered consumer app with lead-gen" (spine, January).

02

Who we serve

A growing design studio at the moment it adds its first hands while its workload doubles.

That's a trigger, not a demographic. The studio owner is delegating for the first time, her own hours are already spoken for twice, and the one thing she can't afford is a new system to learn. Leah's studio first; then a pool of studios like hers, Ring 01 outward. Acquisition is 1:1 through Leah's network — never SEO strangers.

Homeowners

The studio's clients.

Arrive by word of mouth. The first beta cohort is Middle West's active clients. Patina doesn't sell to them.

Makers

The studio's vendors.

Patina qualifies them — "Patina-grade" — and carries the transaction.

Neither is Patina's customer in its own right — yet. The studio is. Supersedes "The Aspirational Homeowner" as primary persona.

03

How we earn

Two streams. The first real dollar comes from margin on a furniture sale.

Floor

The studio subscription.

Studios pay for The Document. Leah's would; others will. Price and tiers: open (V2).

Upside

Margin on furniture.

A piece sold through a designer-led project carries Patina margin. Whose pocket it comes from: open (V1).

The Pledge

Returns a share of marketplace margin to the designers who teach the engine. Working read: it rides the upside stream alongside the subscription — whether "you pay us and we pay you" survives Leah's ear is open (V6). Nothing about it goes public or contractual before counsel reviews it.

Supersedes: affiliate revenue (spine) · "designers never pay for their own client relationships" (v4 one-pager, August). v4's figures — 18% blended take · 4% client project fee · 25% Pledge — remain the working numbers pending V1.

04

What we promise

To the studio

You won't notice Patina.

It isn't a place you go. It prompts and collects when and where you need it, then gets out of the way. Success is that she doesn't notice — so we never optimize the studio surface for engagement.

To the homeowner

You're engaged every day, on one agreed direction.

You and your designer are looking at the same settled thing. Fewer surprises. The decision record is the relationship.

To both: pricing is one page, public, and stable. No lock-in, no hidden fees. Your data exports.

05

What sets us apart — next twelve months, ranked

  1. 1

    The Document

    One living document per engagement. No dashboards, no task manager, no tab bars. Truth-framing over taste-framing. Her own voice never qualifies.

  2. 2

    The Pledge

    Teaching royalties back to designers — a covenant Houzz, Wayfair, and 1stDibs can't copy without rebuilding their business. Legal-gated: not for outside eyes until counsel signs off.

  3. 3

    Patina-grade makers

    Midwest heritage makers, qualified by a designer's standard, provenance carried on every piece.

  4. 4

    Capture

    Any product into the library in under ten seconds; a room scanned from a phone.

  5. 5

    The Engine

    Designer-Taught Intelligence — the long-term thesis and the reason this ranking exists. Not the wedge.

What we refuse, and the customer will notice: we will not lock studios into an expensive platform with hidden fees and unclear pricing.

—

Saying no to

  • The "AI" label.Non-negotiable. It's Designer-Taught Intelligence.
  • Consumer-first thinking.The homeowner is the studio's client. A consumer push that outruns the studio count is drift.
  • Engagement metricsas a success measure for the studio surface.
  • Tab / zone / dashboard UI, shadows, red/green, badges.One living Document, typography-first.
  • Funnel-spam growth.1:1 and relationship-driven.
  • Scope creep and side journeys.A feature that doesn't serve the studio at its moment waits.
  • Launching to an empty room.Slip rather than demo something broken.

### Changes accepted into this presentation

- Flag inconsistent buyer/contracting/instruction/payment arrangements. Design may continue, but the relevant commitment stays held pending a documented exception or aligned roles.
- Name cabinetry as a studio **design and package** service; identify the maker's contracted work acceptance as linked evidence, distinct from final studio service acceptance.
- Add no-response and returned-item acceptance examples with owners and dates, without deemed acceptance.
- Give excluded cabinetry a fixed, independent reference arrangement. Keep excluded coordination explicitly outside the studio service.
- Preserve Sam as the author of the fictional site verification. Reassignment cannot inherit his evidence; it holds the fabrication example.
- Route electrician instructions through the GC in the fictional site arrangement, and identify hardware/appliance supply responsibility.
- Add the missing appliance/services record; remove the issue's self-reference from its own contents.
- Make the one-package inheritance limitation explicit; only purchasing inherits in this study.
- Improve small labels, use single-column phone fields, move keyboard focus with paper jumps, distinguish saved draft from previewed arrangement across reloads, and announce acceptance changes without reading the whole insert.

### Design choices retained / deferred

- Moment and collaborator selectors remain **presentation comparison controls**. Laying every alternative state out in full would repeat the same package many times; this deck does not propose those selectors as portal navigation. The approved continuous paper remains the underlying approach.
- The fixture retains two named client signatories. An actual job setup needs its agreed signatory/decision rule; acceptance itself stays mandatory. This is disclosed beside the fixed requirement.
- Multiple package defaults/overrides, appointment/delegation evidence editing, supplier account access, real issuing and signed acceptance need a later detailed product round. They are not silently represented as implemented by the mockup.
- No contractual or professional-liability advice, production permission model or construction-standard compliance is claimed.

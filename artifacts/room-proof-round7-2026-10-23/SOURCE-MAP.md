## Public practice sources

Accessed 23 October 2026. These are businesses describing their own processes; they are not universal requirements or endorsements. No firm was contacted.

- [Ferris Custom Cabinetry — Our Process](https://www.ferriscabinetry.com/our-process): maker drawings, site checking, finish/hardware decisions and coordinated installation support keeping review, physical selections and site evidence together.
- [Cutting Edge Woodworking — Our Process](https://www.cuttingedge-woodworking.com/company/our-process): builder-associated field measurement, samples and engineering stages support named interface responsibilities.
- [Sagar Cabinets — Interior designers and joiners](https://sagarcabinets.com.au/interior-designers-joiners-custom-cabinetry/): distinguishes design intent, manufacturing information and site coordination. The mockup's two drawing routes are our interpretation of different agreed responsibilities, not a claim of one mandatory delivery method.

No construction standard, contractual/legal compliance, certified dimension or professional interview is claimed. The generated SVG is conceptual artwork with no fabrication dimensions.

## Proposed rules from this round

1. Full service with a building team is the default experience; cabinet-only, furnishings and client-implemented design remain configurable.
2. Services, buyer, contracted party, payer, instruction authority, measurer, receiver and installer are explicit. The CP-01 purchaser inherits the job purchasing default until changed. Other package roles are explicit; general inheritance across multiple packages is not implemented by this study.
3. Studio intent review, maker verification, client design/finish decisions, commercial authority, exact issue, instruction and acknowledgement are distinct records.
4. An issued baseline remains immutable in the story. A proposed substitution has a separate impact/decision/instruction path.
5. Both fictional clients must accept the exact included scope. Acceptance under the maker’s contracted work is linked evidence and cannot replace acceptance of the studio’s own service. The prototype keeps two named clients fixed; configurable signatory rules remain a future detail.
6. The sample completion policy also holds while a studio-owned correction is open. This is a product proposal to validate, not a universal trade practice.
7. A draft configuration creates no appointment or authority. A scope change needs an agreed new edition; historic issues and acceptances retain theirs.

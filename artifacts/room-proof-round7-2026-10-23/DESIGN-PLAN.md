## Critique against the brief

This is not another recoloring. Preserve the approved visual language and change how the job works: independent scope/responsibility choices, package-level exception, maker development vs designer detailed-set mode, exact build issue, supplier substitutions, site dependencies and an evidenced client acceptance requirement. Four starting arrangements are meaningful defaults, not contractual classes. Working with a GC must not imply that the studio is a design-build contractor.

## Interaction boundaries

Configuration changes are draft proposals. Applying a preview does not grant authority, change permissions or rewrite existing records. A package's contracting/instruction/verification/buying/receiving/install responsibilities remain explicit. Scene selectors show independently labeled fictional moments, not actual sign-offs performed by the viewer. Readiness never itself sends a fabrication instruction. Package acceptance does not close the whole job. The whole service remains unclosable without the required client acceptance and any remaining studio obligations/commercial conditions.

No real CAD, engineering, dimensions, material certification, contracts, email, portal data, payments or signature capture. Diagrams are illustrative; sources inform workflow framing, not fabrication advice or universal standards. Actual practitioner usability is still unvalidated.

## §1 · Verdict on today

Kody asked whether hours are easy to log, easy to see four ways, and carry who ·
what · project · rate · billable. The panel's answer: **capture inside a
document is already best-in-class and must not be touched** (FS-27, CR-28,
VET-1, LEAH-4, OPS-8 — five seats independently). Everything else in the
requirement fails, and two of the failures are live money defects.

Both rate-integrity holes are **CONFIRMED** by the billing seat and the
feasibility seat, and both were re-verified against the head classifier body
(`00578:2599-2820`) during synthesis. Neither was refuted; both got worse on
inspection.

## §4 · The four views

One sheet. The existing Hours ledger, one admin-gated **scope lens** — never a
page, never a tab, never an admin-portal route (OPS-6, REP-3, LEAH-10 and CR all
explicitly reject a second surface). Every scope renders the *same* day-grouped
rows; only the subject and one column change.

| View | Lives in | Who opens it | Columns | Groupings | Totals | NOT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **My hours** | Hours ledger, `/desk?book=hours`, `g h`, ⌘K — default scope, lens absent for non-admins | every active member, own rows (`00177:140`, `00316:237`) | project · activity · duration · **rate + rate_source** · billing state · amount | by day | today, week, billable % (minutes — rate is near-constant for one person, REP-7) | not a timesheet to submit; no streak, no target, no utilisation score |
| **A member's hours** | same sheet, lens = that person — **entered from the person in the People Room**, not from a staff picker inside a money ledger (CR-117) | `owner`/`admin` only, asserted server-side via `is_org_admin_or_owner` — **not** `user_is_org_member` (FS-19) | + the member's **name** as the leftmost fact (REP-4) | by day, switchable to project/activity | that member's week + billable split + unbilled | **aggregate by default — no free-text notes without an explicit detail act** (LEAH-18); no leaderboard, no ranking, no comparison on screen |

## §10 · Deck

**Working title: The Studio's Own Clock.** From the margin rule that keeps hours
off the paper — *"time is not a margin item the margin prints: it is the
studio's own clock"* (`margin-groups.ts:11-13`). It is the one line in the
codebase that says what hours are *for*, and it is also why the Hours ledger is
load-bearing and must stay the single home (FS-41).

**The three verdict lines.**

1. **Today:** capture inside a document is already the best thing in the
   product — zero taps, and the entry is written before she is asked — and
   everything around it fails: the hour prices at **$0 or NULL** on the
   commonest project kind, a new hire's hours are **stranded money a later
   agreement can never promote**, and three of Kody's four views do not exist in
   any click path while the database has permitted them since 00316.
2. **The shape:** one server-owned answer for rate and billable on every path;
   one sheet with one admin-gated lens for all four views; one new door per
   surface (⌘K, the mobile sheet, a Field tile) and a date field — and a CSV.
   Nothing new to learn, no new route, no dashboard, no daily nudge, **no
   approval workflow** — the invoiced-entry guard already is the lock.
3. **The ask of Kody:** forty rulings in §7, four of which gate the first line
   of code — does the server own the rate on every project (HT-1), does a
   rate-card row bind to a **person** instead of a string (HT-4), is
   `time_entries_studio_read` narrowed or kept (HT-10), and is
   `useStudioTimeReport` wired or deleted (HT-37). Three consent rulings decide
   whether the first hire trusts the clock: disclosure and opt-out on auto-start
   (HT-35), aggregate-by-default with notes withheld (HT-36), and the
   strengthened no-dashboard test (HT-30).

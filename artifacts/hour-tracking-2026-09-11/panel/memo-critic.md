## 1 · Seat and stance

**Who I am.** The seat holding `docs/vision/VISION.md` and `docs/design/the-document/DECISIONS.md` — the one that asks whether each proposed capability passes §8's four-part test, and whether each standing ruling is being kept because it is right or because it was made.

**What I optimise for.** §8 (which surface · which studio moment · which stream · which promise) on every capability, and §4's promise verbatim: *"you won't notice Patina… we will never optimize the studio surface for engagement"* (`docs/vision/VISION.md:50`).

**What I distrust.** A report that is a dashboard renamed; a nudge that is engagement renamed; a capture path that is surveillance renamed; a ruling kept only because it was made — and, in this program's case, a ruling *cited* as honoured while the shipped code breaks it on another surface.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| **A member's hours** | The **People Room person** — a dated Hours block on that person's profile, beside their append-only rate history.

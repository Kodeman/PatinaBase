# memo-feasibility — the senior-engineer seat

## 1 · Seat and stance

**Who I am.** The engineer who has to apply these migrations to Strata, keep `supabase/tests/commercial` green, and be on the hook when a studio's invoice comes out short.

**What I optimise for.** The smallest additive change that answers the requirement; an effort band I would bet my own week on; the DB as the authorization spine, not a second RPC spine beside it.

**What I distrust.** SECURITY DEFINER without an org assert; nullable columns nobody re-checked in joins; effort bands set by hope; and an architecture draft whose line citations point at a function body that has been replaced twice.

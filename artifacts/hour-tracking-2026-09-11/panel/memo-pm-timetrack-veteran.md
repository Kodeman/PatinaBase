# Memo — PM_TIMETRACK_VETERAN

## 1 · Seat and stance

Product lead who shipped a Harvest/Toggl/Clockify-class time tracker to millions. I optimise for entries that actually happen — capture friction (crux i) weighted heaviest, then whether the build stays inside the vision's promise to the studio (crux vi). I distrust anything that asks a person to remember: a timer nobody can find is a timer nobody stops; a manual-entry form more than 2 interactions deep is an entry that never gets typed.

## 4 · The four views

| View | Where it lives | Who sees it | What it shows | What it is NOT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| My hours | The existing Hours ledger, default scope | The signed-in member, always | Week list by day, today/week totals, billable % (unchanged, R77) | Not a personal analytics dashboard — stays the flat list it already is. |
| A member's hours | Same ledger, scope lens set to one teammate (Wave 1) | `owner`/`admin` only (`user_is_org_member(..., 'admin')`) | That member's week list, identical shape to "my hours," subject swapped | Not a leaderboard or a multi-member comparison screen — one subject at a time. |
| A project's hours | Same ledger, `?projectId=` lens (already shipped) + Wave-0/6 rate/rollup fixes | Any rostered project team member (existing `is_project_team_member` read); full breakdown to owner/admin | Entries + resolved rate + unbilled balance for that project | Not a burn-down chart against `project_phases.estimated_hours` — that column has no live editor (PM_TIMETRACK_VETERAN-24) and shouldn't get a visualization before it has a door. |
| The studio's hours | Same ledger, `scope=studio` via `studio_hours_rollup` (Wave 1) | `owner`/`admin` only | A plain total + the same flat entry list, widened to every project/member | Not a per-designer bar chart, not a ranking, not red/green status — Vision §6 forbids exactly this shape for the studio surface. |

## Persona read

**Leah (studio owner, high context, does this herself today):** steps 1–4 and 6–7 match her transcript's own language closely — she already knows to expect "open sheet, fill, save, repeat" for each line, and the lack of per-sq-ft pricing or labor-attach is a known gap she already works around some other way (this walk didn't test what that workaround looks like — out of scope). The stamp-says-SPECIFIED-when-it-isn't (step 3) is the kind of thing an experienced user stops noticing but that still produces wrong-looking schedules if a client (or a first hire) reads it literally.

**A first hire doing item entry for the first time:** steps 3 and 5 are the likely trip points. A `SPECIFIED` stamp on a line that's just a name with no price or maker is actively misleading to someone who hasn't learned to distrust it yet — there's nothing on the unfolded card (step 3) that visually distinguishes "I just typed a name" from "this is actually priced and ready." And step 5's total absence of a delete control (nothing stronger than "SOMETHING'S WRONG…") means a new hire's typo or wrong-room mistake becomes a standing, un-removable line unless they know to escalate through that link — there's no self-serve undo.

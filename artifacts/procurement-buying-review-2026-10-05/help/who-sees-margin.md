Margin, markup and the trade discount show to **everyone in the studio** by default. An owner or admin can change that under **Account → Studio → Who sees margin**, choosing **Owners and admins only**. Everyone else can see which setting is on, but only owners and admins can change it.

With it restricted, members no longer see margin and markup figures on the studio's screens, and the trade discount on your maker accounts is hidden from them.

This setting decides what the studio's screens show. It is not a lock on the underlying numbers. Anyone in the studio who buys still works with trade cost: it is on the order paper they send and on the maker's acknowledgment they check. If a figure must never reach a person, don't give them a buying seat.

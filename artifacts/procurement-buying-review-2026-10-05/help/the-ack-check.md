When a maker confirms an order, by email, by phone or on their own paperwork, log it with the **ack check**. It lays out what you ordered beside what they confirmed, and beside the quote when there was one: **WE ORDERED / THEY CONFIRMED / QUOTED**. It covers each line's SKU, finish, fabric, quantity and price, plus freight and dates.

Each line reads **agrees** or **differs**, and a price difference shows its signed amount in money. If every line agrees, the act is **Everything agrees — log it**, and the order's stamp reads **Acknowledged**.

If anything differs, the stamp reads **Acknowledged · 1 difference** (or however many). Patina drafts a reply to the maker that names each difference. The draft waits in draft review; nothing is sent until someone in the studio reads it and presses Send.

For each difference you can accept the maker's value or dispute it. **On a line the client has already signed, accepting a different price, quantity, SKU, finish or fabric needs a change order.** That change reaches the client, so it goes through the agreement, not around it. Accepting a price is also refused once the studio has paid the maker on that order; open a change with the maker instead.

An **exception** is a single problem with a single order: **Damage**, **Short shipment**, **Acknowledgment differs**, **Delay**, **Backorder**, **Discontinued** or **Price change**. Each one carries a clock: one dated sentence saying what has to happen by when, and why that date. A damage claim, for example, runs on the maker's claim window. Open one from the line, or from Receiving when an inspection finds damage (**Track it**).

Every exception shows the paths open to it. Depending on the kind, that means filing a claim with the maker, waiting with a new date, cancelling, recording a credit or refund, or offering a substitute. Every message to the maker is drafted first and waits in draft review.

**Substitutions.** When a piece can't be had, pick an alternate from the job's schedule. **Ask the client** drafts a decision for the client that names the alternate. For a price change it says the price changed; for an unavailable piece it says the piece is no longer available as specified. The question names no dates. The decision is a draft until you **Release to the client**. Once they choose, **Make the change** carries it through to the order, and you close the exception.

Orders placed through Patina checkout are Patina's to carry: their damage claims don't offer **Track it**, because Patina handles them with the maker.

An open exception shows on the Desk once, as a need that clears when the exception is closed.

Not everything is bought on a purchase order. A lamp from a flea market, a retail run on the studio card, an auction lot, a sample fee: record each one as a **purchase**. From the add sheet, choose **Bought it already**. On a line that already exists, use the same act from the line.

A purchase records who you paid (any name, with no maker record needed), how you paid, the tax as its own figure, the receipt, and a return-by date if the shop takes returns. For an auction, add the buyer's premium. If you paid on your own card, choose **My own card — reimburse me**.

Recording a purchase against a line moves that line to **ordered**, the same as a sent order would.

**Billing it.** Purchases bill to the client at cost, on their own invoice line, unless you change that on the purchase. When you compose an invoice, the composer offers **Bill N unbilled purchases** (and **Bill N unbilled riders** for freight and other riders). Each one adds a line at cost and can only be billed once. If the line the purchase belongs to is already billed, on this invoice or an earlier one, the composer warns you before you bill the piece twice.

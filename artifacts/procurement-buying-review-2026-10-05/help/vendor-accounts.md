Every studio keeps its own account with each maker it buys from. Find it under **Vendors → Terms**. It holds the details a new hire would otherwise have to ask about:

- your account number and your rep
- payment terms and the usual deposit
- where orders go: an orders email that overrides the maker's general address
- the maker's trade portal
- the date your resale certificate is on file
- the claim windows: how long you have after delivery to report damage. Without your own setting, a maker gets 72 hours and a concealed carrier claim gets 5 days.

The order paper reads this account when you start an order. Terms, deposit and the orders email arrive filled in, and you can change them on the order before you send.

The maker itself is shared: one record of who they are. The account is yours. Nothing you write here is visible to another studio.

Your trade discount lives on the account too. Whether everyone in the studio sees it depends on **Who sees margin** (see "Who in the studio sees margin").

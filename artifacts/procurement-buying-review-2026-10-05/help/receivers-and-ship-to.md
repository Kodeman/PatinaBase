Every order has to say where it goes, and Patina never picks for you. The order paper's ship-to asks you to choose, with nothing preselected. It lists your **receivers** first, then the studio and the job site, and you can choose **Somewhere else** and type an address.

A receiver is a place that can take a delivery and inspect it: a receiving warehouse, the studio itself, a workroom, storage, or the site. Add receivers once, under the studio's people and places. One of them can be your default.

An order with no ship-to will not send. You can still preview the paper; the send waits until there is somewhere for the piece to go. Once an order has gone to the maker, its ship-to is fixed on that paper. To change where it ships, start a change with the maker.

Shipping to a receiver rather than straight to the site lets someone open the crate and photograph the piece. It also starts the damage-claim clock before the install.

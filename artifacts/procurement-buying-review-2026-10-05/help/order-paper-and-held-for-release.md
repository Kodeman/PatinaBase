**The order paper** is the purchase order itself, edited in place. It holds the sidemark, the requested ship date, ship-to, bill-to, freight terms, the lines, any riders (freight, crating, liftgate, receiving, storage), terms, and a note to the maker. There is one act at the bottom, with the amount inside its label, for example **Send to Hewn · $12,480**, and one sentence saying what happens when you press it. What you see is what the maker receives.

**Held for release.** A studio can choose that some orders wait for an owner or admin before they go out. The setting is under **Account → Studio**, and it is off unless an owner or admin turns it on. It can hold:

- orders over an amount ("Orders over $5,000 wait for an owner or admin to release them"), or
- every order ("Every order waits for an owner or admin to release it").

When an order needs releasing, the order paper's act reads **Hold for release · $X** instead of Send. Holding it does not send anything. Owners and admins see one need on their Desk, and the order sits in a **Held for release** group on the Ledger. From there they can **Release to {maker} · $X**, which sends it, or **Send back** with a note. Members can hold an order but cannot release one.

While an order is held, nothing reaches the maker: not a send, not marking it sent by hand, and not logging the maker's acknowledgment. The release line is a check inside your studio, and each order is still released on its own.

**Your other orders count too.** The release line reads more than one order. Your open orders to the same maker on the same job, plus the ones sent to that maker in the last 7 days, count together against the studio's line. Splitting an order in two doesn't get under it. The order paper says so before you save it: "With your other open orders to Hewn on this job, this comes to $6,200, over the studio's release line ($5,000)."

**Changed since release.** A release covers the paper the owner saw, not only its total. If someone changes the lines, the header, the ship-to, the payment schedule or a rider after the release, the order is held again and reads **Changed since release, needs release again**. An owner or admin releases it again before it goes out. A sidemark Patina fills in for you when you send doesn't count as a change.

Once an order has gone to the maker, changing it and sending it again needs a **change order**. The paper the owner released is the paper the maker gets, so Patina won't resend a changed copy under the old release.

**Preview before release.** Previewing a held order shows **Draft order** where the number goes. Nothing is numbered and nothing is filed. The order takes its number when it is released.

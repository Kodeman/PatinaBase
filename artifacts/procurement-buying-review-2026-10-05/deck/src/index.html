Take

  • Vendor-record defaults cascade onto each PO: terms, ship-via, account, FOB. [H] Studio Designer help
  • A dated ack lifecycle on every order touchpoint: acknowledged, est. ship, shipped, received. [H] Studio Designer help

  • Push nudges when a client acts on a selection. That is engagement on the studio surface. [M] Houzz Pro
  • Fees disclosed inside an image, and per-seat pricing that taxes the first hire. Breaks “one public page”. [L]/[M] Capterra reviews

  • Ordering that leaves the platform once the client approves. [L]

  • COM as a chain across two vendors: fabric house to workroom. Every source resolves to “the workroom supplies the fabric”. [M]
  • Phone receiving with photos and claims inside the design platform. It lives at receiving warehouses today. [H] Hilldrup · Ocotillo
  • Buying one thing from someone who isn’t a vendor. [M]

Direction

One buying spine per job, kept inside The Document

The spine is the Project section’s existing rows. It gains a reading by maker, and a line unfold that holds every buying act where the designer already looks. No new room, no new route.

  1. One line card. Every source, catalog or not, lands on the same card: maker, what it is, trade cost, lead time.
  2. One readiness rule. It mirrors what the database refuses and says in words what a line still needs.
  3. One order paper. The PO itself, edited in place and sent once. The four-step assistant and its second send screen go.
  4. Plain records. Money out, ack checks, receiving with a dated claim window, and the lanes with no PO (finds, store buys). They copy nouns Patina already built for its maker lane.

The studio should not notice Patina

  • Every total is front matter over its own rows.
  • Every outbound message is a draft a person sends.
  • No screen is shaped to pull anyone back in.
  • Buying screens show trade cost only. Margin stays in one owner-only block.

Order of work

Fix the broken seams first: eleven fixes, no flags. Then wire tables and RPCs that exist without a screen. New models come last, and the ones on V1, R1, R5 or R8 wait for those rulings.

Synthesis settled twelve disagreements among D1 to D3; direction.md §2. Example: vendor payments go through an append-only record behind a lane-guarded RPC, never the raw hook, which would let a member mark a card-processor row paid (use-procurement.ts:602-640, 00584:242-260). [H]

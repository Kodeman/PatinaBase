**Subject:** Margin is now visible to your whole studio, and you can change that

Hello,

Patina now shows buying margin, markup and trade discounts to everyone in your studio by default. Members who buy for a job need those numbers to order well.

If you'd rather keep margin to owners and admins, an owner or admin can change it under **Account → Studio → Who sees margin** and choose **Owners and admins only**.

One honest note: that setting decides what Patina's screens show. It is not a lock on the numbers themselves. Anyone who places orders still works with trade cost on the order they send and on the maker's acknowledgment they check. If a figure must never reach someone, don't give them a buying seat.

Questions or a different arrangement: just reply.

## 0. Name and summary

**Everything Prints.**

Nearly every door this review found missing is already built and already routed — the plan room
route, the spec book route, the call sheet, the claim drawer on an FF&E line, the send-wall line.
What is missing is the *printing*: the door exists but its word sits on a shelf she cannot see at
1280 (F01), or its name lives only in ⌘K where she must already know to look (F50, F82), or the act
that names it is a shrug — `Review active work`, `Review installation` (F18). Direction A changes no
structure. It changes four things doctrine already lets us change: **what mounts where** (the
shelved blocks stop refusing install and care documents, F14), **which act leads a region** (the
head elects the exception, not the default, F34/F08), **what the seven sentences say** (F18), and
**what we call one thing** (three "rooms", two "Schedules", `Design authority` over money,
F17/F35/F09). Every move is a string, a mount condition, an election, or a registry row — and every
one is a revert. Nothing waits on I114.

## 1. Thesis

**One falsifiable sentence:** every artifact and every next act on this surface already exists in
code, so printing each one where she is already looking — on the body it acts on, in the words she
uses — lifts each of the five worst tasks (T2, T5, T6, T13, T14) by at least a full point at 1280
without adding a surface, a tier, a mode, or a single doctrine amendment; if any of the five still
requires recall, or a ⌘K she was never shown, the direction has failed.

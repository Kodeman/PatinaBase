## The two rulings the deck didn't ask for

**Sequence.** One program, not two lanes running at once — A and B edit the same five files
(`ffe-section.tsx`, `doc/[id]/page.tsx`, `command-bar.tsx`, `document-index.ts`, the shelves), so
they can never build concurrently without stepping on each other. Direction A, "Everything Prints,"
ships first: the 20 shared planks, then A's first slice ("the sentence and the spine," 5–7 days,
unflagged), then A's remaining waves. Direction B, "The Shop Ticket," follows on top of A's mount
fix, behind the fail-closed flag `job-ticket`. Because B doesn't start until after A, B's own
rulings — the ones below that touch B — are taken now, up front, so nobody has to re-open this
conversation when B's turn comes.

**Answer:** A then B, never concurrent. B's rulings are decided now.

**Ticket gate.** Before B1 and B2 take effect, the ticket itself gets redrawn: instead of a fixed
block, it becomes a sticky seam that collapses to B's own two-line 390 form once the letterhead
scrolls past — at every width, not just the narrow ones. Kody reviews that redraw himself; no
stand-in walk from P1 or P3.

**Answer:** Redraw as a sticky seam; Kody reviews it directly.

## The ten questions

### 01 · B1 — may the shelved spine's mount gate move?

I136 reads *"The spine grows three blocks — ≥1440px only."* Direction B asks to move it: the map
leaves the spine and clips to the paper, so rooms, drawings, spec book, boards and roster become
one act at every tier on all seven sections. It buys F01, F14, F48, F72 and half of F82, and moves
three of the five worst tasks. It costs the shelved spine as an organ — two shipped components
deleted, about 180px of measure — and rolls back with the flag.

**Answer:** Ratified, conditional. B1 is ratified on the condition that the sticky-seam redraw
(above) passes Kody's review first. Gains: rooms, drawings, spec book, boards, roster at one act on
all seven sections, closing F01/F14/F48/F72 and half of F82. Gives up: the shelved-spine rooms and
shelves blocks. Rollback: `job-ticket` off — and the deletion must be built as a flag branch, not an
unconditional delete, so the flag genuinely restores both blocks (this was the feasibility judge's
own condition on the amendment).

### 02 · B2 — may a held room survive below 1440?

I136 reads *"The hold releases if the window drops below 1440px, where nothing on screen could put
it down."* The clause names its own reason, and the ticket's room chip removes it. B asks to unwind
the auto-release so a lift works at 1280 and 390 — closing F60, and giving the 1280 laptop a
capability it currently has no substitute for.

**Answer:** Stands with B1. The ticket's room chip is the put-down affordance at every width; B2
falls automatically if B1 falls. I136's never-filters clause is untouched.

### 03 · C14 — narrow or broad?

flag-on Worktable walk that is still owed.

**Answer:** Narrow. It binds the `worktable` guard only. A's unflagged first slice and B's
`job-ticket` flag may both ship ahead of Kody's still-owed flag-on Worktable walk.

### 09 · F56 — clay and terracotta fail contrast wherever they carry text

Clay `#C4A57B` and terracotta `#D4A090` are used as text in roughly 374 places at about 2.2:1. The
red-letter band's own eyebrow uses a darker `#C4836F` at about 2.95:1 — still under the 4.5:1 that
WCAG 2.2 AA requires. Its sibling, F55, is smaller and equally unowned — a grep for a skip link
across the whole document app returns zero hits.

**Answer:** Scheduled as its own ticket (with F55 alongside it) — neither deferred nor waived. Both
directions leave the token change and the skip-link fix out of their estimates deliberately; each
gets a bug of its own.

### 10 · "Moved" — which number is it?

On this specimen the ladder's `Moved` rung equals `Authorized`: both read $141,600. An "in motion"
figure means ordered money that has actually left the studio, and that needs vendor payouts — a
number the accounts band holds and the Money region never reads. The only payment figures the
specimen carries are the client's side of the ledger (invoiced, paid to date) — money coming in, not
money paid out to makers — so subtracting it would invent arithmetic.

**Answer:** Expose vendor payouts to the Money region so `Moved` carries its own number — read from
the accounts data the band already holds, computed as ordered minus paid out to makers. Until that
read lands, both lanes keep SP-03 (`Committed` → `Authorized`) and SP-04's gloss, "ordered through
installed (committed, not yet paid out)."

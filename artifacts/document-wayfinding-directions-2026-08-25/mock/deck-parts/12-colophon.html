How this was made, and what it rests on

Method, in order

  1. Boot and state ladder. Local Supabase confirmed, the environment gate checked against the production URL before anything ran, three transient rich seeds re-applied, and five documents lifted up the stage ladder by RPC — never by direct writes to business tables.
  2. Evidence. Two screenshot passes, one per flag state, at 1440, 1280 and 390. Every PNG was opened and classified by hand as verified, blank or wrong surface — the script's own success ledger was not trusted, because the harness writes a fallback image on failure. 65 verified flag-off, 21 verified flag-on, 86 in total. Two states were genuinely unreachable in this seed and are recorded as unreachable rather than counted.
  3. Probe. A live browser drove hover affordances, the Esc chain, the seven chords, scroll-spy at eleven sample positions, fold persistence across reload, focus return on four surfaces, the room lens on resize, console errors across eight routes, and cold/warm timings. Nine probes plus one supplementary. Two probe-side bugs were found, corrected in session, and recorded — the first chord run reported all seven dead because the probe's own click had moved focus off <body>, which the shortcut contract correctly treats as an overlay context.
  4. Grounding. The code anatomy was re-verified against HEAD rather than inherited, and the decision ledger — 9,431 lines — was read by grep and range, never whole. Several constraint citations inherited from the brief were corrected in that pass: two pointed at a heading rather than the rule, and one line number cited for I114 does not exist in the file.
  5. Panel. Nine seats, identical evidence pack, identical sixteen-task script, run in parallel and in isolation. 203 raw findings, deduplicated by a key of surface, width, flag and slug so identical observations collided across seats and their votes were counted. 101 canonical findings; every raw id preserved.
  6. Verification. Three refuters, each over the whole set, each in a fresh context: one against the code, one against the ledger, one re-driving the browser. A finding survived only if none killed it. 92 survived. A finding ruled against by canon still survives — it is simply not free for Direction A.
  7. Directions. Shared planks first, then two authors in separate contexts with opposing lane constraints, then four critics trying to break them, then both authors revising against their own critiques, then two judges scoring the same rubric without seeing each other. Every revision is logged in the direction documents with the critique id that forced it.
  8. Mocks. Ten screens hand-built in CSS from a kit derived from the portal's own tokens and type roles. Zero box-shadow declarations in the entire kit — depth is value contrast and 1px rules, as doctrine requires. Both lanes drew the same specimen and the same twenty planks.

What this deck does not claim

No task score here is a measurement of a person. No improvement claimed by a direction has been walked — each is labelled as that direction's own claim, and both directions state what would falsify them. Nothing was built into the product, no migration was written, and the decision ledger was not appended to: Kody rules first. The nine killed findings appear only as killed. Where a direction rests on ground a refuter removed, this deck says so on the direction's own page.

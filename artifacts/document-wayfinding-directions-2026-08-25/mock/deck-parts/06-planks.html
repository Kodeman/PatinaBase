Twenty planks

The fixes both directions adopt identically

Before either direction was drafted, one pass took the surviving findings and pulled out the fixes so obviously correct that arguing about them would be theatre — a wrong word, a duplicated string, a missing registry row, a dead shelf. Never structure: no new surface, no new tier, no moved region. If a fix needed doctrine, it was not a plank.

Both directions adopt all twenty, and both mocks in this deck draw them the same way. A plank drawn in only one mock is not a plank. They are also, taken together, a week of work that improves the paper whichever direction is chosen — which is the argument for landing them first.

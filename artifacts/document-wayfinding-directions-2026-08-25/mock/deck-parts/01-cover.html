    2026 · 08 · 25 Prepared for the Patina studio team Verified against main@695addb5f

    The standing complaint about The Document is four words long: “designers have to hunt for things.” The pilot designer left for the legacy portal twice. This review took that literally — it is scoped to one question, asked across the whole surface: is it always obvious what to do next, and how to reach the thing you need? Nothing here has been built. What follows is what the paper actually does today, four practitioners saying so in their own words, and two directions drawn on one specimen.

    The three rulings this work was given

    Doctrine
    Direction A tightens within doctrine. Direction B may amend it — every amendment named by its ledger id, its clause quoted, its trade and its rollback stated.
    Evidence
    Local dev, both flag states, no production walk. Everything cited below was rendered and read on a machine, not remembered.
    Baseline
    Both compositions — today's paper with worktable off, and the Worktable with it on. Each direction says how it lands on each.

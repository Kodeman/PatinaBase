One ruling request, not an amendment — C14

Q3 / I138 says “the flag off is main's composition exactly” (DECISIONS.md:8740-8742). Read narrowly, that binds the worktable guard: flag-off must be byte-identical to main's Worktable-free composition. Read broadly, it freezes any feature shipping ahead of the flag-on walk Kody still owes. The ticket ships behind its own fail-closed flag and does not change worktable's off-state composition, so B asserts the narrow reading and asks Kody to confirm it. Read broadly, the ticket waits for the walk and the design is unchanged. The feasibility judge accepts the framing as honestly named, and notes it resolves into a third amendment if the broad reading wins.

Stated so no verifier has to guess: B's flag-on landing deletes nothing ratified. The Speccing rooms rail (I139) and the Delivery money seam (I141) both stand, with the ticket's rows anchoring to them; the Intake spread's inert opens when… seams are left to the Worktable's own wave.

Recommendation

A's slice now. B's ticket next, after two things.

Ratified 2026·08·25 as one sequenced program — A, then B behind job-ticket. See the answers in §12.

Both judges favour Direction A, and neither recommends dropping Direction B. They arrive at the same sequencing from opposite lenses, which is the strongest signal in the pack.

The practitioner judge: A's first slice moves the two things the walks complain about loudest — the shrug at the top of the paper and the blank rail on install week — inside a week, while B's twelve days buy reach and leave the shrug standing. The feasibility judge: A is the only one of the two that can be on a designer's screen this month, and its first slice closes two blockers with one deleted condition and one edited predicate, both read in the tree. And then, plainly: B is the better product answer at 1280 and 390 — one act on all seven sections is reach A cannot buy — but it is a quarter, not a Tuesday.

The sequence

  1. Land the twenty planks and A's first slice, now. Un-flagged, 5–7 days by A's estimate and 7–9 with tests. It needs no ruling from anyone. The seven sentences, the tie-break, the zero-row fall-through, the shelved blocks mounting on install and care, the spec-book gate deleted, the care head keyed off its own section.
  2. A's remaining waves next — the lexicon pass, ⌘K's stage group and This surface rows, the mobile In this document group, the drawer's printed Find anything door, the compact spine's printed label.
  3. Then B's ticket — but not straight to build. Two things Kody owes come first: the flag-on Worktable walk that has been owed since I143, and a C14 ruling on the narrow reading. And one thing the reviewers owe: redraw the ticket as a sticky seam — collapsed to B's own two-line 390 form once the letterhead scrolls past, at every width — and put it in front of a junior and a solo principal at 1280 before B1 and B2 go to Kody at all. The sticky-spine regression is the one thing that could make B's own strongest tier worse than today, and it is cheap to settle.

Build B on top of A's mount fix, not instead of it — A's fix is what proves the four project-scoped hooks behave on install and care spreads before B stakes an organ on them. And do not run them concurrently: both directions edit the same five files.

The one sequencing conflict, stated openly: A's F14 fix is one predicate on components B deletes. One line thrown away for weeks of install-week value is the right trade — and A's lexicon, its seven sentences and all twenty planks survive into B unchanged.

The reading

Sixteen tasks, one week, nine seats

Every seat ran the same sixteen tasks in the same order, in one sitting, as one designer's week — and was told never to skip a task because it obviously has no path. Narrate the search instead; the search is the finding.

Each task was rated twice, on a scale where 1 is could not find it, 3 is second-guessed myself and 5 is without thinking: once for knowing what to do, once for knowing how to get there. The numbers below are the mean of the nine seats' own tables. They are readings of rendered evidence by nine agents, not a timed re-walk with a working designer — read them as a ranking, not a measurement.

The findings that matter most

Below: every surviving blocker, and every finding three or more of the nine seats filed independently — twenty-five in all, grouped by what they are about rather than by who found them. Each carries the seats that filed it, its severity, the verbatim observation, where it lives in the code, and its canon status: open means no ruling governs it, ruled-against means the behaviour is doctrine and only Direction B may move it, known-open means the ledger already knows and has not answered. Each also carries a credit line — what stays true in the same corner of the product, consolidated from the nine seats' own lists, which both directions are forbidden to break.

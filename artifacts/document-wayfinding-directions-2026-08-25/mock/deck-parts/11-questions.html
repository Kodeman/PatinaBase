Questions for the team

Ten things we need answered before anyone builds

Three of these are amendments or readings of ruled canon, and only Kody can settle them. Three are ledger hygiene — a ruling that was made and never written down, a mapping session that is owed, and a persona whose question this review deliberately refuses. Three are defects the evidence surfaced that belong to no direction and currently have no owner, and one is a data question the specimen itself raises.

01 · Amendment · needs a ruling

B1 — may the shelved spine's mount gate move?

I136 reads “The spine grows three blocks — ≥1440px only.” Direction B asks to move it: the map leaves the spine and clips to the paper, so rooms, drawings, spec book, boards and roster become one act at every tier on all seven sections. It buys F01, F14, F48, F72 and half of F82, and moves three of the five worst tasks. It costs the shelved spine as an organ — two shipped components deleted, about 180px of measure — and rolls back with the flag.

We ask: yes, no, or yes-after-the-sticky-seam-redraw.

02 · Amendment · needs a ruling

B2 — may a held room survive below 1440?

I136 reads “The hold releases if the window drops below 1440px, where nothing on screen could put it down.” The clause names its own reason, and the ticket's room chip removes it. B asks to unwind the auto-release so a lift works at 1280 and 390 — closing F60, and giving the 1280 laptop the one capability it has no substitute for. The risk is exactly what the clause was written to prevent: a hold nobody can put down at a width where the affordance has scrolled away. That is the practitioner judge's reason for redrawing the ticket as a sticky seam first.

We ask: does B2 stand or fall with B1, and does the sticky seam settle it?

03 · Reading of canon · needs a ruling

C14 — narrow or broad?

Q3 / I138 reads “the flag off is main's composition exactly.” Read narrowly, that binds the worktable guard and nothing else. Read broadly, it freezes any feature shipping ahead of the flag-on Worktable walk that is still owed. B asserts the narrow reading — its ticket has its own fail-closed flag and does not touch worktable's off-state composition — and asks for confirmation. Under the broad reading, the ticket simply waits for the walk; the design is unchanged either way. Direction A also ships un-flagged onto GA, which the broad reading would touch too.

We ask: which reading, and does it apply to A's unflagged slice as well?

04 · Ledger hygiene

The Thumb Index removal has never been written down

Kody removed the Thumb Index verbally, with the instruction do not re-propose. A grep of the whole decision ledger for thumb index returns zero hits. It is out of canon by omission rather than by ruling, which means the next reviewer — human or otherwise — has nothing to read that would stop them proposing it again. Neither direction re-proposes it, and both were told not to.

We ask for one ledger entry recording the removal and its reason.

05 · Ledger hygiene · a candidate, not a dependency

I114 — is this the section↔stage mapping?

The mapping session has been owed since I114 and named as blocking in three separate waves; every wave so far has left active_section standing as the sealing authority rather than touch it. Direction B offers a candidate, explicitly as a candidate: brief → Consultation, discovery → Schematic Design, direction → Design Development, proposal → no phase (the agreement sits between Design Development and Procurement), project → Procurement & Orders, install → Installation & Styling, care → Completion. Two seats filed the collision independently: seven section names and the Patina Six print on one paper today.

We ask whether this candidate is worth putting on the agenda — not for a ruling now.

09 · A defect the evidence surfaced · no owner

F56 — clay and terracotta fail contrast wherever they carry text

Clay #C4A57B and terracotta #D4A090 are used as text in roughly 374 places at about 2.2:1. The red-letter band's own eyebrow uses a darker #C4836F at about 2.95:1 — still under the 4.5:1 that WCAG 2.2 AA requires. Neither direction touches it: a token change across 374 sites needs its own accessibility pass, and both say so rather than folding it into an estimate. Its sibling, F55, is smaller and equally unowned — a grep for a skip link across the whole document app returns zero hits, so a keyboard-only user traverses the entire spine on every load.

We ask for a decision on whether the contrast pass is scheduled, deferred, or accepted as a known exception.

10 · A data question · no owner

Moved — which number is it?

On this specimen the ladder's Moved rung equals Authorized: both read $141,600. An in motion figure means ordered money that has actually left the studio, and that needs vendor payouts — a number the accounts band holds and the Money region never reads. The only payment figures the specimen carries are the client's side of the ledger (Invoiced to date $96,400, Paid to date $78,900) — money coming in, not money paid out to makers — so subtracting it would invent arithmetic. Both directions now print the ordered figure under SP-04's gloss, ordered through installed (committed, not yet paid out), which is honest and leaves the rung reading twice.

We ask: expose vendor payouts to the Money region so Moved carries its own number, or retire the rung.

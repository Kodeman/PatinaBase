# P1 — Solo residential principal, 6 live projects (Leah-like)

Madison WI · two-person studio · one job always in install · came off Ivy + a Google Sheets
FF&E schedule she still trusts more than any app. Walked T1–T16 in order as one Tuesday,
against flag-off (`w1440-`/`w1280-`/`m390-`) and flag-on (`wt-`). T1 walked twice — once cold,
once as "back after ten days away."

---

## 1. Overall

The paper reads beautifully and puts me down in one keystroke — but everything I need during
install week is bolted to one state. The running index, the shelves (Plan room · Spec book ·
Mood boards · Call sheet · Knowledge) and the rooms block mount **only** when
`active_section === 'project'` **and** only at ≥1440. So the week I most need the spec book —
install — the left rail goes blank, and the FF&E schedule itself prints "No FF&E lines are
scheduled for installation." My money region is called `Design authority`, folds shut by
default, and hides receivables two folds down under `STUDIO EYES ONLY`. On my 13" laptop the
spine is an unlabeled 56px rail. I'd have the old portal open by 10am.

## 2. The walk

### T1 — "Tell me what today actually needs — across everything."

**First glance:** `Good afternoon, Leah` · `TUESDAY · AUGUST 25`, then two italic whispers
(`– This is your Desk…`, `– The studio isn't fully set up.`), then
`NEEDS YOUR HAND 8` and four folder cards: `Aspen Loft Refresh` / `Sample accepted proposal` /
`Olsen Lake House` / `Full Room`.
**Where I'd click:** nothing at first — I read the four need lines. `1 decision overdue —
oldest due Aug 22`, `Signed — open the project`, `AP-012 has an open damage claim`,
`New lead — respond by Aug 28`. That is a real Tuesday sentence. This part works.
**Where I'd hesitate:** the eyebrow says **8** and the foot says `4 IN REACH · 4 FOLDED BELOW`.
Half my day is folded. I have to press `REVEAL 4 MORE FOLIOS ↓` before I've read my week, and
I'm now asking "which four did it decide I don't need?" Second hesitation: `Sample accepted
proposal` is sitting in my Needs-your-hand pile — that's a fixture, not a client, and it makes
me distrust the pile's precision the way Ivy's dashboard did.
**Where I'd give up:** didn't. But the only cross-project number in the whole screen is inside
`STUDIO PULSE` — `4 moving · 3 reconnecting · Field quiet` / `1 decision is overdue, and 4
pieces are on the way.` — and the detail is behind `OPEN PULSE ↓`. Money across six jobs:
nowhere. Dates across six jobs: nowhere.
**Obviousness:** what-to-do **3** · how-to-get-there **4**.

### T1b — the same task, back after ten days away

**First glance:** identical. And that is the problem. Nothing on the Desk says
*what changed while I was gone*. `NEEDS YOUR HAND 8` is a level, not a delta.
`STUDIO PULSE` says `4 moving · 3 reconnecting` — moving *since when?* I cannot tell a folio
that arrived this morning from one that has been sitting for nine days; the need lines carry
dates (`oldest due Aug 22`) but the folio itself carries no age.
**Where I'd click:** `REVEAL 4 MORE FOLIOS ↓` first this time, because after ten days I do not
trust "in reach" to mean "the important four."
**Where I'd hesitate:** the two margin whispers (`– This is your Desk…`,
`APPEARS ONCE · RECEDES ON USE`) are still there, so the Desk is greeting me like day one
while telling me nothing about the ten days. Then: my folds. The probe confirms per-region
folds persist in `localStorage` (`patina:doc-fold:<docId>:<region>`), so when I open Chen the
regions I folded before vacation are still folded — and a folded `Design authority` seam that
reads `NO AUTHORITY YET · $0 COMMITTED` looks exactly like a region with nothing in it. After
ten days I genuinely cannot tell folded from empty.
**Where I'd give up:** I'd open each of the six documents one at a time to rebuild the picture
— which is the exact behaviour the Desk exists to kill.
**Difference from the cold walk:** time-to-true-read roughly doubles. Cold I'm reading four
need lines; after ten days I'm reconstructing state, and the surface offers no "since you were
last here" anywhere.
**Obviousness:** what-to-do **2** · how-to-get-there **3**.

## 5. Special assignment — the ten-days-away replay, and the Leah-01/05 questions

**The ten-days replay** is written in full at T1b above. The one-line version: the Desk is a
level gauge, not a delta. Cold, my time-to-true-read is under two minutes — four honest need
lines with dates on them, and I'd narrate the day accurately. After ten days it roughly doubles
and then stalls, because nothing distinguishes a folio that arrived this morning from one that
has sat since I left, half the pile is folded behind `REVEAL 4 MORE FOLIOS ↓`, and my persisted
region folds mean every document opens looking settled. My recovery behaviour is the one the
Desk exists to prevent: open all six documents in turn.

**Q3 — did the margin feel like my work, or like notifications?**
Like my work, narrowly. `Vendor payment — balance / WS-188 · Woodward & Sons` and
`Vendor payment — deposit / SK-087 · Sawkille Co` are jobs, not pings, and the empty state
(`The margin — decisions, messages, and money gather here`) promises the right three kinds.
Two things break the spell. On the install document the margin fills with
`– A project collaborator changed Service Agreement — Aspen Loft.` ×3 — that is a changelog,
and it is the only voice in that rail. And at 1440 the margin has no closed state at all
(`margin-open` and `margin-closed` are the same screenshot), so I cannot put it away when I
want the paper alone.

**Which phase makes me sigh?** Install, unambiguously — and this surface makes it worse rather
than better. On the install document the FF&E schedule is gone (P1-02), the spec book and plan
room are gone (P1-01), the roster is gone, and the only weighted schedule act is
`COMPLETE PHASE`. Every question install week asks — what's arriving, what's damaged, who's on
site, did the vendor confirm — must be answered from a cross-project ledger sheet.

**The two-hour credenza retrace.** Retracing one piece today: the line
`Custom Walnut Sectional — 3 pc / Woodward & Sons / RECEIVED / $6,800` carries no PO number, no
dates, no ack, and no link. To rebuild its story I'd need the Orders sheet (cross-project), the
Receiving tab, the margin's two vendor-payment items, and `The accounts · this project` two
folds inside `Design authority`. Four surfaces, three of them outside the document. The retrace
is still two hours.

**Old-portal flights, with named triggers.** Three in one Tuesday: (1) T2 — ⌘K says
`No match` for a word printed on my own screen; (2) T4/T14 on the install document —
`No FF&E lines are scheduled for installation.`; (3) T13 — no acknowledgement state on any PO.

**Unaided margin acts:** two. `+ NOTE` is unmissable at the rail head. `+ DECISION` only
appears when the project has a linked client — on Chen (`No client linked — attach one ↗`) the
composer is gated off entirely, so a designer meeting the margin on that document sees a
one-act rail and never learns decisions live there.

## 6. What stays true — do not break these

1. **`← PUT DOWN` and the Esc chain.** One trip out, LIFO through ⌘K → shelf → document, no
   ambiguity about where I land. This is the single strongest thing in the product and it is
   the reason strict one-document focus is bearable.
2. **The Desk's need lines.** `1 decision overdue — oldest due Aug 22`, `AP-012 has an open
   damage claim`, `New lead — respond by Aug 28` — real sentences with dates and objects in
   them, each with one act (`REVIEW DECISIONS`, `REVIEW THE CLAIM`, `ACCEPT · BEGIN`). This is
   how I'd say it to myself.
3. **The send wall.** `SENT Aug 24 · OPENED not yet · READING — · MOST READ —` with
   `SENT YESTERDAY —  NUDGE CLIENT USER` on one line. State and act together, `not yet` in
   plain English, and the nudge printed exactly once.
4. **Honest empty states everywhere.** `NOTHING FILED`, `NO BOARDS YET`, `NOBODY ON IT YET`,
   `Not started yet — open the Drafting Room to write it`, `0 of 5 essentials captured — keep
   going`. Nothing pretends. That is why I trust the numbers that are there.
5. **`ADD A ROOM` in flow at the foot of the room list**, and the FF&E line grammar —
   piece · vendor · stamp · price on one row. It reads like my Google Sheet, which is the
   highest compliment I have.
6. **The schedule's three starting points** — `The Patina Six` / `From a past project` /
   `Start blank`, captioned `TYPOGRAPHIC · NO WIZARDS`. Naming my own six phases and being
   offered my own history as the estimate is the most designer-shaped thing here.

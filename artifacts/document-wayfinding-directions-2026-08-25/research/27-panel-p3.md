# Panel P3 — Junior designer, week one

Persona: 24, Minneapolis, two years out of school. Mydoma internship, Canva, Excel. No legacy
portal to flee to — only escape is asking someone. Walked all sixteen tasks, in order, against
both today's paper (flag off: w1440/w1280/m390) and the Worktable (flag on: wt-). Verified
against `main@695addb5f`.

## Overall (≤120 words)

I can get through my first week, but only by clicking everything and reading labels twice.
Nothing crashes and nothing lies to me — but "Design authority" doesn't say money, "Knowledge"
opens onto a product catalog, and "Call sheet" doesn't exist anywhere I can see until I'm
already inside a specific project. Two whole classes of thing I'd be asked for constantly — the
mood board and the floor plan — vanish completely the moment my laptop is a hair under 1440px,
with zero sign anything is missing. The single scariest gap: typing what my manager actually
says ("show me everything in install") into Find Anything returns nothing. I'd ask a person
before I'd trust the search.

## What stays true (do not break these)

1. The Desk's folio cards already narrate the day in plain sentences — `1 decision overdue —
   oldest due Aug 22`, `Signed — open the project`, `AP-012 has an open damage claim` — this is
   the single best piece of writing in the product for a first-week hire and should be the
   model for everything else.
2. The proposal send-wall state line (`SENT YESTERDAY — NUDGE CLIENT USER` with
   `SENT`/`OPENED`/`READING`/`MOST READ` in one row) answers T7 completely without a single
   moment of doubt — keep this exact shape.
3. `← Put down` and Esc both reliably return to `/desk`, confirmed working with correct LIFO
   order through dialog → shelf → put-down (probe #2) — never regress this.
4. `+ File` folio strips, `ADD A ROOM`, and other scored-ink in-flow acts read as plain
   underlined text sitting where the thing they act on lives — once I learned the visual
   language (underline = clickable, no box), I stopped needing to hunt for buttons.
5. The Orders ledger sheet's grouping by vendor name (`APPARATUS`, `CERAMICA STUDIO`) matches
   how I'd actually go looking for "did Sturdy Oak confirm" — group-by-vendor is the right
   default, keep it even if an acknowledgment chip gets added.
6. Nothing I walked ever silently failed or dead-ended with no message — every empty state
   ("No boards yet," "Nothing filed for this project," "No window is held") told me plainly
   what was missing and usually offered the one act to fix it.

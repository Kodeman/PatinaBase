# Findings — Devon (senior designer, 9-person studio, 5 concurrent projects)

## (a) Friction narrative

Monday I go to seed three "coastal calm" bedroom boards. Blank board is the top, most obvious choice in the New Board picker — it throws a raw RPC error every time (F1). I fall back to the studio template option instead, which the tooling suggests works, but I can't point to anyone actually watching a studio-template pick land on a project board today — the only "works from this exact dialog" evidence on record is for the Patina starter templates, not mine. Materializing "coastal calm" three times reproduces the layout faithfully, but every product pin needs a manual "promote to project selection" click to actually reconnect to that project's FF&E line — times however many pieces are in the template, times three boards. That's the real tax on reusing a signature template at delegation scale, not just today's blank-board bug.

Once seeded, I want to try two rug options on the "keep" board — Cmd+D throws "invalid board item" every time on a project board. I want to nudge the whole furniture cluster after seeing a junior's floorplan — multi-select drag reverts with a generic toast. Both come back from the exact save path (`apply_board_room_state`) that every one of my new project boards rides, since I discover the "healthy" boards-capable proposal room isn't reachable for any new client work this week — it only survives on legacy fixtures nobody can create fresh.

Mid-week I want to look at what my juniors built and leave direction on three specific pins. There's no internal annotation layer at all — the only per-pin comment thread belongs to the client verdict loop. I'd have to screenshot-and-Slack, disconnected from the board. No presence either, so I can't even walk a junior through it live in-app.

Friday, the principal wants approved / procurement / stalled across 5 projects. There's no all-boards view — the desk recents strip that should give me exactly this is built but mounted nowhere; ⌘K only shows my own last few opens. I'd have to open all 5 projects' Boards facets and read verdict summaries card by card, hoping the cover thumbnails aren't the broken ones I keep seeing in the console.

## What's already excellent (protect these)
- Save-as-template → materialize is faithful (thumbnail, piece count, sections, positions, rotation all exact) — the structural half of "coastal calm" reuse genuinely works.
- Canvas gestures (drag/resize/rotate, zoom 5–400%, marquee, Tidy, undo/redo) are solid and persist correctly on reload.
- ⌘K recent-boards search is fast and correctly cross-project-scoped for personal use.
- Export (PNG + 2 PDF kinds) and Present mode are clean and reliable.
- The procurement wiring itself (product pins carry price/vendor, send-to-schedule) is real and is the actual differentiator — none of the above bugs touch that spine directly, they sit around it.

## What the studio needs before rolling this out to 9 people
1. **Fix the project-owned-board save path** (DV1, DV4, DV5, DV12) before any second designer touches it — right now every new board a delegate creates rides the broken RPC path.
2. **Restore or replace the healthy board-creation surface** (DV11) — a 9-person studio creating new client work constantly cannot depend on legacy-fixture-only proposals.
3. **Some form of internal direction/annotation and a cross-project boards view** (DV6, DV8, DV10) — these aren't bugs, they're missing delegation infrastructure; a lead overseeing juniors across 5 projects has no in-product way to direct or to report status today.
4. **Close the two "known-waived-but-now-observed" gaps** (DV14 URL unfurl, DV5 group drag) — they were flagged as risk at GA and have since shown up as real prod failures.
5. **Revisit the promote-per-item template tax** (DV3) at delegation scale — correct today, but a studio seeding the same template repeatedly needs a bulk path, not N manual promotions per board.

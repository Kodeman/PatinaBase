## Verdict

The four things W3 was sent to do are done and done well: both letters build `portalUrl` from `ensureInvoiceLinkUrl` per letter and fall back rather than ship a broken address (verified null-on-every-failure in `_shared/invoice-links.ts`, `deep_link` and `notification_log.metadata` untouched, the token never logged); `packages/utils/src/invoice-link.ts` is a faithful `document-share.ts` twin down to the regex and the trailing-slash normalisation; the folio's Copy link stands on exactly the `canPrint` predicate the architecture named, the Regenerate confirmation and the M11 refusal read in Patina's voice, and R137 is correctly numbered, dated, house-styled and honest about I1 and I2. Two defects live in the seam between the new query and the existing mutations, and both are a few lines: **nothing invalidates `['invoice-link', id]` when an invoice is issued** (F1), so the folio's delivery-recovery band — the exact surface this wave rewired — tells the designer "this invoice has no link yet" for five minutes at the one moment it exists for, and both new acts render greyed out; and **`useInvoiceLink` carries no `errorSurface`** (F2), so a `get_invoice_link` refusal — reachable by a guest studio co-member, whose SELECT policy is strictly broader than `can_manage_invoice` — raises a raw Postgres message in the designer portal's global red toast on a surface R83 requires to fail inline. **DO NOT MERGE as-is; MERGE once F1 and F2 are applied**; nothing else found is blocking, and the client-portal ordering coupling (F5) is a deploy rule, not a code change.

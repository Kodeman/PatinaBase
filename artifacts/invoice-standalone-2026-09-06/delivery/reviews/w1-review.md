## Verdict

The migration is the design as ruled — the four money bodies are the verified 00428/00397 heads plus exactly the described deltas (diffed mechanically, not read), `invoice_link_id` is a real identity term at claim, finalize, recover, session metadata, `assertInvoiceSessionIdentity` and the webhook's own check, `anon` holds EXECUTE on none of sixteen functions (probed), and the 1,380-line SQL suite asserts what its comments say with a recursive forbidden-key walk and no vacuous asserts. Three defects live in the migration body and should be fixed in 00574 *before* it reaches Strata rather than re-headed in a 00575: a second actor can now supersede a **`processing`** (ACH-in-flight) attempt and fail its pending payment (F1); a Checkout return nonce is an unexpiring alias that re-binds to the invoice's **new** token after Regenerate, silently defeating the designer's only revocation act against the S10 threat (F2); and `pay.processing` / `payments[]` count a merely-claimed or cancelled card attempt as money in flight for up to 24 h (F3, spec-conformant to G8 but wrong at the edge). **DO NOT MERGE as-is; MERGE once F1–F3 are applied** (each is a few lines in one function and its test) — nothing else found is blocking, and the deploy coupling to W2's `/pay` route (F4) is an ordering rule, not a code change.

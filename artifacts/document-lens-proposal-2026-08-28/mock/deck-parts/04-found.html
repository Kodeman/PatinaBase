FINDINGS

Ten simulated seats walked the same document. A hundred and sixty-four findings went in; a hundred and fifty-two survived three refuters.

Five attention-and-access lenses, four practitioner personas, one engineering-feasibility seat — each walked the same sixteen tasks against the live portal on local dev, post-R126 main. Every claim below traces to research/31-verified-findings.md and its refutation wave: twelve findings were dropped, eleven of them killed by a direct code read that showed the seat had misread the DOM.

Every seat named on this page — P1, P3, E1 and the rest — is a simulated seat: an agent reasoning from the running app, the code, and the plan, not a design team or a customer panel. No usage data was available to this review.

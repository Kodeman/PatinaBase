BODY · sections, spacing and the margin

The sections are crammed into each other because there are three answers to one question.

Measured button-to-button at every width and every scroll state, the gaps between region heads are {6, 29, 56} px.

Three spacings, on a list that reads as one uniform stack of seams. Header-stack-end to Schedule is 56px; Schedule to Pieces is 29px; Pieces to money-head is 6px. They are scroll-invariant and width-invariant, so this is not a responsive artefact — it is three call sites each deciding for itself.

The sharpest case is in the tree. Approvals open is mt-6 … py-6 at project-approval-document.tsx:588; approvals folded is a bare <div data-index-region="approvals"> with no wrapper at all at :565. So folding a region silently changes the gap around it as well as its height.

One token replaces all three. --doc-region-gap: 24px, owned by the region wrapper rather than the call site, identical whether the region is full, quiet or folded. That last clause is the load-bearing one: it is what makes a fold a change in density and not a change in the paper’s geometry.

Folded by choice, condensed by position — told apart in a still

Today four fold verbs print on one screen and none of them says why. A region folded by a derived default and a region folded by her look and read identically, and a designer returning to a document she folded three weeks ago — the key patina:doc-fold:{docId}:{region} outlives the session — reads the seam as there is nothing here.

The proposal separates the two by giving each its own rule weight, its own typography and its own printed cause. A quiet region keeps its --rule-strong double rule and its 24px Playfair head, and prints a count line and one act. A folded region drops to a single --rule-mid, prints its name in italic with a mono summary, offers UNFOLD ↓, and prints CLOSED BY YOU. An empty region keeps the strong rule and the Playfair head and prints Nothing yet with no count at all.

Which makes the discipline a single sentence: a region with a number is quiet, a region with no number is empty — and a designer reads the difference off a PNG, without hover, without having performed the transition.

The mark that makes the distinction honest is upstream of the drawing. The latched derived default stops producing folds and becomes the region’s initial density instead, after which a FoldSeam can only ever mean you folded this. That has a visible consequence on the first screen of every project document, and it is named rather than shipped quietly: Client approvals and Schedule dates are both folded by a derived default today, and they open on arrival.

Cause printed in words, never on a coloured tick: clay measures 1.82:1 on rail stock, which contrast.test.ts:319-325 asserts is below AA, and the offender scan at :334-338 would not catch it because it greps only for text-[var(${pigment})]. code-read

The line every region must be able to say

A quiet region is the head at 24px Playfair with --rule-strong, one count line of at most forty characters at full ink, and the region’s one inked leader act — with the space to the region’s reserved height left as bare paper.

Three things follow, and each is a refusal. Acts do print at reduced density: the one inked leader is required unconditionally by region-head.test.tsx:128-157, so it is not new. Exceptions never go quiet — unless the band’s line 2 is currently naming that exact exception, in which case the region withholds it and prints it again the moment the band moves on. And a number never softens: the muted ramp’s lightest step has about one small step of headroom before 4.5:1 fails, and this proposal does not spend it. Quieter means fewer words, never fainter ones.

Two printed forms for zero, replacing five: Nothing yet — the region exists and is empty; Not known yet — the value is not knowable on this spread. Never a dash. A fallback already prints identically to a live value in the rail today, and a dash is the same mistake in one character.

What the 232px margin gets

The ask says the margin seems cramped for the space it needs. The measurements support the vertical half of that and not the width half, and both halves get an answer rather than one getting a silence.

Vertical: nine wrapped lines of first-touch prose, about 230px, stand above IN THE MARGIN and the first chip — 25.6% of a 900px column, permanently, on every visit. Width: 200 + 1040 + 232 = 1472 > 1440, so 40px taken from the rail buys the margin 40px at exactly 1440 and nothing above it, and costs the rail its whole value line. So the answer is about 270px of vertical returned and 0px of width, and the ledger is printed rather than asserted.

The margin lifts; it does not filter

Two printed groups — BESIDE PIECES · 3 for items anchored to the region currently at full, then THE WHOLE JOB · 4. Nothing leaves. Items rise and fall between two named groups, which is the room lens’s own ruling applied to the margin.

A group heading is only legible when a group has members, so each item also prints its own anchor line in 11px mono on the card — BESIDE PIECES, or ABOUT THE WHOLE JOB where it has none. That reading survives a still screenshot and an empty anchor set, which is the real case: all seven margin cards on the specimen are MONEY · and TIME · kinds and none is anchored to Pieces. Empty is printed too: NOTHING BESIDE PIECES YET.

At 1180–1439 the closed tab prints its count and its worst kind, MARGIN · 7 · 1 OVERDUE — a printed count in a label, not a badge on the drawer. And one rule governs the whole organ: when the band’s line 2 or a ladder segment is naming a fact, an item anchored to that same fact prints its kind and its subject, never the figure. The band owns money at rest, the Money segment owns it once the band’s right-flush slot has dropped it, and the margin never prints a third copy.

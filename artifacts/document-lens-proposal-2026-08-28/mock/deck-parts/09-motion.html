MOTION · eleven mechanics, and their still forms

Eleven mechanics. Every one has a form a designer sees with motion turned off.

A reduced-motion cell that reads “n/a” is a row that was never designed. None of these do.

Four of the eleven have no transit to reduce at all — two commit off screen, one is a render hint that changes no measured height, and one is an arithmetic gate. Four sit beside a reduce block that already ships in app/globals.css. One needs no new rule because its still form is declared inside the no-preference gate rather than outside it. One new block covers the three crossfades. And exactly one row cannot be covered by a CSS media query, which is stated as an amendment rather than dressed as compliance.

The ambient budget stays at one: doc-breath, 3s, on the active StrataMark, in the rail, at its shipped site. No second ambient move is named. The reading window is position-linked rather than time-linked, and therefore not ambient.

And there are zero hover-only affordances. Every state below is a printed mark or a focus state. The shipped tree has none today; this proposal adds none.

The one declared amendment

L-2 is the single row a CSS media query cannot cover. Its rAF handler reads matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)') in JS and steps the bracket instead of sliding it. hooks/useReducedMotion.ts exists and no file under components/document imports it; the Document’s policy is nine @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) blocks plus one no-preference gate. This proposal keeps that policy except for this one handler, and calls it an amendment rather than compliance. That sentence is what makes the rest of the table trustworthy.

The same eleven rows, drawn

Where the hysteresis is, and where there is deliberately none

Two systems get confused with each other, so they are separated here by their numbers.

The mount threshold has no pair. A region’s body mounts when its root’s top comes within 240px of the frame’s bottom edge — off screen, always — and it never unmounts for the life of the document view. There is no release threshold, so there is no hysteresis pair to tune and nothing for a prober to oscillate on. 240px is defended against the specimen rather than against a sentence: one FF&E line is a 48px catalog crop plus py-2 plus a rule, 65px, so 240px is more than three lines and a three-line nudge cannot re-cross it. It is the same number at 1440, 1280 and 390, because a line is the same height at every width.

The reading-stop resolver is where the asymmetry lives. In the clickable mockup it is two IntersectionObserver bands rooted at the frame, plus a synchronous arithmetic resolver over the same two bands: PROMOTE = [0.08, 0.38], a narrow band 8% to 38% down the frame, and HOLD = [0.00, 0.88], a wide one. A region is promoted by entering the narrow band and can only fall once it has left the much wider one — that gap is the hysteresis. Measured over a 20-step slow scroll at --motion-scale: 4, every region changes data-density at most once per step, and the sequence is monotone.

Two more numbers govern tempo. The settle is 120ms below 40px per frame; it gates every density change and every announcement. The 700ms jump lock is the shipped one, and it gains one job: for its 700ms it forces the target stop to be the reading stop. Returning to a stop already announced within 2s is silent, so a slow re-read across one boundary does not fire repeatedly.

Zero layout shift, by mechanism

Six clauses, in the order they close the hole.

  • a. The band’s box in flow is 56px before the pin and 56px after, so the pin displaces nothing.
  • b. Its height is declared and both lines cannot wrap, so nothing it says can change its size.
  • c. A region’s height changes only while the entire region, and every pixel above it, is below the frame’s bottom edge.
  • d. A region she has passed never changes height again this session.
  • e. --doc-seam-height does not exist, so the value four consumers read cannot change mid-scroll. The schedule’s zero-height glance at schedule-rule.tsx:548 is left at h-0 and given a constant top — giving it a reserved height would create the shift it was removing.
  • f. The band’s own content is what the late-arriving red-letter query resolves into, and the band is 56px before that query returns — so the 0.1189 shift, 92% of the page’s cumulative layout shift in both motion registers, has nowhere to land.

What that measures as. A PerformanceObserver({type:'layout-shift'}) over a 30-step scripted scroll from the top of the paper to its foot on #frame-1440 reports CLS = 0 with 0 shift entries in the normal register, and CLS = 0 with 0 shift entries in the reduced register. The paper’s extent grows from 4381px to 5106px across the same sweep, which is the design: it only ever grows below her.

The word scrollBy appears nowhere in the proposal, and there is no scroll correction of any kind.

The reduced register, at the condensed state

A still picture cannot show an absent transition, so this frame is drawn pixel-identical to the animated one by design. That is the claim: with motion reduced, every mark in the lens is present, at full ink, in place. L-1’s reduced form is the sentence printed instantly in place — same words, same terracotta, no crossfade.

There is no in-product motion toggle, and the position is stated rather than left unraised. 2.3.3 is AAA and the stated bar is AA; the OS query is the control, which is what the tree does today. The opening of a region ahead is essential to the mechanic, because both the first-head recovery and the render-cost control depend on it. The dev-bar toggle is a QA instrument, not a product control.

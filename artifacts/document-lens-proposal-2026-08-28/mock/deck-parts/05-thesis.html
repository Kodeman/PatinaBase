THESIS

A lens is a mechanism. Every sentence in it has a number behind it.

The header is only true at the top of the paper, and one fact travels with her forever.

So the header goes back to being the top of a piece of paper, and one 56px line under it carries what needs her, with its act, at every offset. The rail becomes the map: a drawn ladder of this paper’s own stops, one line per stop, printing what the paper is not saying. The lens only ever opens what is still ahead of her, so nothing above the reading line can move.

Three sentences, three organs. None of them is a description of a feeling. The band’s height is a declared constant, not a measured one. The ladder’s extents are derived from the counts the ticket derivation already produces, never from a rendered box. And “adjusting focus” resolves to a single geometric predicate: a region’s height changes only while the entire region, and every pixel above it, is below the frame’s bottom edge.

That predicate is what makes the whole proposal checkable rather than arguable. It is also what the mockup measures: a 30-step scripted scroll from the top of the paper to its foot, in both motion registers, reports a cumulative layout shift of 0 and zero shift entries.

The four lens laws, and the sentence that makes each one falsifiable

The laws are the brief’s, in the brief’s own words, and they are not renamed here. Under each is the mechanism this proposal answers it with — the form in which a prober, or a designer with a still screenshot, can catch it failing.

Law 1

What is in frame is full.

The region she is reading is at complete strength: every line, every number, every act, at the ratified R126 weight. There is no state in which the thing she is looking at is a summary of itself.

Falsifiable as: every region intersecting the frame is full, every region she has passed stays full, never zero — and no region prints zero readable text. The brief asked for exactly one; that target is refused with the geometry, because a design where nothing above the reading line moves must hold two regions full when two touch the frame.

F11 One screen scrolled and nothing has condensed yet F53 The fold is the only render-cost control

Law 2

What is out of frame yields.

A region she is not in condenses to its head plus one status line. It keeps its place in the paper’s order — the same y-position in the scroll, the same neighbours, the same distance from the top — and it never disappears.

Falsifiable as: yielding runs in one direction only. A body mounts when its root’s top comes within 240px of the frame’s bottom edge, off screen, always; there is no release threshold, so a region she has read is never taken back. The word scrollBy appears nowhere in the proposal.

F04 Ticket pin/fold is a single-frame cut with no hysteresis band F87 Schedule glance drifts continuously under a moving seam

Law 3

The spine is the map, not a second copy of the paper.

Something earns the left edge only if it is true across the whole document at once, or true outside this document. A running index that reprints the headings she can already see is failing that test with a straight face.

Falsifiable as: a segment prints nothing at all while its own [data-region-head] is in frame, and un-yields the moment that head scrolls off. At most one segment is ever silent, and it is the one the reading window brackets.

F22 The index lists regions but not their size or trouble F102 Active label pair duplicates the on-page region heading

Law 4

Nothing hides.

Every condensation is reversible in one act, and its state is readable without hover — from a still screenshot, by someone who did not perform the transition. The specific failure to avoid: condensed must be distinguishable from empty on sight.

Falsifiable as: a region with a number is quiet; a region with no number is empty; and the cause of a fold is printed in words, CLOSED BY YOU, never carried on a coloured tick. Clay measures 1.82:1 on rail stock, which contrast.test.ts:319-325 already asserts is below AA.

What it is not

Six things a reader arrives expecting from a proposal with the word lens in it. Each is refused rather than deferred, and the reason is a number or a mechanism, not a schedule.

The refuses, from source/proposal.md §11
What a reader will expectThe refusalWhy refused rather than deferred
A rail that draws each region at its true height No proportional-extent map rail. On the specimen Pieces is 177px against Money’s 50px — 3.5:1 drawn for a true 10:1. Every segment takes a 36px floor first, and a proportionally-drawn Money rung would be a 6px press target — a 2.5.8 failure before it is a taste failure. The drawing carries order and reach; the count in the value line carries scale. The phrase true proportional extent is withdrawn rather than defended.
The current region’s head pinned under the band No standing rule. Two stacked sticky bands are a header again, and SC2’s 52px of unspent budget would be spent on chrome rather than saved. The fact it would carry — which region she is inside — is printed exactly once per frame, on the paper, and the ladder’s window says where that is.
A seam whose height eases as she scrolls No continuous seam, and no discrete one. The engineering seat’s three costed branches — days, week, weeks — are all declined. That seat’s own sentence is the reason: a continuous seam is not a header change, it is a navigation change. Every branch pays somewhere in scrollToRegion’s once-resolved scroll-margin-top, and every one is invisible to the suite because every seam assertion is jsdom. The variable is deleted and its replacements are declared constants.
A narrower rail, to give the margin its room No 160px rail at ≥1440, and no 272px margin. 200 + 1040 + 232 = 1472 > 1440, so narrowing recovers 32px of measure at exactly 1440 and nothing at 1472 and above — three characters on a 15px line — and costs the rail 40px of its 168px inner measure, which is the whole value line. The margin’s answer to cramped is roughly 160px of vertical returned and 0px of width.
Regions that condense again once she has passed them No re-quieting, and no scroll correction. Every alternative — a same-frame scrollBy(−Δ), overflow-anchor, a scroll-position correction — is a mechanism that has to be right every frame, on every fling, in a browser matrix that had no declared browserslist until Wave 0, to avoid moving a paragraph she is reading.
New doors for the gaps the audit found No new capability: no PO-acknowledgement badge, no damage-claim door, no phase-wide install roster, no cross-document surface. The ask is to make the same information and the same acts occupy less attention. Each gap is real, several cost a practitioner a trip out of the document, and they are named so the next program can pick them up. The one thing taken from that set is the dates — a carrier window is a deadline, and deadlines are what both organs already carry.

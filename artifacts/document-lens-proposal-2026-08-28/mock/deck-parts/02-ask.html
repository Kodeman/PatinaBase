What this deck is

A proposal, not a build. Ten seats read the document under scroll-state capture and code anatomy — five attention-and-access lenses, four practitioner personas walking the same sixteen tasks, one engineering-feasibility seat — and three refuters narrowed what they raised to a verified set. Two rival authors then drafted the strongest answer each could argue in isolation: proposal X, “the spine is the lens,” and proposal Y, “the paper is the lens.” Four critics scored both on design, feasibility, a practitioner's walk, and access; two judges and a merge author combined the winning organs into the one proposal this deck argues — never an either-or. That proposal became a clickable mockup, and this deck.

ROADS

Seven proposals were argued, and seven were killed — each by a number, not a preference.

Every road below was a real design in this program, drawn and defended by a seat that believed in it. Each carries the one sentence that ended it, quoted, and who wrote that sentence.

Six of the seven were killed by an arithmetic fact: a floor, a distance, a measure, a wrap, a frame. The seventh was killed by a person weighing a working instrument against a principle — the harder kind of evidence, and the kind this program exists to produce.

  • 01

    The map rail drawn at true proportional extent

    The ladder as a scaled elevation of the paper itself — each segment drawn at its region's real share of the document's height, so the drawing itself answers extent, distance and position without a number or a legend. It was the most direct answer to F22 The index lists regions but not their size or trouble, and the most beautiful mark either author drew.

    The one thing a map is for — how much of this paper is my schedule — is the thing the drawing flattens.

    C-practitioner critique seat, Dp-11 · simulated seat

    Every stop needs a press target; the floor the target sets eats the proportion — on a 443px track, six floors spend 216px before a single pixel is drawn by extent, and an 11:1 truth draws as 3.5:1.

  • 02

    No lens line at all

    Nothing on the paper pinned. The header stayed a letterhead that leaves, the instrument moved entirely to the margin, and below the fold the paper was only paper. Removing the sticky surface entirely also removed the surface F04 Ticket pin/fold is a single-frame cut with no hysteresis band, F34 Continuous seam height breaks every region landing, F87 Schedule glance drifts continuously under a moving seam and F120 Only region roots clear the pinned seam, not their child controls land on.

    The needs band is non-sticky, so SEND A REMINDER for the six-day overdue approval is ~2,000px above her at s2. The exception's word survives on the ladder; its act does not.

    J1 judge seat, Dj1-06 · simulated seat

  • 03

    The value-free rail — six equal rungs, position and nothing else

    The rail stops carrying figures at all — no counts, no dollars, no exception marks, position only — on the principle that a rail which prints no figure cannot print a false one. The diagnosis was exact: F108 An empty region's index line looks identical to a live one, F29 Approvals emptiness printed twice on one screen and F10 Five money statements, four numbers, one screen are all the same fallback-wearing-a-live-figure's-clothes error, and this road cannot commit it.

    Y deletes it on a principle — that a rail which prints no figure cannot print a false one — which is a good principle bought with her working instrument.

    J1 judge seat, §6 “Who is worse off” · simulated seat

  • 04

    The 40px transfer — a 160px rail funding a 272px margin

    Narrow the rail by 40px and give the width to the margin, so the paper column keeps its measure and the organ Kody calls cramped for its work gets 40px wider. It was the only move in the program that answered the width half of that complaint at all, rather than answering it with vertical space and hoping nobody noticed.

    32px of measure recovered at exactly 1440 and none at 1472 and above, against 40px of the rail's 168px inner measure, which is the whole of the value line.

    J2 judge seat, merge instruction “Sections and margin” · simulated seat

    Forty pixels off the rail also carried F17 The margin never changes as I move down the paper's value line, which is the whole payload the road was trying to protect.

  • 05

    The text-free 1280 tier

    At 1180–1439 the rail would print no words at all — a hairline, a tick per stop, a clay mark for the stop she is in, every tick a press target that opens the sheet with the full names. It was the cheapest correct answer to F07 Stage word breaks mid-syllable in the glyph rail, and it introduced no new iconography.

    A hairline with six ticks is the one place in either proposal where the document stops looking like paper furniture and starts looking like a scrollbar, and it is a tier Kody works at.

    J2 judge seat, axis 6 · simulated seat

    The fight was never necessary: 1180 − 136 = 1044, which is still ≥ 1040 — the words fit at that tier without moving the paper's x-origin by a single pixel.

  • 06

    The measured band — one writer, a ResizeObserver, kept to the letter

    The band keeps its published height's name and its single writer, but a ResizeObserver measures the real box on mount and on every resize rather than only while pinned-and-unfolded — declared is the minimum, measured is the truth. It directly answered F44 Seam height is content-dependent, not a constant, where the published height already depends on which exception string is showing.

    Y's own R2 concedes a live exception string can overrun 56px; the mitigation is a pre-ship gate … which is a decision, not a mechanism.

    J2 judge seat, Dj2-13 · simulated seat

  • 07

    Releasing a region above the reading line, with a same-frame scroll correction

    Density running both ways: a region whose bottom has passed above the frame releases and a same-frame scroll correction takes back the exact height it gave up, so nothing on screen moves. It was the only mechanism that made the lens symmetrical, and it answered the render cost named in F53 The fold is the only render-cost control; FF&E is unvirtualized for a region on either side of the reading line, not just below it.

    A region that releases above the reading line takes its height with it and nothing gives it back… every pixel below it — the line she is reading — rises by 1,728px.

    C-feasibility critique seat, DC-01 · simulated seat

    The correction has to be right every frame; a trackpad fling crossing three releases in 200ms can land the correction one frame late, and no instrument in the tree records it when it does.

Two moves this program did not re-open, because a reader will ask: NG1, one document at a time — no split view, no tabs, no persistent global nav over an open document; and NG3, the Thumb Index, removed by Kody and not re-proposable.

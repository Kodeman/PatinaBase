What the probe measured

The pin at scrollY 280 is not a scroll effect — it is a state swap. 347.25px → 64.06px in one React commit; the first region head’s document-space Y jumps −283.19px inside that single 40px scroll step. Sampled every ∼17ms for 400ms: 23 of 23 samples read exactly 64.0625px — no interpolation in either direction.

Folding a region drops keyboard focus to <body>. Unfolding is disciplined — focus lands on the region’s own heading — but folding calls no equivalent; a keyboard user who folds a region has to re-locate it from the top of the page.

Cumulative layout shift over the scripted top→foot scroll: 0.1286 normal motion (20 entries), 0.1318 reduced motion (8 entries) — both in the “needs improvement” band, within 2.5% of each other. One shift of 0.1189 dominates both passes, arriving at ∼3.3–3.6s as the Schedule region’s “needs attention” banner resolves from a query — a data-arrival reflow, not an animation. The 283px ticket jump measured above does not register in this CLS total at all.

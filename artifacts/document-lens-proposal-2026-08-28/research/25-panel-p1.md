## 4. What stays true — do not break these

1. **`NEEDS ATTENTION · IN ONE PLACE`.** `Invoice INV-2026-W02 · $3,800 overdue — oldest due Aug
   14 — send a reminder` with `SEND REMINDER` beside it is the single best thing on this document.
   It names the trouble, the amount, the age and the act, on one line, in one place, with no icon
   language. A lens must never condense this into a count or a glyph. If exactly one block is
   exempt from yielding, it is this one.

2. **`← PUT DOWN`, always in the same pixel.** At 1440 it never leaves the frame and it costs 2%
   of it. That is the correct trade for a permanent control and it is the reason I trust the paper
   at all — I know how to leave before I know where I am. Whatever the rail becomes, this stays at
   its top-left corner at both desktop widths.

3. **Scored-ink verbs in plain English.** `SPEC THE 3 UNSPECIFIED →`, `BILL 3 UNINVOICED LINES →`,
   `ADD A ROOM`, `ADD A LINE`, `DRAW AN INVOICE`, `NUDGE CLIENT USER`, `MESSAGE THE CLIENT`. Every
   one names the act and its object with the number in it. No boxes, no icons, no hover. This is
   the vocabulary my trade actually speaks and I would notice immediately if it were traded for
   symbols.

4. **The FF&E line as it is drawn.** `Møbler Lounge Chair — Bouclé · ×2` / `Nordic Atelier` /
   `IN PRODUCTION` / `$5,700` — name, vendor, state stamp, price, on one line, with the 48px crop
   to the left. The filled stamp reads at a glance from across the desk and the hover wash tells me
   which line my pointer owns. This is the row my Google Sheet is competing with and it is winning.

5. **The `Pieces` head's honest subtitle.** `the FF&E schedule, by room · 1 group · 3 lines` /
   `3 unspecified · 3 uninvoiced`. The head uses the house word and then immediately translates
   itself into mine, with counts. Any lens that condenses this head must keep a counted sentence,
   not just the word `Pieces`.

6. **One document, one paper.** I resent D1 on T2 and I said so, but I do not want it traded away
   for tabs. When I am inside Chen Residence the whole screen being Chen Residence is why the
   document is calm at all. The fix for T2 is a better desk, not a split document.

### 2.6 Every mechanic doing something to my screen that I did not ask for, mid-sentence

**X.** One, and it is the biggest single defect in this critique. A region **above** the frame quiets from 1,840px to 112px with no scroll correction anywhere in the proposal, so the paper leaps under me every time I scroll back up past a region I have already read — which, on a 36-line schedule I am reconciling against an approval, is constantly (Dp-01). **Y.** One, and Y names it as its own R1: the same-frame `scrollBy` correction. Every release above the reading line takes the exact height delta back out of the scroll offset in the same layout pass. When it is right, nothing moves and this is the correct mechanism. When it is a frame late — on a trackpad fling crossing three releases in 200ms — the paper walks upward and it is the 283px bug redistributed and harder to see. Y is honest about it, prices it, and gates Wave 3 on it. That is the right way to carry a risk.

**Both.** The document's total height changes on every density commit, so the **scrollbar thumb resizes and jumps continuously**. It is the only instrument on the screen that tells me how far into a 60-line schedule I am, and neither proposal mentions it once (Dp-41).

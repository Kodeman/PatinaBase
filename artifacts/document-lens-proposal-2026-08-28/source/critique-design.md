# Critique — C-design

*The Document · The Smart Lens · 2026-08-28. Critic seat C-design, fresh context, author of neither proposal, judge of neither. Both proposals read in full, the shared planks read in full, and eight shots read with the Read tool: `w1440-rich-s0.png`, `w1440-rich-s1.png`, `w1440-rich-s2.png`, `w1280-rich-s1.png`, `m390-rich-s1.png`, `w1440-spine-full.png`, `w1440-ticket-seam.png`, `w1440-margin-rail.png`. Every number below is either measured in `research/12-layout-measurements.json`, printed in a verified finding, or arithmetic done on the proposal's own figures and shown.*

---

## 1 · One line

**X.** X's header is the best deletion in the program and its rail is the worst duplication: it pulls 627px out of the top of the paper by removing things, then rebuilds a smaller version of the ticket in a 200px column — a three-line head whose own strings do not fit the rail's measured 168px, four segments that index 61% of the paper and go blind below `money-head`, five doors that have no route at 1280, and a passed-mark, an act-cell and a yielding crossfade that are three mechanisms spent on one fact. And X's central promise — *a region changes height only while entirely out of frame, so the words she is reading stay on the pixel they were on* — is false above the reading line: collapsing a 1,840px region that sits above the viewport moves everything below it, and X names no scroll correction anywhere.

**Y.** Y's band is honest and its rail is a table of contents. Having argued the rail's values out (§11.7), its extents out (§11.1) and its exception marks out (F22 refused), Y is left with twelve rungs that print the paper's own head words — which is the exact failure Law 3 names, and the exact defect F102 recorded against *one* such copy. Then the band prints the current stop *again*, on line 1, in the same frame, and §11.2 says so out loud. Y quiets the screen partly by moving money off it: at s2 and s3, on the specimen, no figure of money is anywhere in the frame, where today the rail prints one at every offset.

**Where each is emptier rather than uncluttered.**

*Y.* At s2 and s3 the money figure is nowhere. The money region is `quiet` and, by Y's own thresholds, quiet regions are off screen by construction. The rail prints no values. The band prints money only when money is the worst standing thing — and on the specimen the worst standing thing is an approval six days overdue, not the $17,500 outstanding. Today the rail prints `$6,200 OWED` at s0, s1, s2 and s3 (F84: rail ink is identical at all four states; `w1440-spine-full.png` shows the line). Y therefore quiets the frame by removing a fact from it, which is axis 1's named failure.

*X.* The 1280 tier. X's rail prints "no words at all", so at 1280 the current region's name, the job's name and every segment value leave the frame. **And the reverse of emptier — both proposals make a new kind of busy.** Today a region she has not folded is a full body; a `FoldSeam` is 44px and rare. Under both proposals every region not in frame is a stub. X's stub is 68px, or 112px with a standing exception. Y's is ~118px and carries four printed things — head, status line, exception strings, leader act, count line. Y's `condensed` state is explicit that a quiet region keeps its `--rule-strong` double rule. So a frame at s3 can be seven region heads, seven double rules, seven count lines and seven leader acts, stacked. Neither §6 counts that, and neither §10 names it.

### 2.2 Does the lens survive a real Tuesday?

The Vandersteen specimen: approval overdue 6 days, fabric selection overdue 3, PO-2026-0418 unacknowledged 14 days, a gouged console whose carrier window closes tomorrow, the Byrne agreement six days unopened.

**X — where it helps.** The rail head's third line swaps to `OVERDUE 6 DAYS · PRIMARY BEDROOM` the moment the needs band leaves the frame (X-8), so the worst exception is in frame at s1, s2 and s3 at 1440 — today no frame below s0 carries it (F13, blocker). The FF&E segment's value line carries `1 DAMAGED` and its tick is terracotta at every offset, which is the *only* place in either proposal the gouged console reaches her below the fold. The money segment prints `$17,500 OWED · 22 DAYS` at every offset she is not standing in the money head. Three of her five live problems are on the rail at s2.

**X — where she works around it by the second hour.** ⌘F is dead (X §11.5, named and refused). She is looking for `Fond du Lac Ironworks` on a 60-line schedule; find-in-page reaches one or two regions. She will scroll to Pieces first, every time, which is the act the lens was supposed to save. At 1280 she stops pressing the ladder — X's own R5 — because a text-free instrument has to be learned once and nothing teaches it. And at the foot: X's ladder has four segments, the paper past `money-head` is 1,508px of the rich doc's 3,905 (38.6%), so when she scrolls to the roster question or the Record the window is off the end of its own track and the rail's answer to "where am I" is `THE RECORD` on line 3 and nothing on the ladder (F116 unaddressed).

**Y — where it helps.** The band's line 2 carries the worst standing exception *with its act* at every offset: `OVERDUE 6 days — Primary bedroom client approval …` + `SEND REMINDER`. That is the single best moment in either proposal for this Tuesday — today `SEND REMINDER` exists only at s0 (visible in `w1440-rich-s0.png`, gone from `w1440-rich-s2.png`). She can act on the six-day-overdue approval from anywhere on the paper. The ladder's twelve rungs mean she can reach `The accounts` and `Closing the book` in one press, which today do not appear in the rail at all.

**Y — where she works around it by the second hour.** The console. The carrier window closes tomorrow and nothing in Y's lens knows about it: F23/F62/F63/F65 are refused as composition (§11.8), the rail prints no values, and `1 damaged` reaches her only inside the `Pieces` count line, which prints only while Pieces is quiet — that is, off screen. PO-2026-0418's fourteen days of silence: same. She will keep the Orders ledger open in another tab, which is the thing NG1 exists to prevent. And money: at s2, editing a fabric on a $14,880 line, no money figure is in frame. She scrolls to the money head and back, twice an hour, which is F141 made worse rather than better.

**Both.** The third and later exceptions get a count and no door — X's `+2 MORE — OPEN THE LEDGER` names a destination it does not specify; Y's `· +2 more` names none. On the specimen there are at least four standing things. She reads "+2 more" and has nowhere to press.

### 2.4 The hardest question — where a deletion beats a mechanism

**X, per organ.**

*Spine.* X-3, the passed mark: a 1px clay rule growing 0→100% of a segment over 200ms, to say "you have been here". The window already says where she is; the segment ink already says which one is current. Three position signals; delete the third. — X-7, the act cell: 28px permanently reserved in a 200px rail plus a 150ms crossfade, to print one region's leader act during the narrow band of scroll where its head has left the frame and its body has not. Deleting the cell costs her nothing she cannot get by scrolling 200px up, and returns 28px. — X-6, the yielding crossfade: a mechanic whose entire job is to *un-print* a value the rail chose to print. Y's deletion (the rail prints no values) achieves the same silence with no observer, no crossfade and no reduced-motion row. X should not have needed X-6; it needed a decision about what the rail is for.

*Header.* None. This is X's best section and it should be said plainly: X answers the ask's loudest complaint by removing things. The ticket dissolves (−347.25px), the instruments move (−44px), the letterhead sheds 61px, the seam's writer is retired to zero. 627px of recovery, almost all of it deletion. No mechanism was spent where a deletion would do.

*Regions.* X-4/X-5, the whole density system. See §2.4's closing paragraph, which applies to both.

*Margin.* None. Sorting seven chips into `BESIDE PIECES · 3` and `THE WHOLE JOB · 4` is composition, not choreography, and the first-touch note's recession is a deletion worth 230px.

**Y, per organ.**

*Spine.* The ladder. Y removed the rail's values (§11.7), its extents (§11.1) and its exception marks (F22 refused) — every reason the rail could give for existing except position. What is left is twelve rungs printing head words the paper prints at 24px Playfair, and one clay segment. The deletion that would do: keep `Put down`, keep one clay position mark, and let the band's stop word be the name. The whole of Y's rail is a mechanism where a deletion and one word would do, and Y's own §11.2 is the proof — it argues that a second band is unnecessary *because the ladder's clay segment says the same thing*, without noticing that the argument runs in both directions.

*Header.* One item on line 1. The right-flush stop word is M-8 reduced to a word, and Y says it costs nothing against SC2's budget. It costs the standing assignment: it is a third copy of the current region in the s2 frame. Delete it and line 1 is four facts, none duplicated. — And `INSTALL SEP 15`: on the specimen the letterhead's own vitals carry the target date; the schedule region's head carries it; the band carries it at every offset. Argued in as "nine characters", but it is nine characters that already have two homes.

*Regions.* Same as X, plus Y-4's same-frame `scrollBy` correction — the single most delicate piece of engineering in either proposal, and it exists only because Y releases regions above the reading line. The deletion: do not release above. A region that has passed above the frame top costs nothing in frame; unmounting it buys render cost, which the fold already provides (F53). Y's own R1 says "if the measurement is off by a frame, the paper walks upward every time she scrolls past a region — the 283px bug, redistributed and harder to see."

*Margin.* Y-5, the travelling `IN FRAME` rule. A 220ms `top` transition on an absolutely-positioned span in a 232px right-hand column. Y's R4 already predicts somebody will try to drag it. X's printed group heading does the same job, from a still, with no motion and no scrollbar confusion. Mechanism where a deletion — or in this case a printed word — would do.

**And the one that applies to both, which is the answer this program most needs.**

*The density system buys no frame budget.* Look at either §6 table. X's s1 recovery — header/summary 60.7% → 16.9% — is the ticket, the instruments and the letterhead. Y's s1 recovery — 60.7% → 0.0% — is the same three deletions. Neither proposal attributes a single percentage point in any of its twelve cells to a region going quiet, and neither could: **a region entirely out of frame occupies zero of the frame whether it is 1,840px tall or 112px tall.** What the two of them spend on that fact is: an IntersectionObserver per region, a hysteresis pair with two magic numbers, a velocity gate (X) or a two-frame agreement rule (Y), a fourth voice in `use-region-fold.ts`, a focus guard, four new per-region quiet forms, a same-frame scroll correction (Y) or an unnamed dependence on scroll anchoring (X), one new e2e spec each, and a page of state machine. The two things it does buy — render cost on a 60-line unvirtualized FF&E body, and a shorter scrollbar — are respectively already served by the shipped fold (`use-region-fold.ts`, F53) and not asked for by anybody in the ask. The ask's sentence is *content that appears when it needs to and lends to space when it isn't needed in frame*; **space in frame is what it asks for, and density delivers space out of frame.** The deletion here is not of an element but of a mechanism: cut the density system, keep the header work and the rail work, and both proposals lose almost nothing measurable and shed most of their engineering risk.

## 4 · Defects

Every defect carries a severity and a confidence. Confidence below 0.5 appends "what would settle this". Ids Dd-01–Dd-24 are against X; Dd-25–Dd-48 are against Y; paired ids are noted where the defect is the same one twice.

### Against X

**Dd-01 · X · blocker · 0.80 · s1/s2/s3 · 1440 · §3 X-4/X-5, §4 header H5(b)**
A region collapsing *above* the viewport moves the reading line, and X names no correction. X-4's `what never moves` column reads "every pixel inside the viewport, at any offset — the rule is that a region never changes height while any part of it is visible", and H5(b) repeats it. That rule is sound for a region below the frame and false for one above it: shrinking FF&E from 1,840px to 112px at 120px above the frame top pulls 1,728px of document up under her unless the scroll offset is corrected in the same layout pass. Y specifies exactly that correction (Y-4, "a same-frame `scrollBy` takes back the exact height delta") and calls it its own Rank-1 risk. X specifies nothing — no `scrollBy`, no `overflow-anchor`, no mention of browser scroll anchoring, which WebKit does not implement and which `playwright.config.ts:54-68` enables (F35). This is SP-03's whole subject and F04's 283.19px jump reintroduced at eleven times the frequency.
*Fix:* adopt Y-4's same-frame scroll correction for both directions, or forbid a density change on any region above the reading line.

**Dd-02 · X · high · 0.70 · s2/s3 · 1440 · §5 `reading`, §6 SC11 refusal**
X's density rule is self-referential and can oscillate at zero scroll velocity. "Every region intersecting the frame is `full`" (§5) plus a quiet region of 68–112px means that when a region collapses, the regions below it move up into the frame, which makes them intersect, which makes them `full`, which pushes them back out, which makes them quiet. The 120/40px hysteresis is measured against a box whose position is a function of the density decision, and the X-10 velocity gate is inert at rest. X's M3 paragraph reasons only about the reader's motion, never about the layout's own.
*Fix:* compute density against the *unquieted* document positions, held constant per settle.

**Dd-03 · X · high · 0.85 · s3 · 1440 · §4 spine (the ladder), §7 (F116 absent)**
The ladder indexes four regions and the paper has more. `rich.1440.s0.s0Extra` gives `scrollHeight: 3905` and `regionHeadMap: {schedule: 1005.31, ffe: 1665.8, money-head: 2396.53}`; the 1,508px below `money-head` — 38.6% of the paper, and the frame `w1440-rich-s3.png` shows as authorizations, accounts, closing the book and the roster — carries no segment. At s3 the window has no track to sit on and `data-reading-index` has nothing to name, which fails SC12's "never null while the paper is in view" and leaves F116 (`Rail says 'Money' active while the frame shows roster/authorizations`) exactly as measured. X's §4 NG3 defence — "exactly as many marks as the paper has regions — four on a project spread" — states the defect as a virtue.
*Fix:* index every stop the paper prints, as Y does, and accept the `regionHeadingId` throw risk Y names in its R6.

**Dd-04 · X · high · 0.80 · all states · 1440 · §4 spine (the rail head), §6 SC3**
The rail head's "reserved 64px" cannot hold its own strings. `doc-spine.tsx:44` sets `min-[1440px]:px-4` on a 200px column, and the file's own comment reads "The fixed 200px spine column leaves ~168px inside its own px-4". Line 2 is `PROJECT · PROCUREMENT & ORDERS 4 OF 6` — 37 characters at 11px mono with tracking, roughly 250–265px, so two lines minimum; line 3 `INSTALL SEP 15 · 3 WEEKS` is 24 characters, roughly 170px, so two lines.

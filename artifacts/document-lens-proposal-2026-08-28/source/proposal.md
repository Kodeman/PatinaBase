## 0 · Where the judges disagreed, and the ruling

Two organs. Everything else in both merge instructions is concordant and is carried without comment.

**The header. J1: Y's band. J2: X's deletion. Ruled: the band lives, and its height is declared rather than measured.**

### Motion grammar

The whole grammar is §3's table. Five rules govern it.

**M2 — what may animate on a condense.** Ink, weight, and a reserved height. Never a layout property while the element is visible. The band's height is a constant, so there is no header layout to animate. A region's height changes only while it and everything above it are below the frame's bottom edge, so the question of an acceptable visible layout shift never arises. **The word `scrollBy` appears nowhere in this document, and there is no scroll correction of any kind.**

**M3 — one threshold, and why there is no second.** A region's body mounts when its root's top comes within **240px** of the frame's bottom edge — off screen, always — and it never unmounts for the life of the document view. There is no release threshold, so there is no hysteresis pair to tune and nothing for the mockup's `--motion-scale: 4` prober to oscillate on. **240px and not a smaller number, defended against the specimen rather than against a sentence:** one FF&E line on the specimen is a 48px catalog crop plus `py-2` plus a rule = 65px, so 240px is more than three lines and a three-line nudge cannot re-cross it. It is the same number at 1440, 1280 and 390, because a line is the same height at every width.

**Momentum and reverse-scroll, ruled separately, in one paragraph.** Downward: the mount threshold sits 240px ahead of her eye, so a fling arrives at a finished page; a fling suppresses the L-9 settle and the opens fire together at the landing. Upward: everything above her is already mounted and stays mounted, so there is **no upward transition to rule**. One rule, because the other direction has no events in it.

**M4 — the ambient budget stays one.** `doc-breath`, 3s, on the active StrataMark, in the rail, at `globals.css:271-282`. No second.

**M5 — reduced motion is a form, and here is which shipped block each sits beside.** `app/globals.css` carries nine `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` blocks — I grepped them: **`:283` · `:439` · `:496` · `:833` · `:955` · `:1013` · `:1188` · `:1468` · `:1519`** — plus the no-preference gate at **`:429`**. ## 10 · Risks

Six, each with the observation that proves it real.

**R1 — The rail is still the cluttered thing.** This design moves four ticket doors and a six-stop map into a 200px column and asks it to also hold the household, the arc and the stage. The ask's first complaint was that the rail is cluttered, and the ink number goes up.
*Falsifying observation, first week of use:* the probe prints **more than 13 distinct text labels** in the rail at 1440/s0, or a practitioner walk at that state names more than one **tense** above the `--rule-mid` — the head is meant to hold exactly one, *this job and where it stands*. If she reads the arc as one thing and the stage phrase as another, the head has not merged them and the eviction did not go far enough. The label count, not the ink percentage, is the instrument: an ink metric that counts a bordered track as continuous ink can be gamed and the label count cannot.

**R2 — The band's one line is not enough for a real exception.** Line 2 at 15px in a 944px measure holds ~110 characters; the specimen's worst is 96 before its act. The whole height contract rests on the truncation rule.
*Falsifying observation, first week of building:* render the specimen's two red-letter rows and the longest live `deriveTicketSeam` exception into the band at 1440, 1280 and 390 and read them. **R3 — One line at s0 is less than the block it replaces.** Today `NEEDS ATTENTION · IN ONE PLACE` prints **two** exceptions with two acts at s0 and drops the third (F50). The band prints one plus `+3 MORE`. F127 records that block as *"nearly the only colour-coded signal on the first screen and a junior's eye correctly snaps to it."*
*Falsifying observation, first week of use:* on a specimen-grade document a designer at s0 names fewer standing things unaided than she names today, or presses `+3 MORE` and cannot say afterwards how many there were. The compensation is that the sheet lists **all** of them with **their own acts**, which today's block never does — but if the count on the band reads as a dismissal rather than a door, the door has to print its number in the sheet's own heading.

**R4 — One frame of lookahead is the wrong distance on a fling.** 240px is more than three FF&E lines on the specimen, and a trackpad fling can cover three frame heights in 200ms. A region whose body has not mounted has nothing to paint.
*Falsifying observation, first week of building:* seed a 60-line, 4-room schedule, fling s0→s3 at 1440, and record blank paper where a region should be for more than one frame. ## 11 · Refuses

**1 — No proportional-extent map rail.** The ladder does not draw regions at their true height. On the specimen `Pieces` is 177px against `Money`'s 50px — **3.5:1 drawn for a true 10:1** — because every segment takes a 36px floor first, and a proportionally-drawn `Money` rung would be a 6px press target, which is a 2.5.8 failure before it is a taste failure. *Refused, not deferred:* the drawing carries **order and reach**, and the **count in the value line carries scale**. The phrase *true proportional extent* is withdrawn rather than defended. A drawing that lies about scale by a factor of three, and says so, is better than one that lies by the same factor and does not.

**2 — No standing rule.** The current region's head does not pin beneath the band at reduced height. Two stacked sticky bands are a header again, and SC2's 52px of unspent budget would be spent on chrome rather than saved. *Refused, not deferred:* the fact it would carry — which region she is inside — is printed exactly once per frame, on the paper, and the window says where that is on the ladder.

**3 — No continuous seam, and no discrete one.** E1 §2 offers three costed branches for making the seam's height a function of scroll — `days`, `week`, `weeks`. None is taken. E1's own sentence is the reason: *"a continuous seam is not a header change, it is a navigation change."* Every branch pays somewhere in `scrollToRegion`'s once-resolved `scroll-margin-top`, and every one is invisible to the suite because every seam assertion is jsdom. *Refused permanently:* the variable is deleted and its replacements are declared constants.

**4 — No values on the rail beyond one line per segment.** No second register, no counts stacked under names, no extent bars, no exception marks drawn as colour. A segment prints one line. *Refused, not deferred:* today `Money unread` and `$6,200 OWED` print at the same size, weight and row (F108), and `Client approvals` / `0 IN THE LOG` sits 540px from `Client approvals NO DECISION LEAD · NO APPROVALS AUTHORED` in a different register (F29). One line per segment, with the row position carrying the difference between a value and a fallback, is what stops the rail printing a false figure without also stopping it printing a true one.

**5 — No 160px rail at ≥1440, and no 272px margin.** The arithmetic is in §4: narrowing recovers 32px of measure at exactly 1440 and nothing at 1472 and above — three characters on a 15px line — and costs the rail 40px of its 168px inner measure, which is the whole value line. The margin's answer to *cramped* is the ~160px of vertical the two deletions return, plus the anchor lines that make its 232px legible against the paper. *Refused with the number, because S5 asked for the number* — and said out loud, because the width half of Kody's complaint deserves an answer rather than a silence.

**6 — No re-quieting, and no scroll correction.** A region she has read stays full for the session. *Refused rather than deferred* because every alternative — a same-frame `scrollBy(−Δ)`, `overflow-anchor`, a scroll-position correction — is a mechanism that has to be right every frame, on every fling, in a browser matrix that had no declared `browserslist` until Wave 0, to avoid moving a paragraph she is reading. The word `scrollBy` appears nowhere in this document.

**7 — No redesign of the foot.** F83 (310px teaching a concept with no content), F92 (70.3% of the foot frame carrying nothing), F98 (`Closing the book` as unexplained idiom) and F80 (the roster question 2,000px from its door) are all real and all left. The ask points at the top of the paper and the budget is spent there; the s3 frame budget moves by the seam's 8px plus the two new region roots. What the foot does gain is a segment and a door, so F116's *"the rail says Money while the frame shows authorizations"* stops being true. *Refused for this proposal, not for the product.*

**8 — No new capability.** No PO-acknowledgement badge on the FF&E line, no damage-claim door, no phase-wide install roster, no procurement card kind in the margin, no cross-document surface (F06, F23, F62, F63, F65, F66). *Refused, not deferred:* the ask is to make the same information and the same acts occupy less attention. Every one is a real gap, several cost P4 a trip out of the document, and they are named so the next program can pick them up. The one thing taken from that set is the **dates** — a carrier window is a deadline, and deadlines are what both organs already carry.

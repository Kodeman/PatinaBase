Your review, carried forward2

The room works.
Now connect the work.

“This feels cohesive and one job.” You also confirmed that the 36-line sheet is readable and that closing it returns you to your place. Those are the foundation for this pass.

01

A decision on each piece.

One room can hold an approved chair, a lamp awaiting an answer and a console needing a revision. Each answer belongs to the exact edition the client saw.

02

A conversation in context.

A question unfolds beside its selection. A maker’s discrepancy lives with the order. Private notes and unsent drafts are clearly marked, with a job-wide record when you need the whole thread.

03

A clear path to purchasing.

The schedule shows what can be ordered and what is waiting. Prepare the maker’s order, record their acknowledgment, then receive the pieces. A store or auction purchase has its own record.

Client decision→Authority
+ funding & terms→Order→Acknowledgment→Receiving

These are connected records, with separate meanings. Approval alone never places an order.

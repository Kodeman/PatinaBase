# Room Proof / 04 — Decisions stay with the work

Founder review, 22 October 2026. Builds on the approved continuous job, paper aesthetic, 36-line living-room sheet and same-room return.

Open `index.html` for the presentation, or `job.html` for the interactive job. Local fonts and imagery are bundled. For a local server, run `python3 serve.py --port 8080` from this folder and open the printed URL. Keep sibling round-three, round-two and workflow folders beside this one for comparison links.

## What to try

1. **Item decisions:** mixed client outcomes, a fresh review of selected lines, and retained editions. Change an approved price and see that the old review no longer covers the current selection.
2. **Client paper:** approve LR-03 and request changes on LR-04; a change request needs a note. Replies remain conversation, separate from decisions.
3. **Correspondence:** filter by subject and audience. Open LR-36 from its thread. Save a private studio note or an unsent client/maker draft inside a line or an order.
4. **Purchasing:** choose All rooms to see five ready lines. Select LR-12 and BR-03 for a single Still Light order, subtotal $908. The waiting view exposes missing approval, authority, funding or terms.
5. **An order:** record an acknowledgment difference on PO-02 and draft a maker reply. PO-01 already has a matching acknowledgment for a receiving demonstration. Partial delivery, damage and remaining quantities are explicit.
6. **Bought already:** record a fictional store buy, find, antique, expense or sample, including tax, shipping/premium, receipt reference and reimbursement flag. No payment is made.

The original five FF&E views remain available inside the job. Purchasing is the sixth. Notes unfold locally rather than occupying a permanent navigation panel.

## Scope

This is a static, fictional browser-local design prototype. It sends nothing, signs nothing, grants no authority and makes no payment. Earlier signed purchasing authority, maker terms and funding are fixtures used to demonstrate downstream work. New approval does not satisfy those gates. Role permissions, multi-user synchronization, actual messaging, budget/deposit flows, owner release thresholds, claims resolution and full accounting remain integration/design work.

Sample state: `patina-room-proof-round4-job-v1`. Founder feedback: `patina-room-proof-round4-review-notes-v1`. “Reset fictional sample” resets only the former. Refresh other open copies after editing in one tab; this prototype does not synchronize multiple open tabs.

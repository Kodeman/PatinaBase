## Verdict

This is a well-built, unusually disciplined proposal deck. Citation density is high and almost entirely accurate (25/26 sampled), every one of the twelve required rulings is present with a clear recommendation that traces cleanly back to the plan, all seven mockups exist and substantively match their briefs, and the voice/design constraints (no shadows, no red, terracotta for overdue, dark-mode parity, Google-Fonts-only) are all satisfied. The one real citation defect (F1, `threshold.tsx:266` → should be `:272`) is minor and was inherited from an inconsistency between the two source reports rather than invented by the deck's author. The more substantive gap is F7: the deck never says what happens to a studio invoice if its household later opens a project, which is exactly the kind of reasoning hole the brief asked to hunt for and is the one genuinely silent spot on an otherwise thorough page. F8 (refund path) is a lesser version of the same pattern — true and testable, but never stated in prose. None of the findings are blockers; the deck is ready for Kody's rulings pass with F1 as a trivial line-number fix and F7/F8 as one or two added sentences before or shortly after publishing.

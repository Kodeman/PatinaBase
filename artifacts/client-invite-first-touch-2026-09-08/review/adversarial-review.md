## Overall verdict

The deck is substantially faithful to `panel/synthesis.md`'s rulings, vocabulary discipline, and voice, and every spot-checked codebase claim (config values, migration defaults, file paths, checkbox copy, precedent line numbers) held up against the live repo. The two findings worth resolving before this goes out are internal-consistency bugs rather than fidelity failures: the composer specimen's character-counter math doesn't match its own displayed note text (inherited from a miscount in `lens-4-voice.md`), and the "state three" composer mock visually assigns the ClientPicker's collapsed-disclosure rendering to the add-person sheet, which synthesis explicitly reserves for the full, uncollapsed field. Everything else — copy blacklist compliance, Patina-naming discipline, rulings-table fidelity, structural link integrity, the visual/token contract, and the word budget — passed clean.

## 2 · Line 2 — what it prints today, what it must print after the fix

**Today on `…d5`** (four-tier sort, `TIER_ORDER`): `$17,500 owed you` `+7 MORE` — the ticket's `money-at-risk` exception, tier `overdue`, 7 stated days, **no act** — at every offset and width. Line 1's right slot prints the same money at s0 and s1+. So the band's one sentence has no act and repeats line 1.

**After the deadline-order fix (W3-R1) + one dedupe rule:** the standing set on `…d5` is eight rows of which two are one fact — `PAST DUE $17,500 owed you` (ticket) and `PAST DUE Invoice INV-2026-114 · $17,500 overdue — oldest due Aug 22 — send a reminder` (need, with `SEND REMINDER`). **Rule: a ticket exception whose figure appears in a need's sentence is the same standing item, and the need (which carries the act) wins.** Then line 2 = **`Invoice INV-2026-114 · $17,500 overdue — oldest due Aug 22` `SEND REMINDER` `+6 MORE`** (7 days past, the most overdue), and **line 1's right slot drops the money figure while line 2 names it** (the same yield the proposal already rules for the Money reading stop) — `INSTALL SEP 19` alone. Sheet title `STANDING · 7`. On the sheet the order becomes: invoice (7d) · `2 decisions overdue — oldest due Aug 23` (6d) · `1 piece delivered — awaiting inspection` · `FDL-0912 has an open damage claim` (window) · `PO-2026-0418 drafted — not yet sent` · `1 damaged` · `2 unspecified`.

## 3 · The 390 line 2 when the worst sentence is long (joint with the ARCHITECT)

Measured: the line's run is **327px**, **265.62px** after the `+N MORE` door; five of the eight seeded sentences cannot print whole there; `LENS_LINE2_MAX_CHARS` (110) never fires before CSS ellipsis. The mockup's 390 frame prints **`OVERDUE 6D · BEDROOM` `REMIND` `+3 MORE`** — not the sentence clipped, not the eyebrow alone.

**Ruling (what prints):** at 390 line 2 prints the item's **short form** — `<STATE> <DAYS>` · `<SUBJECT>` — where STATE is the sheet's eyebrow word (`OVERDUE`, `CLAIM OPEN`, `NO ACK`, `AWAITING INSPECTION` → `INSPECT`), DAYS the stated day-count (`7D`) when there is one, and SUBJECT the item's head noun phrase capped at **12 characters** (`INVOICE 2026-114` → `INV-2026-114`, `BEDROOM APPROVAL` → `BEDROOM`, `FDL-0912 CONSOLE` → `CONSOLE`); the act shortens to its first word (`shortenAct`: `SEND REMINDER` → `REMIND`, `CHASE THE APPROVAL` → `CHASE`, `FILE THE CLAIM` → `FILE`); the `+N MORE` door prints whole. Same 15px register, same terracotta ink. On `…d5` after §2: **`OVERDUE 7D · INV-2026-114` `REMIND` `+6 MORE`** (≈ 26 chars + 6 + door — inside 327). Rejected: a clipped sentence with an ellipsis (a sentence that lies about what it says), and eyebrow + act with the subject only in the sheet (an act with no named object is F18's shrug in a new coat). **Mechanism note for the ARCHITECT:** this is a per-item short *form* the derivation exposes (`short: { state, days, subject }`), not a per-tier character cap; the band picks the short form at the `narrow`/390 tier or whenever the long form would engage the ellipsis. Signed by the DESIGN LEAD; the architect rules how the form is computed.

# CS5 · Owner's representative / homeowner-side PM

Seat: owner's rep on $1M to $5M residential work. I hold the homeowner's authority matrix, the decision log, and the cadence. My rule on every job: the homeowner hears one story through one channel, and no money moves on a text from the wrong person. Tools used in anger: Basecamp, Notion, Excel, and a lot of email. Fixture: the Okonkwo residence (`fixture.md`). Paths are relative to the worktree.

## 3. Authority and decision rights

| Decision class | May approve | Must be copied | Recorded evidence | Catch rule the CRM must enforce |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Invoice, draw certification | F-05 Chidi | F-04, F-01, F-03, F-26 | approver, channel, date, amount, document id | approver identity must match the authority grant; a text or email from F-04 is logged as "received, not authority" |
| Change order over $2,500 | F-05 Chidi (owner side); F-07 Tom (cost side); F-01 Leah (fee side) | F-04, F-02, F-08 | CO number, signed by both sides, $ and days | reject an approval from F-08 or F-09; they prepare, they do not sign |
| Change order under $2,500 | F-04 Adaeze or F-05 Chidi | F-05 if F-04 approved, and the reverse | same | threshold lives on the household, not in a designer's head |
| Selections and finishes | F-04 Adaeze | F-02, F-05 when it moves price | selection id, date, channel | a selection from F-05 is accepted only if F-04 delegated in writing |
| Design intent, RFI answers | F-02 Priya; F-10 Sam for conformance | F-08 | RFI number, court, due date | court must be able to sit with the architect |
| Schedule slip | F-08 proposes; F-05 and F-04 acknowledge; F-01 for fee impact | F-26 if a draw date moves | new milestone dates, reason, who acknowledged | a slip told only to the homeowner by text from F-09 is not a slip on record |
| Site access, keys, lockbox | F-04 or F-05 grants; F-06 and F-09 hold | F-08 | who holds what, from when, code version | the CRM must know who holds a key before a trade is told "let yourself in" |
| Inspection scheduling | F-09 requests; F-27 rules | F-08, F-02 | date, result, corrections | never text F-27; results are pass or fail, not "looked fine" |
| Draw release | F-26 certifies; bank releases | F-03, F-05, F-07 | percent complete, waiver set, date | draw cannot be marked released without the waiver set |

What the record must hold to catch a text from the wrong person: the sender's identity resolved to a person, that person's authority set on this job, the decision class of the message, and the threshold. If any of the four is missing the approval is logged as unverified and the designer is told.

## 6. Three failure stories

**The thumbs-up change order.** Whole-house remodel, $2.1M. The super texted the wife a photo of a relocated beam and the price, $14,800. She replied with a thumbs-up. Framing proceeded. The husband, who held money authority under the agreement, first saw it on the pay app six weeks later and refused. Three parties spent a month on it. Missing data: an authority record saying who could approve money and above what amount, a decision class on the message, and a record of the channel. The CRM knew the wife's phone and nothing else.

**The lapsed certificate.** A drywall sub's COI expired in March; the job started in October. An apprentice fell from a baker's scaffold in week two. The GC's certificate folder had the prior year's PDF. The homeowner's carrier asked for the sub's COI naming the owner as additional insured; there was none in force. Missing data: an expiry date on a firm-level document, an owner of the chase, and a gate that ties site access to a current certificate. Nobody had lied; the date simply lived in a PDF nobody opened.

**The wrong door at the firm.** A plaster firm's rule was "email the office manager, never call the owner." A new designer at the studio called the owner twice about a schedule slip. He pulled the crew for a week. The office manager had a spreadsheet of the answer the designer needed. Missing data: a route-through pointer from the owner to the office manager, and a do-not-contact flag that the designer would have seen before dialing.

## 9. AMENDMENT-ASK items

1. **Owner's representative seat.** An owner's rep needs a client-side login with delegated authority (money, CO) and read of the decision log. Outside the frame because the primary user is the homeowner's agent, not the studio (PR-3). Compliant version: a `client_rep` party-on-job with authority facts and a contact rule, on paper; the studio records the approvals it receives from the rep as decision records (CS5-1, CS5-10).
2. **Firm self-service compliance upload.** The sub's office manager should upload a renewed COI or a signed waiver through a tokened link, as the evidence upload link already works (`current-state.md` §C `:217`). Outside the frame because the primary user is the sub's office. Compliant version: the studio bookkeeper uploads and dates the document on the firm card; the roster shows the lapse (CS5-5).
3. **Lender draw packet link.** The draw inspector (F-26) should read the draw package and the waiver ledger through a read-only link. Outside the frame because it is lender-facing. Compliant version: the studio exports the package as a PDF from the waiver ledger (CS5-7).

## 10. What would change my mind

1. A real Leah's-studio job where the designer, not the homeowner, held money authority by agreement: CS5-1 drops to P2.
2. Evidence that the GC's own system (Buildertrend or a binder) reliably gates site access on COI and the studio never gets asked: CS5-5 moves to the firm card only, no draw gate.
3. A walk of a five-job studio where nobody has been called at the wrong door in a year: CS5-3 and CS5-4 fold into one preferred-channel field.
4. A specimen showing the client page naming one contact and one channel already: CS5-20 closes.
5. A ruling that approvals live in the Contract Room and never in People: CS5-10 moves out of this room and stays a cross-link.

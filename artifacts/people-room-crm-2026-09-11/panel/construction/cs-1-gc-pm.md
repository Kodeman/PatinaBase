## 6. Three failure stories

**The lapsed COI.** Electrical sub, $1.2M remodel. COI expired in March; we learned in July when an apprentice nailed his hand and the insurer asked for the certificate. The expiry sat in a Sheet column nobody sorted. Missing: a fact on the firm, readable from the job, saying "not covered today," and a named chaser. F-11 is that case.

**The wrong signer.** Kitchen addition, $48,000 steel-beam CO. The wife texted "yes do it" from site. We built it. The husband, who signed the contract and funded the loan, disputed it at draw; the bank would not fund without his signature. We ate $11,000 in carrying cost. Missing: who holds money authority, the threshold, and a rule that a CO over it waits for the signer. F-04 and F-05 are one client row.

**The STOP that was not a STOP.** A plumber replied STOP to a holiday text after a 2024 job. The office set up his 2025 job as a new contact. Six weeks of schedule texts vanished silently. He came on the wrong day twice and walked off. Missing: consent is a fact about the phone; the new record should have been born "opted out on a prior job, call him." F-12 is that case; the Okonkwo row reads `not_asked` while the gate refuses (`supabase/functions/_shared/sms.ts:176-185`).

## 9. AMENDMENT-ASK items

**AMENDMENT-ASK A (touches PR-3, S2). Trade self-service over the field link.** The GC's PM (F-08) adds crew (F-09, a new foreman) to the Call Sheet and uploads the firm's COI, W-9, and license renewals through the field link; each lands unverified until a studio member confirms. Why: the rolodex is only as current as the GC's office, which is one person. Conflict: the writer is a trade. Compliant version: a studio member enters crew and uploads docs from the GC's email; the link stays read-only. Ask: is an upload-only door a trade surface or studio intake.

**AMENDMENT-ASK B (touches PD-1, PR-3). Firm compliance as a platform fact.** Amara (F-16) holds an account from another studio; Stonehaven (F-20) is one `vendors` row saved twice. A COI expiry is true about the firm for every studio. Ask: a platform-level compliance record on the firm, readable by every studio holding it, written by the firm or any studio that verified the document. Conflict: cross-studio state on the firm, beyond PD-1. Compliant version: each studio holds its own copy on its company card; the rolodex pick carries the last verified copy forward.

**AMENDMENT-ASK C (touches PR-3). Second-tier subs.** The framer subcontracts its own crane; the GC contracts them, the studio never does. Ask: a party row with `contracted_by` pointing at another party so site access and COI read for every body on site. Compliant version: the studio lists only firms it agrees with; the GC's field link shows the GC's crew read-only.

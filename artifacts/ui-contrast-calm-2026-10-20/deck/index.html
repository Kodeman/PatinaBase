    What Leah asked for

    I need the contrast that I see on the sign-in page. It helps my eyes know where to go.

    Leah, co-founder and designer

    What we heard. Text contrast on the Document is generally strong, with a few local failures (slide 17). Body ink is 13.5:1 and most action text is 6.3–8:1.

    What helps the eye is salience: one shape that stands out, and a real step in size between where you are and what you can do.

    03 · The reference

    What the sign-in page does.

    1. A large dark pane against the form paper, at area scale: 16.98:1. Dark pixels are 38.9% of this screenshot.
    2. One filled act. The label on the button is 16.57:1, sentence-case Inter 14 semibold, 384×48. At rest it is disabled (2.25:1) until an email is typed.
    3. One 384px column, and very few choices.
    4. A 30px Playfair heading over 11–14px text, about 2–2.7×.
    5. Boxed inputs with oak edges at 4.3–4.5:1.

    The lenses disagree on which of these matters most. Lens A ranks the pane first, Lens B the size ratio, Lens C the one filled act. We don't know which one Leah's eye uses, so we test it (slide 8).

    Local capture, 1440×900, SQ-1496. Prod renders the same layout.

    07 · What the proposals share

    Six principles.

    1. At most one dark shape per view.Only for an eligible next step. Closed, held, waiting and open-sheet states show no fill. Never make up work to fill the slot.
    2. Size before ink.A real heading level between page title and metadata. Region name clearly larger than its metadata (sign-in runs about 2–2.7×); the exact step is chosen by specimen in R2.
    3. Space groups; a rule only opens a movement.12 within a group, 24 between groups, 48 between regions. A region closes with space and a hairline.
    4. One lead per section, chosen by the work's state.A state sentence, one scored lead (unfilled), then quiet tools. The band names the same step when it points there.
    5. Actions read as words.Sentence-case Inter 14–15, weight 400 or more, with a resting underline. Mono caps for metadata only.
    6. Edges where an object needs one.An identifying boundary is 3:1 or more. Hairlines are decoration. An opt-in prefers-contrast: more tier for Leah's Mac.

    16 · Four readings, disagreements kept

    What the panel said.

    Lens A · visual weight · Opus

    One dark field and one filled act.

    Sign-in tells the eye where to go with both; the Document has neither, and spends its darkest ink on full-width rules (24.4% of ink mass in Leah's screenshot, an image statistic). Ranks the pane first. Its dissent on the heading: keep the eyebrow name, put the weight in a strong state sentence.

    Lens B · space, rhythm, calm · Opus

    There is no middle tier.

    Title at 34px, then every region named at 12px, the same size as its status and its buttons. 26px Playfair region names are the biggest single lever, which is where A and B part. Space groups; a rule only opens a movement. Warns bigger heads add ~100px+ over seven regions.

    Lens C · low vision, accessibility · Opus

    Ratio isn't salience.

    Text tokens clear 4.5:1 on every stock (weakest action text 6.35:1); a few rendered spots fail through opacity (slide 17). The effect is salience. Prefers a value step to a dark band: on charcoal, mocha drops to 1.72 and a clay-ink focus ring to 2.41. Found defects to fix regardless, and offered the prefers-contrast tier.

    Astra · cross-provider review · GPT-6 Astra

    Not ready unchanged. 15 findings, all accepted.

    Three required changes: (1) correct the V20 history and ruling map; (2) an unbiased pane-vs-button comparison with explicit review-vs-commit meaning; (3) long-document, responsive, accessibility and ordinary-editing specimens, not a squint test alone. It also narrowed C's "all tokens clear 4.5" to "generally strong".

    17 · Any proposal must meet these

    Accessibility guardrails, and defects to fix regardless.

    • Text 4.5:1 or more on its actual ground, including bands, pools and state inks on rail. No opacity on text.
    • Every action keeps a non-colour resting cue at 3:1 or more. Never colour alone.
    • Focus visible at 3:1 or more on every ground, including dark bands and filled plates; not hidden by the mobile bar or sticky band.
    • 44px targets; ad-hoc links reach 24px or use the spacing exception.
    • 200% zoom and 320px reflow without clipped labels; survives text-spacing overrides.
    • Forced colours: underlines, terminal box and focus survive (SPEC §A11).
    • Reduced motion kept. Held actions use aria-disabled with a visible reason; disclosures aria-expanded; pressed aria-pressed.
    • State readable with colour removed. No shadows (D4).

    Defects, no ruling needed (not built in this story)

    • ffe-section.tsx:637Clay-ink at opacity-70 is 3.07:1. Fails 1.4.3.
    • region-head.tsx:254Heading focus outline in clay is 2.18:1. Fails 1.4.11; change to clay-ink.
    • mobile-bar.tsx:487Held row uses opacity-60. Change to text-faint.
    • SPEC §A11 for .da-*Forced-colors rules not implemented; underlines likely vanish. Needs a runtime check.
    • Token namesPortal muted #5C4A3C / subtle #4E4339 vs canonical #4E4339 / #5A4E43, and --hairline-strong. Needs a role mapping, not a swap.
    • State inks on rail~4.3–4.45:1 (golden-ink 4.452). Keep them off rail.

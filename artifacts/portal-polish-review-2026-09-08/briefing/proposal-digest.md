## 5. Interaction inventory

Deck nav: `#prev`/`#next`/`#chapterSelect` and `[data-goto]` all call `showSlide()`, which updates the counter, disables end buttons, updates `#deckStatus` (`aria-live="polite"`), and pushes `#slide-N` via `history.replaceState`; `[data-source]` links jump to slide 15; arrow/Home/End keys navigate outside form controls. On load, `location.hash` sets the initial slide once — it does not react to later hash changes. `#readingToggle` toggles `body.reading` (stacks all slides) and its own label.

Slide 7: `[data-lens]` swaps concept photo/SVG plan and updates the caption; `[data-finish]` (ignored once the receipt is showing) updates finish text/price context and resets any in-progress approval; `#reviewApprove` opens the confirm panel and focuses consent; `#approvalConsent`'s change handler enables/disables `#confirmApproval`; `#cancelApproval` closes the confirm panel; `#confirmApproval` (no-ops without consent) shows the receipt and disables finish buttons; `#resetApproval` reverts everything; `#requestChange` writes a static "sends nothing" message.

Slide 8: `#densityToggle` toggles `.compact` on the roster; `#findDemo` reveals the search field; `#searchProjects` filters `[data-project]` rows live and reports a count; `[data-demo]` buttons write informational text only. Slide 9: `#compareToggle` shows/hides the finish comparison; `#saveDirection` writes a local "saved" message. Slide 10: `[data-feedback]` specimens each write their own message. Slide 11: `#milestoneAck` and `#retryDemo` each rewrite local text/state. Every feedback string is ephemeral client-side text in a `role="status"` element; nothing is sent over the network, and the copy says so at nearly every interactive point.

Billing recovery flows

The shortest path from each billing blocker to fixed and billed: a drafted invoice that opens as the folio, or a sent email. Today’s path is read from code at f3c7fb53a. The proposed path uses house primitives only: held acts (§A5), the consequence sentence (§A6), ActFailLine (R83), and repair sheets stacked above the composer. Step counts are read from code; Study B’s walk confirms them. The full reasoning, evidence lines and trade-offs are in flows.md.

How to read a flow

Numbered boxes are counted presses. Ticking the lines is the work and is not counted; “+N” is re-ticking after the work was lost. Badges show what happens to the designer’s entered work.

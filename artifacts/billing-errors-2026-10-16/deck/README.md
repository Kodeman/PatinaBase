# Billing errors that tell you what to fix: synthesis deck (US-35, Study E)

`index.html` is a 17-sheet deck that pulls the four US-35 studies together: the inventory (`../inventory/`, A), the walk (`../walk/`, B), the needs-attention prototypes (`../prototypes/`, C) and the recovery flows (`../flows/`, D). It also covers the SQ-1019 fix, which is merged to main but not yet in production; it ships on Kody's go. The deck starts with what Leah hit when she drafted the Kuehn invoice, plus her unblock today (the Document band's "Link a client" row, then Draft again). It then sizes the problem: 27 failure states, 21 of them knowable before the press, 20 that show raw database text or a code, and 7 with no fix destination. It shows the walk's top problems with screenshots, compares the three directions (spotlight, held and land, and the "Before this can go" list) on one table, and gives the spotlight's D4, opacity and depth-ladder reconciliation honestly. Next come the recovery flows and the project-creation prevention trade-off, and a sheet on where the studies disagree. It recommends "Before this can go" with held-and-land's landing, a phased build list and Study A's seven copy rules. The deck links the walk PNGs and prototypes by relative path, so publish it together with the sibling folders. It reads in light and dark and at 390 px.

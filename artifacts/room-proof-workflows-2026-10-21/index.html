04 / Follow the work

Three ways to
test the direction.

Use Reset example inside the mockup before each independent walkthrough. These sequences are designed to reveal how the interface behaves beyond its opening screen.

01

From an idea
to a decision.

Can a designer keep the room in mind while moving through sourcing and client review?

Start in the library ↗
  1. Find the bronze floor lamp

    Add it to Lake Mendota House. The room’s selection, financial totals, and spec book all pick up the same piece.

  2. Open the chair’s proof

    Choose the studio’s oak finish. Inspect configuration, lead time, and the distinction between client price and studio cost.

  3. Prepare the client question

    After changing selections, issue a fresh drawing edition under Room & brief → Drawing sets. Confirm that edition with every reviewer, then issue the request.

  4. Record Nora’s decision

    The designated decision lead answers. Later selection changes require renewed review and preserve the earlier evidence.

Look for

Design approval does not authorize a purchase.

That boundary must survive the redesign.
02

From intention
to installation.

Can the design language support operational detail without losing clarity?

Start with financials ↗
  1. Read the project’s money

    Budget, planned spend, purchase authority, receivables, and vendor balances each keep their own meaning.

  2. Prepare the Northline order

    Inspect the draft and its release holds. The sample deposit is not automatically allocated to this order.

  3. Change a project date

    Review the schedule effect before applying it. The installation punch list belongs to the installation phase.

  4. Inspect a receiving example

    A separate, previously released Oak Street order demonstrates receipt and claim handling without pretending the Mendota order shipped.

Look for

A calm surface can still show a firm hold.

No order release or vendor send occurs here.
03

From a day’s work
to the books.

Does the studio feel connected when the designer steps away from the room?

Start in timesheets ↗
  1. Review recorded time

    Separate eligible billable work from the entry awaiting a rate. Add a time entry of your own.

  2. Prepare an invoice draft

    Use eligible unbilled entries. See exactly which work is included before it moves into Accounts.

  3. Follow the invoice

    Inspect the draft, simulate issue, and record a sample receipt. The invoice retains its source time entries.

  4. Return through the record

    See activity in the studio inbox. Open the source view or find Nora’s household and draft a reply in Contacts.

Look for

The book feels like part of the studio.

All invoice and payment actions are local simulation.

Artwork fallbacks — what an unloaded or image-less piece looks like

Never a grey box. PatinaAsyncImage draws a clay Strata mark on Background.secondary while loading or on failure; a piece with no image URL at all falls back to its category gradient (ProductModel.swift:115-124). The labelled variant is the honest placeholder for a mock: maker plus material, never a stock photograph.

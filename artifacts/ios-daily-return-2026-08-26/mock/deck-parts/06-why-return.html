Rejected, and why

  • Streaks. Rewards the app’s need for opens, not the house getting finished. A room furnished over a season should have gaps.
  • Countdown urgency on pieces. False scarcity against a 21-item catalog.
  • Fabricated “new since yesterday” badges. Already shipped as the hard-coded dot. Remove it; do not extend it.
  • Speculative pushes. A notification that fires when nothing happened teaches people to turn notifications off.
  • Completeness meters that never fill. A progress illusion is not progress.
  • Randomised feed shuffle. Dishonest variability, and it spoils the match the quiz earned.
  • Social proof. No such population exists; no reason to imply one.
  • Loss framing on a room. Guilt as a trigger — the opposite of a companion.
  • A permission ask at cold launch. Spends the one grant before any value is shown.
  • Badges and gamified saving. Turns taste into points.
  • A Wallet pass. No honest artifact — an invoice is not a boarding pass.

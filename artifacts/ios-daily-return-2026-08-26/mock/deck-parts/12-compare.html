Two judges prefer B’s destination. The feasibility judge prefers A’s first move. Read together they are not in conflict.

Each judge scored both directions against one rubric and one set of cells, ten points a cell, forty a direction. The three totals are deliberately never combined: a homeowner’s reason to open the app tomorrow, a designer’s reason to send a client here, and an engineer’s reason to believe the first slice lands are three different questions, and a mean of them would answer none.

Both directions were judged in their second form, with their own critique logs. Neither is credited for a shared plank, because both inherit all twenty.

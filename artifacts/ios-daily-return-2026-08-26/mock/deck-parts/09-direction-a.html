Two rules govern the line

  • It draws only when something moved. No eyebrow, no null sentence, no “Nothing new since Saturday.” An app that prints a dated record of your absence four mornings running is counting the days you were away at you. When nothing moved, Today is exactly as it ships.

Rollback, and its two exceptions

The five client-side items are four fail-closed flags plus one route, and any one turns off without the others. The new function is a drop and three one-line reverts; nothing is written that a drop would strand.

Two things do not roll back. Money that has moved — which is why attribution ships before the button.

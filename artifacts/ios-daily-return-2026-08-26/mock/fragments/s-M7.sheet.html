s-M7 · Lock Screen: the push and the widget · screen sheet
PurposeThe one honest interruption. Both directions send on the same events and both refuse a count on the Lock Screen: the surface says what moved, and the app it opens agrees with it.
Entry pointsAn APNs alert from apns-send, fired on a proposal sent, a decision raised, or an invoice sent or overdue. The widget refreshes on app foreground and by timeline policy — a delivered alert does not run app code, so the widget can be one open behind, and the directions say so rather than promising otherwise.
ComponentsNew: a WidgetKit extension — the app declares three targets and no extension of any kind, so this is an App Group, a second bundle id, and a second thing to keep in review. Existing: apns-send (complete, provisioned, five callers, none of which touch money), device_push_tokens, NotificationRouter (:60-88 already routes proposal, invoice, decision).
CopyBanner: “Leah sent a proposal” / “Aspen Loft — Living Room Refresh · review by Sep 8.” (Direction B’s wording; Direction A words the second line “Read it by Sep 8.”). Lock Screen widget: “PATINA” / “Rug colour — asked Aug 22”. Every push names the person and the object — never “you have an update”. Dates are seed dates: the proposal reviews by Sep 8, the rug decision has been waiting since Aug 22.
DataEach call site passes a notification_log_id, which apns-send updates with delivery status, so the banner and the bell can never disagree. Proposal and invoice sends ride the existing edge functions; a decision needs one new SECURITY DEFINER trigger, because 00092 is a cron schedule and nothing fires on decision creation. The widget reads a small App Group snapshot written on every badge refresh.
StatesPermission not granted — no push; the in-app feed and email carry the same events. Nothing waiting — the widget reads its empty line (“Nothing moved since Thursday.” / “NOTHING NEEDS YOU”), never a zero. Signed out — the wordmark and nothing else. Recurring reminders — the push rides the transition into sent, raised or overdue, once per entity, so a studio’s reminder cadence cannot buzz a client daily.
InteractionsTap the banner → NotificationRouter → the proposal. Tap the widget → the app, plain. Analytics: push_received, push_opened (entity_type), widget_tapped — all new.
TierMoney and decision pushes at activeProject and engaged only. Discovering and guest are never sent one and are never asked for the permission.
Callouts1 · the Lock Screen widget carries what moved and its date — no badge, no tally of chores. 2 · the banner names the person and the object, and carries the date the client is being held to. 3 · it fires once per entity transition, guarded by the same notification_log row the in-app bell reads.
New vs todayThe sends for money and decisions (five push callers exist; none touches an invoice, a proposal, a decision or an order), the widget target, and the pairing of the push with the screen it opens.

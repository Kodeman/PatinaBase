s-M6 · What the partner receives · screen sheet
PurposeThe most-cited finding in the programme, drawn from the receiving end. A homeowner shares a $4,200 table with her husband; today he is handed a card titled “Patina Designer Portal” on app.patina.cloud, and tapping it cannot open the app even when the app is installed.
Entry pointsThe share glyph in the piece-detail floating bar (ShareLink, ProductDetailView:117-121). This phone is the recipient’s, running Messages — not Patina.
ComponentsExisting: the app’s ShareLink and its message. New: a client-facing piece route with its own Open Graph title and image; com.apple.developer.associated-domains in Patina.entitlements; the piece path routed into the existing DeepLinkHandler. The Messages chrome is iOS, drawn to Apple’s greys and blue.
CopyMessage, unchanged and verbatim: “Heirloom Oak Dining Table by Nordic Atelier on Patina”. Preview title “Heirloom Oak Dining Table”, subtitle “Nordic Atelier · $4,200”, domain “patina.cloud”. Today the same preview reads “Patina Designer Portal” / “app.patina.cloud”.
DataThe share URL comes from PatinaPortalLinks.swift:14-22 and points at app.patina.cloud/library/<productId> — the designer portal’s Library route, which is why the preview carries the portal’s title. The repair is portal-side (a client piece route + an /.well-known/apple-app-site-association file) plus the entitlement; no migration, no edge function. The photograph and the price are the seed’s.
StatesApp installed → the link opens the piece in Patina. App not installed → the web piece page, which is a real page and not a login wall. Preview not yet fetched → Messages shows the bare link; nothing in the app depends on it. iOS caches the association file, so this is verified on a device, never in the Simulator.
InteractionsTap the preview → universal link → DeepLinkHandler → piece detail. Analytics: existing share interaction on the sender’s side; new deep_link_opened (kind: piece) on the receiver’s.
TierEvery tier, including guest — sharing is the one act in the app that reaches someone who has never opened it.
Callouts1 · the preview is the piece and its maker, not the designer portal. 2 · the domain is a client-facing route on patina.cloud, not app.patina.cloud/library/<id>. 3 · the message text is unchanged: “{name} by {maker} on Patina”. 4 · tapping it opens the app, because the entitlement and the association file exist — today the app declares only the patina:// scheme, so the link always lands in a browser.
New vs todayThe route the link points at, its Open Graph title, and the associated-domains entitlement that lets an installed app catch it. The share sheet and its message already ship.

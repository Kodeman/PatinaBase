M6 · The return moment (6a–6d) · screen sheet
Callouts6a: 1 the push, naming the person and the object · 2 the Lock Screen rectangular widget — no count. 6b: 1 the small widget, one line · 2 the same widget with nothing to say · 3 the app’s first extension. 6c: 1 the mark · 2 the promise · 3 the four events · 4 the sentence, verbatim · 5 two real buttons. 6d: 1 header · 2 the record · 3 the top row opened to its date, the decision one tap away · 4 the tab bar.
PurposeBring the person back for something true, and land them where it is.
EntryAPNs push · widget tap · an emailed link, once AASA lands (SP-03).
ComponentsPatinaWidget (new WidgetKit target — the app’s first extension) · PushPrimerView (new) · NotificationRouter (existing, extended to invoice, proposal, decision, thread, order).
CopyAs drawn; every push names the person and the object, never “you have an update”. Widget empty: “Nothing moved since Thursday.” Primer: “We’ll tell you when your designer sends a proposal, when an invoice comes due, when a decision is waiting, and when something you ordered moves. Nothing else.”
Dataapns-send from two edge functions (proposal-send, invoice-send + invoice-reminders) and one new AFTER INSERT trigger on client_decisions, each passing a notification_log_id. The widget reads the App Group snapshot written on foreground — it can be one open behind, by design.
StatesWidget with no data → “Open Patina to see your house.” · permission denied → no push, and the record still works. The iOS system alert follows only a tap on “Turn these on”.
Interactionspush_received {kind} · push_opened {kind} · widget_opened · push_permission_primer_shown.
Tierdiscovering and guest get no money pushes; the widget renders only for signed-in accounts.
New vs todayThe app has one off-app surface (push receive), no extension target (F130, A15), and asks for permission once, after a design request, with no copy at all (F47, F167). 6a hides the status-bar clock because the Lock Screen owns the time; APNs, universal links and the App Group are device claims, and this program produced no device pass.

s-M4 · The permission moment · screen sheet
PurposeSpend the one grant well. Authorization is requested exactly once per install, and today it fires silently the moment a design request is submitted — unrelated to money, with no copy at all. This screen is the sentence that earns it, shown before the system asks.
Entry pointsThe first client-facing notification_log row of type proposal, decision or invoice — not cold launch, and not the design-request submit. Settings also reaches it while the grant is still unspent.
ComponentsNew: this screen. Existing: PushTokenService (the request itself, :87-108), PatinaButton, MonoLabel, the Strata mark.
CopyHeadline “Only when something needs you.” · body, verbatim from SP-08 and the only version of it anywhere: “We’ll tell you when your designer sends something that needs you — a decision, a proposal, or an invoice. Nothing else.” · three mono lines: “A decision to make”, “A proposal to read”, “An invoice coming due” · “Turn on notifications” · “Not now”. The headline and the two buttons follow Direction A’s M8; Direction B words the headline “Four things, and nothing else.” and adds a fourth line, an order moving.
DataNone — copy only. What follows it does have data: apns-send is complete and provisioned, device_push_tokens is live, and NotificationRouter already routes proposal, invoice and decision payloads nobody emits yet.
StatesNever asked — this screen. Declined here — we do not ask again; Settings carries the switch. Denied at the system prompt — iOS will not show it again this install, and the Settings row deep-links to the iOS setting. Already granted — the screen never appears.
Interactions“Turn on notifications” → the system prompt (s-M5). “Not now” dismisses and is a real button, the same weight as the other. Analytics: push_permission_prompted (trigger, outcome) — new.
TierEngaged and activeProject only. A discovering reader or a guest is never asked, because nothing can yet be sent to them that this sentence promises.
Callouts1 · the mark, and no chrome — this is a moment, not a settings page. 2 · the promise, in the app’s own voice, before the system’s. 3 · the three events named, so the sentence can be checked against what actually arrives. 4 · the ask. 5 · the refusal, weighted like a choice and not a trap.
New vs todayAll of it. The app has no pre-permission copy anywhere, and asks at the wrong moment.

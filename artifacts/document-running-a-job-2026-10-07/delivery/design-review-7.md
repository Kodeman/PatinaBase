# Design review 7 — the FR6 wave's open DESIGN-Qs and the re-walk's findings (US-19 FR7)

**Headline: one high, and it is the same shape as FR6's — a Next that names a control the paper has not mounted.** On a sent proposal whose client has no login, the band's own act follows the reminder (`Send a reminder`, D1-a); inside the reminder's three-day cooldown that control is not on the paper, so the press lands on nothing. The fix is D2's own rule, applied: a gated act is never Next, and where nothing stands the band prints the fact (`Reminder sent 8 October.`) with no act. The same rule settles the other open proposal question — a proposal sent an hour ago has no Next either; R22 already says so for the Desk. The rest is medium and low: a relabel that would lie with two unacknowledged POs, the 390 rungs, a label table missing one row, a door doubled in the Orders ledger. Two of the re-walk's verdicts are inverted on evidence: the D26 tour note was **right** to be absent on Cedar (the person had pressed `Understood`) and wrong to be present on Olsen and Wren; and `Choose the piece` above a table reading is already with the lines.

- Format as review-6: each row gives the exact string or behaviour, one sentence of reason, its source, and whether main must change (**Fix** = this wave; **Follow-on**; **Park**; **Kody**; **Note**; **Confirm** = built as asked, no change).

## 3. For Kody

Dissents and amendments. Nothing here changes a prior ruling silently; each names what it touches.

1. **Rule of names at the group (529-1; D1).** D1 says the act's name is the label of the control it lands on, verbatim. On the Brief it lands on `Accept · begin` inside a group now named `Respond to the inquiry`. This is the first place the name sits on the group rather than the control. I hold it is right — the Brief's reply is a choice of three, and no single control can carry the act's name without lying — but it is a reading of D1, not D1's letter. If you want a literal control, the post-reply Brief act (review-4 §4-8) and this want ruling together.
2. **`File the claim` names a claim already filed (530-1; D1 named acts).** The `damage_claim` need exists only once a claim row does, so the band's `File the claim` lands on `Notify vendor` / `Mark resolved` — right landing, misnamed act. D1 pinned the string, so it stands; recommendation when you take up §4-7's `Notify the maker`: let the act read by claim state (`File the claim` while no claim exists; `Notify the maker` once filed). Not ruled here.
3. **F5-2 needs a migration.** The story contract allows SQL only from SQ-SEED and SQ-1C. The follow-up kind (`00728`) is additive and local-first, but it needs your or the orchestrator's authority before an executor writes it. The alternative — the readers sniffing `— following up` in the subject — is the smell story log #5 already named; I did not rule it.
4. **R42 and the follow-up body (530-2).** I read R42 as governing the *drafted* date ask (where a greeting precedes drafted sentences). Reading it as "every maker note opens `{maker},`" is defensible; then the hold must also refuse a greeting-only body. Say so and F5-2 grows one check.
5. **499-1's means changed, not its end (534-1; review-4 Fix 17, review-3 WALK note).** 499-1 said *rename Aspen's client*; Fix 17 and the WALK note repeated that. D′ proves the same thing on Tanaka and leaves Aspen as the guard's negative case. I accept D′ as satisfying 499-1; the "rename Aspen" instruction is superseded, on the record.
6. **`the Ashford family have` (529-4).** The article rule makes any `the …` household plural, so a collective (`the Ashford family`) prints `have` — British collective agreement, acceptable to my ear. Say if you want `family`/`household` singular and the rule gains an exception.
7. **A letter out, no repair (529-3).** While a letter is `sent` or `opened`, Message is held with no way to write again until the link lapses. That matches the client-letter row (it offers nothing either). If the studio should be able to write a second letter sooner, that is the People room's rule to change, not the paper's.
8. **Desk deep landing (531-2; 521 (c)).** Still the deferred Desk ticket's contract. Repeating review-4 §4-2: (c) should be that ticket's contract, not an afterthought — and after F5-4 the card and the band agree on every proposal state, so (c) has nothing to reconcile when it lands.

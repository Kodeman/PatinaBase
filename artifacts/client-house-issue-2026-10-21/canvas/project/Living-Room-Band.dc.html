05 · Living room

Walnut, linen, and a lot of light

In production: the sideboard. Waiting for you: the sofa.

The room faces the garden through one long window. Maren kept everything low so the light reaches the back wall: a walnut sideboard under the window, the linen sofa across from it, a wool rug between, and one lamp for the evening.

Four pieces for the room, each with where it stands today. The sofa waits for your approval in chapter 03.

Maren drew the room before anything was ordered, so you could see the pieces together. It is a drawing of intent, not a photograph of your room; it stays at six columns and never becomes the cover.

The walls take four paints from the same family, pale to dark, so the room reads as one colour in changing light.

The paper tab, three ways

The tab is page furniture, not a scrim: opaque paper, ink measured on paper. Code reads the 48px rendition's bottom-left 310 × 44 zone and picks one of three states per image. notch is the default. hairline applies when the zone is within 1.3:1 of the paper. below applies when the zone is busy, the focal point sits in it, or the container is under 320.

notch · the default

Dark wood in the zone: the paper reads as a cut. Tab 310 wide, 44 into the plate.

hairline · a pale zone

Pale shavings in the zone, under 1.3:1 against the paper: a 1px #C9C7BD rule on the tab's top and right edges keeps the cut legible.

below · a busy zone or a narrow screen

The plane's blade and the focal point sit in the zone, so the tab moves under the plate with no overlap. The same state is used at 200 percent zoom.

Photograph credits

Eighteen placeholder photographs from Wikimedia Commons stand in for job imagery on these boards. Each entry gives the file, its author and licence, the plates that use it, its rank as a source, and the caption it carries. Outside the two arrival boards every caption ends "placeholder, not job imagery"; on those two the notice sits beside the frame. Real editions use photographs the studio owns.

Rank, highest first: installed room, then studio-made (scan, board, sheet, upload), then the maker's photograph of her piece, then catalogue (piece and pair only), then the drawn silhouette.

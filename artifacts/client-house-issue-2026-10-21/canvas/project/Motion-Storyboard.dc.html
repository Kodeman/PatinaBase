Reduced motion

With reduced motion set, transforms are off: plate, caption and chapter head fade in together, opacity only, 150 ms linear. The hold still takes 900 ms because it is a safeguard, not a flourish.

Absent

Scroll reveals, parallax, pinned sections, carousels, a preloader. At most three children stagger in any chapter head.

One issue, four moments in the job

What changes is which plate opens the issue and what the standing rows say. Design opens on the studio board, procurement on the workshop photograph of her piece, installation on the first installed room, closed on the studio's finished photograph. The chapter order is identical in all four, and nothing moves under Earlier: settled things stay where June last saw them.

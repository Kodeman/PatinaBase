Plate grammar · nine plates, at size

Every plate carries a caption under it in DM Mono 15: what · whose · when, left to the gutter, never on the photograph. The alternative text describes what is visible; the caption carries provenance. A hairline holds stock; the palette tints only the 4px rule under the frame. Plates never dim, and never two in a row.

1 · Hero · 1440 × 648 bleed from a 16:10 source · 390 × 340 at the phone

One per page. Capped at min(72svh, 648) on desktop, object-position from the stored focal point. Eligible: an installed photograph, a scan above the floor, the studio board, a shared sheet or key plan, a photograph of her own piece. Never a concept render, never a catalogue photograph.

2 · Chapter plate · 1248 × 648 · 390 × 488 (4:5) at the phone

At most one per chapter, and the chapter opens on it. Status in words sits above the plate; the caption below.

3 · Held plate · 390 × min(780, 100svh − 72)

Held plate: phone only, one per page, in the living-room chapter only. It is held by its height, never sticky, never pinned, never dimmed, never in Invoices or Approvals. Source floor 780 × 1696 from the tall crop.

Floors, device pixels after the crop, never upscaled: hero desktop 1600 × 1000, hero phone 780 × 680, chapter 1248 × 832, pair 716, piece 330, sheet 2048 wide, board 1600. A crop discards at most half the source and keeps the whole subject.

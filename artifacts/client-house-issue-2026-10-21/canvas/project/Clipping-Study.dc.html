One catalogue photograph, four ways

A maker's photograph on a plain ground, as most catalogues supply it. The piece plate has to make it read as a clipping pasted onto the studio's stock, not as June's own lamp in her room.

A · Raw, filling the frame

The catalogue's grey ground fills the frame edge to edge and fights the paper. Reads as an advertisement.

B · Contained, inset, hairline · recommended

The whole piece, inset by a tenth of the frame on stock, held by the hairline. Reads as a clipping on the studio's sheet.

C · Multiply over palette tint

Rejected: darkens the product. The blend changes the lamp's real colour, and puts palette behind an image.

D · Silhouette

Honest when there is no photograph yet, or when the maker has not consented to its use. The issue never borrows a picture to fill a gap.

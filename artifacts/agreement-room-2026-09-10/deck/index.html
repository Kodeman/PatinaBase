One caution behind all of it. The two body renderers are a fork, not a shared import: two apps, two types, their own money formatting, their own written-or-not test, their own headings and section semantics, kept in step by copy constants alone. So no direction may promise the same component — only the same sentences, and the same rule that a part with nothing written draws nothing.

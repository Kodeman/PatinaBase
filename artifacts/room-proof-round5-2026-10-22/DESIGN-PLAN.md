# Round five — The room, from first measure to site

## Intent and continuity
The approved paper is the constraint: left-weighted visual work, the job flow in the right margin, dense FF&E folds, a reliable return to the same place. Add design development before item decisions and site/team work after the selections. These are two chapters on the same job, not a new navigation tree.

## Design system
Paper #f7f5ef, ink #302e2a, forest #4c624f, survey blue #58717b, tracing stock #e8ede6, graphite #69665f. Playfair carries room narrative; Inter carries drawing labels, dimensions and operational acts. Left alignment, generous visual plates, compact margins. Keep the approved quiet rules rather than invent another skin.

## Layout
A room spread is the memorable object: concept image above a strip of survey and drawing plates; the margin tells what each artifact means and what remains unresolved. Working with a drawing unfolds a generous drawing bench within this chapter. The survey looks measured, the rendering looks aspirational, the drawing looks issued or unissued, and the site copy looks like a practical work order.

Paper: [room story + large plates | room decisions] → [item review] → [dates] → [dense FF&E] → [site scope + people + returns] → [money] → [closeout] → [record].

## Review against the brief
A file-card library would make this another portal. Replace that with an evidence chain for the north-wall console: scan uncertainty, concept intent, A-201 revision, LR-36 specification, a named trade's scope, a returned observation. Retain the difference between current, shared, issued and accepted. Keep versioned issue snapshots unchanged when the current drawing advances. A field observation creates a reviewable task, never an automatic design change or completion.

## Scope
One deeply illustrated living-room thread. Other rooms remain in the FF&E schedule; full CAD, actual LiDAR processing, uploads, permissions, messages, trade engagement/signature and payments are outside this fictional browser-only prototype. Plans/renderings/scan/roster/scope patterns are source-backed; their unified placement and cross-links are the proposal. See SOURCE-MAP.md.

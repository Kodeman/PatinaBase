The Life Review

The portal is not broken. It is one paper, one rule and one size, wherever you look — and three ways to change that without moving a single thing.

Three directions, named plainly and never mixed together on one page:

  • A — Ink on Paper. A real type scale and a three-weight rule system.
  • B — Honest Materials. Tint by document movement, filled status, product imagery.
  • C — The Dark Desk. The paper as a sheet laid on a dark desk.

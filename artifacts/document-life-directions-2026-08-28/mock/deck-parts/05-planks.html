PLANKS

Nine planks, and no direction skips one

A plank is a repair the surface needs whichever direction the team rules for — a token, a floor, a fill or a ground. “No plank adds a surface, moves a region, changes a label, or changes what any act does” (shared-planks.md). Where a direction restates a plank at its own value — Direction A's stocks are deeper, so A's inks are one step darker — the plank is still adopted, never replaced.

  • SP-01

    A 14px body floor and an 11px mono floor

    F11 F12 F13 F14 F21 F22

    Every text-bearing element on a Document surface lands on one of five steps — mono label 11px, mono state 12px, body 14px, name 16px, head 18px+ — so --type-body-min: 14px and --type-metadata-min: 12px become the floor in fact instead of a suggestion beside it, and the mono floor moves to 11px because 649 of 1,029 mono usages (63%) already sit at or below 10px, 296 of them at exactly 9px. The tokens are the easy part: text-[<n>px] literals run 1,749 deep across 252 files in 25 distinct sizes, and the seven named components are where the decisions are, not where the work is.

    Size L · its own lane, 4–6 days, sequenced before any direction ships · globals.css · 1,749 text-[<n>px] literals across 252 files in components/document/**

  • SP-03

    A rule-weight token set, and one job per weight

    F18

    Three tokens replace the single pearl hairline used 502 times: --rule-hair for a row ended, --rule-mid for a section ended, --rule-double (the existing .doc-region-rule) for a movement ended — one weight per rank instead of one weight carrying all three.

    All three are hardcoded charcoal, so any inverted chrome erases them: on Direction C's three charcoals — desk #37322D, rails #2C2926, well #201D1B — --rule-mid reads 1.141 / 1.000 / 1.159 and --rule-hair reads 1.015 / 1.000 / 1.011, on the rails literally the ground's own colour. The plank owns a dark twin for each weight, spent only by a lane that inverts a ground — today that is C alone, and C prices them.

  • SP-04

    No pigment is spent as text

    F02 F03

    Every base pigment already has a text-grade companion ink and contrast.test.ts holds the rule; the stamp is the hole — stamp.tsx:31 lets an undeclared stamp print its own base pigment as text, as low as 2.01:1. Every stamp descriptor now carries an explicit -ink, and the base pigment stays on the border, where it is material, not type.

  • SP-05

    State gets a ground, not only a hue

    F01 F02 F03

    Stamp and StatusChip gain one fill variant — the state's pigment at 16-18% over the sheet, with the matching -ink for the word — worn when the state is the reason the row is on the page; every figure in this deck shows the fill on Stamp, since StatusChip has no reachable render on the local DB.   • SP-06

    Hover is the next stock the lane already declares

    F17 F01

    --bg-hover moves off rgba(196,165,123,.06) — 1.042:1 over the ground — but it is a rule now, not a hex: a hovered row takes the next stock the direction already declared for that lane, because one value could not clear 1.10:1 across every stock every direction proposes. The one hex this plank tried, --plank-hover-tint: #F3ECE2, measured 1.008–1.125:1 across the declared stocks and inverted direction on two of them — 1.228:1 on A's desk (lighter than the ground) and 1.089:1 on B's rail (also lighter) — and a fill deep enough to clear 1.10:1 everywhere else broke SP-04's floor: charcoal at 6% over A's desk stock reads 1.106:1 and drops A's tightest -ink to 4.137:1 on the hovered row; over B's Project stock, 1.115:1 and 4.363:1. So the plank promises a floor of at least 1.09:1, not 1.10:1, and the floor is 1.097 — C's second sheet, the tightest value any lane's rule produces. Every lane clears it with a stock it already owns: A takes the rail stock (paper · rail · desk — 1.177 · 1.177 · 1.173), B takes the untinted sheet showing through the tint (1.108–1.116 on the six stocks, 1.225 on the rail), C takes the second sheet on paper and the next charcoal on the chrome (1.097 · 1.141 · 1.159). The FF&E row's own local 4% hover is deleted in favour of the rule.

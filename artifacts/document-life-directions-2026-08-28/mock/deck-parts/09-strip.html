One column, four ways

Each direction's strip mock makes the same claim: the same six components — roster row, FF&E line, status chip in two states, section head, margin chip, drawer segment — drawn once per treatment from identical markup, with only the stylesheet differing between today and the three directions. The four figures below let that claim be checked directly, one column at a time.

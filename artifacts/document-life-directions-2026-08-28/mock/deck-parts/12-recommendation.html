RECOMMENDATION

Planks first, A as the floor, then one visible move

The ruling in front of the team is smaller than the deck makes it look, and the work behind it is larger. Three decisions, taken in order, cover the package.

1 · The planks ship as one lane, before any direction

SP-01 through SP-09 close the same findings in every lane — the reading floor, three muted inks that are three colours, three rule weights, an ink for every pigment, a state that fills, a hover that says something, a drawer with a ground, two rails you can see, and one ground under the page at every width. None of them adds a surface, moves a region or changes a label. SP-01 alone is 1,749 text-[<n>px] literals across 252 files, sized L, 4–6 days — the largest single piece of work in the package, and every lane's own figures already assume it has landed.

2 · Direction A is the floor

A ships next, whatever else the team decides: the section head, the three-weight rules, and the three paper stocks 1.15:1 and further apart, with the desk ground scoped to /desk rather than to --bg-primary. It is the only lane whose separations reproduce to three decimals across two critiques — the critic's own words are that A is the one lane in which no arithmetic error can be found — and the only lane that adds rank above the shared floors rather than only inheriting them. Its own work is 2–3 days on top of the planks.

3 · Then the team picks one visible move

The planks and A close the flatness complaint on their own terms; a visible move is a separate, optional decision. The recommendation is C's chrome — the Studio Drawer, the DocSpine, the MarginRail and the /desk route's own ground, taken dark — on three grounds: value contrast is the mechanism canon already names for the role (D4), the mobile bar has already proven the move in production at 14.46:1, and it is the one candidate that gives the desk a desk rather than another tint on the same paper. It is not free to take today: the planks now carry a dark twin of every rule and fill token, not only every ink, priced inside C's own 4–5 days rather than added to them (D42, resolved in v3), and contrast.test.ts's parseTokens still needs its light/dark split, with the exemption list's widening from five files to eleven named as the cost (D19, priced at 1 day and called non-optional).

Two grafts from B survive either choice and should be taken regardless of which visible move wins. The charcoal letterhead band with the red-letter zone carried on it — the palest band on the document becomes the highest-contrast object on it, 12.485:1 on the stock the band actually sits on, still the strongest single answer to F09 anyone in this deck proposes. And the filled stamps: five state fills at 16–18% over the sheet with a matching -ink, the fullest execution of the plank any lane offers, with the one tight margin (damaged, terracotta-ink and sage-ink) held at 4.63:1, not the 4.51:1 v1 shipped.

Two parts of B wait. The movement tints wait: pairwise, the six stocks still sit at only 1.000–1.007:1 — a movement is told from another movement by hue alone, not by value — even now that the rail itself is fixed (D41, resolved in v3: #E8E3DB reads 1.098–1.106:1 against the six sheets it flanks, above today's own 1.081:1, at the price of three of the four paper inks losing the rail entirely). The thumbnails wait: on the local seed, zero of six FF&E lines carry a product_id, so B's headline material move renders no thumbnails here, and nobody in this program has the one number that would say whether that is also true in production.

What this recommendation refuses

  • A three-day reading of the package. SP-01 alone is 4–6 days, it is the plank that answers the actual complaint, and every lane's figures are worthless until it lands.
  • B's tints as the signature. The retune that rescued B's signature (1.001:1 → 1.081–1.088:1 against the ground) is real, and the v3 pass fixed the rail it drove backwards — #E8E3DB now reads 1.098–1.106:1 against every sheet it flanks, above today's 1.081:1 (D41, resolved in v3). What remains true is that pairwise, stock to stock, the six still sit at only 1.000–1.007:1: a movement is told from another movement by hue alone, not by value. The band and the filled stamps are taken regardless; the tints wait.
  • F15 before the production count. Locally zero of six FF&E lines carry a product_id; on that shape B's headline material move renders nothing, and the ruling should wait on the one query nobody in this program has run.
  • C's register before the dark twins are built. The planks now declare a dark twin for every rule weight and the state fill, priced inside C's own 4–5 days (D42, resolved in v3) — ship those, not the untwinned hardcoded charcoal that still measures 1.141 / 1.000 / 1.159 on C's own desk / rails / well.
  • Any shadow before the CSS gate exists. The amendment's real cost is a CSS-level gate that does not exist yet — globals.css is linted by nothing today — not the lint change its first draft claimed. Close R72's dead exception and build the gate first; decide the token second.

RULED · 28 AUG 2026

A, with a little of B's colour

One ruling undid a whole register; the interview that followed rebuilt the direction three sites at a time.

What the team ruled, and then re-ruled

The team first ruled Direction B, Honest Materials, preferred, with Direction A, Ink on Paper, a strong second. The design lead built a hybrid to carry that forward — B's materials laid onto A's skeleton, with the desk's stage groups bled as tinted bands to the page edge, the open document sheet tinted to its own movement's stock, and one charcoal band carrying the letterhead, the instrument row and the red letter — and took the mockup through two rounds of adversarial review before Kody saw it. He ruled against the large tinted surfaces, not against the review's own list:

“the desk is starting to look silly with the banded colors edge to edge. and the document with the dark header and yellow body looks terrible. Direction A would have been better guidance, pulling in a little more of direction B's color, Maybe an animated color highlight on hover.”

Kody, on seeing the B-on-A hybrid. Reproduced verbatim.

The final direction

Direction A, whole
The Playfair document title at 40px, tracking -0.015em; region names at 24px over a 1.5px charcoal rule; the section head — the Strata mark, a mono 11px label, a Playfair name, a 1.5px charcoal rule. Three rule weights, three ranks: hairline 1px rgba(44,41,38,.10) ends a row, 1.5px #2C2926 ends a section, the double rule (2px #2C2926 + 1px rgba(44,41,38,.18)) opens a movement. Body floor 14px, mono labels 11–12px. Three real muted inks, #4E4339 / #5A4E43 / #65594E. The cream desk ground #FAF7F2; one untinted paper #FCFAF6; the spine and the margin rail at one deeper stock #E8E3DB; the margin chips drawn as lifted paper, sitting on the rail.
From B — exactly three things
Filled stamps: the state's own pigment composited over the sheet at ~1.18:1, a 1.5px pigment border, and a charcoal word. The six saturated stage tabs, unchanged, as small plates on the roster heads only. 48px thumbnails on catalog-linked lines.
Removed
The tinted desk bands, bled edge to edge; the tinted document sheet; the charcoal band.
New — the hover wash
Kody's own line, built: a roster or FF&E line opens its own stage or state pigment under the pointer, swept open from the point of contact by the portal's ink-pool mechanic, 260ms, landing at ~1.12:1 over its own ground — the rule is the ratio, not the alpha. The name's score turns clay at the same time. Under reduced motion the wash is a flat tint at three quarters of that alpha, no sweep.
Depth
Unchanged — one token, --elevation-sheet, spent on the margin chips, the open Orders sheet and the drawer. The Q04 amendment, not canon.
Motion
The portal's own vocabulary throughout; the roster settles once per page load, never on return.

The mockup

The design lead turned the revised direction into a clickable mockup, one self-contained file, no external request of any kind:

Click map

  • Desk to document, and PUT DOWN back to the desk.
  • UNFOLD / FOLD on each folded region.
  • A margin chip marks itself and scrolls its anchored line into view.
  • Ledgers in the drawer opens the Orders sheet; PUT BACK · ESC, the scrim, or Esc closes it.
  • Hover any roster or FF&E line for the warm wash.
  • 390 toggles the same desk at 390×844.

Gates passed

  • 43 of 43 sampled text pairs ≥4.5:1, floor 4.68.
  • 0 washed-row texts short of 4.5:1.
  • One elevation token, three sites.
  • 0 external requests.
  • No overflow at 1440 or 390.
  • Reduced-motion sweep: 0.
  • Roster settle: 0, after every return.
  • Two adversarial review rounds, R01–R48, all dispositioned.

What the mockup does not claim

  • The data is the specimen — no number, date or count beyond what it states.
  • One real product crop, against five slots.
  • The Money region, the accounts seam, the Schedule-dates seam and the closing rolodex whisper are outside the click map this mockup draws, and it does not draw them.
  • The earlier B-on-A hybrid build is superseded; its own two-round review stays in the record as history, not as a verdict on this direction.

THE ASK

What was asked, and how we read it

“You are a senior UI designer, Review the current designer portal and make recommendations to bring more life to the page, designers are complaining it is too flat and everything blends together. Only suggest UI improvements and dont change the UX or workflow. Present three optinos in an HTML presentation for the Patina team to weigh in on.”

Reproduced verbatim, spelling included.

How we read it

“UI-only” means tokens, type, rule weights, grounds, fills, imagery and chrome tone. It does not mean routes, acts, copy or information architecture — every one of those stays exactly as it stands today.

What a direction is here

A direction is a token-delta table over the real globals.css, a set of component recipes so the cost is honest, and mockups drawn on the same specimen data in every lane — never a new layout, never a new act.

How the evidence was made

Fresh 1440 and 390 captures of the current build, taken 2026-08-28 against local seed data, signed in as designer Leah Hartwell. A measurement script read every visible element's computed style off the rendered DOM. One audit seat — a simulated designer's eye, said plainly wherever it speaks — walked the captures against those numbers. Priors already on record from four earlier reviews are folded in with their file and line.

The two rulings already taken

  • A lean pipeline built this deck, not the full house review process.
  • A bounded shadow amendment to canon D4 is asked only as a question, later in this deck — no mockup here shows a shadow, and no direction depends on one.
Standing disclosure

This deck rests on one simulated seat and measured numbers, not customer research. The desk capture reads from local seed data, not a production account.

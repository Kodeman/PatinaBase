FINDINGS

Twenty-five findings, four of them blockers

Twenty-five findings came out of the walk: four blocker, eleven high, nine medium, one low. By theme, type carries seven, tone five, state four, chrome four, color two, material two, rule one. Every fix is a token, a type step, a rule weight, a fill, a ground or a crop — nothing that moves a route, a label or an act.

No dedicated finding-card class exists in 00-head.html; the cards below reuse .q-card (the question-card kit), which already carries an id line, a title and a meta row.

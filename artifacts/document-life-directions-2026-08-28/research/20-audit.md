# 20 — The visual audit

One seat, two people. A senior interior designer who runs a Midwest studio and has this
portal open from seven to six; and a measurer sitting beside her with the numbers from
`research/12-measurements.json` and the source.

Scope is **UI only**. Every observation below is about tone, type, rule, colour, material
and chrome. Anything whose fix would move a region, change an act, rewrite a label or
touch a route is listed at the end under *Dropped as UX* and carried no further.

Evidence: the 22 PNGs in `shots/`, opened one at a time; `research/12-measurements.json`;
and the source files named inline. Findings are in `research/31-findings.{md,json}`.

## 1 · The Desk — 1440 · `w1440-desk.png`, `w1440-desk-roster-rows.png`

**Where my eye lands.** On "Good morning, *Leah*." It is the largest thing on the page by
a wide margin and it is the one thing on the page that is not work. After that my eye goes
nowhere in particular — it starts at the top of the roster and reads down, line by line,
the way you read a list of names on a wall. The second thing it finds, eventually, is
"Overdue 3 days — 1 decision overdue — oldest due Aug 25", because it is the only sentence
on the desk printed in a colour. One coloured line on a page of sixteen jobs.

**What blends.** The sixteen job lines blend into each other. Five of them in a row end in
"OPEN THE JOB", set identically, and I have to read left to know which job I am opening.
The group labels "BRIEF · 5", "DISCOVERY · 1", "DIRECTION · 3", "PROPOSAL · 2",
"PROJECT · 4", "INSTALL · 1" are set in the same small capitals as "EVERY JOB · 16 LIVE ·
1 OVERDUE" and as "THE STUDIO" and as "ROOMS" and "LEDGERS" and "BEGIN" and as the word
"SHEET" beside each ledger — so the thing that organises the page and the thing that tags
a row are the same size, the same weight, the same colour. And the job name blends into
its own subtitle: "Full Room" and "Sarah Chen · New lead — respond by Sep 2" are the same
height on the line. Only the serif tells me which one is the job.

**What I cannot tell apart.** The small dot at the left of some rows. "Full Room" for
Sarah Chen has one; "Consultation" for Elena Ruiz has none; "Olsen Lake House" has one
that I believe is a different colour; "Chen Residence" has one I believe is a third. At
six pixels across, on this paper, I cannot tell you which is which without leaning in, and
I would not bet money on it after leaning in. I also cannot tell what the white band
across the middle of the page is. It reads as a strip of paper laid over the desk, but it
is the same white as everything the portal calls a card, and the only thing separating it
from the desk is one hairline.

## 2 · The Desk — 390 · `m390-desk.png`

**Where my eye lands.** On the dark bar. It is the only dark thing in the product and on a
phone it is the whole visual hierarchy — everything above it is one warm field. On the
desk that bar reads "THE STUDIO / The Desk · TODAY 2 min · MORE" and it sits on top of the
roster it is covering.

**What blends.** The roster keeps its wide-screen shape. "Full Room · Sarah Chen · New
lead — respond by Sep 2 · OPEN THE JOB" still tries to be three columns, so the middle
column narrows to about four words and "Sarah Chen · New lead — respond by Sep 2" wraps to
four lines while "OPEN THE JOB" holds a fixed lane to its right. Each job is now five lines
tall, separated by a dotted rule, and eleven of them read as one long grey field. At the
bottom, "Orders / Accounts / Hours / The Post" each carry a dotted leader to the word
"SHEET" — four identical rows of the same length.

**What I cannot tell apart.** Where the page ends. There is a strip down the right edge, a
few millimetres wide, in a colour I do not see anywhere else in the product — a fourth
paper the page is sitting on. I assumed at first it was a design decision. It is not on
any other screen.

## 3 · Document · project, rich — 1440 · `w1440-doc-project-rich.png`

**Where my eye lands.** On the two black chips: "SPEC THE 3 UNSPECIFIED →" and "DRAW AN
INVOICE". They are the only filled darks on a page four screens tall, so they pull the eye
whether or not they are the thing I came for. After them, "Chen Residence" at the top. And
then, honestly, nothing — the page becomes an even grey texture and I scroll it rather than
read it.

**What blends.** The regions. "Client approvals", "Schedule dates", "Schedule", "Pieces",
"Money", "Closing the book" and "*The accounts · this project*" are all serif, all within
a few points of each other, some italic and some roman with no rule I can infer. "Money"
and "Pieces" get a heavier rule above them; "Client approvals" and "Schedule dates" get a
lighter one; I cannot say from looking which of those is the bigger idea. Underneath
"Money", six lines — "Budget · nothing approved yet", "Plan · no working budget yet",
"Authorized · nothing executed yet", "Moved · $0 in motion", "Owed · nothing owed yet",
"Not drawn · $16,330 deposit" — are one grey block with a caption under each in smaller
capitals, and the one number in it that matters, $16,330, is set the same as the five
sentences that say nothing happened.

**What I cannot tell apart.** The band that says "NEEDS ATTENTION · IN ONE PLACE" from the
paper around it. It is the most urgent thing on this document and it is the palest thing
on this document. I found it because it has a thin terracotta line down its left edge, not
because the field itself registered. On the same page, "NEEDS SETUP · 1 →" sits inside a
dashed box; a few inches below, dashed lines separate rows that have nothing to do with
setup, and "Name a phase…" is underlined with a dashed rule again. Dashed means *unfinished*
in one place and *this row ended* in another.

### 3a · The spine · `w1440-spine-detail.png`

I cannot see where the spine stops and the paper starts. "Client approvals / 0 IN THE LOG",
"Schedule / NOT SCHEDULED", "Pieces / 3 PIECES · 0 ROOMS", "Money / $16,330 NOT DRAWN" —
four entries, one weight. Which one I am currently in is carried by a two-pixel clay bar
beside "Schedule" and by nothing else. Below them, "IN HAND / under a min / PAUSE / + LOG"
sits in a bordered box — the only object in the column with an edge, so the timer looks
more important than the four places the document actually goes.

### 3b · The margin · `w1440-margin-rail.png`

The margin has no ground of its own at this width; it is the same paper as the document,
so the three chips inside it are floating boxes rather than notes pinned to a rail. Two of
them read "MONEY · VENDOR PAYMENT DUE" over "Vendor payment — balance" and "MONEY · VENDOR
PAYMENT DUE" over "Vendor payment — deposit". Same eyebrow, same box, same clay edge — the
only difference is one word at the end of a line. The top chip repeats itself: "TIME · AUG
28" over "Time · Aug 28". Above them, "*The margin on the right is where decisions and
money gather*" is set in the same italic serif as the real notes on the Install document,
so the instructions and the content are one voice.

### 3c · The FF&E lines · `w1440-ffe-lines.png`

This is the page where I buy furniture, and there is no furniture on it. "Møbler Lounge
Chair — Bouclé · ×2 / Nordic Atelier / IN PRODUCTION / $5,700" is four pieces of text and
a small outlined box. The name is smaller than the price. The maker — the thing I am
actually managing — is smaller again. And the first row is tinted slightly warmer than the
other two, which I take to mean it is selected; it is close enough to the untinted rows
that I would not notice it if I had not gone looking.

### 3d · The drafting room · `w1440-status-chips.png`

Eight rows: "Rooms / not yet written", "FF&E / no schedule yet", "Palette / no palette
yet", "Boards / no boards yet", "Phases / no phases yet", "Exclusions / none stated",
"Payments / no schedule yet", "Terms / not yet written". Identical white boxes, identical
italic state words. This is a document with nothing in it and it is as visually busy as a
document with everything in it. Its two section heads, "*Scope*" and "*The Vision*", are
the same italic serif at the same size, differing only in the colour of a short dash — clay
against a slate blue, both pale.

## 4 · Document · project, plain — 1440 · `w1440-doc-project-plain.png`

**Where my eye lands.** On "Marrow & Vale Residence", then on the schedule, which is the
one part of this product with real hierarchy — "Consultation" and "Schematic Design & Space
Planning" recede in a lighter ink, "**Design Development**" is dark and heavier, and the
future phases hang off a dotted line. That reads. I know where I am without being told.

**What blends.** Everything above the schedule. This project has almost nothing recorded
in it — "No rooms yet", "No pieces yet", "Nothing filed", "Nothing specified yet", "No
boards yet · start one", "Nothing moving yet", "Nobody on it yet" — and the ticket that
prints those seven absences occupies exactly the same block, in exactly the same weight, as
the ticket on Chen Residence that prints "3 unspecified" and "$16,330 deposit not drawn".
Two documents, one at the beginning and one in the middle of its life, look the same from
across the desk.

**What I cannot tell apart.** "6 DAYS OVER" from "DUE AUG 22". They sit on the same line,
in the same small capitals, in warm colours a hair apart. One is a fact and one is a
problem. Also "ANCHORED · NOV 21" from the "SENT" mark on the proposal and from "IN
PRODUCTION" on a chair — one bordered, rotated, capitalised box does all three jobs.

## 5 · Document · proposal, sent — 1440 · `w1440-doc-proposal-sent.png`

**Where my eye lands.** On "Sent Aug 27 · not opened yet", which is the right answer, and
on the four-column strip beneath it — "SENT / Aug 27", "OPENED / not yet", "READING / —",
"MOST READ / —". Those are the only numbers on this document typeset as numbers, and
three of them are empty.

**What blends.** The whole right and left thirds of the page. The spine holds only
"Proposal / AWAITING SIGNATURE"; the margin holds only "IN THE MARGIN / + NOTE / The
margin — decisions, messages, and money gather here". Both columns are the same paper as
the document, so the page reads as one narrow column of text with two large blanks beside
it, and it is not obvious that those blanks are rails at all. Below, "Walnut sectional
sofa · $6,500", "Hand-knotted wool rug · $4,200", "Walnut coffee table · $2,800",
"Reading lounge chair · $3,700", "Floor lamp · $1,300" — five furnishings, eighteen and a
half thousand dollars, no images, five identical rows and one hairline before "TOTAL".

**What I cannot tell apart.** "MESSAGE CLIENT USER", "NUDGE CLIENT USER", "PREVIEW AS
CLIENT USER", "EMAIL DELIVERY", "MARK SIGNED →" and "SHARE…". Six acts of very different
consequence, all small capitals with a hairline under them. "MARK SIGNED" ends this
proposal's life; it looks exactly like "SHARE…".

## 6 · Document · install — 1440 · `w1440-doc-install.png`

**Where my eye lands.** On the "Closing the book" block, because it is the one region in
the whole product that sits on its own warm paper, and on the "CLOSE THE BOOK" chip inside
it. That block works — I can see it is a different kind of place.

**What blends.** Inside it, immediately. "OPERATIONAL CLOSEOUT STILL OPEN / · 2 project
phases not completed / · 3 coordination items unresolved / · 1 invoice with a balance due"
is a second tint laid on the first, and the difference between them is smaller than the
difference between two sheets of the same paper stock. The six checkbox rows — "Final
walkthrough completed with client", "All punch list items resolved", "Final payment
collected · waiting on billing" and so on — are identical lines with identical empty boxes;
nothing tells me which of them I could actually do today. And the white input fields under
"THE PORTFOLIO SNAPSHOT" are the highest-contrast objects on the page, so the empty
marketing form outranks the closeout that is blocking the job.

**What I cannot tell apart.** The margin's three notes — "*A project collaborator changed
Service Agreement — Aspen Loft.*", "*…changed Floor Plan — Living & Dining.*", "*…changed
FF&E Specification Schedule.*" — from the instruction above them, "*The margin on the right
is where decisions and money gather…*". Same italic serif, same size, same colour, same
column. One is the product talking about itself; three are things people did to my job.
And "1 decision overdue — oldest due Aug 25" sits in the palest band on the page while
"CHASE THE APPROVAL" beside it is set exactly like every other act.

## 7 · Document · brief — 1440 · `w1440-doc-brief.png`

**Where my eye lands.** On "Full Room", then straight down a column of nine sentences that
all say nothing has happened: "No rooms yet", "No pieces yet", "Nothing filed", "Nothing
specified yet", "No boards yet", "Nothing moving yet", "No dates yet", "No roster yet". It

**What blends.** "BRIEF · DECIDE THE FIT · NEEDS ATTENTION" with "THE JOB · BRIEF" and

**What I cannot tell apart.** The three tags "style alignment: japandi", "timeline fit",
"room type experience" from the three fields above them holding "0.85", "$5k – $15k",

set is a control. Below them, under "ROOM SCAN", a small bordered square reading "*No
preview*" with a dark "PRIMARY" tab. That is the only place on the whole brief where an
image could be, and it is empty.

## 8 · The Library — 1440 · `w1440-room-library.png`

**Where my eye lands.** On "Find a piece—or *ask about one.*" It is the biggest type in the
product — larger than the desk greeting — and it is a search prompt.

**What blends.** The filter row: "All pieces / One spec / Variants / Options / Modular /

instead of a pearl one. Under it, "MINE · 0  STUDIO · 0  PATINA · 19" and "ADD TO LIBRARY
↓" and "RAW CAPTURES · FROM THE EXTENSION, PHOTOS, PASTE" are the same small capitals in

**What I cannot tell apart.** Whether this room has anything in it. The header says "THE
LIBRARY · 19 pieces" and the page shows "*Nothing captured yet. Bring something in — it
lands here, raw.*" Nineteen pieces of furniture and not one of them is visible as a piece
of furniture. This is the room where I choose things by eye, and there is nothing here for
the eye.

## 10 · The Orders ledger — 1440 · `w1440-ledger-sheet-orders.png`

**Where my eye lands.** On the sheet itself, because the desk behind it is dimmed. That is
the strongest separation in the product and it is the one place the portal admits that two
things can sit at different depths.

**What blends.** The rows. "AP-012 · RECEIVED / INSPECT · Received and dispositioned ·

ALL | DUE", with "PENDING PAID" fallen to a second line. Which one is on is carried by a
one-pixel underline, and the wrapped pair has drifted away from the word that governs it.

**What I cannot tell apart.** "THE WORK" and "THE DEPENDENCY WEB" — two views — from "ADD
TASK", which is an act. All three are small capitals on one line; the act is a point
larger and underlined.

## 12 · The drawer strip — 1440 · `w1440-drawer-strip.png`

**Where my eye lands.** Nowhere. The strip is white on off-white with one hairline along
its top. If it were not fixed to the bottom of the window I would read it as part of the
page.

**What blends.** "Library · People · The Scans · Ledgers ↑ · Find anything ⌘K" against
"HANDS FREE · THE POST · Leah Hartwell / LEAH HARTWELL". Doorways to four rooms, a search,

**What I cannot tell apart.** Which room I am in, unless I catch the two-pixel clay
underline beneath one word. Compared with the mobile bar's charcoal ground and clay rule,
the desktop chrome is doing the same job with a tenth of the means.

# Measurer's notes

Numbers are from `research/12-measurements.json` (route keys `desk`, `doc-project-rich`,
`library`, `people`), from `apps/designer-portal/src/app/globals.css`, and from counts run
over `apps/designer-portal/src/components/document`. Composited ratios use the WCAG 2.2
formula ported in `src/lib/document/__tests__/contrast.test.ts:85–102`.

**The grounds (all screens).** `--bg-primary` `#FAF7F2` (the room), `--doc-paper`
`#FCFAF6` (the document), `--bg-surface` `#FFFFFF` (every card and the drawer) —
`globals.css:63–64`, `:52`. Ground vs paper **1.025:1**; ground vs card **1.069:1**; paper
vs card **1.042:1**. Picking up a document changes the paper by 2.5 hundredths.

**Desk.** Greeting `text-[1.7rem]` = **30.6px** (`desk/page.tsx:204`) — the largest Playfair
on the route and 47% smaller than the Library headline (45px). Roster job name
`font-heading text-[16px]` (`desk-roster.tsx:50`); its meta line `.doc-type-body` =
`max(14px, 0.875rem)` = **15.75px** (`globals.css:825–830`) — the two differ by **0.25px**.
Desk-contents index labels are `text-[19px]`/`text-[17px]` (`desk-contents.tsx:97`), so the
static furniture outranks the live work by 1–3px. Row separator is
`border-b border-dashed border-[var(--border-subtle)]` = pearl at 60% =
**1.123:1** against the ground (`desk-roster.tsx:34`). Font histogram: 61 of 128 sized
elements at 8–12px (47.7%); 46 uppercase-mono elements; only 3 distinct border colours on
the whole route.

**Desk at 390.** `m390-desk.png` is **874 px** wide at DPR 2 = **437 CSS px** of content in
a 390 px viewport — 47 px of horizontal overflow, and the exposed gutter is the
Tailwind base `--background` `oklch(0.9582 0.0152 90.2357)` ≈ `#F5F1E6` (`globals.css:860`),
a fourth ground **1.056:1** from the off-white. Every other 390 capture is 780 px (390 CSS)
with no overflow.

**Document, rich.** 662 elements, 199 text-bearing, **152 of them (76.4%)** at 8–12px.
Uppercase-mono count **104**. DM Mono 400 is the single largest family/weight bucket at 89
instances, against 16 Playfair 500 and 9 Playfair 400. Largest Playfair on the route:
`27.9px`, the title input (`doc-letterhead.tsx:59`, `text-[1.55rem]`). Eleven distinct
border colours, of which pearl accounts for 38 sides.

**The spine.** `bg-[rgba(229,226,221,0.28)]` over the paper shell (`doc-spine.tsx:44`),
composited `rgb(246,243,239)` — **1.080:1 vs `--doc-paper`**, **1.053:1 vs the off-white
ground**. Entry name `text-[12px]` and entry status `text-[12px]` at ≥1440
(`doc-spine.tsx:129,132`) — same size, separated by family and hue only.

**The margin.** At ≥1440 `bg-[rgba(250,247,242,0.55)]` (`margin-rail.tsx:258`) over an
off-white ground composites to `rgb(250,247,242)` — **exactly the ground, 1.000:1**. The
rail's header, its "+ NOTE" act and its handle are one class:
`font-mono text-[12px] font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.08em] text-[var(--color-charcoal)]`
(`margin-rail.tsx:228, 267, 489, 527`). A margin chip is
`rounded-[5px] border ... borderLeft: 2.5px solid` (`m-item.tsx:93–94`) with an **8px**
eyebrow, an 11.5px title and a 10.5px detail (`m-item.tsx:46,51,55`).

**The red-letter zone.** `bg-[rgba(212,160,144,0.08)]` (`red-letter-zone.tsx:87`) over paper
= `rgb(249,243,238)`, **1.056:1** — the lowest-contrast band the document paints, and it is
the one that says "NEEDS ATTENTION". Its internal rule is
`border-dashed border-[rgba(139,115,85,0.14)]` at **1.173:1** against its own fill
(`:106`).

**FF&E line.** Name `text-[12.5px]`, maker `text-[10.5px]`, two mono meta lines at
`text-[8px]`, quantity `text-[9px]`, price `font-heading text-[13px]`
(`ffe-section.tsx:342, 347, 352, 360, 368, 380`). The price is the largest element on the
line; the product name is second; nothing on the line reaches the declared 14px body floor.
Row grounds: highlighted `rgba(196,165,123,0.08)` = **1.059:1** vs paper; decision-due
`rgba(232,197,71,0.05)` = **1.026:1**; hover `rgba(196,165,123,0.04)` = **1.026:1**
(`ffe-section.tsx:396–399`). Hover against decision-due measures **1.0005:1**.

**Stamps.** One component, one geometry: `border-[1.5px]`, `rounded-[3px]`,
`-rotate-[1.5deg]`, `bg-transparent`, mono 600 caps at 10px or 12px, coloured only by
`style={{ borderColor: color, color: ink ?? color }}` (`stamp.tsx:26–31`), used from 14
call sites. The pigments it separates meanings with are 1.02–1.39:1 apart from each other:
clay↔terracotta **1.024:1**, terracotta↔sage **1.060:1**, clay↔sage **1.085:1**,
clay↔golden-hour **1.388:1**.

**Status dots.** Against the off-white ground: dusty-blue 2.64:1, clay 2.18:1, terracotta
2.13:1, sage 2.01:1, golden-hour 1.57:1 — five pigments inside a 1.07 band of one another
(`12-measurements.json` `people.pairwiseContrast`, `desk.pairwiseContrast`), rendered at
`h-1.5 w-1.5` (6px).

**Type inventory (`src/components/document`).** 1,029 `font-mono` usages and 969
`uppercase` usages. Mono arbitrary sizes span **6.5px to 26px across 18 distinct values**;
**649 of 863 (75.2%) are ≤10px**, and 296 of those sit at exactly 9px. Playfair arbitrary
sizes span 39 distinct values, of which **71 usages sit at 13–15px**. The tokens
`--type-metadata-min: 12px`, `--type-body-min: 14px`, `--type-control-min: 16px`
(`globals.css:73–75`) are honoured only through `.doc-type-meta` / `.doc-type-body`, used
139 and 76 times — against 863 arbitrary mono sizes, so the declared floor governs roughly
one label in seven.

**Rules.** 502 `border-[var(--color-pearl)]` usages and 73 `border-dashed` in the same
directory; one `border-dotted`. Pearl against the off-white ground is **1.209:1**; against
white **1.292:1**; the subtle variant (pearl @60%) **1.123:1**. The only heavier device is
`.doc-region-rule` — `border-top: 2px solid var(--color-charcoal)` over
`border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(44,41,38,0.18)` (`globals.css:738–742`).

**Hover.** `--bg-hover: rgba(196,165,123,0.06)` (`globals.css:64`) composites to
**1.042:1** over the off-white ground and **1.044:1** over white.

**Muted ink.** `--text-muted`, `--text-subtle` and `--text-faint` all resolve to
`#65594E` on all four routes measured (`globals.css:68–69, 92`; `12-measurements.json`
`*.tokens`) — one colour doing three jobs, at 6.51:1 on paper.

**Material.** No `next/image` or `<img>` in `desk-roster.tsx`, `desk-contents.tsx`,
`job-ticket.tsx`, `ffe-section.tsx` or `doc-letterhead.tsx`. Across all 22 captures the
only image slot that renders is the brief's room-scan tile, and it reads "*No preview*".
The one texture in the product is a repeating 1px line at `rgba(139,115,85,0.01)`
(`doc/[id]/page.tsx:1770–1775`) — a 1% alpha over a warm ground, roughly one 8-bit step.

**Dark ground.** Exactly two: `mobile-bar.tsx:216` (`bg-[var(--color-charcoal)]`, below
1180px) and `log-strip.tsx:86` (charcoal below 1180, `--bg-warm` above). Nothing above
1180px is dark except type and two filled action chips.

**Shadows.** Zero product `box-shadow` on all four routes; the one hit per route is the
TanStack devtools button. `.folio-face` (`globals.css:210–225`), the single sanctioned
lift, is dead CSS — no `.tsx` references it.

# Dropped as UX

Real observations, deliberately not carried into `31-findings` because the fix would change
a route, a component's behaviour, copy or information architecture:

1. **"OPEN THE JOB" repeats eleven times on the desk.** The problem is that eleven rows
   name the same act; distinguishing them is a copy change.
2. **"TIME · AUG 28 / Time · Aug 28" in the margin repeats itself.** Copy.
3. **The Library header says "19 pieces" while the shelf says "MINE · 0 STUDIO · 0" and
   the body says "Nothing captured yet."** Content/IA, and possibly seed data.
4. **"PENDING PAID" wraps to a second line away from "PAYMENT ·" in the orders sheet.**
   Fixing the association would mean restructuring the filter group.
5. **The drafting room's eight facet rows are all empty on this data.** Seed data, not UI.
6. **"MARK SIGNED →" carries the same act weight as "SHARE…".** The visual half of this is
   captured in the finding about act sameness; deciding that one act should be promoted is
   an act-hierarchy (UX) call.
7. **The mobile bar and log strip overlap the paper they sit on** — fixed chrome covering
   content is a layout/behaviour matter.
8. **The Patina monogram overlaps its own wordmark in the drawer** (reads "…INA" in six
   captures). A chrome defect, not a tonal one; may be a capture artefact of a fixed
   element and would need a live look to rule on.
9. **"NEEDS SETUP · 1 →" and "3 unspecified" say the same thing twice near the top of the
   rich project.** Copy.
10. **`.folio-face` is dead CSS.** Housekeeping, not a visible defect.

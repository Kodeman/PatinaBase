Patina is where you and your designer keep one picture of your home.

Your designer already works in Patina every day. This is your side of it — the direction you agreed on, the decisions waiting on you, the proposals to sign, the invoices to pay, and the rooms you are building together. Open it in the morning and you know what moved.

FOUR PLACES, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE APP

Today — what changed on your house since you last looked, and what is waiting on you.

Spaces — your rooms. Add one by walking it with your iPhone, or by typing the dimensions in.

Pieces — furniture and lighting from Midwest makers, chosen by designers. Every label carries the maker's name and where the piece was made.

Studio — your designer, your proposals, invoices, documents and messages, in one place.

WHAT YOU CAN DO HERE

Answer a decision. When your designer needs a choice, it arrives here with the options side by side. Your answer goes straight back to your designer, with a record of what you agreed and when.

Sign a proposal. Pay an invoice with Apple Pay. The numbers are the same ones your designer is looking at.

Message your designer without leaving the app. One thread, the two of you.

Add a room and keep its shape, its budget and its pieces together.

Put the Patina widget on your Home Screen and see what moved without opening anything.

Turn on notifications and hear from us only when something actually needs you.

WHAT IS STILL THIN

Pieces is small while we bring makers on. Buying a piece inside the app is switched off for this round — your designer sources for you. Help articles exist for a few screens, not all of them. iPhone only; iPad is not supported in this round.

WHO MAKES IT

Patina is built by Middle West Studio in Madison, Wisconsin. Two people build this, Kody and Leah. If something reads wrong, feels slow, or does not do what you expected, tell us.

To send feedback: take a screenshot and TestFlight will offer to send it to us with a note, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. It comes straight to the two of us and we read all of it.

WHAT PATINA IS

Patina is the client side of an interior design studio. A homeowner who has hired a designer uses it to see what changed on their project, answer the choices their designer is waiting on, sign proposals, pay invoices, message their designer, and keep a record of their rooms. The designer works in a separate web product; this app is the homeowner's half of the same project. Round one is invited by one studio, Middle West Studio of Madison, Wisconsin, to its own active clients.

The account holds one project with an assigned designer, one decision waiting on the client, one sent proposal, one open invoice, one document and one message thread, so every surface has real content behind it. The bar along the bottom has four places: Today, Spaces (the client's rooms), Pieces (furniture from independent makers) and Studio (designer, proposals, invoices, documents, messages).

BROWSING WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT

An account is not required to look around. On the first screen, "Look around first" opens the app as a guest: Today, Pieces and saved pieces are all available without signing in. Signing in is only required for a project — the decisions, proposals, invoices and messages that belong to a specific homeowner and designer.

MESSAGING

Messaging is one-to-one between the homeowner and the professional interior designer engaged on their project. There is no open chat, no user-to-user discovery, no public posting and no way to reach another tester. Threads exist only inside a project the studio created, and the studio moderates them — it can remove a message or close a thread. This is why the age rating declares messaging.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT

A homeowner can photograph or scan their own rooms and write notes to their designer. That content is private to the project — visible to the homeowner and to the designer on that project, and to nobody else. There is no feed and no sharing between accounts.

PAYMENTS

Invoices are for interior design services and for physical furniture, both delivered outside the app, so they are handled outside in-app purchase. Tapping Pay opens Stripe's hosted checkout in a Safari view; Apple Pay is offered there. Nothing digital is unlocked by paying. There is no subscription and no in-app purchase in this build.

PERMISSIONS

Camera and LiDAR are used only when the homeowner chooses to capture a room in Spaces; typing dimensions by hand is always offered instead. Notifications are used to tell a homeowner that their designer needs an answer. Both are optional and the app is fully usable if declined.

ACCOUNT DELETION

Settings → Account → Delete account deletes the Patina account and its project data from our servers, inside the app, without contacting support. The records we are required to keep — paid invoices and their receipts — are retained, and the confirmation screen says so before you confirm.

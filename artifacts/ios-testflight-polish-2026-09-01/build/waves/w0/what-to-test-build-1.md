1.0 (3) — the first one. Thank you for opening it.

Please walk it the way you would on a normal morning, then tell us what felt wrong. Twenty minutes is plenty. There are four places along the bottom — Today, Spaces, Pieces, Studio — and that is the whole app.

WORTH YOUR ATTENTION

1. The first two minutes. Sign in with your email — we send a six-digit code, not a link to click. Sign in with Apple works too. Tell us if the code is slow to arrive, if any sentence reads oddly, or if you got stuck.

2. Today. Does the top of the screen tell you the truth about your project? If a number looks wrong, screenshot it — screenshots come through with your feedback.

3. A decision. Open one, read both options, and either approve it or say "not yet" with a note. The approval sheet is the screen we most want your eye on.

4. A proposal. Open it in Studio and check the scope and the totals against what your designer told you. If there is one to sign, sign it.

5. An invoice. Open it and check the total. Paying is real: if you tap Pay, the card or Apple Pay charge is a real charge against a real invoice from Leah. Pay one only if she has asked you to. If you would rather not, still tell us whether the invoice reads clearly and whether Pay is where you expected it.

6. Messages. Send your designer a note and make sure it arrives. It is one thread — you and Leah, nobody else.

7. Your rooms. Open Spaces and add one. If your iPhone can scan a room, walk it; otherwise type the dimensions in. Tell us if anything strands you — this is the newest part of the app.

8. Notifications. Say yes when we ask. We will send you one during the round. Tell us whether it arrived, whether the wording made sense on the Lock Screen, and whether tapping it landed you on the right screen.

9. The widget. Press and hold your Home Screen, tap the plus, find Patina, and add the medium widget. It should show what moved on your house, and tapping a line should open that line.

10. Dark Mode and larger text. Settings → Display & Brightness, and Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size. Both should still look like Patina, and every button should still be reachable.

ALREADY KNOWN IN THIS BUILD — NO NEED TO REPORT

- Pieces is thin. We are still bringing makers on.
- Buying a piece inside the app is switched off for this round; your designer sources for you.
- Help articles exist for a few screens, not all of them. Where there is no article, we have taken the "?" away rather than open an empty page.
- iPhone only. iPad is not supported in this round.
- Some corners of Studio have had less use than the rest — documents and older projects especially. Tell us anyway if something there looks wrong; we would rather hear it twice.

PLEASE DO REPORT

Anything that quits unexpectedly. Any screen that spins and never finishes. Any sentence that reads as though a machine wrote it. Anything that looks broken in Dark Mode or at large text sizes. Any number that disagrees with a number your designer showed you.

To send it: take a screenshot and TestFlight will offer to send it to us with your note, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback.

### C1-01 — "Look around first" does not look around; it opens a 3-page carousel + a 5-question quiz
- area first-run · **major** · testerVisible · confidence 0.95 · effort M
- where `App/Coordinators/AppCoordinator.swift:258-271`; `Features/Authentication/Views/AuthScreenView.swift:104-129`; `ContentView.swift:55`

## What is GOOD (calibration)
- **The phase machine is genuinely well-built.** `derivePhase()` is a pure function of five inputs; `applySession` is the single seam every one of the nine session installs routes through; `withObservationTracking` re-registers itself; the `waitForAuthReady()` continuation array fixes a real dropped-continuation leak. Reading it, the transitions are legible.
- **`SessionScope`** (`Core/State/SessionScope.swift`) is exemplary: eleven named participants, a pin test that fails the build on any unlisted `static let shared`, and a written exclusion policy. Account isolation was taken seriously.
- **Sign in with Apple is done correctly** — per-instance nonce, SHA-256 on the request, raw nonce to `signInWithIdToken`, rotated after each attempt (`AppleSignInNonce.swift`, `SignInWithAppleButton.swift:32-40`).
- **User-cancellation is filtered on both OAuth paths** (`ASAuthorizationError.canceled`, `ASWebAuthenticationSessionError.canceledLogin`), so backing out of a sheet no longer raises an error banner.
- **The email-not-confirmed branch is a designed recovery**, not a generic failure: typed error, dedicated panel, cooldown-gated resend, a "use a different email" exit.
- **`AccountDeletionService`** is the model the rest of auth should follow — one sentence of the app's own copy, the server's words logged and never shown, the UI torn down only after the call returns.
- **Reduce Motion is honoured** across the splash, the carousel, the onboarding host, the quiz selection springs and `FirstLaunchCoordinator`'s transitions.
- **`OnboardingFlowView` handles accessibility sizes deliberately** — a separate illustration height, a wider text measure, reduced spacers, `accessibilityHidden` on decoration, an `.isHeader` trait, and a page-dot label.
- **The OTP field is correctly specified**: `.textContentType(.oneTimeCode)`, number pad, monospaced, non-digits stripped on paste, capped at six.

## Not verified, and why
- **Nothing here is sim- or device-verified.** Every claim is a code read at main `d7287c3f8`.
- **Contrast ratios (C1-07, C1-17)** are computed from the declared hex values, not measured on a render.
- **Dynamic-Type clipping (C1-16)** is inferred from `.frame(height:)` against `relativeTo:` fonts plus the absent `ScrollView`; the exact size at which the welcome screen's footer leaves the screen needs one screenshot at `accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large`.
- **C1-10's sheet race** is a reading of SwiftUI's single-sheet-per-presenter behaviour plus the file's own comment; the ordering needs a live guest→sign-in walk with a typed room in the store to confirm which of the two triggers actually bites.
- **C1-20 / C1-23** depend on supabase-swift 2.40.0 internals (`AuthClient.signOut`, `APIClient.sessionCleanupErrorCodes`) read from the SPM checkout, not from a live failure.
- **The Apple "Hide My Email" case (C1-12)** could not be exercised — it needs a real Apple ID and a device.
- **Whether `.userUpdated` lands the Apple display name back into `AuthService.session.user.userMetadata`** in time for the first greeting was not traced through the SDK's `update(user:)`; the `profiles` half of C1-12 is certain, the metadata half is not.
- **Prod-side facts** (Google provider disabled, `000000`, the Apple→`role='designer'` row, the missing `delete-account` function) are A3's and A4's; I read the code halves only and did not re-probe production.

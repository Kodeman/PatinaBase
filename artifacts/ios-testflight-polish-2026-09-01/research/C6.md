## Findings

### C6-01 — Settings toggles ship with an empty VoiceOver name
- area accessibility · **major** · testerVisible yes · confidence 0.95 · effort S
- where `Features/Settings/Views/SettingsView.swift:410` (helper), call sites `:109`, `:118`, `:127`
- evidence `Toggle("", isOn: isOn)` … `.labelsHidden()`. The visible `Text(label)` at `:404` is a *sibling* in the HStack, not the Toggle's label. Three rows use it: **Notifications**, **Haptic Feedback**, **Upload scans on cellular**. Contrast with `contextMemoryToggle` at `:264`, which correctly passes `Toggle("Use activity for context", …)` before `.labelsHidden()`.
- why VoiceOver announces "Switch button, off" with no name — the tester cannot tell which of three unnamed switches is Notifications.
- fix `Toggle(label, isOn: isOn).labelsHidden()`, matching `:264`.

### C6-02 — Authentication (the first screen) has zero accessibility labels, traits, or hidden marks
- area auth · **major** · testerVisible yes · confidence 0.95 · effort M
- where `Features/Authentication/Views/AuthenticationView.swift` (713 lines), `AuthScreenView.swift` (185), `AuthSheet.swift` (83), `SignInWithAppleButton.swift` (58)
- evidence 44 interactive controls across the feature; `grep -c accessibilityLabel` = 0, `accessibilityAddTraits` = 0, `accessibilityHidden` = 0. Only `accessibilityIdentifier` (23) is present — the surface was instrumented for UI tests, not for VoiceOver.
- why This is literally the first screen of a TestFlight install. Every heading is untagged, every decorative icon is spoken, nothing is grouped.

### C6-03 — Welcome-screen auth buttons speak a stray glyph before their label
- area auth · **major** · testerVisible yes · confidence 0.9 · effort S
- where `apps/mobile/PatinaDesignKit/Sources/PatinaDesignKit/Components/PatinaButton.swift:160-163`; call sites `Features/Authentication/Views/AuthScreenView.swift:82` (`icon: "G"`) and `:85` (`icon: "✉"`)
- evidence `if let icon, !icon.isEmpty { Text(icon).font(.system(size: 16)) }` — the icon is rendered as literal `Text`, inside the Button's label, with no `.accessibilityHidden(true)` and no button-level `.accessibilityLabel`.
- why VoiceOver reads "G, Continue with Google" / "envelope, Continue with email" on the very first screen. (`AuthButton` also carries a dead `iconImage` computed property at `:152-155` that nothing calls.)

### C6-04 — Auth error and success banners are never announced, and are drawn in raw system red/green
- area auth · **major** · testerVisible yes · confidence 0.9 · effort S
- where `Features/Authentication/Views/AuthScreenView.swift:65-72`; `AuthenticationView.swift:147-170`, `:441-453`
- evidence `Text(errorMessage).foregroundStyle(.red)`; success block uses `.foregroundStyle(.green)` + `Color.green.opacity(0.1)`. No `AccessibilityNotification.Announcement`, no `.accessibilityAddTraits(.isStaticText)`, no focus move. Measured contrast on the `#FAF7F2` canvas: system `.red` **3.32:1**, system `.green` **2.08:1** (WCAG AA needs 4.5:1).

### C6-05 — VoiceOver focus is never managed: `accessibilityFocused` appears 0 times in 66k LOC

### C6-07 — 27 of 28 pushed screens have no title and no navigation bar, so VoiceOver has no screen heading

### C6-08 — The first-launch tour's Skip / Next / Done buttons are collapsed into rotor actions
- area help-tour · **major** · testerVisible yes · confidence 0.75 · effort S
- where `Features/Help/FirstLaunchTour.swift:874` — `.accessibilityElement(children: .combine)` applied to the VStack that contains `Button("Skip", role: .cancel…)` (`:862`) and the Next/Done `Button` (`:865`)
- evidence `.combine` merges descendants into one element; interactive descendants survive only as accessibility *custom actions*, not as separately focusable buttons. The card also overrides nothing else, so a swipe lands on one merged element reading "Step 1 of 3, <heading>, <body>".
- why The coach mark is the tester's very first interruption. Its escape route becomes a rotor action rather than a swipe-to-Skip button. (The popover itself is dismissible — `.presentationCompactAdaptation(.popover)` at `:777` keeps modal popover semantics and the binding's setter treats an outside dismiss as `skip()` — so this is a discoverability defect, not a dead end.)
- fix drop `children: .combine`, or use `.accessibilityElement(children: .contain)`.

## What is GOOD (calibration)

- **Typography is built for Dynamic Type.** Every one of the ~30 tokens in `PatinaTypography.swift` uses `Font.custom(_:size:relativeTo:)`; the only two deliberate exceptions (`monogramGlyph`, `monogramGlyphSmall`, `:106-116`) are documented with a reason. `captionSmall` (`:59-63`) was explicitly introduced to retire raw 9–10pt `.system(size:)` calls.
- **Reduce Motion is handled seriously.** 24 references; all four `repeatForever` animations (`BreathingAnimation`, `PulseAnimation`, `ContemplativePauseView`, `ARGeometryOverlayView`) gate on it, and `CompanionMarkView.swift:68-90` documents why it reads the environment value rather than a static.
- **Dark mode passes contrast** where light mode fails: `textMuted` 6.81:1, `textInteractive` 7.12:1, `textSecondary` 10.21:1 on `#211E1B`.
- **The Companion is the model surface**: per-state label/value in `CompanionPresentation.swift:74-158`, 44pt header buttons via `CompanionConstants.minimumTouchTarget` (`CompanionHearthView.swift:469-472`), `ViewThatFits` for accessibility-size panels (`:214`, `:378`), and content correctly hidden behind an open panel (`HouseFirstRoot.swift:57`, `ContentView.swift:191`).
- **`PatinaTabBar`** is a careful hand-rolled bar: `.isTabBar`, per-item `.isSelected`, canonical VoiceOver names distinct from the drawn word, and a deliberate Dynamic Type cap on the drawn word only, with the reasoning written down (`:93-100`).
- **`PatinaAsyncImage`** hides decorative images on success (`:71`) and labels the failure state (`:92-93`) — the right default.
- **`QRScannerView`** is the only screen that gets tap targets right by construction (`:67-73`, `:254-260`: a 36pt circle inside a 44pt frame with `contentShape`), and both its controls are labelled and hinted.
- **`ScanFallbackEntryView`'s stepper** (`:222-248`) does the same 32-visual/44-thumb trick with a comment naming the ruling, and labels both buttons in plain language.
- **`ThreadListView`/`ThreadDetailView`** build real spoken sentences (`item.accessibilityLabel`, `accessibilityLabel(for:isOwn:)` at `ThreadDetailView.swift:242`).
- **`HouseRecordCard`** composes its VoiceOver strings from state (`:35-90`), tags its heading, and hides its ornaments.
- `Design/Accessibility/AccessibleHitTarget.swift` is exactly the right helper — it just needs adopting.

## Not verified (and why)

- **Nothing here was run.** No simulator, no VoiceOver, no Accessibility Inspector — this lane is static reading only, per the brief's read-only constraint. Every claim is *compile-green* level.
- **Actual VoiceOver output** for `.accessibilityElement(children: .combine)` on the tour card (C6-08) and the Companion coach mark (C6-36) — the merge-to-custom-actions behaviour is documented SwiftUI semantics, but the exact rotor phrasing needs the Accessibility Inspector.
- **`.textCase(.uppercase)` spelling behaviour** (C6-40) — whether VoiceOver spells "APR" letter-by-letter depends on the engine's heuristics; needs a listen.
- **Reduce Transparency** (C6-37) — SwiftUI `Material` and `glassEffect` self-adapt to a degree that only a screenshot pair with the setting on/off can settle.
- **Real Dynamic Type clipping** — C6-16/17/18 predict clipping from fixed heights and non-wrapping stacks; the actual breakage point per screen needs `xcrun simctl ui <udid> content_size accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large` and screenshots.
- **Contrast against live imagery** — the computed ratios (C6-11…C6-15) are token-on-token. Text over `PatinaGradients`, hero photos, or camera feed (`RoomGalleryCard`, `ScanWalkView`, `ARPlacementView`) can be far worse and cannot be computed statically.
- **`PatinaWidget`** and `apps/mobile/Patina/Patina/App`, `Core`, `Services`, `Utilities`, `Generated` were out of scope for this lane.

Before any HTML

A cold Worker holds the first byte for up to 3.7 s. Nothing in a route’s loading.tsx can paint before then. SPA navigation: the rule (Q) or bar (X) lives in the persistent (document) layout, so it runs while the Worker wakes. If the loading.tsx was prefetched, the frame paints at once regardless. Hard load or sign-in: the page that was clicked keeps the press state (“Signing in…” on the submit) until the browser unloads it. After that only the browser’s own tab indicator speaks. Closing that gap is engineering work (E3: warm or placed Worker, immutable static caching), not a loading treatment.

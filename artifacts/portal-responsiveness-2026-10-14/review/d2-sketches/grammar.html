One grammar for every click

Within 100 ms exactly one thing answers: the new value (optimistic classes) or the verb in progress (pending-only classes). A second signal appears only when the first is not enough. Thresholds are set against measured prod request durations (p50 243 ms, p90 803 ms; baseline/BASELINE.md). P6–P9 click timings were not measured yet.

Scale is split: 0–500 ms takes the left half, 0.5–3 s the right half. Shaded band: today's click → settled estimate (write + 2–4 refetches, SQ-957 comment 1). At 8 s (off scale) a pending-only act adds “Still working. You can leave this open.”

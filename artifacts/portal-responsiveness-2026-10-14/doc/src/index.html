Why the portal feels slow, and what to fix first

The ask: the portal has started to feel sluggish. Page loads should show a loading indicator or skeletons, a click should make something happen at once, and engineers and designers together should find the code and infrastructure changes that remove the wait. This document is the team's answer. Nothing in the product changed to write it.

A hard load spends about seven seconds before the studio sees its work, and no screen says anything is happening while it waits. The two largest causes are a queue of sixteen user lookups run one at a time and the script weight, about 3 s. Removing the queue should save about 2.7 s (an estimate) with one small change; the script weight only shrinks.

The top five moves all in Fix now · no ruling needed · no flag

  1. Read the signed-in user once, from the session. Sixteen auth/v1/user calls run one after another, typically about 172 ms each, and the Desk's data reads start only after fifteen of them.about −2.7 s per hard load ESTF1 · review/e2-page-architecture.md §0 · synthesis/pairings.md M11 · about −4.2 s on a Document, EST · overlap not credited
  2. Stop shipping every icon and the crypto polyfill to every page. Three chunks ride on every route, sign-in included.−220 to −390 KB gzip ESTF8, F9, F10 · review/e3-infra-bundle.md §4e · baseline/bundle.md
  3. Let the browser keep the hashed scripts. Every hard load asks the server again about all 63 scripts.63 → 0 revalidationsF7 · baseline/BASELINE.md:99-102 · prod walk
  4. When a task is ticked, refresh only what the tick changed. Today one tick reloads the whole Desk as well.27 → 3 refetches per tickF5 · baseline/data/e1-P6-1440.json · review/e1-data-writes.md §1.1
  5. Answer every press, and never show a blank room. A press state on every act, 44 px task rows, and shaped placeholders in the rooms that draw nothing today.B1 met at the press · no blank roomF13, F16 · baseline/BASELINE.md:52-53 · screens/P4-200ms.jpg

Said plainly

The proposed budget B3, usable content within 1.5 s, cannot hold on a cold hard load in the lab on any track. After every track, about 1.2 MB of JavaScript still crosses a link of about 1 MB/s and is parsed at 4× CPU slowdown. A hard load lands at 2.6–3.6 s at best EST. Clicks inside the portal can meet 1.5 s, and most already do. PQ1 therefore asks to split B3 into B3a for navigation (≤1.5 s) and B3b for hard loads (≤3.5 s).

synthesis/plan.md §2 "Estimates" and PQ1

Loading direction, recommended: Q, Quiet paper, with arrival option C, where the placeholder becomes the arrival instead of hiding the finished page. Action feedback, recommended: one quiet grammar. Something answers within 100 ms, a word appears only when a write is slow, and nothing ever claims a send, a payment or a delete before the server confirms it.

Engineering

A session-user store seeded once from the session, replacing hook-level user lookups.

Design

None needed. It removes the wait the placeholders would otherwise have to cover.

Every hard load shorter, by about 2.7 s EST. The largest single change.

F13 · N10 · D1.1–D1.7 · E2-1

The two loading directions, side by side

Both directions share the same structure and meet B2 equally, because the timing comes from the engineering mount points, not the material. They differ in voice. Q builds from what the paper already uses: pearl bars that breathe slowly and a hairline rule. X adds shimmer, a glowing bar and toasts. The team recommends Q (PQ2), because the studio promise is that the studio won't notice Patina.

Q · Quiet paper recommended

A Document at about 200 ms, 390 wide. The job title is set at first paint (arrival C).

X · Expressive

The same moment, with shimmer and the glow bar.

The strongest argument against Q. Kody asked for "a loading indicator or skeletons", and a 1 px rule at 40% ink may not register as an indicator at all. Branaghan and Sanchez (2009) found people prefer more feedback, and no first-party source shows calm tools deliberately leaving progress out. Q's quietness is a taste call, not evidence. If a studio walk shows the rule goes unnoticed, the fallback inside Q is a 2 px rule at 60%, still without X's bar and toasts.synthesis/plan.md §4 · review/r-patterns.md §1-2 · pairings.md R-5, R-6

Rooms with no arrival get placeholders now

The build room and People draw nothing while they load today. Their placeholders ship in Fix now (F13) as still pearl bars, with no ruling needed. The breath and the rule wait for PQ2 and PQ5.

The navigation rule

A click on a link or a router.push starts it. It ends only once the destination has committed and then has no busy region; the old page is usually idle while the next one is still on its way, so idleness alone would end it too early. A newer navigation takes the rule over; a click on the current address ends it at once. It appears only after 300 ms, so warm navigations show nothing and a slow one does. On failure it stops and an inline line takes over. Press the live frame to play it.

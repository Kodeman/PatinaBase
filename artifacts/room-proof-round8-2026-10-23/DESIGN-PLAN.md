# Round eight — The whole studio, one job paper

The user asks to roll the approved studies into a complete designer portal mockup. This is presentation work only. Product routes, data, permissions and deployments are out of scope.

## Design direction

Keep the accepted paper #f7f5ef, ink #302e2a, forest #4c624f, survey blue #58717b and tracing stock #e8ede6. Local Playfair gives rooms and commissions their voice; Inter carries exact operational records. This deliberate continuity follows the founder direction, not a new visual skin.

The studio is a working desk: a large project image to the left, the next acts in a right-hand margin, and compact registers of jobs and people. The job is one continuous document with a fixed right-hand contents list. Deep operations unfold within the paper and return to their context. The only top-level destinations belong to the whole practice: studio, library, people, correspondence and administration.

Studio [active commission and image | next acts] + jobs/leads register.
Job [brief and agreement] → [design and room evidence] → [cabinet package] → [client decisions] → [schedule] → [dense FF&E and buying] → [site/team] → [money/time] → [client acceptance/care] → [record].

## Reconciliation decisions

- Reuse round five's working 80-line FF&E engine (36 living-room lines), item decisions, purchase-order/receiving flow, audience-scoped correspondence, drawings, scans and Field copy. These are native in this round, not links to earlier studies.
- Integrate round seven's job configuration, cabinet package and acceptance rules into the job itself. The cabinet drawing becomes K-201; living-room A-201 keeps its own history.
- Cabinet procurement belongs to the GC in the default arrangement. It remains separate from the studio's furnishing estimate and orders. It is not counted twice.
- Studio acceptance concerns the studio's agreed service; the cabinetmaker's contracting party records linked trade acceptance. Neither implies the other's authority.
- Frozen issues, current design and proposed changes stay distinct. Client item decisions, purchasing authority, funding and fabrication instructions remain separate.
- One deep sample job is interactive. Other commissions establish studio context and open readable scope briefs without masquerading as equally developed projects.
- Native local proposals, sample ledgers and draft work copies are visibly identified. No real send, order, payment or client signature occurs.

## Review against the brief

The risk is a collection of round-specific demos. Remove old “previous round” placement caveats from the product paper and give schedule, agreement, money and handoff useful integrated inserts. Put review explanations in the presentation. The portal should first read as a place to work, with controls named for the work, not for implementation.

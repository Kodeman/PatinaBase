# Room Proof 08 — The whole studio

Round eight combines the approved room-paper direction into a complete designer-portal design study. It is a standalone HTML presentation and interactive mockup, not a production implementation.

Start with [the presentation](index.html), then [enter the studio](studio.html). [Lake Mendota House](job.html) is the deeply operable job specimen. The other commissions have illustrative scope briefs.

## A useful review sequence

1. Read the studio's next acts, commission register and inquiry origin. Browse the library, people, studio books and correspondence.
2. Open Lake Mendota. Its ten chapters remain on one paper: brief, design, cabinetry, client decisions, schedule, FF&E, site, financials, acceptance and record.
3. Unfold the living-room schedule. Its 36 starting lines have six working views, including pricing, finish surfaces and purchasing. The job starts with 80 lines.
4. Open the survey, concept and A-201 drawing history. Follow the north-wall question into LR-36 and the GC-held TS-04 coordination scope.
5. Unfold kitchen CP-01. Read Northline's held IS-03, the separate CH-01 hardware proposal, exact issue contents, supplier correspondence and site return. Save a review or pricing draft; fabrication purpose remains held.
6. Compare service arrangements in the brief. These are unsigned proposals; the agreed full-service brief and issued records keep their identity.
7. Record time and prepare an unsent invoice draft. Studio books and the job read the same entries. Voiding the draft releases the time; issued fees remain separate.
8. Inspect the later acceptance examples and care book. The current job remains open. Final acceptance requires Nora and Eli for the studio's included service; trade acceptance is a different contract record.
9. Return to the presentation to save and download founder review notes.

## Shared local journeys

- A library chair can be placed as a new room candidate. It does not replace LR-01, gain a price or become approved automatically.
- Studio correspondence, the job record and CP-01 read the same note records, with client, maker, GC, supplier and private studio audiences.
- Order links open their exact PO; returning to the studio retains the originating register.
- Job time, invoice drafts and studio books share a local record.
- The care book's rug and drawing references offer a return to the same handoff context.

## Running the package

The review is served on the existing loopback server at:

`http://127.0.0.1:4178/room-proof-round8-2026-10-23/index.html`

From an extracted review bundle, serve its root with `python3 -m http.server 4178 --bind 127.0.0.1`, then open the URL above. Use another port if 4178 is occupied. Fonts and visual assets are bundled locally. The prior studies are included as history in the review ZIP; round eight's primary paths do not depend on them.

Use one active editing tab per job. Local storage has no concurrent merge or production access control. The job footer's **Reset fictional sample** resets round-eight job records, proposals, time and drafts; it preserves the separate founder notes. The founder form has its own two-step clear action.

## Scope and limits

All people, prices, records and decisions are fictional. No external messages, payments, live orders, signatures, invitations or production data changes occur. Drawing plates illustrate document handling; they are not construction documents or a CAD editor. The agreement is a readable sample, not an executable contract. The two final signatories are fixed in this specimen. Relationship and membership views illustrate proposed placement; they do not grant access.

Codex contexts reconciled source, workflow and visual design; Claude Code independently reviewed the integration. This is not a record of practitioner interviews. See [SOURCE-MAP](SOURCE-MAP.md), [COLLABORATION](COLLABORATION.md) and [VERIFICATION](VERIFICATION.md).

## Judgments retained

The visual family stays close to the founder-approved paper direction; this round tests the whole product rather than inventing another skin. A single deep job is more reviewable than several superficially functional jobs. Source-backed placement is distinguished from proposed package/acceptance behavior. Contractor access, real signatures, legal agreement execution, accounting calculations and multi-user concurrency remain implementation work outside this design specimen.

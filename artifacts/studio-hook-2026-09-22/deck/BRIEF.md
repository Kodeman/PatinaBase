# Deck brief — "The Second House"

Audience: Patina Leadership (Kody, Leah) and the Beta Studios. Purpose: present what a team of agents (Claude Fable + GPT-6 Astra, with eight researchers, four proposers, four cross-family critics, nine verifiers, two critics) found about why studios set up the designer portal and then drive their jobs somewhere else, and the program it proposes. Date: 22 September 2026.

Source of truth for every claim: `artifacts/studio-hook-2026-09-22/research/01..08*.md` and `artifacts/studio-hook-2026-09-22/design/*` (program-final.html is the merged program; completeness-critic.md §4 is the must-not-claim list; redteam-fable.md and critique-A1/A2.md hold the disagreements). ## Voice and constraints

- Never the word "AI". Say "Designer-Taught Intelligence" where the engine is meant; name Anthropic as a processor where the FF&E extractor is discussed.
- Never "Hartwell". Leah's surname is Kochaver; the deck mostly says "Leah" and "Middle West Studio".
- No dashboards, progress bars, scores, streaks, red/green status — in the product we describe and in the deck's own visual language. A table with rows beneath a count is fine; a lone big number is not.
- No time-saved numbers, projected or measured. Nothing has been timed. Hypotheses are labelled hypotheses.
- No vendor statistics (36% admin time, 6 tools, 20–40 h/month, Chameleon/Intercom lifts).
- Do not claim: anyone besides Leah is a beta studio today; "delegated designers churn"/"day-0 value retains" (rests on Kody's seat); invoicing is Leah's habit or ~1.4/week; the trial is quiet or sends nothing (an outside-house invoice sends at least one email to a real homeowner); "zero proposals ever signed" (twenty `accepted` rows exist); Leah "captured nothing"; "your data exports" is currently true; the funnel views were "repaired" (they are retired); the drip was paused "for Middle West"; that the program authorizes no production change (Wave 1 authorizes four, name them); that the trial informs pricing V2, Pledge V6, or margin V1.
- Slides addressed to studios (the "Bring one piece of work" brief) use Patina's studio-facing voice: plain, warm, no jargon, no "platform". Load the `patina-brand-voice` skill for those two slides.
- Leadership slides are direct and evidence-first. Cite sources in a small mono footer line per slide: report number and section (e.g. "03 §2 · 08 G1").

10. **The outside view: what held up.** Three short columns. Mechanism: switching cost and incumbent habit, not misunderstanding (Kim & Kankanhalli 2009; Polites & Karahanna 2012); "quiet reversion" to spreadsheets when a new system adds re-entry without immediate personal payoff. Patterns that move switchers: never start from zero — your own data in the product fast (Notion); a human 1:1 first session (Superhuman); progressive disclosure over setup wizards (NN/g); checklists complete around one in five and get worse past five items; streaks and badges backfire on weekly-cadence tools. Incumbents: paid migration ($4,000 Studio Designer; $300 Mydoma) still draws "steep learning curve" reviews; DesignFiles templates and Programa's clipper remove the blank canvas; HoneyBook ingests your existing brochures and contracts; QuickBooks offers partial migration; easy-in/hard-out erodes trust. Source 05, 06 (mark: web sources, not Patina data).

11. **In designers' own words.** Three or four verbatim quotes with source names (from 07, VERIFIED ones only): "if your process only exists in your head, it's not really a process — it's a habit" (IDCO Studio) · "you spend 45 minutes hunting for that chair again — only to discover it's been discontinued" (Programa) · "It would've been quicker to do it myself… this isn't a hiring problem. 21. **How we measure, without a dashboard.** Measure a removed task: normal-method active minutes vs all studio time in Patina (setup, search, entry, review, correction, support, export, duplicate maintenance); first-use separate from repeat; Kody's time separate; remembered baselines are estimates; never recreate collectible work for a stopwatch. The 'told' count — "she had to be told N things to finish one bill" — is the learning-a-new-system instrument. Continuation rule: two natural comparable opportunities with positive net savings, the later without founder preparation, no financial or privacy error, and a voluntary repeat. One studio establishes a case, not retention causality. Ask at the end: "What did this remove from your day?"

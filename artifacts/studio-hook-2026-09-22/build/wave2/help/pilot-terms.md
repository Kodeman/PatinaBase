A studio comes into Patina by introduction, and the first ninety days run under their own terms — the pilot. The full legal wording lives on the Pilot Terms page; this is a plain restatement of the same facts.

The pilot is free for ninety days from the day a studio accepts. No card on file, no invoice, no charge at the end of it. If a studio wants to keep going after that, that's a separate, written conversation before anything changes.

The Terms of Service still cover the service itself. The pilot's own terms only take over on two things: what it costs, and how long it runs. Everything else stays under the Terms of Service.

During the pilot, a studio gets a sit-down on request — screen shared, a real invoice, however long it takes — any time a bill needs walking through. There's no queue and no ticket number for it; the studio says when.

A studio's invoices, payments, and clients come back out as a spreadsheet any time it's asked for during the pilot, and for thirty days after the studio stops. Stopping itself needs no explanation: telling us in writing or simply stepping away both end it.

One address handles anything at all: hello@patina.cloud.

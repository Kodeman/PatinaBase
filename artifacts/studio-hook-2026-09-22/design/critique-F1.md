## Moves that survive

- M1 — A personally run working session on one genuinely needed task survives under R133, after correcting the sequence, commercial path and mandatory-send rule.
- M2 — Passing the actual studio name in the same is_designer profile upsert matches the provisioning trigger and removes a real rename step for newly provisioned owners.
- M3 — Honest, permissioned preparation survives as a limited concierge tactic once provisioning order, access consent and verified removal are defined; the 30-day blanket seat is not established as necessary.
- M4 — Keep the intake-based decision gate and reuse assessment, not the current importer scope or time-saving promise.
- M5 — The owner-plus-hire working handoff and existing teammate persona survive; success must be an independently completed delegated task.
- M6 — An optional, bounded human follow-through cadence survives as discovery when it asks neutral questions and does not substitute for client communication.
- M7 — A private session record and measurement preflight survive; the proposed event-window queries and two-name funnel repair do not.
- Stop-doing — Withdrawing QA-based retention claims and removing public Pledge promises until counsel are justified; removal avoids introducing an otherwise unlicensed new flag.

## Missing

- A cold-studio rehearsal contract covering invite delivery, household creation, current agreement defaults, correct project attachment, preview, actual dispatch and recovery without contacting an unintended real client.
- A named pilot cohort beyond the founder, a record of current R133 practice, and an explicit hypothesis separating switching cost from capability gaps, reliability and trust.
- A per-job source-of-truth boundary, a retired duplicate task, an owner/hire responsibility agreement and a usable exit package.
- A repeat-use test after Kody steps back: can the owner or hire complete the next occurrence independently, with less total effort and no increase in errors? A founder-assisted second project alone is insufficient.
- A real-client continuation check covering viewing, signing, countersigning, payment and support when a link fails; initial value need not depend on these, but retention can.
- A total-cost and stop/continue decision for the pilot, including owner time, founder time, unwanted outreach, duplicate billing, correction burden and privacy incidents.
- A concrete PDF review contract and representative-file trial measuring extracted-field coverage, omissions, corrections, unresolved rooms and total effort rather than upload speed.
- A shareable beta-studio version stripped of private seat identities, raw intake, unruled pricing/Pledge material and unsupported behavioral claims.

The honest diagnosis

Patina has substantial working software, but we have not established which recurring job it makes easier for a growing studio. The September 22 production snapshot contains no independent third-party studio. Middle West is the only identified real studio case; its records include founder assistance. It has one project, six proposals with one out, ten invoices, thirteen client relationships, and no studio products or contacts. Billing is the strongest lead, not a recorded explanation of Leah’s habits. The supposedly retained studio-manager seat is Kody; QA accounts cannot substantiate designer churn. Nobody has recorded the actual spreadsheet, inbox, legacy portal, or other tool meant by “manual ways.” Switching cost, missing functionality, reliability, unclear delegation, and commercial uncertainty remain competing explanations. Leah’s older observed departure sought complete functionality. We need to see the work before choosing its replacement.

03-prod-evidence.md §§1–4, 6; 08-gap-critique.md G1–G6. The snapshot was queried September 22; the studio’s age and maximum row-creation date are not the age of the evidence.

Make one real piece of work easier to finish and hand on, then earn the next one without asking the studio to keep two books.

The customer moment is the owner adding her first hands, not a designer completing setup. The Document remains the working surface; the subscription is the stream this trial tests. A furnishing schedule may later support furniture margin, but this program promises neither a transaction nor a margin policy. “Hooked” means choosing Patina for the next appropriate obligation because it removes work—not more visits.

Corrections that change the program

  • Do not rebuild working paths. Hours already feed the invoice composer through “Bill week,” with entries claimed when the draft is created. Field reports already have review and correction paths. Deposit invoices already demonstrate draft-then-notify behavior. Those are reuse opportunities, not missing bridges.
  • Agreement readiness is not five mandatory studio-setup chores. The Galley already edits composed parts and focuses blockers. Fresh standard parts can include an hourly fee basis, an unset retainer, and empty required Terms. Adding a flat fee alone is not a sendable agreement. Preserve the real commercial model.
  • Withdraw “zero proposals ever signed.” Signing can produce accepted; the dossier also counts accepted proposals. Reconcile signature records and commercial state before making a real-client completion claim. Lifetime first-event rows cannot count repeated work or identify every acting colleague.
  • The dormant importer is both narrower and more complete than advertised. Spreadsheet staging, PDF staging, duplicate decisions, atomic batch commit, and idempotency exist server-side. PDF fields are name, quantity, room, category, and provenance—not prices, vendor identity, units, codes, or lead times. There are no portal callers in the searched designer/package sources.

Continuation and stop rules

Continue only after two naturally occurring comparable opportunities show positive net studio savings, the later one without founder preparation, no material financial/privacy error, and voluntary choice of the same path. A named hire must complete the delegated task without rescue for the delegation claim. One studio establishes a case, not retention causality; obtain an independent case before broad rollout claims.

Propose a three-hour Kody budget per studio for the initial two-week experiment, including research, preparation, support, and observation. This is a capacity limit to approve, not an observed cost. Stop expanding when it is exhausted; return the working slice and agree responsibility for live obligations. If preparation still exceeds forty-five minutes per small output after three attempts, narrow or stop rather than disguising a service as software. Unauthorized sends, duplicate collection, lost work, or cross-studio disclosure halt the affected trial immediately.

Measure a removed task

For each eligible opportunity, compare observed ordinary-method active minutes with all studio minutes required in Patina. Include intake, setup, search, entry, owner review, corrections, support, export, and duplicate maintenance. Report the first-use total and repeat total separately; do not hide setup through speculative amortization. Show Kody’s labor and machine wait separately, plus fully loaded human effort. Mark remembered baselines as estimates and avoid repeatedly performing client work just for measurement.

Use the actual business object, actor, studio, dated transition, and dispatch evidence beside the observation. Membership acceptance or the first eligible session is the arrival clock—not account creation for an existing account joining another studio. Count blocked, abandoned, and unavailable opportunities. No opportunity is not churn. Lifetime activation firsts, current proposal status, first-document stamps, email opens, and project count cannot substitute for completed work. A failed transaction cannot durably log its own refusal before raising; any future attempt telemetry needs a separate committed write.

Leadership reads individual cases and asks, “What did this remove from your day?” No studio-facing savings tile, engagement score, streak, dashboard, tour-completion target, or shrinking-tool-count target. External tools that serve the job can stay. Competitor and playbook research supports testing this mechanism, not numerical lift claims. Beta discussion brief · safe to excerpt without the internal sections

Bring one piece of work

We are testing whether Patina can remove a repeated task, not whether you can learn another system. Keep the tools that already work. Nothing needs to go to a client or vendor to make the conversation successful. Before you share paperwork, we will agree access, participation terms, what stays authoritative, and what you get back.

  • Thirty minutes: show us the last real task you completed outside Patina. Which file or thread did you open? What did you copy, check, or ask someone to explain? Redact anything unrelated; screen-share is enough.
  • Owner and colleague, thirty minutes: choose one task the colleague can own. Give them the source and completion standard, then let us watch where they need help. If there is no colleague available, tell us; we will not pretend to have tested delegation.
  • At the next natural occurrence: try the same bounded work without Kody preparing it. Tell us what was easier, what still needed doing twice, and why you chose Patina or another tool.
  • Ten minutes before relying on it: open the returned files outside Patina. Can you continue the selected work? What is missing? Separately, which fees, cancellation terms, and data assurances would you need before paying?

Good ideas that did not make this program

  • More tours, sample jobs, progress mechanics: rejected by doctrine and not evidence of removed work. Pull-only help and wholesale checklist deletion also wait; they reverse recent rulings without a novice observation. Preserve acceptance/first-open facts even if decoration is later removed.
  • Rebuilt hours billing, Field correction, and project URL capture: already implemented. Verify the relevant path when real work calls for it; do not sell rediscovery as new value.
  • Automatic hourly cutoffs, payment-to-next-action, and approval-to-PO: potentially useful, but no observed trigger or named next-action storage justifies them here. Deposit drafting is precedent, not permission to invent obligations, vendor identity, or procurement authority.
  • Vendor quote landing and mail forwarding: a genuine gap, but first observe an actual selected attachment. The trade RFQ form is closer precedent than compliance alone; vendor/contact identity and V10 scope need clarification. Mail receiving, quarantine, and attachments are new infrastructure, not a small inbox patch.
  • Both import formats, commercial-field extraction, whole-book contacts, and historical receivables: too much authority and reconciliation before evidence. Only one narrow observed format may proceed; contacts retain their own ruling.
  • Whole-studio ZIP, phone share extension, calendar sync: valuable possible exit/input work, but cross-domain archives and native/integration reliability exceed this bounded trial. Even a small ICS door needs an observed scheduling burden before it wins the next slot.
  • Mandatory send, second-house target, blanket concierge admin, and global pulse/drip shutdown: each confuses the experiment’s convenience with the studio’s job. Founder-heavy drafts do not prove customer burden; a research call does not replace a homeowner update.
  • Two-line funnel repair as the pilot gate: insufficient semantics and unnecessary delay. Repair or retire misleading analytics separately. Invite-only signup hygiene also remains worthwhile maintenance, not the hook.

Leadership decisions

  1. Approve the three-wave experiment and its capacity cap, with Kody accountable for interviews, terms, case records, and readout. Name the first willing owner and colleague; approve no retention claim from founder/QA behavior.
  2. Approve removal of unreviewed Pledge promises across exposed surfaces and actual sending templates before further affected invitations. Assign copy approval and counsel review separately; no new rollout flag is presumed.
  3. Rule pilot cost, duration, access, return, cancellation, and support obligations before intake. Resolve V2’s tier structure and price plus applicable V1/V6 terms before a paid offer; publish only supported commercial promises.
  4. Approve the bounded project export and its private-cost authorization, not a claim of universal export. Decide whether any exceptional concierge access is necessary; default to screen-share and record revocation.
  5. Authorize only the needed teammate rollout after a live read; accept the studio-name correction for new provisioning. Keep tour/checklist reversals out until real-user evidence and explicit rulings justify them.
  6. At the readout, choose one outcome: continue the existing path, fix the observed capability failure, approve P6’s format-specific scope and processing limits, or stop. Positive repeat value and a useful exit are gates, not optional follow-up work.

The bet

Studios may leave because Patina asks them to reconstruct work before helping with the next obligation. The mechanism is plausible: vendor replies land in the designer’s inbox, project schedules cannot absorb existing spreadsheets, and hours do not feed the invoice composer. The owner still connects those steps herself. But nobody has observed which of these costs makes Leah leave; “manual ways” is not yet a named tool or file. [02 §1.3–1.6; 08 G3–G4]

Bet on a completed handoff, not a fuller system. After a real event, prepare the smallest useful next paper from its source, keep it beside the relevant record, and bring one reviewable decision through the channel the studio already uses. Start with invoicing because it is the clearest completed loop in Leah’s actual record. Do not require importing her practice, installing Field, recruiting a client, or configuring an automation.

The evidence does not support a retention claim. Production has no third-party studio cohort; the apparently retained studio-manager seat is Kody. Middle West has one project, six proposals with one out, ten invoices, and no studio products or contacts. The absence of a proposal_sent event for Leah personally is not the absence of all studio proposal sends. These facts nominate an experiment, not a diagnosis of every studio. [03 §1–2; 08 G1–G2, G10]

Compared with broad migration, this approach removes one recurring handoff without requiring a cutover. Compared with more teaching, it produces work rather than instructions. Materio’s approval-to-PO claim is useful precedent, not independent evidence that the mechanism retains Patina studios; the literature supports reducing switching costs but supplies no defensible Patina effect size. [05 §6, Evidence Quality; 06 §7–8; 07 §8]

The shared contract

A source event is evidence, not authority. Each draft carries the studio, project, source record and version, proposed destination, named reviewer, and exact consequence of approval. Unknown terms remain unknown. Financial fields come from structured, attributable records, never invented prose. Someone’s “love it” text cannot become a formal approval; a bank screenshot cannot make an invoice paid. [02 §1.5; 07 §3 item 4; project Agent OS rules]

Use the existing agent_tasks queue and enqueue_agent_task, not another inbox table or queue. Agent reads and writes follow the existing reader/writer privilege boundary; agents do not directly update business records. Trusted application handlers validate a reviewer’s authority and current source version before applying an approved change. Every queue transition is audited. Studio reviewers must receive only their authorized studio’s task projection, not access to an internal administrative inbox.

One event version and one intended artifact identify one live candidate. Replays find it; revisions invalidate it. Never replace a human-edited draft silently. Before sending, recheck recipient, version, amount, permission, and whether the action already happened. Financial review does not implicitly send, pay, release an order, or activate a contract.

No new automated external sends. This includes a “draft ready” email or text to the studio. For the trial, Kody explicitly reviews and sends each delivery message; the studio separately authorizes each client/vendor send with the exact recipient and contents visible. Background work may prepare awaiting_review drafts only. Existing Field Line behavior is reused within its existing authority and consent, not treated as blanket permission for new prompts. No homeowner SMS expansion.

The canonical record remains The Document. Email and phone provide selected inputs and links to the exact working sheet; they are not a parallel task manager. A missing-information question is about this work, not a re-armed tutorial. Hours review stays in the drawer ledger. Opening an email, precomputing a draft, or loading a link preview never starts billable time. [04 §b R94/R97, R129/R131; V11 and HT-32/33]

Stop, defer, and decide

Stop treating setup, drip completion, or growing draft counts as evidence of value. Do not arm a re-engagement campaign or multiply weekly-pulse drafts as this experiment’s “hook.” Do not invent new tours, a sample project, a workflow builder, an inbox app, broad contact import, mailbox OAuth, or a Field installation requirement. Keep existing exports available and attach source materials to pilot deliverables; do not claim this fixes the broader export deficit. [02 §1.15, §6; 03 §6.3; 04 §b; 08 G7]

The central risks are review becoming more expensive than authoring, an irrelevant “next step,” incorrect money, stale approvals, cross-studio leakage, and new messaging mistaken for consent. Mitigate by one real obligation, source-backed review, explicit authority, version checks, and a zero-tolerance unauthorized-send stop. The largest commercial risk remains testing willingness to use free assistance while calling it subscription demand. V2 and the public pricing page are leadership prerequisites, not values this proposal sets. The exposed Pledge needs a separate counsel-led correction. [08 G5]

Decision requested: authorize discovery and one human-reviewed, real-job proof—not autonomous sending or the seven builds. If the proof fails, stop at the failed handoff and learn why. The defensible promise is not “Patina runs your studio.” It is “You did not have to prepare that paper twice.”

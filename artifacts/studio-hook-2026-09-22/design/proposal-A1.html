Bring the job in

Ask for the work she already has. Give back one job she can hand off today.

Decision draft for Patina leadership and subsequent beta-studio discussion. This is an independent proposal, not an approved roadmap, a customer interview, or a record of work completed. All time savings below are test hypotheses, not customer promises.

The bet, and its limits

Studios may leave because their current work remains outside Patina: returning to the old file finishes the job; entering Patina first requires rebuilding it. The bet is that a real job, brought in without transcription and used for one consequential handoff within an hour, makes Patina cheaper to use than to avoid. We should test that bet with Leah before building a general migration platform.

The dossier proves missing doors, not this causal explanation. The project schedule has no spreadsheet import; its PDF extractor has no portal callers; furniture-vendor replies return to personal email. Those are concrete barriers. But there are no independent third-party production studios, and the apparently retained delegated seat is Kody. Withdraw the tempting story that day-zero activation already predicts retention. [02-manual-workflow-gap.md §§1.3–1.4; 08-gap-critique.md G1, G4]

Leah’s studio is the real starting case: one project, six proposals with one out, ten invoices, thirteen clients, and no studio products or contacts in the reported census. The principal’s first-project milestone took 32 days. This suggests a narrow invoicing foothold, not proof that she wants procurement first; studio-level records include Kody’s activity, and current proposal status is not a complete historical signature audit. [03-prod-evidence.md §§2, 4, 6.1; 08-gap-critique.md G1–G2]

“Bring the job in” means bring the working slice needed for the next obligation, not eight complete histories. A job is not successfully imported merely because its title appears on the Desk. It must have recognizable source material, usable rows, a named person who can act, and an output the studio actually uses. Accounting history, existing signed contracts, and open purchase orders remain authoritative in their original systems unless separately reconciled.

Three approaches were considered: teach the existing system more thoroughly; build broad integrations; or personally transfer one live working set and automate only the repeated burden. Choose the third. Teaching cannot remove transcription, while broad integrations bet on an incumbent tool nobody has identified. Existing calls, sheets, and the dormant extractor make a small experiment possible. Competitor migration services and import-first playbooks support trying this, not a promised activation lift. [04-prior-art-internal.md (a), (c); 05-competitor-activation.md §§6, 10; 06-activation-playbooks.md §§3–4]

A1-1 · Replace setup homework with the working-set call

Kody’s existing setting-up call begins with “What must leave your desk this week?” rather than a feature tour. With permission, he prepares one real job through existing supported surfaces, then the owner confirms identity, household, next obligation, and source authority in one sitting. Use the existing direct “Open a project” path, not a fictional lead and Discovery interview for an established engagement. Confirm the studio name from her actual letterhead; do not treat the provisioner’s person-name default as an intentional brand. [01-first-session-path.md §§1a, 2–3; 08-gap-critique.md G6]

The first-hour agenda is ten minutes of source inspection, twenty of assisted preparation, fifteen of review and one real output, and fifteen of handoff. At the R133 hire call, the hire retrieves the agreed item and performs the next correction without the owner narrating where it lives. If source cleaning exceeds the window, reduce to the due-this-week slice and record the failure to transfer the rest. Do not claim an afternoon of founder labor as instant product value.

Vision: Document; owner handing a live job to first hands; subscription floor; no new system to learn. Rulings: implements R133, preserves R94/R97/R129, and never opens a document just to demonstrate it; do not bill preparation or assistance as client work. Proof: compare observed entry and owner-explanation minutes with assisted minutes, including corrections. Existing direct-project and teammate arrival are reused, not re-proposed. An import alone cannot transfer the owner’s tacit process; the hire’s unprompted completion is therefore part of the test, consistent with the ethnography’s delegation findings. Sequence, measurement, and stopping rules

September 22–23 is identification, consent, the real workflow observation, and a measurement baseline. September 24–25 is one assisted working-set transfer, one real output, and the existing handoff call. September 28–30 is the next obligation on that same job and, if recruited, two network-studio observations. The only initial engineering candidate is the narrow PDF review door plus its matching export, contingent on actual PDFs. Spreadsheet import needs its ruling; vendor responses need recurring observed quotes; phone sharing comes last. This is not seven builds promised in nine days.

The minimum internal case log records studio and actor classification, actual source, task, source-of-truth agreement, start/end times, active studio minutes, founder minutes, machine wait, corrections, and the output used. Count uploading, mapping, review, recovery, and exporting—not just keystrokes removed. Keep raw customer files out of analytics. Server-side import attempt, candidate, adoption, and export receipts should reconcile with business writes; distinguish retry from a second job. Repair the known event-name mismatch before using activation views, and never treat the first-occurrence activation ledger as repeated weekly usage. [03-prod-evidence.md §§4–5; 08-gap-critique.md G9]

Measure four outcomes: first usable job within sixty elapsed minutes; net studio labor saved against the observed normal method; a hire completing the agreed task with less owner explanation; and the next real obligation completed without retyping in both systems. Ask afterward, “What did this remove from your day?” Record the answer, including “nothing.” Report individual cases and exceptions, not cohort percentages. Founder preparation must be visible: if it stays above forty-five minutes per small working set after three attempts, narrow the promise or stop the concierge offer.

Proceed beyond the pilot only if the actual tasks show positive net time savings, critical money/identity fields reconcile, export reconstructs the adopted rows, and the studio chooses the same path for its next obligation. Fewer sessions may be success. No time-saved dashboard, progress bar, streak, or engagement target belongs in the studio surface. No accumulated review debt qualifies as value: reject unsolicited batch extraction that produces rows nobody asked to review. The unsent weekly-pulse inventory is a warning about that failure mode, not evidence every draft is unwanted. [04-prior-art-internal.md (b)8–10; 03-prod-evidence.md §6.3]

Stop proposing more tours, sample jobs, setup checklist completion, or drip volume as the fix. Keep the original accounting tool authoritative rather than importing receivables that could be collected twice. Do not build calendar, CRM, or mailbox integrations without seeing the specific transfer they remove. Defer whole-library cleanup and historical backfill; attach old material as reference where needed.

Finally, a successful free pilot proves neither willingness to pay nor furniture margin. Kody must resolve V2 and publish the stable price before a paid recruiting promise; V1 and V6 remain open, and the exposed Pledge wording requires a separate leadership/counsel decision. No import may infer markup or sell a royalty promise. These are commercial gates, not invitations to widen this build. [08-gap-critique.md G5; VISION.md §§3–4, 7]

The first decision requested is small: authorize the observed, personally assisted transfer of Leah’s next real working set, then decide which one input deserves a product door. If the real problem is missing functionality rather than transcription—as her older walk once indicated—reject this bet and fix that missing function instead. [08-gap-critique.md G3–G4]

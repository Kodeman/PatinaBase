## 5. The one risk I want Kody to rule on

**Ownership language, printed at the top, in a document a client may be reading over my shoulder.**
"Waiting on Edna: budget comfort" is exactly what I need and exactly what I don't want her seeing
across the dining table — "$17,500 overdue since 12 August" doubly so. Rule: **does the designer head
speak in studio-private voice** (candid about money, lateness, and who is late), with the client
surface carrying its own gentler sentence — **or does the Document keep one voice both parties read?**
My vote is studio-private with neutral phrasing ("Waiting on Edna:", never "Edna owes you"), because a
head that can't name who is late can't do the one job I need it for. But it decides whether the money
line may appear at the top at all, so rule it before it's built.

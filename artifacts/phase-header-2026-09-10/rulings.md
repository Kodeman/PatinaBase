## The direction

**The Band.** The 56px sticky band is the page's only leader region; the letterhead keeps the name alone;
the region head is silent at pre-work; the readiness band demotes to a caption that becomes the band's
sentence when ready; the `+N MORE` door is fixed and its rows split by kind; the undo prints its
consequence always. R140 is crossed knowingly: the agreed figure takes band line 1's right slot on
project spreads and the owed sentence stands beneath it.

## Defects D1–D7

**Folded into the build**, not patched standalone. D1 (shared act on every `+N MORE` row) and D2
(region-head focus ring suppressed) are build items one and two.

## Deviations from the panel

- R3: the panel leaned "a section rule"; Kody ruled "keep it, silent" — consistent with choosing The Band.
- D1/D2: the interaction seat asked for a standalone patch first; Kody folded them into the build.

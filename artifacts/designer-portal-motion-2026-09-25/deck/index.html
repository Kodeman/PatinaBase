The tensionDECISIONS.md:390–393 (R15) · :6603 (I107) · :221–222 (D12)

A surface that promises not to be noticed, asked to move more.

Ambient motion stays banned. Performed motion is the grammar.

Ambient

Moves without her addressing the thing: a loop, a timer, a greeting on arrival. R15: "nothing on the Desk ever moves." Its one exception, the breath on the active spine marker, stays exactly as it is.

Performed

The direct, finite consequence of her act, and reversible. The log already obeys this without naming it. I107: "All motion is interaction-triggered, R15 intact." D12: "raise-to-fill scale (~270ms; reduced-motion: crossfade)."

What Her actually does

Installing OS1, "the screen goes blank, then comes back and looks exactly the same as it did before, except now her voice comes out." The screen can be nearly empty because voice carries the work. A text tool can borrow the intent. It cannot borrow the vanishing.

Eight we borrow

  1. A near-empty resting screen is a resolved choice about attention, not an absence of one.
  2. Warm, restrained color can do the job chrome usually does, giving the eye one focus.
  3. One typeface, used large, reads as considered rather than sparse.
  4. Cutting a whole color family (Her removed blue) signals "not tech-coded" better than adding warmth.
  5. Confirmation appears after an action, not before, so her own work stays the center.
  6. Hand-evident graphics read as authored rather than generated.
  7. Warmth by default is an antidote to machine-coded visual clichés.
  8. Uncluttering comes from color restraint, not from hiding real structure.

Five we refuse

  1. No persona. A professional portal never implies it is someone.
  2. No "the system speaks first." It inverts the promise not to court her attention.
  3. No voice-first architecture. Voice is why Her's screens can vanish.
  4. No narrative prop-dressing. The cigarette-case device was storytelling.
  5. No "blank screen means nothing changed." She needs to know a save or send landed.

Borrow the physics, not the personality: the Dynamic Island's interruptible mechanism, without its living-organism character.

Grammarconcepts.md §1 Two states · Large type and density

At rest

Large: identity, or the fact explaining why this needs a hand. Small: ownership, date, vitals, section names, action words. Absent: secondary explanation behind visible doorways. Never absent: an owed act, error, permission explanation, field label, decision-critical amount or confirmation.

Addressed

The large line stays put. A bounded block (fact, act, doorway) arrives as one unit from the nearest seam: reading order, not delayed beats. Then everything stays still.

Large means 34px Playfair Display, "not a slogan at 70px." It names the work or a sourced standing fact, never a greeting. One address adds one reason (up to two lines), two fact rows and one doorway. Wrap, never truncate.

Mandatory floor in every direction: the Desk's needs and day's line (R143), the editable household and vitals line, the Room head action, field labels and complete ⌘K rows stand at rest.

Grammarconcepts.md §1 Laws (restored in SQ-301)

The rules every direction keeps

  • Never before her act

    Mount, incoming updates and passive intersection cannot invent intent. Pointer address is a 100ms cancellable dwell in a labeled seam; focus, click, tap, Enter and Space are immediate.

  • Never mid-task

    Focus inside, text selection, dirty input, validation, a pending action or an open Sheet holds the region. Scrolling or hovering elsewhere cannot collapse it. Animation never saves, sends or discards.

  • No lure

    No new badge, dot, count or pulse; no count-up, list stagger, entry ceremony, auto-scroll or snapping.

  • Type never reflows under a reading eye

    Wells reserve content-sized space before interaction and never shrink. A full-section opening lays out once, anchored to its heading.

  • Interruptible

    Reverse from the current frame, with no flourish queue, finish-before-close or input lock. Semantic state follows the act, not the animation's end.

  • Reads frozen

    A frozen frame never falsely implies that a save succeeded, a record vanished or a different engagement became active. Find, print and plain reading expose the complete text.

  • Keyboard parity

    Pointer and keyboard reveal identical facts and acts. Touch uses the same visible Detail control, never a long-press. Targets at least 44px.

  • Motion off keeps everything

    Reduced motion preserves every fact, act and hold. No transform, parallax or smooth scroll. The OS preference and the mockup toggle both count.

Recommendconcepts.md §4 · §7 · SQ-280 synthesis part 1

Two honest positions

R-DM1 is common ground: all three directions need performed motion allowed and ambient motion still banned. The decision this deck asks for is R-DM2, how much may disappear at rest.

Astra, as the designer

Open Edges as the default

"Take Open Edges forward as the default candidate. It improves a real studio moment without asking Leah to exchange her working map for our visual idea. Its restraint must not become timidity."

Astra's own doubt: "Open Edges may be too slight to satisfy the desired change in feeling. The correct response is to compare the working mockups, not add idle motion."

Kody's ask, as the brief

Plain Lines is what the brief describes

"Peaceful, large informational text that animates and reveals the details… think Her" describes Plain Lines: large text as the resting state, detail as the performed answer.

Its cost is real and named: cross-job context suffers, and a permanent Show all means the premise failed. The walk tests that cost; it does not disqualify the direction in advance.

Graft from the runners-up

  • From Plain Lines, its truthful hierarchy, and a trial of its large Desk needs, "but do not silently apply its R150 amendment to Open Edges."
  • From Reading Line, its guarantee that stopping, selecting or entering work holds the place. Never its automatic scroll-disclosure.

What would change it

"Plain Lines wins only if the reduced surface makes those tasks clearer without extra hunting." The tasks are the walk on slide 24.

How far the checking goes

Nobody has rendered these in a browser, on a phone, or with a screen reader. Nobody has walked them with Leah or a first-week hand.

Rulingsconcepts.md §6, verbatim

Rulings for the team

These are proposed rulings, not decisions already taken. The mockups compare consequences; they do not amend production doctrine. The filled letter is the recommendation.

  1. R-DM1 · the R15 amendment

    May R15 explicitly allow performed motion while ambient motion remains banned?

    • Keep its literal "nothing on the Desk ever moves";
    • Allow finite, reversible motion caused by her act, retain only the existing active-spine breath as an ambient exception;
    • Consider another ambient exception in a separate program.

    Recommend BGeneralize the I107/D12 test. No concept here depends on C. R35's actual pending-operation sweep remains operation feedback, not permission for a resting loop.

  2. R-DM2

    How much secondary information may disappear at rest?

    • Existing readable Document plus optional seam explanations (Open Edges);
    • Truth-line-first with visible Detail controls and a Show all hold (Plain Lines);
    • Reading-place-first with bounded scroll reveals (Reading Line).

    Recommend Afor the default candidate; compare B and C in the nine mockups before choosing any broader removal. In every option, Desk needs/day line, errors, labels, primary acts, household/vitals, decision-critical amounts and command sub-labels remain visible. They are not bargaining chips for quiet.

  3. R-DM3

    May section standing facts join document identity at 34px, amending R150's silent heads and band-only leader allocation?

    • Keep document name 34 / standing fact 16 / silent region head;
    • Permit one 34px section fact at its own place, removing any duplicate standing fact;
    • Make each section name large regardless of its information.

    Recommend Afor Open Edges; authorize B only as the explicit Plain Lines/Reading Line concept amendment. Decline C. A heading earns scale by stating work, not by naming navigation. The household/editable vitals remain controls, not expendable small print.

  4. R-DM4

    Does deliberate scrolling count as addressing a section?

    • No, detail requires labeled focus/tap/click;
    • Yes for optional explanation, with stable geometry, an equal explicit control and task holds;
    • Let scroll automatically open and close working sections.

    Recommend Bfor the Reading Line experiment only, A for the default's working content; decline C. Reading Line also needs the named D12/D3 layout amendment for all-width single-column margin access. No scroll-triggered loss of draft, selection or place is acceptable.

  5. R-DM5

    When should a revealed explanation recede?

    • As soon as pointer leaves;
    • On the next unheld address or explicit close, with blank paper and reading pauses doing nothing;
    • Only on explicit close for every preview.

    Recommend Bplus C once she pins or enters work. Show all is a persistent click/Space toggle within the current surface, never a physical long press. No idle countdown. If B proves unpredictable in a walk, use C before adding more choreography.

  6. R-DM6

    What should reduced motion preserve when the concept depends on scroll-disclosure?

    • Same hidden summaries but no travel;
    • Fully printed summaries in the same column, with all full-section acts intact;
    • Remove those summaries.

    Recommend Bfor Reading Line; A for the explicit-control Open Edges/Plain Lines variants. Decline C. Information and task parity matter more than matching the sparse screenshot. Motion off must be a first-class working surface.

  7. R-DM7

    Should a first hand receive a different visual arrival?

    • Same still Desk, with only an already-eligible once/in-place teaching note;
    • A special animated welcome or tour;
    • No teaching note even when the existing program calls for one.

    Recommend AThe studio's first hand should inherit its working language, not be greeted by a second interface. This motion program must neither expand nor silently disable the existing teaching program.

Nextconcepts.md §4 What would change the recommendation · §5 Prototype limitations

If a direction is picked, what happens next

No code is authorised by this deck. Every step below is design work. A build is a separate request, after the rulings and the walk.

  1. Rule R-DM1 and R-DM2

    The R15 amendment first, since every direction depends on it; then how much may disappear at rest. R-DM3 to R-DM7 follow from the direction.

  2. Walk the picked mockups with Leah and a first-week hand

    Find the finish-approval job, compare it with the delivery job, reach the record, enter a draft, pull Accounts and return. By keyboard, at phone width and with reduced motion. Watch for wrong choices, repeated reveals, lost place and requests to show everything. Never measure dwell or delight.

  3. Render them properly for the first time

    A real browser, a real phone and a screen reader. None of that has happened yet; layout, focus order and announcements are unverified.

  4. Write the rulings into DECISIONS.md

    Including the R15 amendment and any R150, D12 or D3 amendment the chosen direction names, so doctrine changes by ruling, never by a mockup.

No direction earns production approval from a beautiful replay alone.Astra · concepts.md §4

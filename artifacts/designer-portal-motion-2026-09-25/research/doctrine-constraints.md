# The Document's motion doctrine, vision constraints, and studio moments

*Recon memo for SQ-273 · 2026-09-25 · read-only against the repo. No designs proposed.*

## §1 The vision test

**Which surface.** The Document — "the studio's working surface... this is Patina for the next twelve months" (`docs/vision/VISION.md:21`). Not the iOS app, not the marketplace.

**Which studio moment.** §2's trigger: "A growing design studio at the moment it adds its first hands while its workload doubles... The studio owner is delegating for the first time, her own hours are already spoken for twice, and the thing she cannot afford is a new system to learn" (`docs/vision/VISION.md:29-31`). She is not a new user learning Patina; she is a busy owner installing a second person into a tool she already trusts.

**Which stream.** Mostly the floor — the studio subscription (`docs/vision/VISION.md:42`). Motion that makes the tool feel more capable or more trustworthy protects renewal; it is not upside-stream work.

**Which promise.** "*you won't notice Patina.* It is not a place you go... Success is that she doesn't notice — so we will never optimize the studio surface for engagement" (`docs/vision/VISION.md:50`). Confirmed and strengthened by `docs/vision/VISION-DECISIONS.md:212-213`: "no engagement chrome in the studio's own working tools (S4, unchanged)."

**The tension, stated plainly.** Kody is asking for *more* motion on a surface whose founding promise is to be unnoticed, and whose standing rulings actively ban ambient motion: R15 rules "nothing on the Desk ever moves. 'Pulsing' beyond this is declined — ambient motion is what the no-badge discipline exists to prevent" (`docs/design/the-document/DECISIONS.md:392-393`). Any motion concept for this program has to survive that sentence, not route around it.

**The resolving distinction.** The doctrine already draws — without ever naming — a line between two kinds of motion:

- **AMBIENT** — moves on its own, without her hand causing it, on a timer or a loop. Banned everywhere except one sanctioned exception: "MOTION: exactly one — a slow breath... on the ACTIVE spine marker only, where 'alive' is literally true" (`docs/design/the-document/DECISIONS.md:390-392`), later confirmed as "R15's single sanctioned ambient motion" (`docs/design/the-document/DECISIONS.md:1458`).
- **PERFORMED** — starts because she acted, runs while the consequence of that act is still resolving, and stops the moment it's resolved. It reveals what she asked for and nothing else.

The clearest existing statement of this test, though never elevated to a general rule, is I107's line on the button primitive: "All motion is interaction-triggered — R15 intact. No shadows — D4 intact" (`docs/design/the-document/DECISIONS.md:6603`). D12's pick-up/put-down transform is the other clean example — "Pick up = raise-to-fill scale (~270ms; reduced-motion: crossfade); put down recedes to the Desk" (`docs/design/the-document/DECISIONS.md:221-222`) — motion exactly as long as the act of opening or closing the document takes, then still. Nowhere does a doc name "ambient vs. performed" as the organizing test; a reveal-on-intent concept is the first thing that would need to state it as a rule rather than leave it implicit in R15 and I107 separately.

## §3 The Arrival Arc and Scored Ink

**The Arrival Arc** (`docs/design/the-document/the-document-arrival-arc-package.md`) already decided how a designer *arrives* at a new relationship: accept is "a threshold, not a button" (`:18`), and the Match Ceremony that follows is explicitly "full-screen, typography-first, zero shadows" (`:22`) — the same materials vocabulary as every Room. Her copy is never system-authored on her behalf: "Truth-framed: it reports what happened, it does not speak in the designer's voice" (`:20`). This is a template for "arriving" moments generally: weight comes from full-bleed paper and typography, not from motion.

**Scored Ink** (I107, `docs/design/the-document/DECISIONS.md:6584-6604`) already settled "large text at rest" for *acts*: buttons lost their boxes and became bare DM Mono words with proofreader's scoring, and the only motion left is the wet-ink press — "All motion is interaction-triggered" (`:6603`). R150, "The Standing Head" (`docs/design/the-document/DECISIONS.md:11020-11054`), settled it for *identity*: the document's name prints at 34px at rest (R6, amending R126, `:11041`), and the standing sentence at 16px — large, static text carrying the page's one true fact, not an animated header. Detail is already progressive elsewhere too: `.uf-body`/`.m-body` unfolds (margin items, FF&E lines) and the sweep's indeterminate fill are both act-triggered reveals of more text, never a decorative loop.

## §4 The prototype v4

`docs/design/the-document/patina-the-document-prototype-v4.html` is the canonical look/feel/motion reference (`apps/designer-portal/CLAUDE.md:14`). Every motion rule in its CSS is act-scoped and short: screens pick up on `.26s ease-out` (`:17`), a document raises on `.27s ease-out` matching D12's ~270ms (`:18-20`), unfold bodies (margin/FF&E) transition `max-height` over `.25-.3s ease` (`:140,188`), and the bottom sheet slides on `transform .3s ease` (`:214`). Nothing in the prototype loops or runs without a cause. Its reduced-motion query is blanket: `@media(prefers-reduced-motion:reduce){.screen.on,.screen.doc.on{animation:none}.sheet,.uf-body,.m-body{transition:none!important}}` (`:21`) — every one of those motions is disabled outright, with no crossfade substitute specified at the prototype layer (D12's crossfade fallback is a later refinement on top of it). On density: the prototype's unfolds default closed (`max-height:0`) — density at rest is low, detail arrives only on the act that asks for it.

## §5 The six studio moments

1. **Arriving at the Desk between tasks.** Doctrine: nothing on the Desk moves (R15, `DECISIONS.md:392`); no badges or pulsing counts (D8, `:19`); folders claim only an available act (R22, `:871-873`). Peaceful at rest = a still page of true folders, no ambient signal competing for her eye before she's chosen to look at anything.
2. **Picking up a held document.** Doctrine: raise-to-fill scale, ~270ms (D12, `:221-222`); full-bleed paper, no charcoal surround (`:213-215`). Peaceful at rest = the transform finishes and the page goes still; nothing keeps moving once the document is "in hand."
3. **Reading a section then acting** (approving, sending). Doctrine: Scored Ink's wet-ink press is the only motion, and it is interaction-triggered (I107, `:6598-6603`); a terminal act is the one filled control she ever meets (`apps/designer-portal/CLAUDE.md:48`; V9, `VISION-DECISIONS.md:170-173`). Peaceful at rest = the act's consequence renders as a change in state (a stamp, a fill), not a celebratory flourish.
4. **Pulling a Sheet to glance and put back.** Doctrine: D14 — "pull, glance, put back; the document stays mounted beneath" (`:987-988`); Accounts is named explicitly as Sheet-weight, not Room (`:1534`). Peaceful at rest = the Sheet arrives and leaves on the existing slide/put-back grammar; the document beneath never re-renders or loses state.
5. **⌘K to go somewhere.** Doctrine: "One box... no toggle, no 'ask mode'... the Engine 'thinking' state uses the R35 Strata sweep, never a spinner" (`DECISIONS.md:1552`); results carry the fill-state mark (`:707`). Peaceful at rest = the box is empty and still until she types; the only motion during a wait is the one sanctioned sweep.
6. **A hand (second seat) arriving for the first time.** Doctrine outside this file but load-bearing: VISION §2's trigger is exactly this moment (`VISION.md:29-31`); the return-teaching recon already ruled the owner "cannot afford a new system to learn" and that any onboarding note must be in-place, once, never a badge or a tour (`artifacts/return-teaching-2026-09-25/research/vision-framing.md:20,57,62`). Peaceful at rest = the new seat's first hour looks exactly like the owner's first hour did — the same still Desk, the same act-triggered reveals — with nothing added to mark her as new.

## §6 Brand voice rules binding copy in mockups

Per `.claude/skills/patina-brand-voice/SKILL.md`: never lead with AI, algorithm, or engine mechanics (`:23-24`); no exclamation-point energy — "understatement over exclamation" (`:45`); Midwest plain-spoken, sensory, story-driven (`:14-19`); prefer *patina, provenance, grain, workshop, maker* — avoid *disrupt, revolutionize, AI-powered, curated, elevated, bespoke* (`:34-38`). Deck/mockup typography: "Playfair headlines, Inter body, DM Mono labels" (`:44`), matching the house style already in force for the product itself (`apps/designer-portal/CLAUDE.md:23`). Numbers must be true and sourced — no puffery (`:31`).

## §7 Open questions only a human can rule

1. Does "more motion" mean more PERFORMED motion (longer/richer reveals tied to her acts) or a request to relax R15's ambient-motion ban itself? These are opposite directions and the concepts need to know which before storyboarding.
2. Is a second ambient exception (beyond the R15 breath) even on the table, or is the breath permanently the only one — a ceiling, not a precedent?
3. For the second-seat arrival (moment 6), does anything visibly mark "this is your first time" for the new hand, or does doctrine's silence-by-default apply to her exactly as it does to the owner?
4. Does the Strata sweep's ~2.2s restart loop (`DECISIONS.md:1462`) count as "ambient" during a long wait, or is it exempted as PERFORMED because it started from her act (a save/scan) even though it can outlast that act's natural duration?
5. Can a concept propose a new short-lived, act-scoped motion (e.g., a reveal beyond ~300ms) without a Kody ruling first, or does every new motion primitive require its own DECISIONS.md entry before a prototype can show it?

**Word count:** ~2,150.

**Ruling most likely to block the program:** R15 (`docs/design/the-document/DECISIONS.md:381-396`) — "nothing on the Desk ever moves... ambient motion is what the no-badge discipline exists to prevent." It is the single explicit ban standing directly against "more motion," it already has exactly one named, deliberated exception (the breath), and any concept that reads as ambient rather than performed will be measured against this sentence first.

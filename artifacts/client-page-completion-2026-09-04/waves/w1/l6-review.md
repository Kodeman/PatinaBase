## What is right

- Payload fidelity where it counts: `useSubmitReview` `{ reviewId, rating, reviewText }`, the 1–5 / ≥30 validation, `reviewVerdictFromLabel` + `{ reviewItemId, verdict, comment? }`, `{ requestId, projectId, approvedByName }`, `{ requestId, projectId, declineReason }` — all byte-equal to the old routes.
- The closed-edition line and the "does not authorize a purchase or change an authorization" legal sentence are verbatim.
- Hooks discipline is clean: every hook in all five components sits above every early return; nothing new is added to `@patina/supabase`; `derive.ts` is untouched.
- No hydration hazard in practice — `editionIdFromUrl()` reads `window.location` during render, but `threshold.tsx:774` holds the whole body until `useHydrated` resolves, so the ask only ever mounts client-side.
- Cross-project safety on the deep link is real: `useClientProjectReviewBundle` throws when `bundle.projectId !== expectedProjectId`, and the lane passes `projectId`. Scope changes are RLS-scoped to `projects.client_id = auth.uid()`. No path reaches another client's or project's data.
- `data-never-dim` is applied to the open asks and dropped on the settled stamps, matching `door-gate.tsx:284` / `wall-gate.tsx:186`.
- No shadow, no badge, no tab, no header, no red/green semantics, no "AI"; money goes through `moneyInWords`/`countInWords`/`joinClauses`; the old amber/green/red `StatusBadge` was correctly *not* ported.
- No overlay or sheet is introduced, so the dialog/focus-trap/Esc criterion has nothing to fail.
- Tests are behaviour-shaped (payloads and rendered words, not internals) and the mock style matches `making/__tests__`.

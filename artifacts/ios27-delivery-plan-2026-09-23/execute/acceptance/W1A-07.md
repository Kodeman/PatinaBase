### 2.4 What the simulator tier cannot prove

In the simulator, `runsRealServices` is false whenever the app runs on the Simulator or has
`-CaptureUITest` (`AppConfiguration.swift:101-104`). So `verificationHarnessAllowed`
(`CaptureDeepLink.swift:251-257`) is true, and a refused harness link cannot be observed there.
The Simulator's `utsname.machine` is the host's `arm64`, so the Ready screen always takes the
no-Action-Button branch (F4). In mock mode, `MockSessionProviding` starts signed in on every
launch. So the signed-out state could only be reached by signing out during the session. A cold
launch with no session is the device's job.

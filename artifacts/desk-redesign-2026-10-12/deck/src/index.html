06 / 324 · The principledirection.md §1, §2

The principle

Today's Desk ranks but has no edges; the old Desk had edges but no rank. Put the edges back around the ranked thing, and let a small colour tell the eye where each job lives, never how loudly to worry.

  • OnceEach thing on the Desk once. A need on the day's five never repeats below with a second act.
  • An edgeEach unit with its own edge. Name, need and act read as one thing; every section closes on a full-measure rule.
  • Colour orientsColour is spent on orientation. The word always prints; urgency lives in the order and the words.

Where the seats split, and how the synthesis ruled

Direction B

The day board: the day itself is the structure

Need cards on three columns: Now · Waiting · Coming back. The Day Sheet becomes the Now column, at most five cards. A need-class tab sits on each card and is lit only while the need is yours.

Pressing an act opens it inside the card, not over the page. The card grows in place to hold the scoped draft and a named second control ("Send to Nora and Sam Holloway"), with Put back beside it. One card is open at a time. After sending, the card settles in place, its tab drops to faint, and its slot does not refill.

## Seat A: Leah, principal, Hartwell Studio (simulated persona)

About 16 live jobs. Jordan is in his second week. The workload is doubling. She wants the day in five seconds, and she is out of the studio more than she is in it.

### A1. What she hunts for

- **8am, coffee, laptop at 1440.** She wants the three things she must do before she leaves, and the one that can wait. Then she wants to know what she handed Jordan and whether it is in hand.
- **Noon, in the car between site visits, phone at 390.** She wants the one job she is about to walk into: what is next on it, and whether money or a maker is stuck. Thirty seconds, one thumb.
- **4pm, back at the desk.** She wants to know what moved today, what is waiting on clients and makers rather than on her, and what lands on her plate tomorrow. She wants to be able to close the laptop.

### A2. Today's Desk

**The header** (`W/today-header-tips-crop-1440.jpg`, `W/today-header-390.jpg`)

> "Record an inquiry, then 'Create an inquiry brief' underneath. That's the same sentence twice. I read five little capital phrases and I can't tell which ones I'm meant to press."

**The sheet** (`W/today-leah-mine-fold-1440.jpg`)

She likes the act sheets. SEND REMINDER opens a drafted note with "PUT BACK · ESC" in about a second, and nothing goes out until she presses send (`W/m4-holloway-reminder-1440.jpg`).

Mine and The studio's show her the same five lines, because she owns every live job (`W/today-leah-studio-fold-1440.jpg`).

### A3. The old card Desk (US-24)

**What she misses** (`U/desk-fold-1440.jpg`, `U/desk-full-1440.jpg`):

> "I could find Holloway without reading. The umber PROJECT, the slate BRIEF, the olive PROPOSAL, the brick INSTALL: I knew where a job was in its life from across the room."

> "Each job had its own box. The name at the top was a link, and the one thing to do sat on its own line at the bottom. Name, problem, button: one thing."

> "The overdue sentence was in the red-brown ink, so Chen and Holloway stood out from the leads."

> "'Six things are overdue — Chen, Holloway, Halloran and 3 more.' I liked a sentence with names in it."

> "WITH THE CLIENT, WITH THE MAKER. On Holloway, Halloran and Marsh Street, that word told me the ball wasn't mine."

**What annoyed her:**

> "Thirty-two boxes, and twenty-nine of them said YOUR PEN. Ten said 'Friday Pulse drafted — review & send'. That's one line, not ten cards."

> "'And 29 more below.' Thanks."

> "The coloured labels were the loudest thing on the page, louder than the money. And red on a one-day invoice and red on a 150-day one: once everything's red, nothing is."

> "'The studio isn't fully set up · FINISH SETTING UP', every evening. And the boxes were different heights, so the rows looked ragged."

### A4. What a colour tag should tell her

**Stage, at a glance.** Where each job is in its life: brief, discovery, direction, proposal, project, install. It is the way she already sorts her jobs in her head, and it is what the old plates did well.

> "Colour should be the job's address, not its temperature. It tells me where the job lives. The words tell me what's wrong."

**Overdue money** can keep the red-brown ink, but only in the sentence. Not as a second tag.

**What would make colour noise, or nagging:**

- a coloured block on every card as solid as the old plates;
- colour plus a dot plus a custody word on the same card;
- red on everything late, from one day to 153;
- a colour that changes because the software decided something is urgent;
- any colour on Jordan's rows that grades him.

> "If it looks like a dashboard, I'll close it."

### A5. Leah's three changes

1. **One-line header. Drop the tips.** Record an inquiry, Create a project file and Find anything, once each. On the phone, the greeting and date take one line.
2. **Make each sheet line a card she can enter.** It has an edge, the job name as a link, a small stage tag, and the money or the wait as the headline. A job on the sheet should not appear again in the list below with a second button.
3. **Give the studio scope a reason to exist.** Show what is with Jordan, and what is waiting on clients and makers, as separate groups with an edge between them. That way 4pm has an answer. She wants to see where things stand, not how busy Jordan was.

3. **Make the quiet state true.** When the sheet is quiet, the page under it agrees. A maker's silence reads as waiting on Verellen since 5 October, not as overdue. What he put off shows as coming back tomorrow.

## What the seats agree on, and where they split (out of character)

**They agree:**

- **The header is one line, with no tips.** Neither seat can tell an act from its description (`W/today-header-tips-crop-1440.jpg`), and at 390 it costs a quarter of the screen.
- **Bring back the edge, not the shout.** Both miss what a card gave them: one box per job, the name as a link, and the act on its own foot. Neither wants 32 cards back, or seven registers per card. The bounded, ranked sheet with Not today stays.
- **One need, one place.** Holloway's second SEND REMINDER and Jordan's quiet sheet over a busy list both break trust. The sheet and the workload need a visible edge, and a need on the sheet should not repeat below it.
- **The job name must open the job** from the sheet itself (WALK M6).
- **Act sheets that draft but don't send** are the part of today's Desk both would defend hardest (`W/m3-…`, `W/m4-…`).
- **Colour must not grade, count, or pulse.** Red-brown ink stays in the sentence for real money trouble, not on every late thing.
- **Close of day has no answer.** Neither sees what moved, or what returns tomorrow.

**They split:**

- **What the colour tag means.** Leah wants **stage**: the old plates' orientation, quieter. Jordan wants **whose move it is**: mine, Leah's, waiting on the client or the maker. This is the founders' real choice for the tag. One direction could carry stage in colour and ownership in words, or the reverse. Carrying both in colour brings back the old card's noise.
- **What The studio's scope is for.** Leah wants it to show her Jordan's side and what waits on others. Jordan wants it to stay clearly labelled as Leah's, and to be safe to read without acting.
- **Visibility.** Leah wants to know Marsh Street was chased. Jordan does not want a surface that reports his pace. A tag that says "with Jordan" serves both seats. A tag that says how long Jordan took serves neither.
- **Order of the sheet.** Leah wants it ranked by money and trust. Jordan wants his hand-off first, because it is context for everything else he does that day.

The choice is in the weight

One direction. Two editions.

Compare the feeling of the page. The structure, typography, project content, and interactions are shared.

Paper uses image, type, and warm stock for weight. Bound adds a forest-colored field. Both keep the continuous project flow and the same light working paper.

Our recommendation

Start the next round with Bound. It keeps the left-hand comfort the team chose in Common Ground, while the real Patina typefaces and paper tones bring it back to the current product.

Use Paper as the calibration: if the darker cover competes with the next decision during a real working session, keep its visual composition and return to the lighter stock.

This is a design recommendation. Test the choice with one real project handoff before treating it as a usability finding.

# Bound Paper

A forest cloth cover holding a warm paper project journal. The left page carries the atmosphere of the room; the right page carries the next decision and the history that brought us here.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest cloth | `#3E5145` | The current portal's forest, given a larger, quieter role on the left |
| Document paper | `#FCFAF6` | Existing portal paper, retained on the project-flow side |
| Linen | `#E8E3DB` | Comfortable reading text against forest |
| Pale clay | `#D7BC98` | A lighter relative of Patina clay for readable small details |
| Binding seam | `#2C3C31` | Fine physical join between the two pages |
| Charcoal ink | `#2C2926` | Existing portal's primary ink on the paper page |

## What stays familiar

Playfair Display remains the editorial voice; Inter carries practical information, and DM Mono stays reserved for precise record details. Warm document paper, fine rules, restrained underlining, generous margins and Patina's forest all come from the current designer portal. Photography and material samples carry the visual interest.

## What Common Ground contributes

The project has one emotional home on the left and one continuous account of progress on the right. The room, the designer's note and its materials live together. A decision is read in the context of what is already settled and what follows, with people visibly attached to the work.

## The slight variation

Bound Paper changes material and visual weight only. It shares the Paper variant's content, typography, image crop, geometry and interactions. An almost imperceptible woven line texture and a fine binding seam make the forest page feel held and tactile; the right remains a light reading surface. This is an intimate bound volume within the existing Patina language.

Founder choice: Should the project feel like an open magazine on the studio table, or a quietly bound volume you return to throughout the work?

## Contrast

Paper text on forest is 8.16:1; linen secondary text is 6.66:1; pale clay labels are 4.67:1. Original clay `#C4A57B` would be 3.65:1 on forest, so it is reserved for material identity rather than small text. Focus outlines remain paper-white on the dark page. Texture opacity is kept below 5%; no typography, image sizing or layout changes are introduced by this stylesheet.

## What to keep

1. **The two-persona framing itself, and its narrowest fix.** Diagnosing that `desk-walkthrough.tsx` hard-codes `persona="designer"` at both call sites (verified, lines 453/462) while the owner and the first hire have opposite day-one questions is the proposal's sharpest and most original contribution — none of the sibling proposals frame the persona split this cleanly against VISION §2's "moment it adds first hands." Even though F1/F2 show the plumbing is less pre-built than claimed, the *shape* of the fix (extend the existing prop/schema rather than build a new mechanism) is exactly right in spirit.

2. **Deriving "Invite your crew" on acceptance, not invitation, plus the new "first hire opened a document" row.** This is a small, surgical change (`crewInvited` moves from `memberCountBeyondSelf > 0` to an accepted-membership check, which `accept_workspace_invitation` — confirmed live in `00295_studio_workspace_provisioning.sql` — already supports) that closes a real, well-evidenced blind spot: the checklist currently can't tell invited from landed. It stays entirely inside the existing derive-don't-demand idiom.

3. **The stuck-signals list as a human, not automated, recovery path (§6).** Precise, falsifiable triggers (72-hour unaccepted invite; 3+ live jobs with no hire document after 7 days; pick-up/put-down thrash; a dry-query term that maps to a real capability; any flight event) handed to a named person rather than a second in-product nudge is the cleanest possible way to get "the ambient hundred hours" coverage without adding chrome or violating the no-engagement-metrics rule — modulo F4's finding that the flight event itself needs to be built before this can run.

4. **"Never measured" list in §8.** Explicitly writing down that sessions, time-in-app, DAU/WAU, tour-completion-rate, help-article-views, and drip-open-rate must never become success metrics — and naming that a CS function is exactly where VISION §6's engagement refusal usually erodes first — is the proposal's most disciplined moment and the one most worth preserving verbatim in whatever gets ruled on.

5. **The email-drip retiming principle (state-triggered, not calendar-triggered, one-a-week cap) plus exactly two additions (H0, O1).** Restrained, reuses the existing M1–M4 state-trigger pattern rather than inventing a new one, and correctly identifies the handoff moment (owner + accepted-but-silent hire) as the one gap in an otherwise complete 17-email deck — without touching T0/N1/N2, which the proposal rightly leaves alone.

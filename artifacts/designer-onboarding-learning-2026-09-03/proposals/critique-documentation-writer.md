## What to keep

1. **The registry-blurb-as-single-source discipline, with a genuinely actionable rollout.** Extending `registry.tsx`'s `help.blurb` field as the one content source for both the contextual panel and the proposed glossary (§4, "every entry... renders its `registry.tsx` `help.blurb` verbatim as its first line; only the second paragraph... is authored") is the most concretely engineered idea in the pack — it turns the "one definition, one place" canon rule into a literal content-authoring contract, and it comes with a sequenced, capacity-aware wave plan (§6, Waves 1-5, sized to "a single sitting's worth") that the other two proposals gesture at but don't schedule as precisely.

2. **Catching the R96 contradiction on the shipped Orders sheet.** §4's closing note — that the Orders sheet renders four tabs (`LEDGER · THE WEEK · RECEIVING · VENDORS`), which sits against R96's "a sheet stays one page" guardrail, and the explicit refusal to write that guardrail into a glossary entry until it's reconciled (§9.9) — is a sourced, specific, load-bearing catch that neither sibling proposal raises as directly. It's exactly the kind of constraint-vs-shipped-reality check a documentation lens is best positioned to make.

3. **The owner-authored handoff note, feature-tested inline.** The one genuinely new object the proposal commits to building (§3, "the one genuinely new object I would build") — an optional line from the owner, carried on the invitation, rendered once as a margin note in her own words — is well-scoped (one field, one render, one recede condition) and is the proposal's clearest example of actually running the VISION §8 feature test in the open ("surface — The Document... moment — §2's literal trigger... stream — the subscription floor... promise — 'won't notice'"), which the brief specifically asked every proposal to do.

4. **Pairing every metric with a stated anti-goal.** §8's discipline — "the number of opens is not the target," an explicit expectation that `help.help_center.viewed` should *fall* as the panel starts answering in place, a named threshold at which a margin note should be judged noise and cut — is a clean, reusable articulation of "reference is not engagement" that any final synthesis should carry forward regardless of which proposal's specific mechanisms win.

5. **Crisp, sourced retire calls.** The reuse/retire table's decisions to delete `first-signin-tour.tsx` (280 lines, zero imports, actively misleading PRD 09), mark `CODEBASE-MAP.md`'s route table as superseded, kill the ten-video track rather than let it block the shelf, and stop silently CSS-hiding the Featured section when it's empty ("makes a bare Help Center look intentional rather than broken") are all well-grounded, low-risk housekeeping moves that reduce confusion for whoever builds next, independent of which onboarding-mechanism proposal is chosen.

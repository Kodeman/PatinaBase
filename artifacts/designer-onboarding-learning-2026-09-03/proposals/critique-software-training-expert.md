## What to keep

The proposal's strongest ideas, worth carrying into the synthesis regardless of the findings above:

1. **The tour's last step should act, not describe ("Begin with a lead" → opens `CaptureLeadSheet` before the tour closes).** This is the single sharpest idea across all three proposals: it converts the one structured teaching moment from telling to doing, stays inside D1/R4 by never entering `/doc/[id]` during the tour itself, and correctly identifies that the sheet opens *over* `/desk` so the timer only starts once she's chosen to act. The proposal is honest that this needs a ruling (§9.4) rather than shipping it as an assumption.

2. **Re-cutting the Studio Setup Checklist around acts instead of account admin.** "Put one client on paper," "Send something to a client," "Draw the first invoice" replacing "Seed the rolodex" makes the checklist's own footer copy — *"do the thing and the box fills"* — actually true. This is a small, surgical change to an existing, already-correct component, and it directly serves the proposal's "competence, not completion" principle.

3. **The `document_first_act {act, nth}` competence signal.** Distinguishing exposure (`help.*` fired in the same session) from unaided repetition (`nth: 2` with no help event) is a genuinely new and well-designed measurement idea not present in either sibling proposal in this exact shape — it operationalizes "does she now do the thing again, unaided" as a concrete, buildable event rather than an aspiration.

4. **Content-rot answer for margin notes (`doc-first-touch@2` version-suffixing).** A small, precise mechanism that answers R94's own acknowledged risk ("notes written once... slowly describe an interface that has moved on") without inventing a new nagging surface — the note still fires at most once per version, and the proposal correctly flags it as a ruling-worthy bend of canon rather than sneaking it through.

5. **The keys sheet's content discipline** ("Doorway rows read from `registry.tsx`, so they cannot drift") and the three-rung shortcut-teaching ladder generally — chips stay, one new permanent reference surface, three once-each progressive prompts, "no fourth prompt, ever." This is the most fully-specified answer among the three proposals to a real, well-evidenced gap (13+ undocumented Board Room bindings), and it reuses `registry-shortcuts.tsx`'s existing guard set rather than inventing a new one.

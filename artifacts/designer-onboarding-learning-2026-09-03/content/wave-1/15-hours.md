Hours is the studio's time ledger — every hour the studio worked, who worked it, on which document, what the work was, and what it bills at. Reach it with `G` then `H`, or ask the command bar for "Hours"; it slides over whatever document is in hand.

Most hours land on their own. Pick up a document and the studio's clock starts; put it down and it stops, then offers you the time with a small strip: nudge the minutes, name the work, mark it billable or not, and log it. Under a minute is discarded quietly. Idle stretches are noted, never trimmed. The first time the clock runs for you it says so once; if you would rather start it yourself, turn auto-start off on your own profile and a single tap starts it instead.

Not every hour happens inside a document. A call from the truck, a drive to the site, a morning at the market: log it by hand. Type "Log time" into the command bar from anywhere, or use the add row at the foot of Hours — pick the document, the minutes, and the day, which can be any day back to the last invoice. On the phone, the timer sheet has a manual entry; in Patina Field, Log time sits on the Today screen and at the end of a site visit. Studio time with no document logs as non-billable.

Every hour prices itself from the studio's rates. Owners and admins set a rate per person on the studio account page; a services agreement's rate card binds to roster roles, and a member holding two roles picks which role an hour was. An hour with no rate yet reads "rate pending" until the owner sets one — it never prices at zero in silence.

Your own hours are always yours to see. Owners and admins open the same sheet wider: a person's week from their card in People, a document's hours from its paper, or the whole studio — one sheet, one lens at the top, totals sitting above the rows that made them. A person's hours open as totals first; the notes behind them take one more act. The lead of a document sees her team's hours on it.

When it is time to bill, the unbilled balance is one act from the invoice composer, and Export writes a file: one row per hour, with the person, the day, the document, the rate, the amount, and the invoice it landed on.

Nothing here nags. A clock left running past eight hours leaves one quiet line in the Record. No emails, no reminders unless you ask for one.

Esc puts Hours back and returns you to whatever document you were holding.

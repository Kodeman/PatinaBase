Every purchase order, from drawn to delivered. That's what Orders is — reach it with `G` then `O`, or from the drawer's Ledgers popover, and it slides over whatever document you're holding without putting it down.

The sheet is organized in tabs. LEDGER is the running list — every PO the studio has drawn, newest first, each carrying its own status: draft, confirmed, in production, shipped, delivered, or cancelled. THE WEEK narrows that same list to what needs your attention in the next seven days: pieces due to ship, orders waiting on a maker's confirmation, anything about to slip. RECEIVING is where a delivery gets checked in against what was ordered — quantities, condition, whether it matches the PO — and where a damage claim starts if something arrives wrong. VENDORS lists the makers you've ordered from — a maker's whole order history in one place — and it's also where "Order all — N items" queues up every approved line you hold with that vendor at once.

A PO here isn't a form you fill out and forget. It's tied to the project it belongs to, and its status moves as the real order moves — draft through delivered — so the ledger is always a true read of where the studio's money and furniture actually stand, not a snapshot from whenever someone last updated it.

Esc puts the sheet back and returns you to whatever you had in hand.

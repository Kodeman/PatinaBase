Capture a lead is one of the five Verbs on the Desk — the front door for a name you haven't started working with yet. It's built to take under five seconds: a name, a way to reach them (email or phone), one line about the project, and where they came from.

"Where from" is free text with a few suggestion chips to speed you along — Referral, Website quiz, Instagram, Past client — but you can type any channel that fits; whatever you enter is what feeds the studio's own referral and pipeline picture later, so it's worth a real answer rather than a placeholder.

The moment you submit, two things happen. A Brief folder for that person appears on the Desk immediately, and you're taken straight into it at its own document address, so you keep filling in the rest — what they're after, what you've learned so far — while it's fresh, rather than losing the thread to a separate intake form. The lead also gets a one-day response window by default, which is why a fresh capture shows up as a need on the Desk rather than sitting quietly until you remember it.

You don't have to know very much to start one. The sheet asks for just enough to begin; the Brief is where the rest fills in as the conversation goes on.

Every invoice action lives on the invoice itself — open it from Accounts (`G` then `A`), and its folio carries the acts row: Issue & send, Record payment, Resend, Void, and Print.

**To settle it** — meaning to record that money came in — open the invoice and choose "Record payment." A paid-in-full payment moves the invoice to paid; a partial one moves it to partially paid, and the folio keeps tracking what's still owed. There's no separate reconciliation screen; the invoice's own status is the record.

**To void it** — for an invoice drawn in error, never for one a client simply hasn't paid yet — open it and choose "Void." You'll be asked for a reason before it's confirmed; that reason stays on the record. A voided invoice can't be issued, sent, or paid again, and it drops out of the studio's receivables.

**To print it** — the folio prints itself. Choose "Print" and the browser's print dialog opens on the invoice exactly as it reads on screen, laid out for paper; there's no separate PDF export step. Printing is available on any invoice that isn't a draft and isn't void — a draft has nothing final to print yet, and a void invoice shouldn't circulate as if it were live.

Not every action is available on every invoice — a paid invoice can't be voided the way a draft can, for instance. The folio only shows what actually applies to the invoice's current status.

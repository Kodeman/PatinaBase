When you invite someone into the studio, two separate questions get answered, not one. The first is their seat: owner (you — the studio can't be left without one), admin, member, or guest. The second is whether they're a Designer or a plain member/collaborator — that choice is independent of seat, and it controls whether they're granted designer-level standing in the studio's own work, separate from how senior their membership is.

In practice, a member seat is a full working seat. Someone you invite as a member has full access to the studio's data — the same documents, the same rooms and ledgers you do — just without the rights to manage who else is in the studio. Inviting or removing other people, changing someone's seat, or transferring ownership stays with owner and admin. Day-to-day work — opening documents, drafting proposals, drawing invoices, placing orders — isn't gated by that ladder at all.

So the honest answer to "what can my first hire see" is: nearly everything you see, from day one. The studio doesn't hand a new person a cut-down view of the same desk — it hands them the same desk. The one thing worth deciding deliberately when you send the invite is whether they're joining as a Designer or as a Member/collaborator, since that shapes how their own work gets attributed and credited going forward, not what doors are open to them.

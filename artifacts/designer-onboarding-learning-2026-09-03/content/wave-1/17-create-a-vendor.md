Open ⌘K and choose "Add a maker" — under Begin, described as "A maker on your roster." Or, from the People room, use "+ Add person" and set the record's type to maker rather than client or trade. Both roads land on the same form.

You'll want a name and enough contact information for someone in the studio to reach them — email, phone, or both. Beyond that, the form asks what they make or supply, which matters because it's how a maker turns up later: when you're drawing a purchase order in Orders, the vendor list you pick from is built from this same roster, so a maker worth ordering from is worth a minute here first.

A rating and a note about past work are optional, but worth filling in if you've worked with the maker before — the People room's "Your Eye" shelf collects that judgment over time, so a maker you rated once is a maker your first hire doesn't have to guess about later.

Once saved, the maker shows up in People under Directory, in Orders under Vendors, and in ⌘K's search results by name. There's no separate "vendor list" living apart from the People room — a maker is a person in the studio's roster with a maker's fields filled in, the same record wherever you find it.

Letters and the record — what arrived, what needs review. The Post is the studio's mail and messages, gathered in one sheet away from any single project. Reach it with `G` then `T`, or the bell at the right of the drawer, which carries a small dot when something's waiting — never a number.

It's organized in two tabs. LETTERS is correspondence — messages from clients, notes from makers, anything that arrived and is waiting on a read or a reply. THE RECORD is the studio's own history of what happened: sends, signatures, payments, deliveries — the events themselves, not conversation about them, so a designer can reconstruct what actually occurred on a job without digging through a dozen documents.

"Mark all read" clears the unread state across everything at once, for a Monday morning when three days of mail arrived while you weren't looking.

The Post is deliberately separate from any project's own margin, where decisions and messages specific to that job gather beside the work. The Post is the studio-wide inbox and ledger; the margin is where a single job's own conversation lives. Between the two, nothing that arrived or happened should be findable in only one of them and nowhere else.

Esc puts The Post back and returns you to whatever document you were holding.

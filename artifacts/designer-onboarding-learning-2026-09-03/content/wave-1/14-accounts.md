Invoices drawn and paid, receivables, the studio's earnings. Accounts is the studio's money ledger — reach it with `G` then `A`, and it slides over whatever document is in hand.

Every invoice the studio has drawn lives here, newest first, each stamped with where it stands: draft, sent, partially paid, paid, or void. A draft is still yours to edit; once it's issued and sent, the client sees exactly what you see. Payments recorded against an invoice update its status automatically — there's no separate reconciliation step, and no spreadsheet running alongside this one.

Accounts also holds the studio's receivables — what's owed and not yet collected — and a running read on earnings, so a designer can tell at a glance whether the quarter is ahead or behind without pulling a report from somewhere else.

An individual invoice opens as its own folio, where the real work happens: issuing it, sending it, recording a payment against it, resending it, voiding it if it was drawn in error, and printing it, which the folio does by printing itself rather than generating a separate PDF. "How do I void, settle, or print an invoice?" walks through that folio in detail.

Esc puts Accounts back and returns you to whatever document you were holding.

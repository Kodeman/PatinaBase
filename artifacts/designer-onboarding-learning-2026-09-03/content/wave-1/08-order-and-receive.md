A single item orders from the FF&E schedule itself — "Order with Assistant" on the line. Placing several with the same maker at once starts instead from Orders' own Vendors tab, where "Order all — N items" queues every approved, unordered line you hold with that vendor. Either door opens the Order Assistant, which walks the purchase order through in steps rather than one long scroll: review the items and a deep link into the vendor's own portal (with a copy-details helper so you're not retyping specs by hand), then confirm the vendor's PO number, ETA, and payment terms, then a short confirmation once it's created.

If you're ordering through Patina's own catalog rather than an outside vendor, the flow shortens: the details step is skipped, and confirming hands you straight to Stripe's hosted checkout to pay at order time.

Every purchase order you've placed lands in Orders, one of the four Sheets — drawn through delivered, in one list.

Receiving is a separate moment, once the box actually arrives. On desktop, a short drawer records the outcome — clean, or something short of it — and any notes, against the delivered PO; if fewer pieces arrived than were ordered, the count itself pushes the outcome toward "partial" until you say otherwise. Anything short of clean auto-drafts a damage claim so it's not something you have to remember to start separately. The fuller, photo-rich version of receiving is built for the phone in the field, where the box actually is — desktop is the quick fallback when it's already sitting at the studio.

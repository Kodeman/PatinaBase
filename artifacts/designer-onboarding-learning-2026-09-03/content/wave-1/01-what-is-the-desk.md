The Desk is `/desk` — the only page you land on after signing in. There is no other default route, and no dashboard sitting in front of it: no metric tiles, no badges, no activity feed. It shows every live job you're carrying, grouped by stage, one line each.

Everything you see there is either a document you can pick up or a Verb you can start. A document is one client engagement — Brief through Care, seven sections walked in order. Picking one up opens it at `/doc/[id]`. The Verbs are the five front doors that begin something new rather than resume something that exists: Capture a lead, Open a project, Draft a design agreement, Draw an invoice, Add a maker.

The quiet rows on the Desk are still in motion — a job that hasn't needed you today is not a job that stalled. Nothing on the Desk is trying to tell you to check in more; the studio promise is that you'll notice Patina less over time, not more.

Three Rooms are reachable from anywhere on the Desk and beyond: Library, People, and The Scans. A fourth, the Drafting Room, only appears once a proposal is in hand. Four Sheets slide over whatever you're holding without disturbing it — Orders, Accounts, Hours, and The Post — all reachable from the Studio Drawer, the fixed strip at the bottom of the screen.

If you're arriving from another studio tool, ⌘K (or Ctrl K) will usually understand your old vocabulary: type "POs" and it resolves to Orders, "moodboards" resolves to Boards, "timesheet" resolves to Hours. You don't need to relearn the words before you can find the door.

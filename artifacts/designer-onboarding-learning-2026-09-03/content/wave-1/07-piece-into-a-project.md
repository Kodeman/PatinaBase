Every piece in the Library has its own Room — The Piece — reached by opening any item from the three-shelf catalog (your own pieces, the studio's, and the marketplace). Inside, you'll find "Add to Project" alongside the option to adapt the piece first if it needs a change before it's right for the job.

Choosing Add to Project doesn't just drop the item into a list — it opens a short sheet asking which project and, once you've picked one, where the piece belongs inside it. That's what makes the FF&E schedule you see later actually organized by room rather than a flat pile of everything you've ever specified.

Once it's in, the piece carries its own life from there: specified, quoted, approved, ordered, in production, shipped, delivered, installed — the eight stages the FF&E schedule tracks for every line. You don't have to move it through those stages by hand from the Library; that happens as the order itself progresses.

If you found the piece from the catalog's quick view rather than its full Room page, the same action is there too — Add to Project sits next to Add to favorites, so a piece you're only saving for later and a piece you're specifying right now are two different, deliberate choices rather than one button doing both.

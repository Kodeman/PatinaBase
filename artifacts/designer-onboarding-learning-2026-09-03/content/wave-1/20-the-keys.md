Everything in the studio that has a keyboard shortcut, on one page — so a designer who skipped the walkthrough, or dismissed a margin note, still has somewhere to look them up.

Anywhere in the portal, ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows) finds anything, and Escape closes whatever's open. Press `?` — anywhere you're not typing, with nothing else open — and this same page rises as a sheet, so you never have to leave what you're doing to look a shortcut up. Every room and ledger takes two keys: press `G`, then the second key within about a second — `L` for Library, `P` for People, `R` for The Scans, `O` for Orders, `A` for Accounts, `H` for Hours, `T` for The Post. These are the same chips you've seen trailing the rows inside ⌘K — this page is just where they're gathered without needing to open the palette first.

While writing a note — in the margin, in a thread, on a board — ⌘+Enter (Ctrl+Enter on Windows) saves it without reaching for a button.

The Board Room, the studio's mood-board canvas, carries its own full set: undo and redo, copy and paste, duplicate, lock, bring-forward and send-back, delete, an auto-tidy layout, present mode, and zoom controls — a design-tool-grade set that nothing else in the portal has taught until now. The full list is below.

And inside the Desk walkthrough itself, Enter advances to the next stop (or finishes, on the last one), and Escape skips it.

None of this replaces the mouse. It's here for the days the mouse is slower than you are.

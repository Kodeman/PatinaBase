Editions drive the hero and its crop, chapter headlines and sentences, their order, and the captions. The card is not part of the edition; code draws it from what is open.

Every row is a decision for the founders, not something the mockup settles. The closest miss is the $100.00 invoice due today: it only appears on the papers view. A variant would let the card show a third line when something is due today.

Kept, in every direction

  • The studio is the author; Patina only signs the foot

  • Nothing measures June: no counts, streaks or bars
  • Status in words, never red; green only on Pay and Approve
  • 16px text, 44px targets, works at 390 and 1440

Open to change, as your decision

  • The purpose sentence on arrival
  • The six-link row as page furniture
  • Three standing lines above the first image
  • Every invoice visible on the main page
  • Photographs capped at 72% of the screen
  • No image above the first money act

Measured on every arrival screen: no more than 40 words, and photographs covering at least half the screen.

The left column comes from the vision rulings (V8, V9, V23) and the brand voice; nobody touched it. The right column comes from the usability spec; Kody let the teams propose changes, so each direction lists the ones it makes.

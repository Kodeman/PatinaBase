# Room Proof — The whole job

User correction: the room contains dozens of lines; FF&E is a serious editor; opening a job must keep the current continuous page flow and document feeling.

## Direction
Retain the approved room photograph and warm stock, but the photograph is a room frontispiece, never the item database. A continuous job paper carries approvals → schedule → pieces → financials → closeout → record, matching the current active-project flow. A quiet right-margin running index moves to anchors on that paper.

The FF&E schedule unfolds exactly where Pieces lives. It borrows the page width for working tables, then closes to the same room summary. Five lenses change the columns/instruments on the same set of lines; they are not five destination pages. The project title, room scope, item identity, revision and return stay present. An item unfolds on its row; the job remains behind it.

Paper #f7f5ef, working white #fffefa, ink #302e2a, muted #69665f, moss #4c624f, warm rule #d4cec1. Playfair for the job and chapter titles; Inter for writing, fields and figures. Tabular numerals. No status conveyed only by color.

Reading: [large job paper and room plate | running index / contextual note].
Working: [job / return] [five lenses] [room scope / search / filter] [dense editable sheet at full useful width].

## Brief review before building
Round two made each operational act its own destination and treated four photograph pins as the project. This pass changes the structure, not just the skin: one scrolling paper; 80 sample FF&E lines including 36 in the living room; complete operation-specific tables; explicit unfolding and returning. The margins and photo become quieter while doing detailed work.

## Demonstrate
Room filtering/search, keyboard line entry and paste preview, library selection, editable quantities and units, placement/grouping/removal with undo, in-place specifications including COM and labor, two-way pricing and target/tax totals, cost-change resolution, locked issued/released values, client-safe purchasing-authorization preview, finish surfaces and print preview, and stable return to the room on the same job page.

## Boundaries
Static local prototype, fictional sample data. No production modifications, sends, purchases or authorization grants. All production capabilities must be mapped as working here, shown as a study, or explicitly deferred. Client edition privacy must be visible, and a price or design decision must never imply purchase authority.

05 / 304 · The principleuplift.md (a), (b) · BRIEF §4

Separation and depth, without a shadow

The Desk stopped bleeding together because four things happened at once. The uplift makes those four moves into seven shared components and one depth ladder, and applies them everywhere by a single test.

  1. An edge you can seeEvery unit that is one thing gets a 1 px --card-edge at 3.21:1. Hairlines (1.41–1.48:1) only separate rows; they never stand as a boundary.
  2. Sections closeEvery section ends on its own --rule-mid at full measure. Nothing closes because the next thing opened.
  3. Colour orientsOne form only, meaning stage: a 3 px edge on a job's card or title, a 2 × 24 px tick beside a job's row. State is always an ink word. Terracotta-ink marks only an overdue, blocked or do-not-contact word.
  4. Stocks layerDepth is the order of the paper stocks, the weight of the edge, and the space around a unit, on the 24 px module.

The card test

A unit earns a card only when it is one thing someone opens, holds something to do or see now, sits in a set bounded by a rule, and is ranked. Everything else is a ledger row, a plate with a caption, or text. Cards are promotions, capped at six per section, with the rest always as rows directly beneath. That is what keeps the portal from becoming the catalogue wall US-26 retired.

The depth ladder, front to back

The nearer the layer, the purer its stock and the heavier its edge. In light, purer is lighter. The dark set inverts, and its stock steps are tiny (1.05–1.30:1), so in dark the edge carries the depth. Each swatch shows the unit on the stock it sits on; ratios are the edge against the unit's face, then against the ground.

06 / 305 · The systemuplift.md (b) · specimens/shared.css

Seven components, reused unchanged

The same card, section and ledger row recur in every area. A component reused beats a variant; a variant needs a reason in its area. Every specimen in this deck draws from one stylesheet, specimens/shared.css, verbatim, and no specimen restyles it.

  1. 1 · Section .section

    An eyebrow (clay ornament, DM Mono 12/16 caps, controls on its baseline), an optional title, the body, then its own closing --rule-mid at full measure. Spacing 48 / 72 / 24 / 12. Never a box; never inside a card or a sheet.

  2. 2 · Card .card

    Eyebrow (kind or stage · holder when not yours · freshness), a name link whose upper block is the hit area, lines split by --rule-hair, a foot with one act and at most one tertiary. --paper-doc, 1 px --card-edge, 2 px radius; a job's left side is the stage edge. Grid 3 / 2 / 1-up at 900 / 600. Lifted (L3) while its sheet is open.

  3. 3 · Ledger row .lrow

    Name (a .t-d4 link), a sentence, when, one right-aligned act; columns at 900 and up, stacking below. A job's row carries the 2 px tick. Full-measure separators. State is an ink word, never a chip.

  4. 4 · Sheet, popover, modal .sheet · .popover · .modal

    One stock for every sheet: --paper-doc, 1 px --card-edge, 2 px radius, retiring three stocks and the 5 px radius. A mono head with “Put back · Esc”; a foot with at most one filled act. Never a sheet over a sheet. Focus traps on the head and returns to the opener, whose unit lifts.

  5. 5 · Empty state .empty-t

    The section's eyebrow, one sentence with its starting act inline, the closing rule. Never a dashed box, never a rule with nothing above it. One exception: a section of cards that empties keeps one card frame for its sentence.

  6. 6 · Header line .hline

    One 44 px row on --paper: a back act when nested, the title, acts at the right (a secondary ＋ creates; tertiary Find, with ⌘K shown only for a fine pointer), then the strong rule. Sticky only on long lists, and then opaque. At 390, two rows with full accessible names.

  7. 7 · Stage edge .st-*

    A 3 px rule on a job card's left side or a document's title block, or a 2 × 24 px tick beside a row's name. Only on a unit that stands for a job. The stage word always prints in ink beside it. CanvasText in forced colours.

  8. Supporting parts

    Plate and caption (A10); the ink state word; the note edge --edge-note; facts as a dl; money rows; house fields and checks; the dock, an L1 bar on a --hairline-strong rule. Targets are 44 px for every act, filter word, name link, fold and Undo.

Where the seats split, and how it resolves

  • Empty state: one sentence and a rule. Dashed means “not filled in” and survives only on the A15 stamp and the canvas's drop zones.
  • Library results, the Boards index, judgments: plate and caption by the card test (U-Q20). A job's own boards tab is bounded, so it takes cards.
  • Help topics: rows; they are scanned by name and grow.
  • A person's record: a laid paper page view with “← Studio contacts”, not a card and not in place.
  • The note edge: 2 px oak. 3 px is the stage width.

What stays deliberately different

  • The Document is a paper. Only the Desk has stage-coloured job cards.
  • The Build room keeps its white drafting stock and A15 stamps (V15 Q2).
  • Money is a ledger. The board canvas is the designer's. Print stays print stock. The galley keeps its sheet.
  • Auth and (legal) take the token pass only.

The rule

Today is the top five of one ranked list of open needs and needs settled today. Settling never changes rank, so no slot ever refills. A newly arrived need that outranks a line enters Today (M7), and the displaced line moves to the ledger with its state. Needs past five live in Also yours and settle in place there. Today never shows more than five lines. A slot frees only when a need is resolved at its source, such as an invoice paid.

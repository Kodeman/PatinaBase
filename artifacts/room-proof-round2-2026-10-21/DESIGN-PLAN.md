# Room Proof — a working rhythm

The approved room-led composition remains. This round changes the hierarchy around it, not the theme. The left side holds the room, piece or working document; the right side holds its current standing and the next named act.

Paper #FCFAF6, stock #E8E3DB, ink #2C2926, quiet #65594E, annotation blue #355267, agreement green #3E5145. Playfair expresses the room and meaningful totals; Inter carries work; DM Mono is reserved for document references and short annotations. Avoid new decorative labels.

One global masthead carries studio destinations and a project selector. A contextual project row carries room, piece, agreement, approval, flow, account and specification book. Local tabs remain inside the document. The desktop bottom dock and walkthrough strip leave the working surface; mobile receives compact task navigation. Founder controls belong in the presentation, outside the product.

The desk is a composed contact sheet of six fictional projects on the left, with named work on the right. The Lake Mendota project retains the full interactive workflow. Other projects are clearly labelled illustrative project overviews, not live or cloned transactional data.

Operational views use compact running heads and meaningful live figures. Approval preparation puts the existing issued revision on the left and the question and reviewers on the right. A common margin shows independent design, purchase and money facts, followed by the order's release outcome and its blockers. It does not imply one fact grants another.

Mobile is deliberately reordered for three tasks: inspect a room decision, enter time, receive an item. Billing state remains visible without sideways reading. All existing business-state distinctions and sample limits remain.

The presentation will include current/proposed comparison, six desktop scenes, three mobile task views, a state comparison, and the agreed feedback. No backend, production, sends, payment or purchase authority action is introduced.

Review against the brief: the paper and type palette are explicitly approved; originality should come from the room-to-evidence relationship and distinct document forms. We will not disguise a generic dashboard with cream paint. The contact sheet, evidence-led approval and working ledgers must each earn their composition.

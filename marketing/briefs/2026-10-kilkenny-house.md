# The Kilkenny house

Every Madison studio walks the Parade. They see finished rooms and never the job behind them: the
brief, the revisions, the morning the finish carpenter needed an answer. This batch shows one job
from the inside, a real house in a real Parade neighborhood, designed by Leah's studio and built by
a Sauk City crew that does its own millwork [C25, C26].

The studio story is the handoff. Leah writes the direction once, on one page per client [C04]; the
Kippley crew works from it without a phone call [C30, todo]; the client looks at the same page [C05].
That is the promise in a form a studio owner can walk through and touch.

Angles worth testing in the concept step:

- The page behind the rooms: "You've walked the house. This is how it was run."
- The crew that didn't call: the finish carpenter opens the page on site and the answer is there.
- One direction, agreed: the client, the studio and the builder all looking at the same thing.

Sequence: posts land as the build reaches each beat (brief now, millwork over winter, install in
spring); poster, email and the studio one-pager go out once MABA publishes the 2027 Parade dates;
the flyer prints for Parade week.

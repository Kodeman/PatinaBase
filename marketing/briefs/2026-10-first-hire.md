# First hire, double the work

The studio has more houses than hands. The owner is hiring her first designer or assistant, and
the hardest part is not the hire; it is handing over a job that only exists in her head
("It would've been quicker to do it myself" [C11]).

Patina is one living page per client, from brief to install [C04]. The new hire opens it and sees
where the job stands. The client sees the same page [C05]. The owner goes back to the drawings.

Angles worth testing in the concept step:

- The handoff: the morning the new hire opens the page and does not need to ask.
- The two clocks: twice the houses, the same owner, one page instead of a head full of notes.
- The process that outlives the person who wrote it ("You can't duplicate yourself" [C10]).

The ask in every piece is the same, and small: bring one live project.

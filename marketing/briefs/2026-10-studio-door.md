# The studio door

patina.cloud today speaks to homeowners and makers ("A more personal way to find what belongs",
the Founding Circle of 200, "For makers"). The only door a studio can walk through is the
Founding 50 page for designers, and even that page never says what the studio gets. This batch is
the studio-facing story the site is missing (VISION V3: the marketing has been pointed at the
wrong door).

What a studio gets, in the studio's words: one living page per client, from brief to install
[C04]. The new hire opens it and sees where the job stands. The client sees the same page [C05].
No dashboards, no task manager, no tab bars [C06]. The owner goes back to the drawings.

Angles worth testing in the concept step:

- The door: "You've seen the rooms. This is the page behind them."
- The handoff: the morning the first hire opens a client's page and does not need to ask.
- The process that outlives the person who wrote it ("You can't duplicate yourself" [C10]).

The ask, every piece, small and real: apply to the Founding 50, bring one live project [C23].

## P06 · One-pager · Studio leave-behind

**Title:** The page behind the Kilkenny house

**The year you hand off the job.** Patina is built for the year a studio brings on its first designer
or assistant and its workload doubles [C03]. The job has lived in your head. Now someone else has to
carry it, and so does the builder's crew.

**One page, from brief to install.** Every decision for the Kilkenny house lives on one living page
[C04]. No dashboards, no task manager, no tab bars [C06]. Leah writes the direction once. The
Kippley finish carpenters work from it on site. The client reads the same page and sees one agreed
direction [C05].

**Where it stands.** Patina is pre-launch [C24]. It starts with studios in Madison and Milwaukee
and grows outward through Leah's network [C08]. What a studio gets today is the page per client and
a seat at the table while it takes shape.

**What it asks of you.** One live project and an honest hour. Apply to the Founding 50, a circle of
working designers shaping Patina; Kody and Leah read every application [C23].

**CTA:** Apply at patina.cloud/designers and bring one live project.

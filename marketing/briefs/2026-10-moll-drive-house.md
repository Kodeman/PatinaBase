# The Moll Drive house

Parade visitors walk finished rooms and picture their own. They never see how the rooms came
together: the brief, the choices, the questions from the site. The Moll Drive house is Leah's work
at Middle West Studio [C28], built by a Sauk City crew that frames, finishes and builds its own
millwork [C25, C26], open to the public for two weekends [C27].

The story is honest and simple: this is what working with Middle West Studio looks like from the
finished side. Bring your own house to Leah, and the two of you work from one page, from brief to
install, so you always see the same direction your designer does [C04, C05].

Angles worth testing in the concept step:

- Every room began as a brief: the decisions you can't see from the doorway, and how yours would start.
- The warm wood: one piece, the drawing and the crew behind it.
- Ask for Leah: a designer talking with a homeowner, standing in a finished kitchen.

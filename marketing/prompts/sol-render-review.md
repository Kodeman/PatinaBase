# The Press — review of rendered pieces in {{run}}

You are reviewing finished renders of Patina marketing pieces ({{pieces}}). The images attached
above are the rendered outputs: each is labelled with its file name, which starts with the piece
id. `voice.md` and `art-direction.md` are attached as the canon to judge them against.

Look at each render as the studio owner who will see it in a feed, an inbox or on a wall: a
growing design studio at the moment it adds its first hire while the work doubles.

Check each render for:

- Words: anything vague, untrue, salesy or off-voice. No "AI", "artificial intelligence",
  "machine learning", "algorithm" or "powered by" (the intelligence is Designer-Taught
  Intelligence, outcome first). No Pledge language and never the held tagline. Numbers on the
  page must trace to a verified claim (copy cites them as `[Cnn]`; the markers are stripped
  in render, so flag any number that looks unsupported).
- Craft against `art-direction.md`: palette, type, composition for the channel, legibility at
  the real size, crop, text over busy image areas, clipped or overflowing text, wrong aspect.
- Place: Midwest only. People and rooms that read as a working studio, not a showroom.

## Output

A Markdown report with one section per piece (`## P01`). Under each heading, a numbered list of
findings, each with: what you see, why it matters in one sentence, and the concrete fix (copy
change, crop, swap image, move text). Write "No findings." for a clean render. No praise.

# The page behind the next house

## The year you hand off the job

Patina is built for the year a studio brings on its first designer or assistant and its workload doubles [C03]. The job has lived in your head. Now your new hire has to carry it, and so does the builder's crew.

## One page, from brief to install

One living page per client, from brief to install [C04]. No dashboards, no task manager, no tab bars [C06]. We're building it so your new hire and the crew can work from it, and your client sees the same page: one agreed direction [C05].

## The next house, not this one

Patina is pre-launch [C24]. Our studio designed the Moll Drive house's interiors [C28] before the page was ready, so it did not run on Patina. It is the kind of job we are building the page for. We start in Madison and Milwaukee and grow outward through Leah's network [C08].

## What it asks of you

One live project, the one you most want to hand off, and an hour with us. Apply to the Founding 50, a circle of working designers shaping Patina; Kody and Leah read every application [C23].

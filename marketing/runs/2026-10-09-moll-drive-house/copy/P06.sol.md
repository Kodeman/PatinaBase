# The page behind the next house

## The year you hand off the job

Patina is built for the year your studio brings on its first designer or assistant and the workload doubles [C03]. The decisions that lived in your head now need somewhere the new hire can find them.

## A direction someone else can carry

We're building one living page per client, from brief to install [C04]. The aim is a place for the new hire to find the direction and for the client to see that same page [C05]. No dashboards, task manager or tab bars [C06].

## The next house, not this one

The Moll Drive house did not run on Patina. It's the kind of job we're building the page for. Patina is pre-launch [C24], starting with studios in Madison and Milwaukee before growing outward through Leah's network [C08].

## What it asks of you

Easel sign at the house for the second weekend, at the channel size with the channel's bleed, print
by Thursday. Read from six feet by Parade visitors, most of them homeowners walking through.

Layout: J04 fills the top half. The headline sits under it in Playfair, the subhead beneath in a
smaller weight, so a visitor reads who designed the rooms and who built them before anything else.
The body is set small in the bottom quarter and opens on "Designers:", so the one ask is plainly
theirs; nothing on the sign asks a homeowner to do anything. No QR code; the words carry it. The
Patina mark once, small, at the foot.

Image rule: if J04 is the Midjourney texture, it runs with no caption and no credit line, and
nothing on the sign calls it the house or Kippley's work. If a real detail photo of the house
replaces it, it carries a small photographer credit and still no description beyond the credit.

Honesty: the house was not run in Patina. The body says it is the kind of job Patina is being built
for and that the next one can run from the page; keep that tense if the wording changes. No
homeowner-client line, no day or hours for Leah on this sign.

# The Moll Drive house

Studios walk the Parade to see finished rooms. They never see the job behind them: the brief, the
revisions, the questions from the site. The Moll Drive house is Leah's work, built by a Sauk City
crew that frames, finishes and builds its own millwork [C25, C26], open to the public for two
weekends [C27].

The story is honest and simple: this is the kind of job Patina is being built for. It was run the
way most studios run a house. The next one runs from one living page per client, from brief to
install [C04], that a new hire or a builder's crew can pick up without the owner retelling it.

Angles worth testing in the concept step:

- Every room began as a brief: the decisions you can't see from the doorway.
- The warm wood: one piece, the drawing and the crew behind it.
- Ask for Leah: a studio owner talking to another studio owner, standing in a finished kitchen.

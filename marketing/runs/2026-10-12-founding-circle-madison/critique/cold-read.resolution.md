# Cold read resolution: Founding Circle, Madison

The cold reader read as a studio owner at the first-hire moment. This batch is the Founding Circle campaign Kody asked for ("targeting home owners for the Founding Circle in the Madison area"), ruled under VISION-DECISIONS V21 and voice.md §1: it speaks to Madison-area homeowners about their own homes. Findings that only ask for a studio-owner address are therefore rejected. Findings about truth, labelling, claim markers, the tagline and readiness are judged on their merits.

P03 #2: accept: "Image: an illustration." read like a stray production note, so it is cut from the caption and the label now sits on the image as a credit (the notes say so, and the alt text opens with it).
P04 #1: reject: the pin is for a homeowner planning her own room this fall, which is the brief's stated moment, so "rooms for clients" would mismatch both the audience and the image.
P05 #1: accept: a placeholder line can't stay in a final, and the run cannot verify a personal detail, so the sentence is removed and the letter reads complete without it.

# A place at the table

## The room you keep looking at

Fall comes to Madison and the light drops low across rooms you know by heart. The living room that never got finished. The dining table you want made to last, in white oak that will look better in twenty years. A home like that is made by people: a designer's eye for the whole house, and Midwest makers who let the grain show.

## A seat while it is still being built

An invitation in, not a purchase: the app and marketplace are pre-launch [C24].

- First access: first in when the app launches, first to test it as it's built [C34].
- Real influence: monthly questions on makers to bring in and styles to explore [C35].
- Behind the curtain: updates from Kody and Leah as it takes shape [C36].
- Your name at launch, always a founder: credited by name, for good [C37].

## Who is building it

Kody and Leah, at Middle West Studio in Madison, Wisconsin [C01]. Patina starts with design studios in Madison and Milwaukee and grows outward through Leah's network [C08]. It is rooted here, and the circle is open wherever you call home [C39].

## What it asks of you

Nothing to buy and nothing to shop. Join at patina.cloud/founding, and your membership begins with a first letter from Kody [C38]. The circle is limited to 200 founding members [C33]. After that, your part is the one you choose: answer the monthly questions, read the updates, and be among the first through the door.

**Join the Founding Circle at patina.cloud/founding.**

Kody and Leah

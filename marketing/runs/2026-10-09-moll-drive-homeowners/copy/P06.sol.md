# What you can't see from the doorway

## The part you can't walk through

At the Moll Drive house, you can walk through the finished rooms Middle West Studio designed [C28]. What you cannot see from the doorway is how a home takes shape: the brief, the choices, the questions from the site. If you are picturing your own home, that is where our conversation begins.

## A direction you can both see

On your home, Middle West Studio will work with you from one living page, from brief to install [C04]. You and your designer will see the same direction there [C05]. The page is designed without dashboards, a task manager or tab bars for you to learn [C06].

## Where the page stands

The Moll Drive house was designed before the Patina page was ready. It did not run on Patina. Patina is a venture of Middle West Studio in Madison [C01], and its app is pre-launch [C24]. The page is for the work we would do together on your home, not a story about how this house was built.

## What it asks of you

Just a conversation about your own home. You can start with the room you keep thinking about, or the question you have not found a place to ask. Leah will listen before anything goes on a page.

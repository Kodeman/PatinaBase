### Designed by Middle West Studio, Madison [C28]. Built by Kippley Custom Carpentry.

### The hands

Kippley Custom Carpentry built the house. The family-owned Sauk City builder has built homes since 1987 with its own framing and finishing carpenters [C25]. Their shop builds its own mantels, built-ins and cabinetry [C26]. It is work you notice up close.

### The house

A traditional home with warm wood accents, a kitchen built around a generous island and walk-in pantry, rear windows onto the landscape, and a finished walkout lower level [C29]. Rendering: Kippley Custom Carpentry.

### Picturing your own house

From the doorway you can't see how these rooms came together: a brief, then a long run of decisions. Leah would work with you from one living page, from brief to install [C04]. That page is Patina, Middle West's own venture in Madison [C01]. This house did not run on Patina. Ask Leah how she would approach your own rooms.

# The page behind your house

## What you can't see from the doorway

At the Moll Drive house, you can walk the finished rooms: the warm wood, the kitchen's generous island, the rear windows onto the landscape [C29]. Middle West Studio designed these rooms [C28]. What no doorway shows is how a room comes together: the brief, the choices, the questions from the site.

## One page, from brief to install

When Middle West works with you, your house lives on one living page, from brief to install [C04]. Your designer writes the direction once. You see the same page she does: one agreed direction you can both refer to [C05].

## Where it stands, plainly

That page is Patina, a venture of Middle West Studio in Madison [C01], and it is pre-launch [C24]. The Moll Drive house was designed before the page was ready; it did not run on Patina.

## What it asks of you

A conversation about your own home. Start with the room you keep thinking about, the light you want, what has to stay. Leah listens before anything goes on a page, then tells you how the two of you would start: with a brief, on one page, all the way to install [C04]. Rendering: Kippley Custom Carpentry.

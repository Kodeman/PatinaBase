Middle West Studio · Waunakee · Fall Parade, October 9–11 and 16–18 [C27]

# The page behind your house

<!-- Image band: J06, a real credited photograph of the Moll Drive house, no people, or the builder's rendering credited "Rendering: Kippley Custom Carpentry". Credit line under the band. Never a generated image. -->

## What you can't see from the doorway

At the Moll Drive house, you can see the finished rooms: the warm wood, the kitchen's generous island, the rear windows onto the landscape [C29]. Middle West Studio designed these rooms [C28], and you may be picturing your own. What no doorway shows is how a room comes together: the brief, the choices, the questions from the site. That is where working with Middle West begins.

## One page, from brief to install

When Middle West works with you, your house lives on one living page, from brief to install [C04]. Your designer writes the direction once. You see the same page she does: one agreed direction you can both refer to [C05]. Leah will talk you through the choices for your house.

## Where it stands, plainly

That page is Patina, a venture of Middle West Studio in Madison [C01], and it is pre-launch [C24]. The Moll Drive house was designed before the page was ready; it did not run on Patina. It is the kind of house Middle West will work on with you from one shared page, going forward.

## What it asks of you

A conversation about your own home, whether you are building, remodeling or furnishing. Start with the room you keep thinking about, the light you want, what has to stay. Leah listens before anything goes on a page, then tells you how the two of you would start: with a brief, on one page, all the way to install [C04].

**Talk to Leah at Middle West Studio: middlewest.studio**

Kody and Leah, Middle West Studio

# Everyone on the job, reading the same page

<!-- Image band: J01, a designer, a contractor and a client in a framed-out kitchen in a new-build house, reading one floor plan on a tablet. Label it as an illustration; never caption it as a real project, client, studio or house. -->

### The month every call comes to you

The client wants the latest. The contractor wants the kitchen elevation. Your new hire wants to know what was agreed on Tuesday. Every question on every job still comes to one phone, and it is yours. Patina is built for the year a studio brings on its first designer or assistant and its workload doubles [C03].

### Who reads the page

The studio keeps one living page per client, from brief to install [C04]. Your client sees the same page: one agreed direction [C05]. Your new hire, the contractor and the trades each hold a seat on the job, and that access ends when the job does [C31]. A trade sends its insurance certificate, W-9 and licence through a link, and the studio confirms them [C32].

### What it is not

It is not a place you go. No dashboards, no task manager, no tab bars [C06]. Patina is a venture of Middle West Studio in Madison [C01], starting with studios in Madison and Milwaukee and growing outward through Leah's network [C08]. The app and the marketplace are still pre-launch [C24].

### What it asks of you

One live project: the job you are carrying now, the one with the most people calling you about it. Kody and Leah read every application [C23]. That is the whole ask.

**Apply to the Founding 50 at patina.cloud/designers and bring one live project [C23].**

Kody and Leah, Middle West Studio

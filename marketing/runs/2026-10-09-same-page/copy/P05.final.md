# One page for the studio, a seat for the job.

![Illustration: a designer, a contractor and a client in a framed-out kitchen, looking at one floor plan on a tablet.](J01)

*Illustration, not a client project.*

### The month every call comes to you

The client wants the latest. The contractor wants the kitchen elevation. Your new hire wants to know what was agreed. Every question still comes to your phone. Patina is built for the year a studio brings on its first designer or assistant and its workload doubles [C03].

### Who is on the job

The studio keeps one living page per client, from brief to install [C04]. Your client sees the same page [C05]. Your new hire, the contractor and the trades each hold a seat on the job, and that access ends when the job does [C31].

### What it is not

No dashboards, no task manager, no tab bars [C06]. Patina is a venture of Middle West Studio in Madison [C01]. The app and the marketplace are still pre-launch [C24].

### What it asks of you

Start with one live project: the job with the most people calling you about it. Kody and Leah read every application [C23].

**Apply to the Founding 50 at patina.cloud/designers and bring one live project [C23].**

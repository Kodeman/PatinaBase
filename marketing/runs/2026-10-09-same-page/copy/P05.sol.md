# The job has a page

*Madison, WI · Middle West Studio*

![Illustration of a designer, a contractor and a client looking at a tablet together in a kitchen under construction.](J01)

*Illustration, not a client project.*

## When the phone becomes the job

You bring on your first designer or assistant as the workload doubles [C03]. The client asks what was agreed. The contractor needs the current drawing. Your new hire asks where to look. Every question still comes to you.

## Who reads it

The studio keeps one living document per client, from brief to install [C04]. Your client sees the same page and one agreed direction [C05]. The contractor and trades hold seats on the job; their access ends when the job does [C31]. Trades send paperwork through a link, and the studio confirms it [C32].

## A quieter desk

No dashboards, task manager or tab bars [C06]. Patina is a venture of Middle West Studio in Madison [C01], starting with studios in Madison and Milwaukee [C08]. The app and marketplace are pre-launch [C24].

## What it asks of you

Bring a live project you know well. Kody and Leah read every Founding 50 application [C23].

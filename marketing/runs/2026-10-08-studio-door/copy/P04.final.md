When a new designer asks why the Shorewood Hills client chose the walnut, the answer should not have to come from my memory, a sketchbook and a thread of emails. The year a studio brings on its first designer is the year its work doubles [C03]. Otherwise every question comes back to my desk, and I cannot re-tell every house.

What I wanted was one page per client, from the brief to the install [C04], that she could open in the morning and know where the job stood. The client looking at that same page, one agreed direction [C05]. No dashboards, no task manager [C06]. Just the house, written down once.

That page is Patina. Kody builds it here in our studio in Madison [C01].

I'll be plain about where it stands: the app and the marketplace are pre-launch [C24]. What I can offer today is a seat in the Founding 50 [C23]. If you are hiring this season, apply at patina.cloud/designers and bring one live project; Kody and I read every application [C23].

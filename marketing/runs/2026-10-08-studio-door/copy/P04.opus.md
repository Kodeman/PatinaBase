The month we hired at Middle West Studio, here in Madison [C01], I realized I couldn't re-tell every house to the new person. Each job lived in my sketchbook, a pile of email threads and my head, so every question came back to my desk.

What I wanted was one page per client, from the brief to the install [C04], that she could open in the morning and know where the job stood. The client looking at that same page, one agreed direction [C05]. No dashboards, no task manager, no tab bars [C06]. Just the house, written down once.

That page is Patina. Kody builds it, here in our studio [C01].

I'll be plain about where it stands: the app and the marketplace are pre-launch [C24]. What a studio gets today is the page per client and a seat at the table while we shape the rest.

If you're hiring this season, apply to the Founding 50 at patina.cloud/designers and bring one live project with you [C23]. Kody and I read every application [C23].

# The handoff morning: speaker notes

A first sit-down with a studio owner, across her own work table. Eight slides, one idea each.
Say less than the slide. Let her talk on slide two.

**Cover.** Eyebrow: "The page behind the rooms". Sub-line: "One page per client your first hire can
read." Footer: Kody and Leah, Middle West Studio.

**Slide 1, the moment.** Image J05, full bleed: a small studio mid-job above a Main Street
storefront in Mineral Point. Ask her where she is: has she hired yet, or is this the month she
decides? Don't pitch here. Footer: none.

**Slide 2, the symptom in someone else's words.** No image. Read the two lines and stop. Ask which
one she has said out loud. DM Mono footer line: "Interior Designers Hub, How to delegate without
losing your mind · IDCO Studio, The interior design process outline successful studios use".

**Slide 3, what the studio gets.** Image J01, full bleed: hands opening a drawing set beside a
handwritten client page on a white oak table in Vilas. Keep it to the outcome: the new hire opens
one page and does not need to ask. Don't walk through how it works inside. Footer: none.

**Slide 4, the client on the same page.** No image. The client here is her client, seen through
her work. One agreed direction is the whole point of the slide. Footer: none.

**Slide 5, what it is not.** No image. This is the slide owners lean in on. Don't defend it with
features; the absence is the feature. Footer: none.

**Slide 6, who we are.** No image. Byline only: Kody and Leah, Middle West Studio. No titles.
Don't say any other studio uses Patina today. Footer: none.

**Slide 7, honest status.** No image. Say it plainly and move on. Don't offer dates, prices or
anything beyond a seat at the Founding 50 table while the page per client takes shape [C23]. Footer: none.

**Slide 8, the ask.** Image J06, full bleed: an open studio doorway in Bay View, late sun across
plank floors. One ask only. If she has a job in mind, ask her to name the house before you leave
the table. Footer: patina.cloud/designers.

**Closing slide.** Close-line: "Bring one live project." The Patina mark once, small, at the foot.

Images are illustrations made for this deck; never caption one as a real project or a client's
home.

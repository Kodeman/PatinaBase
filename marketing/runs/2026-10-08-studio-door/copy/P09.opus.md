# The morning she doesn't ask

Thirty seconds, vertical. Three Midjourney clips, then the ask on paper, then the mark. One idea:
the first hire picks up the job without asking, and the owner goes back to the drawings.

## Voice-over, read straight through

> It's the week your first hire starts.
>
> She opens the Shorewood client's page, brief to install [C04].
> She doesn't need to ask where the job stands.
>
> Your client is looking at the same page [C05].
> You go back to the drawings.
>
> Apply to the Founding 50 at patina.cloud/designers [C23].
> Bring one live project.

Seven lines, one breath each, read slowly and plainly, the way you'd tell a colleague. Say
"patina dot cloud slash designers" out loud.

## Scene notes

1. **J10, six seconds.** Hands press the last brass pin into the sample board. This is the first frame,
   so the piece opens on hands and material, not a logo or a title.
2. **J11, eight seconds.** A second pair of hands settles the handwritten client page flat on the white
   oak table. The new hire's hands, not the owner's.
3. **J12, six seconds.** The half-finished dining room from the doorway, the room the page is for.
   The on-screen line lands as the slide settles.
4. **J08 still, seven seconds.** The page on the table again, held still or with one slow pan. The ask
   sits on the paper in the middle third.
5. **End card, three seconds.** The Patina mark once, small, on paper. End line:
   "patina.cloud/designers. The app and marketplace are pre-launch [C24]."

## For compose

- The four scenes plus a three-second end card fill the channel's full length exactly. Skip
  the scaffold's title card, or the piece opens on type instead of hands and runs over the
  limit. The title lives in metadata only.
- On-screen text: Inter, middle third, 7 words or fewer. Strip claim markers before render.
- Sound: room tone only, or nothing. No music cue that builds or promises drama.
- The stills and clips are illustrations. Don't caption the room as a real client's home or the
  studio as a real studio.

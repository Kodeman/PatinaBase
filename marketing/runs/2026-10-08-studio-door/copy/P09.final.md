# The morning she doesn't ask

Thirty seconds, vertical. Three Midjourney clips (J10, J11, J12, all in hand), then the ask on
paper, then the mark. One idea: the first hire picks up the job without asking, and the owner goes
back to the drawings.

## Voice-over, read straight through

> The linen is still pinned.
> It's the week your first hire starts.
>
> Your new hire opens the Shorewood client's page, brief to install [C04].
> She doesn't need to ask where the job stands.
>
> Your client is looking at the same page [C05].
> You go back to the drawings.
>
> Apply to the Founding 50 at patina.cloud/designers [C23].
> Bring one live project.

Eight lines, one breath each, read slowly and plainly, the way you'd tell a colleague. Say
"patina dot cloud slash designers" out loud.

## Scene notes

1. **J10, six seconds.** Hands press the last brass pin into the sample board; the linen swatch is
   still pinned when the line lands. This is the first frame, so the piece opens on hands and
   material, not a logo or a title.
2. **J11, seven seconds.** A second pair of hands settles the handwritten client page flat on the white
   oak table. The new hire's hands, not the owner's. On-screen names the Shorewood client, since
   the piece has no recorded audio yet and the on-screen line is carrying the idea alone.
3. **J12 clip, six seconds.** The half-finished dining room from the doorway, the room the page is
   for: one slow push-in from the doorway, the clip Kody made from the J09 still. The clip carries the
   motion on its own, so no pan is added over it. The on-screen line lands as the jambs clear the frame.
4. **J08, seven seconds.** The two-person handoff frame (not the single-hand frame scene 2's clip
   already opens from), held still with one slow pan. The on-screen line is the ask itself
   ("Apply to the Founding 50" [C23]), so a viewer with sound off still reads an ask; the address lives
   on the end card alone, with no trailing period, so it reads cleanly as something to type back.
5. **End card, four seconds.** The Patina mark once, small, on paper. End line, address on its own
   line: "patina.cloud/designers" / "The app and marketplace are pre-launch [C24]."

## For compose

- The four scenes plus a four-second end card fill the channel's full length exactly. Skip
  the scaffold's title card, or the piece opens on type instead of hands and runs over the
  limit. The title lives in metadata only.
- Scene 3 is the J12 clip itself (`mj/inbox/J12/J12-1.mp4`, slowed to fill six seconds like the
  other two clips), not the J09 still with a pan that stood in for it. The J09 still is no longer
  in the cut.
- On-screen text: Inter, middle third, 7 words or fewer, end card included. Strip claim markers
  before render.
- Sound: room tone only, or nothing. No music cue that builds or promises drama.
- The stills and clips are illustrations. Don't caption the room as a real client's home or the
  studio as a real studio.

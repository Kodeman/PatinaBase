# The morning your first hire doesn't ask

<!-- Image band: J01, a designer's hands opening a drawing set beside a handwritten client page on a white oak work table. An illustration; never caption it as a real project, client home or maker's piece. -->

### The year you hire

The walnut sample is beside the drawings. Your first hire asks where the job stands. Patina is built for the year a studio brings on its first designer or assistant and its workload doubles [C03].

### What the studio gets

One living page per client, from brief to install [C04]. Your client sees the same page: one agreed direction [C05]. No dashboards, no task manager, no tab bars [C06].

### Who is behind it

Patina is a venture of Middle West Studio in Madison [C01], starting with studios in Madison and Milwaukee and growing outward through Leah's network [C08]. The app and the marketplace are pre-launch [C24].

### What it asks of you

One live project: the job you are carrying right now, the one you most want to hand off. Kody and Leah read every application [C23]. That is the whole ask.

**Apply to the Founding 50 at patina.cloud/designers, and bring one live project [C23].**

Kody and Leah, Middle West Studio

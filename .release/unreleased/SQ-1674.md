With a project's makers switched off, get_client_project_edition (00846) now rebuilds any image alt that names one of the job's makers from the studio caption, whatever the plate's kind, drops a caption what that names one, and shows the studio for a whose override that names one. Maker names are the project's vendor and vendor_name values plus the frozen facts' makerName, matched case-insensitively, at least 3 characters. Makers on is unchanged.

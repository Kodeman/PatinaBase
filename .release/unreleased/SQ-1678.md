When the compose dispatcher's sweep fails a drafting edition whose lease ran out, the project now gets one recomposition (00847): a `recompose_after_failed_draft` request through the normal 30-minute debounce, lease, attempts and two-a-day cap. No recovery is queued when the project already has an open request, is frozen, or its previous draft was swept too (N = 2). The approved-edition reader also trims en dashes and slashes from a maker-stripped caption, and a caption left without letters or digits falls back to the category or kind words.

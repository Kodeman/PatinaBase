title: Read Purchase lens readiness from the one readiness rule
bump: patch
plugins: []
---

The round-8 Purchase lens now decides Ready to order with the same readiness rule and authorization context as the line's own purchasing gate. A line with no linked maker, no client selection, no authorization or an unpaid deposit reads Not ready to order with its reason, and the lens shows nothing as ready when the job's purchasing authority cannot be read. External fulfilment lines read "Buying and payment sit outside the studio." The round-8 E2E spec now asserts that a lens change replaces the address in place.

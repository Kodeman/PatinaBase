Client service acceptance (inactive delivery)

Authenticated clients can open /acceptance/[requestId] from their house's service-acceptance door. Required unanswered recipients can accept, accept with named conditions, or return with a reason after explicit confirmation. Optional recipients and observers have a read-only view. Responses retain the presented scope/evidence hashes and signer-policy revision; identical uncertain retries reuse their request key. Permission refusals keep the client's words and show a calm paused state.

Scope and evidence are REFERENCES ONLY in this delivery: request number, scope edition, short and full hashes. Client review of actual evidence content is an activation prerequisite (SQ-2240 reader, SQ-2232 UI integration). No activation, grants, migrations, outbound sends, or legacy approval changes are included. DB-backed behavior and browser/device walks remain W6 work.

Repair: condition identifiers are server-owned; the client submits trimmed text only. Response receipts use the 00886 pending-signer array. Scope-capability pauses preserve drafts without conflating authorization refusals. Conditions are removable and bounded to 20 rows of 2000 characters; invalid entries stay editable. Waiting copy is lifecycle-aware and reflects the receipt; optional recipients and observers read neutral evidence copy.

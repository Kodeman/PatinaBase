The composer (repaired after the SQ-1592 review):

- Pictures reach a model only as each plate's stored `piece` rendition (512 or 768 px WebP), read from the media worker and sent as base64. No address, path or file name is in a request. A picture the worker cannot serve is dropped alone.
- Provenance comes from every `client_plate_requests` row for an asset, in any status. An asset with no row, or with a sheet or concept source, is never sent and never the hero.
- A flagged picture (`perceivedFlags`, or flagged in this run) is never the hero, held, or offered to the hero pick. Image rows keep `perceivedAlt`.
- A held choice must meet the held floor. Designer-framed pictures are perceived for flags and alt with a null frame. Model frames are sent on the recrop's 1e-4 grid.
- Unchanged is judged on the source hash and a fingerprint of every frozen render fact. Plates-only compares the designer-corrected chapters (by key) and doorstep, without the notice. Call B sees the corrected words, and none of them when makers are hidden.
- One lint (canon, machinery words, and model or vendor attribution with the project's own names allowed) covers the words, alt text and the hero's why. A judge that cannot answer twice leaves the deterministic headlines.
- One time budget for the whole invocation: one request is claimed at a time, only while an edition fits, and each call gets only the time left. Every failure path settles the request with its spend, or reports it unsettled.

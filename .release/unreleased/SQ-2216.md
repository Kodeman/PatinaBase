ref: SQ-2216
title: Keep external FF&E outside studio commerce and preserve native drawings
bump: patch
plugins: []
---

Lines under external responsibility now share one classification across the schedule, the Bill composer and the Price lens. The composer lists them as external work and never offers them as a checkbox or a Price it row. The Price lens gives them no studio price, edit or stamp, and lists them apart in their room. The round-8 Design chapter shows native drawings, issue history and the drawing-sets link even when the room and package read fails; that grouping appears only once it loads.

The Bought it form uses that same responsibility rule: external FF&E lines are not offered in either its global or bound-line selector. External purchase evidence remains on the C5 rail. Supersedes SQ-2213 and SQ-2205.

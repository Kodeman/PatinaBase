Closing a job now freezes its approved homeowner edition (00848). A trigger on the move to `completed` sets `frozen_at` under the per-project edition lock and records `facts.portfolioSnapshotRef`: a pointer to `projects.portfolio_snapshot` and a sha256 of it, never a copy. Approved editions of jobs completed earlier are frozen the same way. A closed job (`completed` or `archived`) refuses approve, undo, begin and finish with a plain message. The compose dispatcher marks its queued requests done with `close_reason` 'closed', and 00847's recovery queues nothing for it. `client_plate_source_owned` now fails closed: only `project_ffe_item_images`, `project_rooms`, `room_scan_images` and `project_boards` have rules, and any other table, including `field_captures`, is refused. The installed-photo lane from Field site photos is deferred pending a ruling.
